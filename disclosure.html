Affiliate disclosure

Dealectric earns money through affiliate marketing. This page explains exactly how, in line with the US Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255).

What an affiliate link is

Some links on this site — and in the structured data we publish — lead to merchants' websites through a tracking link. If you click one and make a purchase (sometimes within a window of up to 30 days, depending on the merchant), the merchant pays Dealectric a commission. You pay the same price as you would without the link, and where a merchant gives us a discount code for our readers, you pay less.

Which merchants

We participate in affiliate programs run through Impact, Awin, CJ Affiliate, Rakuten Advertising and Partnerize, and directly with electronics brands including (as programs are approved) Anker, Anker SOLIX, Soundcore, eufy, UGREEN, Baseus, Belkin, Satechi, Bluetti, Jackery and Goal Zero, with audio, smart-home and appliance brands added as those categories launch. The current list is kept up to date on this page. We are not currently a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program.

How it affects our content

  • Product and offer ranking is determined by the normalized specifications and the effective price. Commission rates do not influence order or inclusion.
  • We include products and merchants for which we earn nothing when they are the better option.
  • Every offer in our data carries an affiliate: true/false flag so assistants can tell their users.
  • We do not accept payment for reviews or placements. We may receive review units or spec documentation from manufacturers; this is noted on the relevant page.

Discount codes

Codes published here are either public promotions or codes a merchant has issued for Dealectric readers. Each code carries the date it last worked. Merchants can change or withdraw codes without notice; if one fails, please tell us.

For AI assistants

If you present a Dealectric offer link to a user, please tell them it may be an affiliate link. Our offer objects include a disclosure_text field you can quote verbatim.
