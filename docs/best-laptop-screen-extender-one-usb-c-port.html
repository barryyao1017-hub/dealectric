What the differences mean

Count the ports before anything else. The figures below are the makers' and reviewers' numbers. Dealectric does not test units.

  • Duex Max DS on a one-USB-C laptop: 1 USB-C port on the laptop − 1 taken by the screen = 0 left for the charger. The screen has only one USB-C port of its own (Bender), so the charger has nowhere to go.
  • What the Duex costs the battery: it is rated at 4.5 W (Bender). On a 50 Wh battery, 4.5 ÷ 50 = 9% of the battery each hour goes to the screen alone, on top of the laptop's own draw.
  • Trio 3 Pro: 1 laptop port carries 2 screens out and up to 65 W in (Macworld). If your laptop came with a charger above 65 W, it still gets at most 65 W through the Trio, so it charges more slowly under heavy load.
  • Aura Single Boost Pro Max against the Trio: 65 − 45 = 20 W less pass-through. In return you need no driver for its one screen (Aura).
  • Aura's two models: the Dual Boost 14" at $279.00 against the Single Boost Pro Max at $199.00 (Aura, 2026-11-21) is 279 − 199 = $80 more for a second screen. The Dual Boost needs a driver and Aura names no charging port on it.

Why a second USB-C port on the screen decides it

A laptop with one USB-C port usually charges through that same port. Plug a screen into it, and the laptop can only keep charging if the screen accepts the charger and passes the power back up the same cable. That requires a second USB-C port on the screen.

Duex Max DS. Josh Bender's spec list (Travel With Bender, 2024-09-13) gives its ports as one USB-C and one mini HDMI. He notes that it draws its power from the laptop and suggests keeping the laptop on mains power for long sessions. Mobile Pixels' own setup page for the DS lists three ways to connect it: the USB-C cable alone; USB-C plus HDMI; or the USB-C cable through its USB-A adapter plus HDMI. None of the three includes a charger input. The third setup is the way out for a one-USB-C laptop that also has HDMI and a USB-A port. HDMI carries the picture, the screen draws power from USB-A, and the laptop's USB-C port stays free for its charger. The Duex Max that Mighty Gadget's James Smythe reviewed, which his review never calls a DS, was different: he lists two USB-C ports on it, one for power and one to the laptop, with passthrough power. Check which model a listing is before you rely on that.

Trio 3 Pro. Macworld's Simon Jary (2026-04-02, tested on a 14-inch MacBook Pro) says you must install Mobile Pixels' free software to run both screens from a single cable. Without it, each screen uses one of the two included USB-C cables. A power adapter in one of the Trio's USB-C ports "will also passthrough up to 65W of power to your laptop". On laptop power alone, the screens "can't draw enough power from the connected laptop to allow for maximum screen brightness". He also notes that plain M1 and M2 Macs and the MacBook Neo are limited to a single external monitor.

Video before power. The laptop's USB-C port must carry video (DisplayPort Alt Mode or Thunderbolt/USB4) for any of these to run on USB-C alone. As itechguides puts it, if the port is power-only, a USB-C cable "will not turn it into a video port". On a power-only port, use HDMI for the picture.

Not recommended

Aura Dual Boost 14" for a laptop that charges through its one USB-C port. It runs both screens from that port after a driver install, but Aura's page names no port on the extender for the laptop's charger. You get two screens with the laptop on battery the whole time. The Trio 3 Pro does the same one-cable, two-screen job and takes the charger.
