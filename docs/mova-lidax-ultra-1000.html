Who it's for

Flat or gently sloped quarter-acre lots with shade, fences or a house close by, where an RTK mower would drop signal. Owners who will spend an hour mapping and marking no-go zones once.

Caveats

  • Week-one rescues are common: New Atlas needed about 25 rescues on day one until it mapped no-go zones around a 2-in ledge, shrubs and a driveway lip, then ran 12-plus hours with none.
  • Obstacle detection is the main reviewer complaint: inside digital saw it drive into large flat objects, and Notebookcheck found small objects were only caught at the most sensitive setting. Turn sensitivity up and leave Obstacle Avoidance on Edges enabled.
  • Rear-wheel drive with front casters digs in on soft or sandy soil and side slopes; for roots and hills choose an AWD mower.
  • Measured edge strip was about 4 in in one test despite the 2-in claim; expect to trim around fences and the dock.
  • Runtime per charge is 60–80 min, so a full quarter acre takes several charge cycles across a day.
