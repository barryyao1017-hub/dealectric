Who it's for

Laptop owners (MacBook Pro 14 and 16, Dell XPS, gaming ultrabooks) who want a 140 W bank and the fastest recharge available without paying Anker Prime money.

Caveats

  • Sustained full-load output trips thermal protection: ChargerLab's bench test cut off after 10 minutes at a constant 140 W and 23 minutes at a constant 100 W. Real laptops taper as they fill, so reviewers did not hit this in normal use, but it is not a bank for running a 140 W load flat out.
  • Gets hot when charging itself and a laptop at the same time; the display shows 0 W while overheat protection holds.
  • 1.4 lb and 6.7 in long: a bag item, not a pocket item.
  • Output over 100 W needs a 5 A E-marked cable; use the included 240 W cable.
  • The 240 W headline is the sum of the ports; the spec sheet lists 222.5 W total.
  • Usable energy is about 80-91% of the 99.36 Wh rating depending on load (ChargerLab).
  • Display is hard to read in bright sunlight.
