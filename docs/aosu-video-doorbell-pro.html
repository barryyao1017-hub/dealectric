Who it's for

Homes that want a chime and indoor recording in one box for under $100 and will not pay a monthly plan. Best when the base can sit between the router and the door.

Caveats

  • You must add a microSD card (up to 128 GB) to the base before it records anything.
  • 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only; keep the base within a room or two of the door.
  • The 180-day battery claim assumes 3 to 5 short events a day; AOSU's forum shows owners getting about 2 months at busy doors.
  • Battery only; it does not take doorbell wiring.
  • Package and familiar-face detection are paid cloud features; person detection is free.
