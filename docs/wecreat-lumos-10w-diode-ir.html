Who it's for

Craft-fair sellers and jewelry or gift makers who engrave small metal, wood and leather items on site and want to carry the machine by its handle. Buyers who need to cut sheet material or work larger than a coaster are better served by a gantry machine such as the Vista, and those who want deep metal engraving should look at the Lumos Flex or another fiber laser. On 2026-11-18 the LaserPecker LP4 was $1,039 and the xTool F2 $1,299 at their makers' stores.

Caveats

  • Tom's Hardware found no shutoff when the cover is raised mid-job and no safety glasses in the box, yet the rotary and slide extension need the cover up; it called the machine suitable for experienced users only.
  • The 3 W infrared laser engraves metal but cannot cut it, and the galvo beam cuts at a slant toward the edge of the field, which Tom's Hardware warned can spoil tight-fitting parts.
  • Tom's Hardware had to calibrate the camera, found the material presets limited, and was nagged to buy a premium membership each time the laser connected; most library designs are paid.
  • The Gadgeteer asked for better written documentation and noted that adding the Lumos over Wi-Fi means typing in its IP address, and that WECREAT warns against lengthening the exhaust hose.
  • The Essential pack is the bare machine: rotary ($299), slide extension ($399), goggles ($69) and a fume extractor (from $279) are all extra, and it shipped in 3 to 5 business days on 2026-11-18.
