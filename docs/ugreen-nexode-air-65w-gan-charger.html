Caveats

  • UGREEN's product page could not be opened on 2026-11-27 (HTTP 429), so UGREEN's own wording on AVS, weight, colours and box contents was not read. Price and stock come from the store's search listing.
  • Charge-Test (Michael Barton, 2026-09-29) found three hardware versions under one product name and model number. Version 1 has no AVS, and only Version 3 has PPS at 5–21 V and holds 65 W. Its tested unit was linked to Amazon.de, and it notes specifications may differ between markets. Which version UGREEN's US store ships is not established.
  • Charge-Test said black and silver stock appeared more likely to be earlier versions, based on a July 2026 snapshot; its orange unit was Version 3.
  • One port only: it charges a single device at a time.
  • It runs hot at full load: about 75 °C peak surface per Charge-Test, 50.9 °C per Notebookcheck (Schmidt).
  • The '50% in 15 minutes' figure for the iPhone 18 Pro Max is UGREEN's claim as reported by Notebookcheck. Charge-Test did not run an iPhone charge-time test on this charger.
  • The included cable carries data at USB 2.0 speed only (480 Mbps), per Notebookcheck (Shanto).
  • UGREEN's US store also lists a 'UGREEN x Honkai: Star Rail 65W Charger' at $22.79 (compare-at $39.99) on 2026-11-27. It is a separate listing, and its AVS support was not read.
