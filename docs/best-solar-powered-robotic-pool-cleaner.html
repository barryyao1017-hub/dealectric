Best solar powered robotic pool cleaner

Updated 2026-11-17

Short answer. The best solar powered robotic pool cleaner for floors and walls is the WYBOT S2 Solar Vision ($1,599.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/wybot-s2-solar-vision): a 30 W solar dock charges it underwater, it drives back on its own at 20% battery, and it is rated for pools up to 3,229 sq ft, so you never carry it to an outlet. What owners complain about

  • The sun can't keep up with a floor robot. This came up in four of our sources. PCWorld's full floor-and-wall run ran out of battery before the floor was done. Android Authority saw 5-7% charge per hour of sun. WYBOT's own support pages say a solar-only dock may not give enough energy for frequent cleaning of dirty pools.
  • App and connection drops. Three sources. PCWorld had disconnects and wrong battery readings, and one firmware update broke Wi-Fi until WYBOT sent a replacement. Underwater, the robot loses Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, so you set it up before it goes in.
  • Early failures and slow support. We found four owner reports across WYBOT models: a robot that started flipping after three weeks, a battery stuck near 85%, a unit that died within months, and a 2.6-of-5 average on an Australian review site for the C2. Test it well inside the 30-day return window.
  • Skimmers stall in shade and fill up fast. Solar skimmers slow down under trees and in cloudy weeks, and a 4 L basket fills fast during heavy leaf fall. The Betta also drove into the wall for an hour when the pool was overflowing in Smart Home Hookup's test.

Which one by need

  • You never want to lift a robot out, and the pool gets sun: WYBOT S2 Solar Vision. Use floor-only mode on sun power, and add the $19.99 dock adapter for daily runs.
  • A big or leafy pool with an outlet nearby: WYBOT S2. It has a 210-minute rated runtime for $759.99, and you lift it out to charge.
  • Floating leaves in a sunny pool: Betta SE, at $369.90 with 30+ hours rated per charge.
  • A shaded or screened pool: Aiper EcoSurfer S2. It used the least battery per hour in Smart Home Hookup's test.
  • A leafy pool on a budget, and you want app control: WYBOT F1, at $299.98 with a 7 L basket. Expect noise.

Not recommended

  • The S2 Solar Vision for a big, shaded or very dirty pool. Sun alone won't power a full clean every day, and the pool stays dirty between charges.
  • Bargain no-name skimmers. In Smart Home Hookup's 20-leaf test, the Wavio caught 11 leaves and almost no foam. The Betta Neo caught all 20.
