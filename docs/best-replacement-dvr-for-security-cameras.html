Best replacement DVR for security cameras

Updated 2026-11-14

Short answer. The thing that decides whether your old cameras will work is the video format, not the brand. The best replacement DVR for security cameras is the ANNKE DR88 ($99.99 at ANNKE, seen 2026-11-14). It reads all four coax formats (CVBS, AHD, HD-TVI, HD-CVI) on 8 BNC inputs, takes TVI cameras up to 4K and adds 4 IP camera channels. For 9 to 16 older 1080p cameras on the tightest budget, the ZOSI ZR16FK ($99.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/zosi-zr16fk-16-channel-1080p-dvr) reads the same four formats on 16 channels for $30 less than ANNKE's 16-channel DR165.

A 4-in-1 DVR reads CVBS, AHD, HD-TVI and HD-CVI. None of these recorders take HD-SDI cameras, and network (IP) cameras can't plug into BNC ports. Lorex MPX cameras use HD-CVI, so they are covered. Read the model number on a camera's label and look up its format before you buy.

You can keep the coax runs and camera power supplies. Move each BNC plug from the old box to the new one and fit a hard drive. If you reuse the old drive, expect to format it, which erases the old footage.

What owners complain about

The wrong video format. This is the most common problem in forum threads and store reviews. Owners with Samsung cameras and some Lorex cameras got no picture on a 4-in-1 DVR. Others moved old Q-See cameras to a ZOSI DVR and got remote viewing back. Check the camera's format first.

Recorder and hard drive failures, and support. ZOSI scores 1.3 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 550 reviews. Of the 20 newest, 9 describe a failed or rebooting recorder, 4 a hard drive problem and 15 slow or missing support. Two owners of older ZOSI recorders wrote that the clock would not set to 2026. ANNKE scores 4.4 from 3,573 reviews, and most recent reviews praise quick support. ZOSI's store warranty is 2 years from the purchase date.

A new app. Q-See owners lost remote viewing when the company closed in 2021. Any new DVR brand also means a new app, such as ANNKE Vision or ZOSI Smart.
