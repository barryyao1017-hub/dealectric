Check how your cameras get power before you swap the DVR

Matching the video format is only half the job. The other half is power. Look at a camera's cable where it reaches the camera.

  • A 12 V adapter, a 1-to-4 splitter, or Siamese cable (coax with a power pair beside it): the cameras have their own power, and any hybrid DVR above will work. ZOSI's support page tells owners with video loss to check the cameras' power supply and 1-to-4 power splitter cable, and to test each camera on another working 12 V supply. The ZR16FK ships with a 12 V / 2000 mA supply for the recorder itself. Viewtron pairs its DVR with RG59 Siamese cable that 'runs both the video and power'.
  • One coax and nothing else: the old DVR was sending power up the coax (PoC). An ordinary hybrid DVR leaves those cameras unpowered. Dahua's XVR7208A-4K-I3 runs on a 12 VDC 5 A input, draws under 15 W without drives, and lists no camera power. Dahua's XVR5108H-I3-8P has an '8-ch HDCVI PoC AT&AF Camera Power Supply' fed by 53 VDC at 1.812 A (53 × 1.812 ≈ 96 W in). The only cameras it names as powered are HDCVI PoC models. An older Dahua PoC model, the XVR5108H-4KL-X-8P, shows the limits of this design: 48 W in total, enough for 8 AF cameras (48 ÷ 8 = 6 W each) or 4 AT cameras (12 W each), with no balun or twisted-pair runs. If your PoC cameras are another brand, add a 12 V supply at each camera instead.
  • Long runs: Mammoth Security's tests got HD-CVI and HD-TVI to about 1,000 ft on RG-59, against the 'over 1,500 ft' makers claim.

Not recommended

The Dahua XVR7208A-4K-I3 is a poor replacement for most home systems. It cannot power cameras that ran on PoC. Its '4K at 30 fps' applies only to channels 1 and 5, with CVI cameras and Encode Enhancement on, and TVI and AHD cameras record 4K at 15 fps. Dahua's page gives no price. The FCC's Covered List includes Dahua Technology and Hikvision, and FCC 25-71 lets the FCC bar future imports and sales of their equipment. If your cameras are 1080p or older CVBS, a 4K recorder will not make their picture any sharper.
