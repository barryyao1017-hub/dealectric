Who it's for

Hobbyists and small-shop users scanning small mechanical parts, figurines, knobs, brackets and other objects that fit in two hands, for 3D printing or CAD reference.

Caveats

  • Shiny, black and metallic parts need matte scanning spray, which then has to be washed off.
  • Tracking can drop on smooth, symmetrical or featureless parts; add surface features or use the optional turntable.
  • JMStudio has a learning curve and has crashed on low-spec PCs; meshes are dense and usually need cleanup before printing.
  • Standard package ships with no tripod or turntable; the maker's multi-axis turntable is a separate purchase.
  • 3DMakerpro's own store has recent reviews complaining about slow shipping and support; retailers such as MatterHackers and Best Buy also stock it.
