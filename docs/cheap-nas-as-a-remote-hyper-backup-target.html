What the differences mean

Each yearly figure is watts × 8,760 hours, priced at the US residential average of 18.16¢/kWh for August 2026 (EIA). None of the figures include the drive's cost.

  • DS124: 3.44 W × 8.76 = 30.1 kWh a year with the drive hibernating, about $5.47. At Synology's 10.69 W access figure all day and night it would be 93.6 kWh, about $17.01.
  • DS223j: 4 W × 8.76 = 35.0 kWh hibernating, about $6.36. At its 16.31 W access figure, 142.9 kWh, about $25.95.
  • TerraMaster F2-212: PC-WELT's 3.9 W asleep × 8.76 = 34.2 kWh, about $6.20. Left at its 14.7 W idle, 128.8 kWh, about $23.38.

  • Small backups: at 1 TB, B2 bills $6.95 a month, and the DS124 takes about 25 months to pay back. Below 1 TB, cloud stays cheaper for more than two years.

What a backup target costs to keep running

A remote target works for perhaps an hour a night and waits the rest of the day. Its running cost is set by what it draws while waiting, not by its access figure. Synology publishes a drive-hibernation draw for both of its boxes: 3.44 W for the DS124 and 4 W for the DS223j. PC-WELT's wall measurement of the F2-212 shows what sleep is worth: 3.9 W asleep against 14.7 W idle, about 73% less. A box whose drives hibernate between nightly runs costs about a quarter as much to run as one that idles all day.

Features matter as much as watts. Synology's Hyper Backup spec lists only a remote Synology NAS or Synology C2 Storage as destinations for entire-system backup. Rsync servers and S3-compatible clouds take folder and package backups. The DS124 runs DSM 7.4, and the current Hyper Backup Vault release (4.2.2-4262, 2026-07-07) requires DSM 7.3 or later, so it can host Vault. The TerraMaster and UGREEN boxes join as rsync servers instead. TerraMaster's TOS has an rsync Server under Control Panel › Network Services › File Services, and NASCompares lists rsync among the DH2300's protocols on UGOS Pro. Any destination needs at least 8 GB free. Hyper Backup keeps up to 65,535 versions and offers AES-256 client-side encryption.
