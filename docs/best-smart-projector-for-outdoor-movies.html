What the differences mean

On-screen brightness is lumens × screen gain ÷ screen area in square feet, measured in foot-lamberts (ft-L). A 120-inch 16:9 screen is 42.73 sq ft and a 150-inch one is 66.76 sq ft. Every figure here assumes a gain-1.0 screen and Movie mode, the mode you would watch a film in. The SMPTE cinema target is 16 ft-L, within an allowed range of 12–22. ProjectorCentral suggests about 50 ft-L for a room with some light, which is closer to a backyard with porch lights or lit windows nearby.

  • Nebula X1: 2,193 ÷ 42.73 = 51.3 ft-L at 120 in; 2,193 ÷ 66.76 = 32.8 ft-L at 150 in.
  • XGIMI Horizon 20: 1,682 ÷ 42.73 = 39.4 ft-L at 120 in; 1,682 ÷ 66.76 = 25.2 ft-L at 150 in.
  • Nebula Cosmos 4K SE: 1,298 ÷ 42.73 = 30.4 ft-L at 120 in; 1,298 ÷ 66.76 = 19.4 ft-L at 150 in.
  • Nebula Mars 3: 686 ÷ 42.73 = 16.1 ft-L at 120 in; 686 ÷ 66.76 = 10.3 ft-L at 150 in, below the 12 ft-L floor.
  • Largest screen at 16 ft-L: diagonal = √(lumens ÷ 16 × 144 ÷ 0.4273). For the X1, √(137.1 × 337) = 215 in.
  • Measured against rated: X1 2,193 ÷ 3,500 = 63%; Cosmos 4K SE 1,298 ÷ 1,800 = 72%; Mars 3 686 ÷ 1,000 = 69%.

How big a screen each one lights, on wall power and on battery

The Mars 3 on battery. Soundcore's battery-life table rates the Mars 3 at 700 lumens for 1 h 30 min in Standard mode on battery, and 300 lumens for 5 h in Eco. An optional brightness setting switches it to Eco as soon as you unplug the adapter. ProjectorCentral measured 261 lumens in Movie mode and 442 in Bright mode in Eco. That reaches 16 ft-L only up to about 74 and 96 inches. So the 5-hour figure applies to a picture of roughly 100 inches or smaller after full dark, and 1 h 30 min at Standard brightness is shorter than most feature films. buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C1S95YZQ?tag=dealectric-20

Plug-in projectors without an outlet. Notebookcheck measured the X1 at about 160 W at its highest brightness, which is 0.32 kWh for a 2-hour film (160 × 2). Notebookcheck says a 500 Wh power station covers most outdoor movie nights. The BLUETTI Elite 100 V2 holds 1,024 Wh, so 1,024 ÷ 160 = 6.4 h before inverter losses. $499 on bluettipower.com, $474.05 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/bluetti-elite-100-v2 Laurent Willen measured the Horizon 20 at 97 W at laser level 10, so 1,024 ÷ 97 = 10.6 h before losses. XGIMI lists its power dissipation as 180 W or less.

Brightest modes. In Conference mode, ProjectorCentral measured the X1 at 3,187 lumens. Laurent Willen measured the Horizon 20's High Power mode at 3,020 lumens (70.7 ft-L at 120 in), at a measured contrast of 1,304:1, but says its greenish tint makes that mode unusable. The X1's Conference mode is the option for a twilight start, before the sky is fully dark.

Streaming. Nebula's spec sheets list both the X1 and the Cosmos 4K SE as Google TV with Official Netflix. On the Mars 3, Soundcore's support article says Netflix is a mobile build installed through the Nebula Play app, and you control it in mouse mode from the phone app. If Netflix is your main service, plug a streaming stick into the Mars 3's HDMI port.

Not recommended

The Nebula Mars 3 for a 150-inch screen. Its measured 686 lumens in Movie mode give 10.3 ft-L at 150 inches, below the 12 ft-L floor. Even its Bright mode (959 lumens) gives only 14.4 ft-L. On battery in Eco, the most it can light to 16 ft-L is a 74-inch picture. For a 150-inch backyard screen, choose one of the plug-in projectors and add a power station where there is no outlet.
