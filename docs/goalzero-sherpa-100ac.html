Who it's for

Flying with a laptop, a barrel-plug gadget or a camera charger that needs a wall plug.

Caveats

  • Through the AC outlet expect about 70% of the 94.7 Wh to reach the device (TechAdvisor measured ~70% on a 99.9 Wh AC bank); a USB-C laptop should use the USB-C port instead.
  • Modified sine wave: fine for most laptop bricks and chargers, not for some motors or audio gear.
  • Owners report the fan running often; one Amazon review reports a dead unit in week three.
