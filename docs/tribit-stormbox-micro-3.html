Who it's for

A car or truck with no working radio or no Bluetooth, where you want music, navigation prompts and calls from one speaker that stays put.

Caveats

  • Bass thins out at high volume; it fills a cabin but will not match a car's own speakers on the highway.
  • No charger in the box, only a USB-C cable.
  • Lithium battery: take it indoors when you park in the sun.
