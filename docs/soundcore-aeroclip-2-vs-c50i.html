Soundcore AeroClip 2 vs C50i (2026)

Short answer. Buy the AeroClip 2 ($169.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/soundcore-aeroclip-2) only if calls are the job: six mics (4 MEMS + 2 bone-conduction) and the top certified speech-quality score among clip-on earbuds. For music, running and the gym, the C50i ($69.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/soundcore-c50i) is the better buy at about $40: runners report it doesn't slip over a 10K, it is lighter (5.5 g vs 6.2 g) and gets 7 h vs 8 h per charge.

The AeroClip 2's firmer clip can feel tight after 1–2 hours (Expert Reviews), and both leak sound at moderate volume, so neither suits a quiet train or office.

Which buyer gets which

  • Calls, office, commute: AeroClip 2. Expert Reviews rates it head and shoulders above rivals for calls; Guinness certified its speech-quality score (G-MOS) on 2026-08-31.
  • Running and the gym: C50i. The AeroClip 2's extra money buys mics, IP57, LDAC and a wireless-charging case, not a better fit.
  • Glasses: owners of both clip-on designs report no clash with glasses; no reviewer tested the AeroClip 2 with glasses.
