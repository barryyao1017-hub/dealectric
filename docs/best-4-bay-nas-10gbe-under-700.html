Which 10GbE port

RJ45 10GbE (UGREEN, TerraMaster, Asustor) plugs into Cat6a copper and the 10GBase-T ports on prosumer switches and Thunderbolt adapters. SFP+ (QNAP TS-432X, UniFi) needs a transceiver or a direct-attach cable at each end, cheaper per port on a UniFi or MikroTik switch but useless with a copper-only desk. A single 10GbE link moves a 1 GB file in about a second against about 4 seconds on 2.5GbE, provided the drives keep up: four spinning disks in RAID 5 give roughly 400–600 MB/s, so the NVMe slots matter for reads above that.

Plex and transcoding

Hardware transcoding needs an Intel iGPU with Quick Sync (the DXP4800 Plus, TS-464 and F4-424 Pro) or an AMD iGPU with a Plex Pass and driver support (the DXP4800 GT's Vega 8, reported working in reviews but not promised by UGREEN). The ARM units (TS-432X, UNAS Pro 4) cannot transcode 4K; the UNAS Pro 4 runs no apps at all.
