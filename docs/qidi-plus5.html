Who it's for

Makers printing large ABS, ASA, PC or nylon parts, such as helmets, enclosures and brackets up to 12.6 inches square, who do not need Bambu-style multi-color.

Caveats

  • Print PLA and TPU with the lid and door open; the insulated chamber causes heat-creep clogs otherwise.
  • The predecessor Plus 4 had owner fire reports tied to the chamber-heater relay in late 2024 and 2025; QIDI shipped replacement relays. The Plus5 uses a new heater design, but we found no independent safety test of it.
  • Multi-color needs the separate QIDI Box, which reviewers found unreliable on the Q2.
  • A 64 lb printer: plan for a sturdy table.
