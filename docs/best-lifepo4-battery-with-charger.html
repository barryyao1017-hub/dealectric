What owners complain about

  • "It reads 0 V and won't charge." The single most common thread. A LiFePO4 BMS disconnects after a deep discharge, and many chargers refuse to start unless they see battery voltage. The fix is a charger with an activation or 0 V mode, which the Redodo 20 A and Vatrer kit chargers have. A plain lead-acid charger often cannot do this.
  • Lead-acid chargers never finish the job. A 12 V lead-acid profile tops out near 14.4 V, drops to float, and may not restart bulk charging until the pack is nearly empty. Owners report batteries stuck at 70–99%. Use a 14.6 V LiFePO4 charger.
  • Charging below freezing. Charging a cold lithium cell plates the anode and the damage does not heal. Both Redodo batteries above cut charging off at 32°F; the self-heating version warms itself first instead of just refusing.
  • 10 A chargers are slow. A 100 Ah pack takes about 10 hours on 10 A and about 5 hours on 20 A. The 20 A charger is the sweet spot for 100 Ah; go to 40 A only for 200 Ah and up.
  • Fan noise and heat. Most 20 A bricks have a small fan that owners call loud, and the case runs warm. Charge in a garage or outside, not next to a bed.

Not recommended

  • Any 5 A or 10 A charger for a 100 Ah pack unless you have all night and never run the battery flat.
  • Multi-chemistry chargers left in AGM or lead-acid mode. They charge to the wrong voltage and float when lithium should rest. One Slickdeals owner reported a 20 A unit stuck at 99% on a 100 Ah ECO-WORTHY pack.
  • Redodo's 12V 320Ah lead-to-lithium kit for this search. It bundles an inverter, not an AC battery charger, and is more than most shoppers asking for a battery with charger need.
