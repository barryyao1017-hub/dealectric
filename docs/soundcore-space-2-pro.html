Who it's for

Remote workers and office users who take calls all day and have been told callers can hear their background. Pairs with a laptop and phone at the same time.

Caveats

  • ANC is good but not Bose or Sony class; Engadget called it average on steady noise like fans and white-noise machines.
  • Bass-heavy out of the box; use the app EQ.
  • Ships with a soft pouch, not a hard case.
  • No IP rating, so not for the gym or rain.
  • LDAC and voice control shorten battery; one reviewer logged about 26 hours with ANC and LDAC.
  • The AI note-taker has a free tier, then a paid plan; the headphones work fine without it.
