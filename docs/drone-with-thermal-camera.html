What the differences mean

All five drones carry a 640 × 512 thermal detector with a 12 µm pixel pitch, so resolution does not separate them. The lens does. The figures below are geometry with the camera pointed straight at the target; they say how much ground one pixel covers, not how sharp the lens is.

  • Ground per pixel at 100 m. EVO II Dual 640T V3: 12 µm ÷ 13 mm = 0.923 mrad, × 100 m = 9.2 cm. EVO Lite 640T: 12 µm ÷ 9.1 mm = 1.319 mrad, × 100 m = 13.2 cm. Matrice 4T (DJI gives a 45° diagonal field of view): 2 × tan 22.5° ÷ 819.6 diagonal pixels = 1.011 mrad, × 100 m = 10.1 cm.
  • EVO II against EVO Lite. 13 ÷ 9.1 = 1.43 times as many pixels across the same target, for $4,399 − $3,359 = $1,040 more.
  • EVO II against Matrice 4T. 9.2 cm against 10.1 cm per pixel: no thermal detail is lost, and the Rugged Bundle costs $6,965 − $4,399 = $2,566 less. The DJI money buys the tele camera, the laser rangefinder and 11 more rated minutes (49 against 38).
  • A deer from 400 ft. nighthunting.net counts 14 pixels nose to tail on the EVO II and puts the limit for seeing an animal's shape at about 12. Scaled by lens: EVO Lite 14 × 9.1 ÷ 13 = 9.8 pixels; Matrice 4T 14 × 0.923 ÷ 1.011 = 12.8 pixels.
  • Ground strip from 400 ft (121.92 m). EVO II: 0.923 mrad × 121.92 m × 640 pixels = 72 m (236 ft). Matrice 4T: 1.011 × 121.92 × 640 = 78.9 m (259 ft). EVO Lite: 1.319 × 121.92 × 640 = 102.9 m (338 ft), so it sweeps a strip 1.43 times as wide per pass.

Why 640 × 512 is not one level of detail

A thermal pixel sees a fixed angle: detector pitch divided by focal length. A longer lens narrows that angle, so each pixel lands on less ground and more pixels land on the target. Digital zoom (16× on the Autel drones, 28× on the DJI) enlarges the picture without adding detector pixels, and DJI's 1280 × 1024 output is a Super Resolution mode on a 640 × 512 detector.

The makers' own range lines do not show this. Autel's US store prints the same sentence, "capable of recognizing subjects at up to 100m", for the 9.1 mm EVO Lite 640T and the 13 mm EVO II Dual 640T V3. At that 100 m a 0.5 m-wide target spans 0.5 ÷ 0.092 = 5.4 pixels on the EVO II and 0.5 ÷ 0.132 = 3.8 pixels on the EVO Lite.

Solar panels. Sitemark states that IEC TS 62446-3 requires a geometric resolution of 3 cm per pixel at most, and Greg Nagler of Teledyne FLIR, quoted in Commercial UAV News, gives the same 3 cm per pixel on the panels for a detailed inspection. Distance = 0.03 m ÷ angle per pixel: EVO II 0.03 ÷ 0.000923 = 32.5 m; Matrice 4T 0.03 ÷ 0.001011 = 29.7 m; EVO Lite 0.03 ÷ 0.001319 = 22.8 m. The EVO Lite has to fly about 10 m closer to the array to meet the same standard.

In the field. Jules Whicker (Shooting Times, 7 July 2026) used a Matrice 4T on deer ground for six months: the thermal camera detected deer in the open at up to 1,500 m, he identified them at 200 m to 400 m using the zoom camera, and he expects 30 to 45 minutes per charge against DJI's rated 49. Detection is a warm dot; identification needs pixels on the animal, which is what the lens figures above measure. DJI's own hover figure is 42 minutes, 7 fewer than the forward-flight rating.

The DJI Mavic 3T that still appears in some listings has a 61° diagonal thermal lens: 2 × tan 30.5° ÷ 819.6 = 1.437 mrad, or 14.4 cm per pixel at 100 m, the same class as the EVO Lite. nighthunting.net and bestthermaldrone.com both list it as discontinued. DJI also warns against pointing either thermal camera at the sun, lava or a laser beam.

Not recommended

The Rugged Bundle, if you will ever want a spotlight, loudspeaker, strobe or RTK. Its listing states: "RTK modules, speakers, and additional accessories are not supported by this configuration." The RTK module's listing gives the reason: the Rugged Bundle aircraft does "not have interfaces on the top of the fuselage". The $149 spotlight and $625 RTK module therefore cannot be added later. The Enterprise Bundle has the mount and includes strobe, spotlight and speaker for $4,899 − $4,399 = $500 more. For looking at heat only (wildlife, roofs, solar), the Rugged Bundle keeps the same 13 mm thermal camera for less.
