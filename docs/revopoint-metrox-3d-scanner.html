Who it's for

Makers reverse-engineering brackets, car parts and machined pieces into CAD, where crisp edges matter more than color.

Caveats

  • Both laser modes need reflective markers on or around the part; owners report 'need more markers' errors and marker prep that takes longer than the scan.
  • Full-field structured-light mode still needs scanning spray on black or glossy surfaces.
  • Tethered: 508 g in hand plus a USB cable and a wall power brick, so it is a desk scanner, not a walk-around one.
  • Marker-free feature tracking in full-field mode is weak on plain shapes; one owner thread calls it terrible.
