Best smartwatch without a phone

Updated 2026-11-06

Short answer. The best smartwatch without phone is the Apple Watch SE 3 GPS + Cellular (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HJB73N19?tag=dealectric-20): it takes calls and texts, streams music and dials 911 with the iPhone left at home, runs up to 18 hours of normal use per charge (32 hours in Low Power Mode), and the carrier line costs about $10 a month plus tax. The catch shoppers ask about most: can it replace a phone? Not fully. Every cellular watch still needs a phone to set it up at least once, and only three mainstream watches do calls on their own: the SE 3 (iPhone), the Samsung Galaxy Watch 8 LTE (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F7PX6RSB?tag=dealectric-20, Android) and the Google Pixel Watch 4 LTE (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FJWRJLXX?tag=dealectric-20, Android). The SE 3 is the one a person with no phone at all can wear, because a family member's iPhone can set it up through Apple Watch For Your Kids.

If you only want music and GPS on a run with no phone and no monthly fee, the Amazfit Active Max ($169.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/amazfit-active-max) stores 100 hours of audio in 4 GB and lasts up to 25 days, but it cannot make calls away from the phone. For a child, the TickTalk 6 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HCH3VMR4?tag=dealectric-20) has its own eSIM and a plan from $9.99 a month.

What owners complain about

The monthly fee. In one 25-post MacRumors thread on whether cellular is worth it, 8 posters argued about the line cost; one pays $17.10 a month after taxes and fees. Verizon charges $15 a month for its Unlimited watch plan ($7.50 with an eligible phone plan), and T-Mobile and AT&T sit near $10 to $11. Two Android Central writers dropped LTE after a year because they rarely left the phone behind.

It still needs the right phone. Apple watches need an iPhone (or a family organizer's iPhone). Galaxy Watch 8 and Pixel Watch 4 refuse iPhones, and Samsung limits some health features to Samsung phones. Carrier forums (T-Mobile, EE, Telus, Samsung community) are full of standalone-line activations that took several calls, especially with non-Samsung Android phones.

Battery drops on LTE. Notebookcheck measured the Galaxy Watch 8 LTE at 24 hours with always-on display and the phone connected, and it would not last a day with the phone away. Apple Watch owners report 10 to 15 percent extra drain with cellular on and that a long call can empty the watch in about an hour.

Plan type changes what arrives. A standalone watch line (its own number) does not carry iMessages or app notifications; number-share with your phone does. Some low-cost carriers (Tello, Mint, Google Fi for non-Fi phones) cannot add a watch line at all.

Which one, by need

  • Leave the iPhone at home for runs, gym or errands: Apple Watch SE 3 GPS + Cellular (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HJB73N19?tag=dealectric-20). Add it to your plan as number-share so texts and iMessages follow you.
  • A parent or grandparent with no phone: the same SE 3 cellular model, set up from your iPhone through Apple Watch For Your Kids. A cellular plan is optional for setup but needed for calls away from Wi-Fi. Expect to lose Sleep, ECG, Blood Oxygen and card payments in Wallet.
  • Android, Samsung phone: Galaxy Watch 8 40 mm LTE (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F7PX6RSB?tag=dealectric-20). Charge it nightly.
  • Android, Pixel or other phone: Pixel Watch 4 41 mm LTE (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FJWRJLXX?tag=dealectric-20). Confirm your carrier supports watch calling, not just data.
  • A child aged 3 to 12: TickTalk 6 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HCH3VMR4?tag=dealectric-20), eSIM built in, plan from $9.99 a month, no games or open internet.
  • Music and GPS with no phone and no fee: Amazfit Active Max ($169.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/amazfit-active-max), 4 GB for 100 hours of podcasts or music over Bluetooth earbuds, 25-day battery. The Amazfit Balance 2 ($299.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/amazfit-balance-2) adds free offline maps and a 21-day battery for runners who navigate.

Not recommended

  • GPS-only versions of these watches (Apple Watch SE 3 GPS, Galaxy Watch 8 Bluetooth, Pixel Watch 4 Wi-Fi). They cost $50 less but cannot call or text without the phone, and a GPS-only Apple Watch cannot be set up for a family member.
  • Unbranded 4G SIM-slot watches on Amazon. The searches for "smartwatch with SIM card" return no-name kids and adult watches with no listed US carrier certification and no independent reviews. Carrier band support is the thing you cannot check before buying.
  • Any Amazfit or Garmin for calls away from the phone. They have no cellular radio; Bluetooth calling only works with the phone in range.
