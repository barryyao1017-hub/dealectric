Can I fly with it?

The FAA allows spare lithium batteries up to 100 Wh in carry-on only; 101–160 Wh needs the airline's approval (two at most); above 160 Wh is not allowed. Every 250–300 Wh station is over the line, and so is ALLPOWERS's 154 Wh S200 without approval.

Why the AC outlet drains it fast

An AC outlet turns the battery's DC into 120 V, and the laptop's brick turns it back into DC: TechAdvisor measured about 70% end to end on a 99.9 Wh AC bank, and GearJunkie found a Sherpa 100AC nearly drained while a laptop gained 5% on AC. Use AC only for devices that have no USB-C or DC barrel option. Barrel-plug laptops that don't need 120 V can use a DC-output bank such as Omnicharge's Omni Mobile 25600 (92 Wh, 72 W DC, no AC).

What owners complain about

Unknown-brand AC banks dying within weeks (Reddit r/powerbanks, Amazon reviews of the mophie Gen 2), fans that run whenever the outlet is on, and banks that can't keep up with a laptop in use. Neither Baseus nor UGREEN sells an AC-outlet power bank in the US.
