Best wireless gaming headset under $50

Short answer. What owners complain about

  • Quiet or static mic is the most common gripe (4 of the 7 negative Logitech G435 reviews we read, plus a Reddit thread about a mic too quiet for Discord at max volume). Built-in mics sit far from your mouth. A boom you can pull to your lips fixes most of it, which is why the E900 BT and Mako BT both have one. Neither has been lab-tested, so expect usable Discord chat, not streaming quality.
  • Battery shorter than the box says (3 mentions). Ratings assume lights off. The E900 BT's 50 hours is with its logo light off; the Mako BT's 60 hours is Bluetooth with RGB off and only 25 hours on the dongle.
  • Dongle dropouts and pairing trouble (3 mentions). Put the dongle on a front USB port or the E900 BT's USB-A adaptor, not behind a metal PC case.
  • Does not work with Xbox (2 mentions). No sub-$50 dongle pairs with Xbox. Only the headsets with a 3.5 mm cable work there, through the controller jack, and in that mode the headset runs wired.
  • Fit. One reviewer found the E900 BT light but loose during sudden head turns. The G435 is the lightest at 165 g and fits small heads best.

Which one by need

  • PC, PS5, Switch and phone, plus Xbox by cable: EKSA E900 BT. Three connections, 50 hours, retractable boom.
  • Hard $40 ceiling: EKSA Mako BT. Same three connections and a detachable boom; accept 25 hours on the dongle.
  • Lightest, for a kid or a small head: Logitech G435 at 165 g, but only when a sale drops it under $50. At its $79.99 list it has no cable, no boom and 18 hours.
  • PC or PS5 only, lowest price: EKSA E1000 WT, dongle only, 36 hours.
  • Willing to spend $80: Corsair HS55 Wireless. Flip-to-mute boom, 24 hours on the dongle, Dolby on PC, but still no cable and no Xbox.

Not recommended

  • OXS Storm G2. Tri-mode and 40 hours, but it is $79.99 and out of stock on the maker's store on 2026-11-06.
  • Bluetooth-only "gaming" headsets. EKSA's own page pegs ordinary Bluetooth at about 100 ms, which you hear as lip-sync lag. Insist on a 2.4 GHz dongle.
  • Used or renewed G435 units under $30. The 18-hour battery ages, and the warranty is gone.
