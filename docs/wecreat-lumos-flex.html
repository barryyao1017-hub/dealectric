Who it's for

Knife makers, jewelers and gift sellers who need deep or relief engraving on steel, brass and aluminum as well as wood and leather, in a machine they can carry to a booth. Shoppers who only mark coated tumblers and anodized tags can spend $1,050 less on the Lumos, and shops that cut metal or run relief work in volume need a higher-power fiber or MOPA laser. On 2026-11-18 the 20 W fiber-plus-diode LaserPecker LP5 was $2,429.10 and the xTool F1 Ultra $2,549 at their makers' stores.

Caveats

  • Zelpio's reviewer called the software the weakest part: the material library has only a few entries, with no presets for deep brass engraving or relief work.
  • Relief engraving is slow at 15 W: a detailed piece took eight hours in Zelpio's test, and wood needed a slower speed and a second pass to come out dark.
  • Metal cutting is limited to foil-thin sheet: WECREAT states 0.3 mm stainless steel and 0.5 mm brass at most.
  • WECREAT says the Lumos Flex has no phone or tablet app and that LightBurn support depends on the version, and its manual states the rotary is not included.
  • The work field is 116 × 116 mm; a full wrap on a 40 oz tumbler needs both the Rotary Pro ($299) and the Slide Extension ($399), per the manual.
