Best robot lawn mower with a bag

Short answer. The best robot lawn mower with a bag is the ANTHBOT N8 ($1,359; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/anthbot-n8): a built-in 23 L (6.1 gal) bag that it empties by itself at a spot you set, on lawns up to 1,500 m² (0.37 acre). It does pick up leaves: in PCWorld's test it emptied the bag without a single failure, and Notebookcheck found it left only a few leaves behind. What owners and testers complain about

  • Noise while collecting (4 tests): the fan that pulls clippings into the bag is loud. Testers measured 68 to 70 dB on the N8 and almost 70 dB on the YUKA with its sweeper. For plain mowing, Anthbot rates the N8 at 58 dB.
  • Wet or matted leaves (4 sources): collection works best on dry leaves. Leaves packed down by rain still need a rake, and the YUKA sweeper needed several passes on wet clumps. Mulch-only robots don't clear leaves at all.
  • Edges and turning room (4 sources): the N8 is about 3 ft long. Testers saw uncut strips up to about 6 in along edges, and it needs room near the dock and the dump spot.
  • Rain in the bag (3 tests): water pools on the N8's bag cover if it rains while the mower sits at the dock.
  • 4G fees (3 reviews): cellular service is free for a trial, then it's a paid yearly plan. You only need it where your RTK antenna can't see the sky or Wi-Fi doesn't reach.
  • Support: on one page of Anthbot's Trustpilot reviews, 13 of 23 complained of slow replies or slow refunds and replacements. Buy from a seller with a clear return window.

Which one by need

  • Trees that drop leaves, or you hate clippings on the grass: ANTHBOT N8. It's the only built-in bag we found, and it's rated for 45% slopes.
  • Bigger lawn, light leaf fall, quieter mulching: MOVA LiDAX Ultra 2000. It covers 0.5 acre and needs no RTK antenna.
  • Small yard under heavy tree cover: ANTHBOT M5 LiDAR. LiDAR doesn't need a clear view of the sky.

Not recommended

  • A mulch-only robot if you get a thick fall leaf layer. It will chop leaves at grass height, but it won't clear them.
  • The YUKA with its sweeper on slopes steeper than 10°.
  • Any bagging robot if quiet night mowing matters. Mow without the bag or pick a mulching model.
