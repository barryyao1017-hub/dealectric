What the differences mean

  • Bag size is a tie. 23 L (N8) vs 22 L (YUKA sweeper) is a 1 L difference, about 4%.
  • Slope decides it. T3 lists the N8 at 44.5% (24°) and gives no lower figure for use with the bag. The YUKA falls to 18% (10°) once its sweeper is fitted (Notebookcheck). 44.5 ÷ 18 ≈ 2.5, so on paper the N8 is rated for a slope about 2.5 times as steep.
  • The rated area takes several charges. Notebookcheck measured just under 200 m² per charge, and PCWorld reports that Anthbot quotes about 2,150 sq ft (about 200 m²) of continuous mowing with the grass catcher fitted. 1,500 m² ÷ 200 m² = 7.5 charges to cut the full 0.37 acre once.
  • Time per cut. PCWorld timed 1,200 sq ft in 97 minutes, or 1,200 ÷ 97 × 60 ≈ 740 sq ft an hour. A 5,000 sq ft lawn takes 5,000 ÷ 740 ≈ 6.8 hours of mowing.
  • The collector costs extra on the YUKA. The N8's bag comes in the box. Notebookcheck lists the YUKA sweeper at €699 on its own, so a YUKA 2000 that collects comes to €1,749 + €699 = €2,448 at EU list prices.
  • Noise is a wash. Collecting, the N8 measured just under 68 dB (PCWorld). Notebookcheck measured about 70 dB but did not say which mode it was in. The YUKA with its sweeper measured almost 70 dB (Notebookcheck).

Why the N8 needs dry grass and a clean deck

PCWorld describes the blade wings making the airflow so that the disc acts like a blower. Anthbot says that while the N Series mows, a strong airflow lifts and sucks in the clippings and sends them to the rear bag. A radar sensor in the bag detects when it is full and triggers a dump. Notebookcheck saw it empty about every 100 minutes and once at the end of each session.

Because it relies on airflow, the N8 has one weak point, and Anthbot names it: when grass builds up on the underside, it blocks the airflow and clippings drop in thick piles. Anthbot also says wet grass is the primary cause of clumping because it sticks together and to the mower's undercarriage. In practice:

  • Mow dry. Anthbot's test is to walk on the lawn, and if your shoes come away wet, wait. Rain can take hours or even days to drain. The N8's rain sensor pauses it for a preset 3 hours (PCWorld), and that is not the same as a dry lawn.
  • Keep the deck clean. Anthbot's guides say to clear the underside after each mow or every week or two. The N Series blade disc can be rinsed with a garden hose.
  • Plan a place for the dumped clippings. They stay on the lawn. PCWorld lists up to 5 dump areas, each at least 5 × 5 ft, inside the mowing zone.

The YUKA's sweeper picks up mechanically with a rubber brush roller (Notebookcheck), so it does not depend on airflow under the deck. No reviewer has tested either mower collecting on wet grass, so nothing shows the YUKA does better there. $1,359: https://dealectric.com/go/anthbot-n8

Not recommended

The YUKA with its sweeper on a sloped lawn. The sweeper is what makes the YUKA collect, and it also cuts the slope limit from 45% (24°) to 18% (10°) (Notebookcheck). If any part of your lawn is steeper than 10°, the YUKA can only bag the flat parts, and the N8, rated 24° (T3), is the one to buy.
