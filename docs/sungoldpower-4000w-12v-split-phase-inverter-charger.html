Who it's for

Cabins, shops and home backup on a 12V battery bank, where the charging source is a 240V generator or the grid and some loads need 240 V.

Caveats

  • Needs 240 VAC input to charge and pass power through. A 120 V-only source (30 A RV shore power, a 120 V generator) is below its 140 VAC minimum.
  • At 4,000 W it pulls about 379 A from a 12 V bank (4,000 ÷ (0.88 × 12)), so it needs heavy cables and a fuse rated for that current.
  • Idle draw of 45 W is about 84 Ah a day from a 12.8 V LiFePO4 bank unless power-saver mode is used.
