Not recommended: BLUETTI Elite 400 + 2× 350 W

This kit has the most panel watts of the Elite 400 kits, but the station cannot take all of them. BLUETTI lists its 350 W panel at 33 V open-circuit. Two in series are 2 × 33 V = 66 V, above the Elite 400's 60 V input limit, so they have to be wired in parallel. In parallel the input's 20 A limit caps power below 20 A × 33 V = 660 W, because a panel's working voltage is always under its open-circuit voltage. The refill is therefore at least 3,840 / 660 = 5.8 h, not the 5.5 h the 700 W rating suggests.

It costs $521.55 more than the 2× 200 W kit, $1.74 per added rated watt. The ALLPOWERS R3500 + 600 W kit refills in 5.3 h at rated output for $571.67 less ($2,325.60 − $1,753.93) and ships now, with 672 Wh less battery.
