  • Idle over a 3-day outage: Explorer 5000 Plus 58 W × 72 h = 4.18 kWh; Apex 300 20 W × 72 h = 1.44 kWh; S5000 10 W × 72 h = 0.72 kWh.

Idle draw: the figure that decides a multi-day outage

Whenever a battery station's AC outlets are on, its inverter, battery management and screen draw power, even with nothing plugged in. A refrigerator needs the outlets on around the clock, so this overhead runs for the whole outage. In a 4-hour outage it hardly matters. Over three days it can use up most of a battery.

  • Anker SOLIX S5000: Andrew Riha (pv magazine USA, 2026-10-28) saw a 5% charge drop in 24 h with the 120 V output on and nothing connected. That is about 10 W, in line with Anker's 8.8 W rating. During a PG&E shutoff it ran a large fridge for 61.5 h.
  • BLUETTI Apex 300: The Solar Lab measured about 20 W idle and about 85% inverter efficiency. BLUETTI's own page also gives 20 W.
  • Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus: The Solar Lab measured about 58 W with the inverter on and no load, and estimated about 3.5 days for the unit to drain itself.
  • Stations without a published idle wattage: OutdoorGearLab (Kenji Mowrey) measured 3,790 Wh delivered (93%) from the EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3, and The Solar Lab measured 89% efficiency at 3,650 W. The Solar Lab measured 84% inverter efficiency for the Anker SOLIX F3800 Plus. Anker says a June 2025 firmware update halved the F3800 Plus's idle consumption, but it gives no watt figure.

Sizing: multiply the kWh per day (about 1.6 kWh for the large fridge in pv magazine's test) by the days you want covered, then add idle watts × hours. For three days on the S5000 that is 4.8 + 0.72 = 5.5 kWh, a little over one charge. With the Explorer 5000 Plus it is 4.8 + 4.18 = 9.0 kWh, which means adding a battery pack. Recharging from solar during the day lowers both totals.

Transfer switch: feed home circuits only through a transfer switch or inlet box installed by an electrician, never by back-feeding a wall outlet. The makers' kits on 2026-11-13: Anker Home Backup Kit for the S5000, $499.99; Jackery Manual Transfer Switch (240 V 50 A), $550; BLUETTI Apex 300 Home Integration Kit, $699. Orders for the S5000 placed on or after Nov 6 ship in 9–10 weeks, according to Anker's page. BLUETTI's page says the Apex 300 is expected to ship in late January.

Not recommended

The Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus is a poor set-and-forget refrigerator supply. Its 58 W idle uses 1.39 kWh a day, 28% of the battery, before the fridge draws anything. At $694 per kWh it also costs the most in this tier, and Jackery lists it as pre-order shipping Nov 28. Buy it for its 7,200 W output for central AC or a well pump, and turn the inverter off between uses.
