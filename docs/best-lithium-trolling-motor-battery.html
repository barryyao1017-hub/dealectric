Will lithium work with your trolling motor?

Brushed Minn Kota motors: 85% speed cap. Minn Kota's help center says lithium batteries hold a higher voltage for longer, which can be too powerful for motors designed for flooded lead-acid. On those brushed motors it is recommended to limit the motor speed to 85%, or Speed 8.5 or lower. Minn Kota adds that running the motor at maximum speed with lithium batteries can result in damage. It describes its QUEST motors as optimized for LiFePO4 cells. On a brushed motor you give up top speed whichever battery you buy, so pay for continuous amps and watt-hours, not brand.

Continuous discharge has to cover max draw. Minn Kota says to verify there is enough continuous amperage for the motor to pull its max amp draw. If there isn't, the battery's management system will restrict power to the motor. Minn Kota lists the symptoms as shutting off after a short run, the motor running while the prop doesn't turn, steering problems, and Spot-Lock or i-Pilot not working.

Garmin Force needs 60 A. Garmin's Force Kraken installation manual requires a 24 or 36 Vdc battery bank capable of supplying 60 A continuously, through a breaker rated for 60 A continuous. That is 2,160 W at 36 V (60 A × 36 V).

On-board chargers. Minn Kota PCL on-board chargers top out at 14.5 V, and Minn Kota tells owners to check that this matches the battery maker's charge specification.

Rated capacity holds up in testing. Techtest.org (Michael Barton) measured 102–103 Ah from LiTime's 12V 100Ah trolling-motor battery across five discharges at 10–40 A. Its BMS shut off at 10.43 V.

Not recommended

Ionic 24V 50Ah and 36V 50Ah on a Garmin Force. Ionic rates both at 50 A continuous, 10 A short of Garmin's 60 A requirement (60 − 50 = 10). Ionic also says the 36V 50Ah is parallel-only and must never be wired or charged in series. If you are paying the premium for Ionic's 11-year warranty, read the terms first. Repair is free only in years 0–5. Years 5–8 carry a $175 service charge per battery, and years 8–11 bring only a 30% discount on a replacement.
