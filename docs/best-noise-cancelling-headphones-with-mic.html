What owners complain about

The most common complaint, by far, is that callers hear the room. Reddit threads from a library worker in a loud apartment, a couple working in one room, and a project manager in an open office all describe the same thing: the headphones block noise for the wearer, but the mic still sends neighbours, traffic and a spouse's voice to the other end. The second theme is voice processing that over-corrects. Sony community threads on earlier WH-1000X models run for pages about callers hearing a muffled, echoey voice once background noise appears, and one Space One Pro owner on the eufy forum described a robotic voice. Third is the laptop problem: Windows drops Bluetooth headphones to the mono hands-free profile when the mic opens, so playback sounds thin in meetings; the fix is to use the laptop mic as input or plug in by cable.

Which one by need

Daily calls from home or a desk: the Space 2 Pro. Its 6-mic AI array held a clear voice in reviewers' tests on a busy street, a kitchen with the hood running, a construction site and an airport lounge. It also pairs with a phone and laptop at once, and a 5-minute charge gives 8 hours.

Long flights and the deepest quiet: Sony WH-1000XM6 or Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (2nd Gen). Both cancel more low rumble than the Space 2 Pro, and the Sony uses six beamforming mics for calls. They cost about twice as much and are Amazon or maker purchases.

A partner or colleague talking a few feet away: the Jabra Evolve2 85. Ten microphones on a retractable boom reject a nearby voice far better than mics in the earcups, and the Teams-certified version includes a USB dongle that sidesteps the laptop Bluetooth profile problem.

Occasional calls on a budget: the soundcore Space One ($99.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/soundcore-space-one, code AFSC20 saves $19.99). Three call mics and 40 hours with ANC on are fine in a quiet room, but one reviewer preferred the laptop's own mic, so it is not for noisy spaces.

Folds into a bag: the Space One Pro ($199.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/soundcore-space-one-pro, code AFSC20 saves $39.99) collapses to half its size, has 4 call mics and 40 hours with ANC on, but reviewers found its mic only average and it is heavier at 286 g.

Not recommended

Earbuds for all-day meetings. Commenters in the open-office thread were blunt that earbuds still pass background voices, and only a proper headset fixed it. Gaming headsets with mic-side noise reduction but no speaker-side ANC, such as the EKSA E-series, leave you hearing the room even if callers do not. Older Sony WH-1000XM3 and XM4 bought used for calls: the forum record on their call mics is long and unhappy.
