Who it's for

Crafters and side-business sellers who engrave tumblers, boxes and other tall items as often as flat sheet and want one enclosed machine with a camera. Shoppers who only engrave flat stock get nearly the same work area from the Vista 10W for $900 less, and those who cut thick wood daily should compare the Vision Pro 45W or an xTool S1 40W ($1,448 list at xtool.com, out of stock there on 2026-11-18).

Caveats

  • Cuts are slow, as with other 20 W diodes: Make: magazine's reviewer said so, and advised venting outdoors because the optional extractor helped but did not remove the fumes.
  • 3DWithUs listed smoke escaping when cutting thicker material and a chuck-only rotary with no roller option, with a dead zone near the chuck.
  • TechRadar counted the 420 × 290 mm work area and the separately sold fume extractor ($599 today) as its two drawbacks, and noted the full 140 mm height needs the bed taken out.
  • Tom's Hardware said the external air-assist pump vibrates intensely, called the AI image tool a gimmick, and noticed a strong smell when cutting cast acrylic even with the extractor running.
  • Clear acrylic and bare metal are out: WECREAT lists only "some dark opaque acrylic" as cuttable with the 20 W diode and sells a separate 2 W infrared head ($499) for engraving metal.
