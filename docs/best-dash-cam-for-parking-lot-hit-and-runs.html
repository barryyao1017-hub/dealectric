What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test units. Every figure below comes from the maker or from the named tester or retailer, and the sources are listed at the end.

  • Radar against motion detection on the same camera. SafeDriveSolutions measured the U3000 PRO, front and rear, at about 12.5 V. Radar mode: 7 mA × 12.5 V = 0.09 W, so 0.09 W × 24 h = 2.1 Wh a day. Motion detection: 270 mA × 12.5 V = 3.4 W, so 81 Wh a day. That is about 39 times the radar draw (270 ÷ 7).
  • VIOFO's low-power impact mode. 0.24 W × 24 h = 5.8 Wh a day (VIOFO's rating). The catch is that recording starts within 2 s after the impact.
  • What a 6.8 s wake gap means. Take a car leaving at 5 mph, which is 2.2 m/s (our example speed). In 6.8 s it covers 2.2 × 6.8 = 15 m, about 50 ft. That is the gap before the N4 Pro S records its first frame in Quick Start, as timed by Vortex Radar.
  • What motion-detection parking costs in battery. rcg530 measured the older Vantrue N5's motion detection parking mode at 9.89 W on average. That is 9.89 ÷ 12.6 V = 785 mA. Mile Witness gives VIOFO's BP100 pack up to 32 h at 3 W, which is 3 × 32 = 96 Wh. At 9.89 W it would last 96 ÷ 9.89 = about 9.7 h.
  • VIOFO's total cost with the parking kit. A329S 2CH + HK6: $459.99 + $25.99 = $485.98. A229 Pro 2CH + HK4: $349.99 + $17.99 = $367.98. Both are VIOFO's own store prices, read 2026-11-21.

On the Vantrue cameras, the fix is the setting, not the camera. Use buffered motion detection, which keeps 10 s before the trigger on the N4 Pro S and N5S, and give it continuous power. The N5S manual describes both of its collision start-up modes as recording only after the hit. Buffered recording draws far more power, so it suits a few hours' parking on a hardwire kit with voltage cut-off or a battery pack. It won't last a weekend.

The U3000 PRO has its own catch. Thinkware says Smart Parking drops to impact-only recording when the cabin gets too hot, and Thinkware states no before-impact footage for that Energy Saving mode. In a hot lot, a parked U3000 PRO can behave like the impact-only cameras above until the cabin cools.
