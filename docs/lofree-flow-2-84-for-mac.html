Caveats

  • Only Surfer linear switches on the Mac version; the standard Flow 2 84 adds Pulse tactile and Void silent.
  • VIA remapping needs a JSON file from Lofree support; reviewers spent hours finding it.
  • The right-side touch strip can change volume when your palm brushes it; Fn + Space for 3 s disables it.
  • Backlight on cuts battery to roughly 13 to 21 hours in reviews.
  • USB-C port is on the right edge, and there is no slot to store the dongle.
