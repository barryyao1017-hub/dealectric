Who it's for

Suits RV, van or camper owners mounting a fixed panel on a roof where vents, racks or trees shade part of it during the day. BougeRV's FAQ says it works with 12 V and 24 V battery systems through a suitable charge controller.

Caveats

  • The 85% partial-shade figure is BougeRV's own claim; no independent shade test of this panel was found.
  • BougeRV's FAQ expects 150–170 W in real use, not the 200 W rating.
  • At 23.4 lb and 1.2 in thick it is a framed rigid panel, not for curved surfaces or carrying around.
