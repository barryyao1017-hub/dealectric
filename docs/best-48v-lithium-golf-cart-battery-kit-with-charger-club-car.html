What the kit replaces, and the bypass older carts need

One 48 V LiFePO4 box replaces the six or eight flooded lead-acid batteries in a 48 V Club Car, and the kit's charger replaces the cart's old one, so nothing else has to be sourced. Club Cars from the mid-1990s to about 2014 carry a Lester on-board computer that talks to the factory charger (Lester's bypass kit lists 1995–2014; dealers selling Allied's sets say 1993–2013), and it has to be bypassed before a lithium pack charges normally; a 1996 48 V DS discussed on Cartaholics this week has the OBC 27155-01. That is a general Club Car note, not Vatrer's, so check it against your model year before ordering.

Capacity and cycle life

Vatrer rates the pack at 105 Ah and 4,000+ cycles with a 200 A BMS that handles overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent, shorts and abnormal temperatures and stops charging below freezing. Vatrer quotes 5,376–5,736 Wh for the 48 V 105 Ah family across its pages; 51.2 V × 105 Ah is 5,376 Wh, which is the figure to plan range on. LiTime's 100 Ah kit is about 5% smaller.
