ERIC or on-board computer: your Club Car's charging decides the kit

Club Car made its ERIC charging system standard on all Precedent electric cars built after September 2013. It 'cannot be used on older cars with the on-board computer', and its enhanced controller replaces the old OBC (Club Car's announcement, reprinted by the Golf Range Association, 2014-04-03). CartVolts lists the 2014+ Precedent and the Tempo as 'No OBC, ERIC charging'. It also warns that 'some 2014 carts still have an OBC', so check a 2014 car before you order.

On the older carts the computer sits in the charging path. Club Car's 1998/1999 PowerDrive Plus service supplement lists the charge circuit as 'the charger receptacle, fuse link, onboard computer and the batteries'. So a lithium charger plugged into the stock port charges through the OBC unless you bypass it. Lester's 17-283 bypass kit costs $123.99 at Buggies Unlimited. Its fit table covers 48V Club Cars from 1995 to 2013 (the product title says 2014). It is 'NOT Compatible with 36V (pre-1995) or ERIC systems (2014-Up)', and 'OEM Powerdrive chargers will not function once installed'.

The bypass also changes how the cart behaves. Lester's CGR-123 version ($127.99 at Pete's Golf Carts) disables 'all OBC functionality... including the dash light'. It provides the drive lockout while the charger is plugged in only when 'paired with a compatible Lester Electrical charger', and it fits only OBC carts with the 6-pin Deutsch connector. None of the kits above say their chargers provide that lockout, so on a bypassed cart, unplug before you drive.

Not recommended

On an ERIC cart with hills, skip the LiTime 48V 100Ah conversion kit. LiTime rates it at '1C continuous', which is 100 A. CartVolts lists the ERIC-era controller as a 250 A replacement part, so the battery's BMS sets the limit on a climb. For $99.32 more, LiTime's own 2C battery and the same 18 A charger give 200 A ($1,253.98). Redodo's kit states 5,000 W, about 98 A, so the same caution applies to it on hills. On flat ground both are fine.
