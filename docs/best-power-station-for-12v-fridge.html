What the differences mean

The Solar Lab measured how much power each station uses to stay on with nothing plugged in. The inverter was on for every station except the Elite 200 V2, where the review doesn't say which outputs were on. Over a 3-day trip, that draw comes out of the battery before your fridge gets anything:

  • BLUETTI Elite 200 V2: 9.5 W × 72 h = 684 Wh; 2,073 − 684 = 1,389 Wh left; ÷ 72 h = 19 W fridge average.
  • EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max: 13.6 × 72 = 979 Wh; 2,048 − 979 = 1,069 Wh; ÷ 72 = 15 W.
  • Jackery Explorer 2000 v2: 17 × 72 = 1,224 Wh; 2,042 − 1,224 = 818 Wh; ÷ 72 = 11 W.
  • Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2: 18 × 72 = 1,296 Wh; 2,048 − 1,296 = 752 Wh; ÷ 72 = 10 W.
  • EcoFlow DELTA 3 Max: 27 × 72 = 1,944 Wh; 2,048 − 1,944 = 104 Wh; ÷ 72 = 1 W.
  • Anker SOLIX S2000: 6 × 72 = 432 Wh; 2,010 − 432 = 1,578 Wh; ÷ 72 = 22 W. The fridge has to run from its own AC adapter, and that adapter's loss isn't counted here.

1,500 Wh vs 2,000 Wh: with 10 W of idle, a 1,500 Wh station leaves (1,500 − 720) ÷ 72 = 11 W for the fridge, and a 2,048 Wh station leaves (2,048 − 720) ÷ 72 = 18 W. Both use the battery's rated watt-hours and don't count 12V conversion loss, so treat them as upper limits.

Idle draw: the number that decides 3 days

A 12V compressor fridge cycles on and off, so its average draw is small and can be close to what the station uses just to stay on. The Solar Lab measured idle draw from 9.5 W to 27 W across stations of the same 2 kWh size, a 2.8× gap (27 ÷ 9.5). The Elite 200 V2 also tested highest for efficiency at 94%; the DELTA 3 Max tested at 79%.

Anker advertises 9 W idle for the C2000 Gen 2. The Solar Lab found that figure holds only with the screen and USB ports on, and measured about 18 W with the AC outlets on standby. It measured the S2000 at 6 W with the inverter on and 2 W with it off, the lowest here, but Anker's own comparison table lists the S2000 with '12V DC Port: No'.

Setup for a 3-day run: plug the fridge into the 12V car socket and switch the AC inverter off. The Solar Lab's S2000 test shows the saving: idle fell from 6 W to 2 W with the inverter off. Jackery's troubleshooting page says its car port can switch off under a low-power load mode, so check your station's DC auto-off or ECO setting before you leave the fridge unattended. $899 on bluettipower.com, $854.05 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/bluetti-elite-200-v2

Not recommended

Anker SOLIX S2000 for a fridge with only a 12V plug. It has no 12V car socket, so a fridge with only a 12V plug can't connect to it. It works only for fridges that come with an AC adapter, and then the inverter has to stay on for the full 72 hours.

EcoFlow DELTA 3 Max with the AC left on. At The Solar Lab's 27 W, the station would use 1,944 Wh of its 2,048 Wh in 72 hours on its own. It does have a 12V car socket, so use that with the AC switched off.
