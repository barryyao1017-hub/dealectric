How much power a 12V fridge uses in a day

The makers' own figures: Dometic CFX3 55IM, 1.11 Ah an hour at 32 °C outside and 4 °C inside, about 320 Wh a day; ICECO VL45, 0.347 kWh per 24 hours (lab data). In mild weather, with the lid shut and the fridge pre-cooled, it is less; in the sun or opened often, more.

How many days each size of power station runs it

Days ≈ battery Wh × 0.85 ÷ the fridge's Wh a day. At 320 Wh a day: 1,024 Wh about 2.7 days, 1,500 Wh about 4, 2,048 Wh about 5.4. A 12V fridge on the car port avoids the AC inverter's conversion loss and its idle draw.

Solar for longer trips

A 400 W panel returns about 1.2–1.6 kWh on a sunny day, several times what the fridge uses, so with sun any of these runs a fridge indefinitely; the battery size only decides how many cloudy days you can ride out.
