Who it's for

Suits pet owners who want to look into more than one room without mounting cameras, on hard floors or low-pile carpet. Those who want scheduled patrols, two-way video or a robot that follows a pet need the EBO Air 2 Plus ($319 on Enabot's store), and a fixed pan-tilt camera such as the Petcube Cam 360 ($46.99) costs far less if one room is enough.

Caveats

  • The Irish Times' Ciara O'Brien (June 2025) found it occasionally got trapped and ran flat, returned to its dock only most of the time, dropped Wi-Fi in parts of a mesh-networked house and may struggle on deep-pile carpet.
  • Her cat refused to interact with it, and Digital Camera World's Adam Waring (June 2025) had to bribe his cat with treats; he also found the app not intuitive and the preset actions fiddly.
  • It is not autonomous: there is no scheduled patrol on the Air 2, and video is one-way (Enabot FAQ).
  • Battery runs 4–7 hours, and it climbs only 4° slopes and 6 mm obstacles (Enabot).
