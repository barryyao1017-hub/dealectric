What goes wrong in hotels, and the fix

  • The login page won't appear (the most common complaint, on every brand): connect your phone or laptop to the router, let the router's login mode pass the hotel page through, or clone the laptop's MAC; failing that, ask the front desk to whitelist the router.
  • Slow VPN is usually the hotel's connection, the VPN server's upload or the MTU, not the router; GL.iNet's forum fixed one slow Slate AX by setting the MTU to 1380.
  • Work laptop on a corporate VPN: turn the router's VPN off for that device (policy routing), or the two VPNs stack and the laptop loses the internet.
  • Cruise ships: Royal Caribbean and Carnival list travel routers as prohibited items.
