Measured VPN speeds, and why hotels can force OpenVPN

WireGuard runs over UDP only. The WireGuard project says it "explicitly does not support tunneling over TCP". OpenVPN's comparison page says that a hotel firewall that blocks UDP traffic it doesn't recognize will also block WireGuard. OpenVPN can fall back to TCP port 443, where it looks like ordinary HTTPS traffic. On GL.iNet firmware 4, you set up that fallback by uploading your VPN provider's TCP .ovpn file. GL.iNet's docs quote CyberGhost that TCP is "a bit slower" than UDP. Download the TCP config before you travel. The OpenVPN figures below are upper limits for that fallback. Expect OpenVPN over TCP to run slower.

  • Beryl 7: NASCompares (Rob Andrews, February 2026) measured OpenVPN-DCO "close to 1000Mbps over Ethernet" and WireGuard that "approached the advertised 1100Mbps ceiling under favorable conditions". VPN traffic ran 20–25% below non-VPN traffic. The router reached a public Wi-Fi captive portal (the hotel login page) about 35 s after start-up, booted in about 51 s and switched between internet sources in 4–5 s. The case measured 41–42 °C after an hour of streaming. $129.99: https://dealectric.com/go/glinet-beryl-7-gl-mt3600be
  • Beryl AX: The Gadgeteer (Joe Porletto, 2023) got WireGuard speeds "closer to 300Mbps" and 116 Mbps on OpenVPN over a month of use. The review doesn't state the test server or line speed. $98.99: https://dealectric.com/go/glinet-beryl-ax-gl-mt3000
  • Slate 7: Mighty Gadget (James Smythe, 2026) measured about 540 Mbps on WireGuard, above the 490 Mbps rating. It says OpenVPN "tops out around 100Mbps", about a quarter of the 385 Mbps OpenVPN-DCO rating (100 ÷ 385 = 26%). A GL.iNet forum owner (bigbang) measured about 500 Mbps WireGuard in a local wired test. On this router, use WireGuard.
  • Slate AX: its only VPN figures come from GL.iNet's own 2022 pre-launch test: up to 550 Mbps WireGuard and 120 Mbps OpenVPN.

Power for the Beryl 7: it runs on USB-C PD. A GL.iNet staff member (will.qiu) warned on GL.iNet's forum that with only 5 V/3 A it "may not function properly". Use a charger or power bank port that supplies 9 V/3 A (27 W) or 12 V/2.5 A (30 W).

Not recommended

GL.iNet Slate AX (GL-AXT1800). At GL.iNet's US store it costs $10 less than the Beryl 7. For that it has half the WireGuard rating (550 vs 1,100 Mbps) and about an eighth of the OpenVPN rating (1,000 ÷ 120 = 8.3×), on older Wi-Fi 6, and its only VPN figures are the maker's 2022 pre-launch test.
