Best smart gimbal recessed lights

Updated 2026-11-14

Short answer. The best smart gimbal recessed light is the Lumary RGBAI Gimbal Pro ($149.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/lumary-rgbai-gimbal-pro-4-inch-4-pack): 90° tilt and 270° rotation, 780 lumens per 4-inch light, and a 12-segment color ring. Lumary's warranty is 1 year, against 3 years on CloudyBay's 6-inch smart gimbal.

Tilt is what you buy a gimbal for. A 6/12 roof pitch is about 27°, and CloudyBay's 6-inch smart gimbal lists 30° at most. The most common owner complaint is a light or its power supply failing within about a year (10 of 20 recent Lumary Trustpilot reviews). None of these smart gimbals dim from a wall dimmer: leave the switch on and dim in the app or by voice.

On a flat ceiling where you only need a little aim, the CloudyBay 6-inch smart gimbal is brighter (850 lm, 15 W, CRI 90+) and cost $109.99 per 4-pack on its store on 2026-11-14. For a colored beam aimed at a wall, plus a 2200 K night light, pick the Lumary Ultra ($169.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/lumary-rgb-gimbal-ultra-4-inch-4-pack). For insulated ceilings, the Lumary 9 W ($129.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/lumary-wifi-gimbal-4-inch-9w-4-pack) is the only Lumary gimbal whose store page lists an IC rating.

What owners complain about

  • Failures within about a year (10 of 20 recent Lumary Trustpilot reviews). Most of those reviewers say support sent a replacement, but Lumary's warranty is 1 year and you pay return shipping on a return.
  • Pairing and Wi-Fi drops (6 reports across Trustpilot, a Home Assistant forum thread and retailer reviews). All of these lights need 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. One owner said a light sometimes lost its setup after being switched off at the wall.
  • Wall dimmers and switches (5 sources, including Lumary and CloudyBay). A smart light switched off at the wall goes offline, and none of these smart gimbals dim from a wall dimmer.
  • Less light than expected (2 reports). A 4-inch smart gimbal puts out 600-810 lm, while the 6-inch CloudyBay lists 850 lm.
  • Less tilt than advertised (2 reports, one of them about an unnamed brand). We did not measure tilt. The figures here are the makers'.

Which one by need

  • Steep or vaulted ceiling, or aiming at art and walls: Lumary RGBAI Gimbal Pro, for 90° tilt and the most light of Lumary's 90° models.
  • Colored wall wash or a hallway night light: Lumary Ultra. Its aimed main beam changes color, and it has a 2200 K night light.
  • Insulated ceiling: Lumary Wi-Fi Gimbal 9 W. It is IC rated, airtight and ETL listed, with 40° of adjustment.
  • Flat ceiling, most light, longest warranty: CloudyBay 6-inch smart gimbal, with 850 lm, a 3-year warranty and 30° of tilt.
  • You want a normal wall dimmer: skip smart gimbals. Use a dimmable gimbal such as the CloudyBay 4-inch 5-color and put a smart dimmer switch on the circuit.

Not recommended

  • Fixed smart downlights from Govee or Philips Hue when you need to aim light. They point straight down.
  • Any smart gimbal on a circuit with a wall dimmer you plan to keep using.
  • A gimbal with less than 30° of tilt on a ceiling steeper than 6/12.
