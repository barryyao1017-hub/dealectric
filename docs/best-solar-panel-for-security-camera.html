Solar panel compatibility by camera model

Reolink. Reolink's 3 W, 6 W (Solar Panel 2) and 12 W (Solar Panel 3) panels fit 'all Reolink battery-powered cameras except Reolink Argus and Keen', and Reolink's DC-powered cameras can't use them. Solar Panel 2 and 3 use USB-C. The 3 W panel comes in Micro-USB or USB-C versions; for a port mismatch, Reolink says to use a USB-C to Micro-USB cable. Reolink advises no more than 9 m (30 ft) of extension cable in total, because longer runs slow or destabilise charging. The Argus 4 Pro takes only the included USB-A to USB-C cable. All three panels charge only between 0 °C and 45 °C (32–113 °F), and Reolink says charging may stop below 0 °C to protect the lithium battery. For cold sites, Reolink's Solar Panel 2S carries a 5,900 mAh (42.48 Wh) battery that powers the camera directly from 0 °C down to −20 °C, by Reolink's support article. Its product page gives a narrower working range of −10 °C to 50 °C.

eufy. eufy's 3 W add-on panel, the T8705011, fits the cameras in the table. eufy says only certified eufy panels can sync with eufyCam. On the eufyCam S3 Pro, the external panel and the built-in panel charge the camera at the same time. The SoloCam S340's included panel can be mounted away from the camera on the supplied USB-C extension, and eufy recommends a 20°–30° tilt.

Ring. The USB-C Solar Panel plugs straight into the USB-C models. It reaches the barrel-jack Battery and Solar models through its included adapter. Ring lists the 1st-gen Stick Up Cam as not compatible.
