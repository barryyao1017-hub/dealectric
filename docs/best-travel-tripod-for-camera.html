Can you change the head later?

The head is the part travel shooters most often outgrow: video needs a fluid pan head, and panoramas need a levelling base. Here is what each tripod allows.

  • Peak Design Travel Tripod: yes, two ways. Peak Design's $29.95 Universal Head Adapter entirely replaces the built-in ball head; Shutter Muse notes the swap takes "a bit of fiddling around with screws and hex wrenches". The adapter takes "any standard 3rd party tripod head" and fits both the carbon and aluminum versions, with or without the center column. For an occasional second head, keep the stock clamp instead. Dan Carr of Shutter Muse mounted an RRS BH-55 and an Acratech GXP by fitting an Arca-Swiss plate under each head. He reports the clamp takes other Arca plates and L-brackets once its two stopper screws are removed; with the screws in, only Peak Design's plate fits. Peak Design's Standard Plate ($19.95 on its own) "works with most ARCA-type tripods".
  • Ulanzi Zero Y: only with an extra part. Ulanzi's store page says "The tripod ball head is not detachable" and recommends its F38 1/4 center column for anyone who wants a different head. David Lowe (Ridgeline Images) confirms the clamp is Arca-Swiss compatible but found the head "tends to sag under heavy loads".
  • Sirui Traveler 5C: yes. Camera Jabber found that the centre column unscrews and a head screws straight onto the crown in about a minute; the base plate is Arca Swiss-style. $109.90 on store.sirui.com, $101.11 through this link with code AFFSRBFBO: https://dealectric.com/go/sirui-traveler-5c-carbon-travel-tripod
  • Already own Manfrotto plates? On the Manfrotto Befree Advanced AS (twist lock, aluminum), the Arca-type plate holder also works with Manfrotto's RC2 system, according to Manfrotto. It has a 3/8" top thread and a 9 kg safety payload rated to UNI/PdR 105:2021, and it weighs 1.51 kg and closes to 40 cm.
