Who it's for

A cabin used on weekends and holidays that needs about 2–3 kWh a day of 120 V power and has a fixed roof or ground mount for the panels.

Caveats

  • 120 V output only. Sun Gold Power's 5000W 48V inverter gives 120/240 V split phase only with more than 2 units in parallel, so this kit won't run a 240 V well pump.
  • The listing names a standard 100 Ah 51.2 V LiFePO4 battery. Sun Gold Power's self-heating SGH48100T (heats below 41 °F / 5 °C) costs $1,035 against $981 for the SG48100P.
  • The box has no AC disconnect, grounding kit or combiner. Plan for those and for an installer.
