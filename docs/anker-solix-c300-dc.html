Who it's for

A Wi-Fi router or router-plus-modem that runs from 12 V or USB-C, for an overnight to one-day outage.

Caveats

  • No AC outlet: a router with only a wall adapter needs a 12 V barrel cable or a USB-C 12 V trigger cable instead.
  • The low-power USB-C4 port tops out at 15 W; use one of the 140 W USB-C ports or the car socket for a router.
  • techtest.org tested the European C300X DC; Anker sells it in the US as the C300 DC with the same 288 Wh and 140 W USB-C.
