Who it's for

Travellers to the UK plus the continent, or anyone charging a USB-C laptop, phone and more from one wall socket.

Caveats

  • Only USB-C3 gives 65 W, and only when used alone; the other four USB ports stop at 15 W each.
  • Its Type C pins fall under the Europlug standard's 2.5 A at 250 V (about 575 W on 230 V); TESSAN does not state a higher pin rating, so keep hair dryers and other heat tools off it.
  • Not a voltage converter: devices must accept 100–250 V.
  • The side AC outlet is 2-prong only; a 3-prong US cord needs the front outlet.
