Best CD player with Bluetooth (2026)

Short answer. The best CD player with Bluetooth that sends to headphones or a speaker is the FiiO DM13 BT (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DKXDKBG5?tag=dealectric-20): aptX HD and aptX Low Latency transmit, a 60-second anti-skip buffer and a 10-hour battery (about 8 h measured). Want a CD player with its own speakers that also plays your phone? The Panasonic SC-PM270 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CZ9Q8332?tag=dealectric-20).

Check which way the Bluetooth goes. Most "CD players with Bluetooth" (the Panasonic systems, Sony's boomboxes, every TEAC and Tascam) only receive from a phone and can't play a CD through Bluetooth headphones or a speaker. Already own a CD player? A $40-class transmitter such as the 1Mii B06TX (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0838YPSZT?tag=dealectric-20) adds Bluetooth transmit through its optical, coaxial or RCA output.

What owners complain about

Pairing that fails or drops mid-song, or needs redoing at every power-on, on $25–90 portables (the most common review theme at Best Buy and Amazon); players bought to feed Bluetooth headphones that turn out to be receive-only; skipping despite "anti-skip"; cheap units dying within weeks; and battery life under the claim.

Home stereo to Bluetooth headphones

No mainstream component CD player transmits Bluetooth. Plug a transmitter into the player's optical or coaxial output; aptX Low Latency matters only for video and only if your headphones support it.

Not recommended

$25–90 no-name Bluetooth portables (pairing and durability complaints), and any TEAC or Tascam model if you need to send to headphones: their Bluetooth is receive-only, and TEAC's US PD-301-X has none.
