What the differences mean

  • Shared codec. A player and a pair of headphones use the best codec they both support. DM13 {aptX HD, aptX LL, aptX, SBC} ∩ AirPods Pro 3 {AAC, SBC} = SBC. DM13 ∩ soundcore Space One {SBC, AAC, LDAC} = SBC. Either headphone gets SBC from all three portables here.
  • Full CDs per charge (rated). DM13: 10 h × 60 ÷ 74 min = 8.1 discs. EC Play: 12 h × 60 ÷ 74 = 9.7 discs, or 5.7–6.5 at Mobile Audiophile's 7–8 h estimate. DM15: 6 h × 60 ÷ 74 = 4.9 discs.
  • Average draw. DM13: 3.75 Ah × 3.8 V = 14.25 Wh ÷ 10 h = 1.4 W. DM15: 4.7 Ah × 3.85 V = 18.1 Wh ÷ 6 h = 3.0 W. The DM15 has the bigger battery but a 40% shorter rated runtime (6 ÷ 10 = 0.6).
  • Price step. DM15 at $275 minus EC Play at $199 = $76 more for aptX Lossless and aptX Adaptive.

Which codec your headphones actually get

Under the Bluetooth A2DP 1.4 specification (§4.2.1), every Bluetooth audio source must encode SBC and every headphone or speaker must decode it. All other codecs are optional and are used only when both ends support the same one (§4.2.2). So any of these three portables will play through any Bluetooth headphone or speaker, and the codec on the spec sheet only matters if your headphones share it.

  • FiiO DM13 BT sends aptX HD, aptX Low Latency, aptX and SBC on a Qualcomm Bluetooth platform. FiiO's own footnotes say some headphones and speakers connect at SBC only and some car Bluetooth systems are not supported. To pair, short-press power until the screen shows BLUE OUT, then long-press Previous (FiiO support). buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DKXDKBG5?tag=dealectric-20
  • Shanling EC Play lists LDAC, but only for receiving from a phone. When it sends CD audio, it uses SBC. Shanling's spec sheet says so, and Chris Love of Mobile Audiophile confirms that in transmitter mode it sends SBC, not LDAC or AAC.
  • FiiO DM15 adds aptX Adaptive and aptX Lossless but still has no AAC, so Apple headphones fall back to SBC.
  • Headphones checked: What Hi-Fi lists AAC and SBC for AirPods Pro 3. soundcore lists SBC, AAC and LDAC for the Space One. Neither has aptX, so both get SBC from every player above.

Measured performance is published only for the DM13. Audio Science Review's NTTY measured 0.0015% THD+N on the balanced output and 98 dB dynamic range. The player also played through 3 mm dropouts on defect test discs and ran 10 h on battery. That unit was the non-Bluetooth DM13, so the Bluetooth link itself has not been measured.

Not recommended

The standard FiiO DM13, if you want wireless headphones. It has the same name and the same CD drive, but FiiO's page says the standard version cannot transmit Bluetooth. If a listing doesn't say "DM13 BT" or "Bluetooth version", it will play only through wired headphones or a cable.
