Who it's for

Homeowners who need to run 240 V circuits such as a well pump or central AC through a transfer switch, especially with a large solar array.

Caveats

  • High idle draw: about 58 W measured by The Solar Lab and 85 W by HOBOTECH (Jackery lists 50 W standby)
  • Struggled to start a 10,000 W-surge air conditioner in The Solar Lab's test
  • 240 V charging needs Jackery's optional Smart Transfer Switch; 120 V wall charging measured at 1,750 W (HOBOTECH)
  • Weighs about 61 kg, and the extra batteries don't stack or roll with the unit (HOBOTECH)
  • The L14-50R outlet tops out at 7,200 W, which is 30 A at 240 V
