Who it's for

Rideshare, taxi and delivery drivers who need both seat rows on video at night, and fleet owners who want one unit that covers the cabin and the road.

Caveats

  • Parking mode needs a hardwire kit, sold separately ($29.99 Voltage Display Hardwire Kit at Vantrue on 2026-11-14).
  • Front camera has WDR but no HDR; DashCamTalk found night license plate capture nearly impossible on it.
  • Large front unit (about 4.9 × 2.7 × 2.0 in) that can be hard to hide behind the mirror.
  • The rear module tilts up but not far down, so it fits a rear window, not a headliner.
  • In an SUV with a third row or child seats, the rear cabin camera sees less (New Atlas).
  • No microSD card, hardwire kit or CPL filter in the base box.
