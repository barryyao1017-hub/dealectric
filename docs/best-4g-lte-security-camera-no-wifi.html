What owners complain about

Across the cellular cameras we read about, the same five problems keep coming up. The data plan is first: every one of the buying guides and listings we read says the camera stops streaming when the trial or plan runs out, and the brand-locked plans (Arenti, and many no-name cameras) cannot be swapped for a cheap carrier SIM. Coverage is second: a buyer with a remote hut needed a different carrier's SIM than the one in the box, and a Minnesota tester saw one to two bars of AT&T at his cabin. Winter battery is third: that same tester had to hand-charge his Reolink Go PT Ultra every few weeks below -10°F even with the solar panel, and Tom's Guide expects the eufy S330 to need topping up over winter. Data use is fourth: continuous HD streaming ate about 1 GB every two hours in security.org's test, so keep motion zones tight. For Arenti specifically, Trustpilot shows 61 reviews at 2.8 out of 5; slow support is the most common complaint (about 9 of the 21 visible reviews), followed by hardware that failed within a year and cloud plans that were hard to cancel. Arenti's app scores 4.5 out of 5 from about 1,900 ratings, with dropped connections and too many alerts the main gripes.
