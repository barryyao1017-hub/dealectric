Who it's for

Suits shooters with a full-frame mirrorless or DSLR body and a standard f/2.8 zoom who want 14-hour battery life and Arca-type plates. Phone shooters and owners of light APS-C kits should take the SCORP Mini 3 Pro ($207.20 on FeiyuTech's store), and anyone who needs object tracking should look at Hohem's iSteady MT3 Pro ($314.99 on Hohem's store).

Caveats

  • Camera only: no phone holder, and the tracker follows faces by hand gesture with no object tracking (FeiyuTech FAQ).
  • Digital Camera World's George Cairns (Sept 2025, Sony A7 III with Tamron 28-75mm, 1.37 kg) found it a little wobbly at speed and on low-angle walking shots, and balancing took him 5–10 minutes.
  • At about 1.4 kg it is heavier than the SCORP 2 (1,070 g).
  • A store reviewer reports the trigger only switches modes when the gimbal is held still, which FeiyuTech support told him is by design.
  • Not waterproof; FeiyuTech advises against rain or very humid conditions.
  • $299 is FeiyuTech's Black Friday price (sale runs Nov 16–30); the product page shows $399 before the sale.
