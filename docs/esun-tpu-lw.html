Who it's for

Lightweight flexible parts that must hold their shape, such as cosplay armor, RC plane and drone bumpers, and protective covers, when cost per kg matters.

Caveats

  • Its softest setting is 77A. For insole-soft parts near 65A, Siraya Tech Flex TPU Air 85A and colorFabb varioShore go softer.
  • Stringing is unavoidable. eSUN says to turn retraction off and print one model at a time.
  • eSUN advises against the Bambu Lab AMS. Feed it from an external spool or dry box through a short guide tube.
  • Owners on the Bambu Lab forum disagree on settings: one printed well at about 0.5-0.6 flow and 491-500 °F (255-260 °C), while another said no profile printed well.
