What the differences mean

Five cameras that their makers sell as 4-channel were at or under $200 on 2026-11-21. A channel is one camera recording at the same time as the others. The five differ in what the third and fourth cameras see, and in what you still have to buy for parking mode and GPS.

  • Front camera detail. The Botslab's 3K front frame is 2880 × 1620 = 4,665,600 pixels. A 1080p frame is 1920 × 1080 = 2,073,600 pixels, so the Botslab front records 2.25 times the pixels of the front camera on the Rexing C4 or LAMTTO DC21. Rear and side cameras are 1080p on every row.
  • GPS. Botslab and REDTIGER list GPS built in. The Rexing C4 has none until you add Rexing's Type-C Inline GPS Adapter: $199.99 + $19.99 = $219.98.
  • Parking mode. REDTIGER F17 Plus: $189.99 + $35.99 = $225.98. Rexing C4: $199.99 + $29.99 = $229.98 with the Type-C Smart Hardwire Kit, or $199.99 + $39.99 = $239.98 with the 360° Motion Detection kit. The Botslab's $35.99 kit on top of $192.70 also passes $200 on the date read. The LAMTTO DC21 ($129.99) and DC25 ($149.99) have the kit in the box.
  • Hours before the card loops. DashCamTalk lists the F17 Plus at 16 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 46 Mbps. 46 ÷ 8 × 3,600 = 20,700 MB, about 20.7 GB an hour, so its 128 GB card holds 128 ÷ 20.7 = about 6.2 hours of four-channel video. It is the only row with a bitrate we could source.

Parking mode and GPS: which part each camera takes

All prices below are the makers' US stores on 2026-11-21.

  • Botslab G980H 4-Camera. The Type-C Hardwire Kit for Dash Cam is $35.99 (list $45.99). Its listing names the G980H 4-Channel, has a 9 ft cable, takes DC 12-30 V and supplies 5 V at 2.4 A, for up to 24 hours of time-lapse or collision-triggered recording. Botslab's Type-C Dash Cam Hardwire Kit with Fuse Tap is $44.99 with an 11 ft cable, and it is the listing that also names sentry mode. Vortex Radar found that sentry-mode motion detection works from the front camera only, that the G-sensor has very low sensitivity, and that time-lapse records 1 frame a second without audio.
  • Rexing C4. Rexing's listing says "Parking mode requires a Smart Hardwire Kit (sold separately)" and does not say which. The Type-C Smart Hardwire Kit is $29.99 (12-30 V in, 5 V at 2 A out, cuts off at 11.8 V); its listing names the R4, DT2, M3, M2 Max, M2-4, M4-4 and RH2 Series. The Smart Hardwire Kit with 360° Motion Detection is $39.99 with a 13 ft cable and "supports nearly all models except 4G LTE, R4-RD, and Multimedia Receiver units". Ask Rexing which one the C4 takes before ordering. For GPS, the Type-C Inline GPS Adapter is $19.99 and its listing names the C4. Rexing's $39.99 hardwire kit with a GPS logger built in lists only the R4, L4 Series and RH2 Series.
  • REDTIGER F17 Plus. The Redtiger Dash Cam Hardwire Kit For Parking Monitor is $35.99 and is "compatible with all Redtiger dash cams". Input is 12-24 V, output 5 V at 2 A, and it cuts power 60 seconds after the battery falls below 11.8 V (±0.2). REDTIGER states it is "not applicable to trucks with voltage above 24V". DashCamTalk lists the camera's parking mode as non-buffered plus time-lapse.
  • LAMTTO DC21 and DC25. Both include the hardwire kit. On the DC21 you connect the included ACC cable to choose a parking mode, and a trigger saves a 15-second locked clip. On the DC25 the kit has battery protection and gives a 24-hour standby mode that starts recording when an impact is detected.

Not recommended

The LAMTTO DC25 for its 2.5K mode. LAMTTO's example of a higher-resolution combination is 2.5K + 720p + 720p + 1080p, so raising one camera above 1080p drops two others to 720p. If 2.5K means 2560 × 1440, that mode records 3,686,400 + 2 × 921,600 + 2,073,600 = 7,603,200 pixels per set of frames, fewer than the 4 × 2,073,600 = 8,294,400 of its plain four-1080p mode. The DC25 also takes cards only up to 128 GB. If you want a LAMTTO, the DC21 is $149.99 − $129.99 = $20.00 less and has a 128 GB card named in its title.
