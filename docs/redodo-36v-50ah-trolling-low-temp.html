Who it's for

Two in parallel on a 36 V motor (101–112 lb or 36 V QUEST): each pack then carries about 30 A at the motor's maximum draw.

Caveats

  • A single pack is rated 60 A for only 30 minutes, against the 60 A a 36 V QUEST motor can draw on high speed.
  • Won't charge below 32°F.
  • Needs a 43.8 V LiFePO4 charger. A Minn Kota onboard bank tops out at 14.5 V.
