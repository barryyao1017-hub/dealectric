What Bluetti owners complain about

Across the Bluetti forum, Trustpilot, the BBB, Reddit mirrors and reviewer tests, the complaints cluster into five themes.

  • Slow warranty service. The most common theme. The BBB shows 45 complaints in 3 years, Trustpilot's 1-star reviews describe replacements that took weeks, and one Reddit owner waited almost a month for a replacement. Many 5-star reviews praise support, so it is inconsistent rather than absent.
  • Early failures and error codes. Forum threads report E013 relay faults, E004 inverter faults and AC180 units that shut down and lock up during grid fluctuations. The small EB3A draws the most failure reports, including two units dying the same way for one Reddit owner. It carries only a 2-year warranty.
  • Fan noise under light load. Elite 200 V2 owners report a fan that cycles on and off at low loads; Bluetti staff called it normal "hiccup mode". One reviewer measured 54 dB at night, while APH Networks found it quiet in every test. PCWorld measured the AC180 at 40 dBA at 200 W and 45 dBA at 1,500 W.
  • App dependence. Turbo charging and UPS settings live in the app, and reviewers at PopSci, New Atlas and Amazon's review summary call the Bluetooth link glitchy.
  • Weight. The Elite 200 V2 is 53.4 lb and the Elite 300 is 60 lb. Buy the Elite 300 if you need wheels.

Returns are strict: the 30-day return policy gives a full refund only for an unused, complete unit, and return shipping comes out of the refund.
