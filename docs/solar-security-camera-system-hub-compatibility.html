What the differences mean

  • Room to grow on one Reolink hub: the Argus 4 Pro Kit ships 2 cameras, 2 Solar Panel 2s and a Home Hub, so 8 − 2 = 6 more cameras fit. A Home Hub Pro raises the cap to 12 cameras, or to 24 battery cameras on some hardware versions.
  • eufy cost per camera with a hub: the SoloCam E50 4-Cam Kit with HomeBase S380 costs $999.99 ÷ 4 = $250.00 per camera, hub included (eufy, 2026-11-13).
  • What the eufy hub adds: the E50 2-Cam Kit is $649.99 with HomeBase S380 and $449.99 without, so the hub costs $649.99 − $449.99 = $200. Each E50 already records to its own microSD card (up to 512 GB) with no monthly fee. The hub adds BionicMind family-vs-stranger recognition and storage up to 16 TB.
  • Daily draw on Reolink's own figures: a 5,000 mAh battery lasting about 60 days at 300 s of recording a day works out to 5,000 ÷ 60 ≈ 83 mAh a day.
  • The panel is not optional: James Smythe at Mighty Gadget saw his Argus 4 Pro at about 25% after about 3 weeks without solar, (100 − 25) ÷ 21 ≈ 3.6% a day. On solar in sunny weather, it read 100% consistently over the first few days.
  • Panel cable reach: Reolink panels have a 4 m lead, and Reolink advises against more than 9 m of extension cable, so 4 + 9 = 13 m from panel to camera before charging may slow.

Why the hub, not the panel, defines the system

A single solar camera records to its own card. A system means one hub that stores and manages every camera. The two biggest consumer hubs set hard rules on which cameras they accept.

  • Reolink Home Hub: Reolink states that 'the total number of connected cameras cannot exceed 8, regardless of camera type', lists 4G cameras as 'Not Supported', and says a camera 'must be on the same LAN as the hub' to be added. The Argus 4 Series works out of the box. The Argus Eco Ultra needs its compatibility firmware first. Reolink also says battery-powered 4G cameras can't be used with its NVRs.
  • eufy HomeBase 3 (S380): eufy's compatibility table marks the SoloCam S340 compatible for both storage and BionicMind. The 4G LTE Cam S330 stores to the hub 'only under Wi-Fi mode', and the 4G Starlight Cam S230 is 'Not Compatible' because it 'does not connect to Wi-Fi'. The SoloCam E50 needs HomeBase 3 firmware V3.8.8.0 or later.
  • What that means off-grid: on a site without Wi-Fi, every cellular camera is its own island with its own SIM, data plan and app view. The $245: https://dealectric.com/go/anywherecam-eagle-4g-pro ships with its panel and battery pack and runs up to 7 days without direct sun, by AnywhereCam's figure. On a budget, the Arenti GO3G 4G LTE camera is $39.99 at Arenti (2026-11-13) $39.99 on arenti.com, $36 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/arenti-go3g-4g-lte-camera, and it too stays outside any Reolink or eufy hub.
  • Cold sites: Reolink says its cameras' lithium batteries 'cannot charge properly' below 0 °C (32 °F) and gives 0–45 °C as the panels' charging range. In a freezing week a bigger panel won't refill the battery, so size the reserve for it.
