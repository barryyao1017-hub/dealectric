Who it's for

Hobbyists, machine shops and product designers reverse-engineering parts from 10 mm up to about a meter into CAD, who want one scanner that covers both marker-based laser scans and marker-free turntable scans.

Caveats

  • Laser modes need reflective markers in view at all times; featureless parts or small objects without markers stop with a tracking-lost warning.
  • Revopoint's forum has crash reports for Revo Scan MetroX 5.6.7 and 5.6.8 and for Revo Metro 5.8.5; owners roll back versions, disable rendering acceleration and send logs to support.
  • Needs a strong Windows PC: 32 GB of RAM minimum, 64 GB and an RTX 4060 recommended; older laptops lag and fail to process large scans.
  • Chrome, mirror-finish and transparent parts still need scanning spray in every mode.
  • At 508 g it is heavier than the Creality Raptor (372 g).
  • The $899 Advanced Edition adds magnetic marker blocks; the scanner itself is the same.
