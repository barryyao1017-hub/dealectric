What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test units. Each figure below names its source, and all were read on 2026-11-21.

  • Why radar at all: about 6 times the parking hours. Thinkware's parking-mode table gives 45.2 mA for Energy Saving 2.0 with radar on, against 274.5 mA for 2-channel motion detection: 274.5 ÷ 45.2 = 6.1. That matches its runtimes of 99.6 h against 16.4 h on the same battery (99.6 ÷ 16.4 = 6.1). On a DashCamTalk bench, SafeDriveSolutions measured a U3000 at 16 mA in radar mode against 260–270 mA in motion detection at 13.8 V: 0.016 A × 13.8 V ≈ 0.22 W.
  • U3000 Pro over U3000: $70. $549.99 − $479.99 = $70.00 at Thinkware US. The extra buys a STARVIS 2 rear sensor (IMX675 against the U3000's IMX335, per DashCamTalk), cards up to 512 GB instead of 256 GB, and the LTE option. Thinkware also claims 10% lower power use than the U3000 but gives no base figure.
  • LTE adds $140, and it pauses in radar mode. $689.99 − $549.99 = $140.00 for the U3000 Pro LTE bundle. Thinkware's help page says Connected features are unavailable in RADAR Parking Mode, so remote alerts work while driving and in Thinkware's other parking modes, but not while the radar is watching.
  • Range counts from the rear camera, not the bumper. In Thinkware's example, a rear camera 1.5 m from the car's rear end at the default 3 m setting covers 3 − 1.5 = 1.5 m behind the car. Long-tailed cars need the 4 m or 5 m step.

Built-in radar or add-on module: what wakes the camera and what it keeps

The Thinkware U3000 and U3000 Pro have radar built into both the front and rear units. Thinkware's help page gives the details. You set the range to 1, 2, 3 (default), 4 or 5 m, measured from the rear camera. The radar is designed for movement slower than 20 km/h (12 mph). Each event saves a 20 s clip: 7 s before and 13 s after an impact, or 10 s and 10 s for a slow approach. Radar parking needs hardwiring or the OBD-II cable. Other 24 GHz devices and metal-containing tint film can shorten the range. Digital Camera World's Alistair Charlton found that the U3000 Pro's default sensitivity woke it for rain, recording a few dozen clips in one night. Turning the sensitivity down one step fixed it.

Thinkware's add-on Radar Module (TWA-RADS, $89.99), made for the U1000 Plus, U1000, Q1000 and X1000, works differently. Its page says 'Sedan or greater sized vehicle can be detected' and 'People passing by will not be detected', with a range of about 7 m across about 80°. When it senses motion the camera gets ready, but if no impact follows, the page says, the camera does not save the footage and goes back to Energy Saving Mode 2.0. The ARC 900 radar bundle describes the same wake-then-impact behaviour.

DDPAI sells its radar as an $80.00 Radar Accessory titled for the N5 Pro and N5 Dual, and inside the N5 Dual 2026 radar kit ($269 on store.ddpai.com, $236.72 through this link with code SAVEBIGBF: https://dealectric.com/go/ddpai-n5-dual-2026-radar-kit). DDPAI's radar records on motion: DashCamTalk reviewer lufa6977 found that the N5 Pro's radar at high sensitivity 'captured video snaps of small bumps in the road or people walking by', and turned it down ($179 on store.ddpai.com, $157.52 through this link with code SAVEBIGBF: https://dealectric.com/go/ddpai-n5-pro). Wiring matters. DashCamTalk owner thatbwnguy reported that an N5 Dual on the OBD kit in a 2019 Mazda CX-9 shut down about 15 minutes after parking. DDPAI's forum reply said the OBD port may not report ignition status and recommended wiring to the fuse box.

On LTE, Thinkware's U3000 page says 'Not compatible with the LTE Module'. The U3000 Pro is sold with one in a $689.99 bundle, though its LTE module and its infrared cabin camera cannot be used at the same time.

Not recommended

The Thinkware U1000 Plus with the add-on Radar Module, if your worry is someone keying or breaking into the car. The module does not detect people passing by and keeps no clip unless an impact follows. Thinkware's U1000 Plus page also showed Out of Stock on 2026-11-21, and its rear camera is 1080p. Camera plus module cost $369.99 + $89.99 = $459.98, only $479.99 − $459.98 = $20.01 less than the U3000, which has radar built in.
