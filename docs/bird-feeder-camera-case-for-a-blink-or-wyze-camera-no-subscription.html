Can I turn the Blink or Wyze camera I already own into a bird feeder camera without a subscription? (2026)

Short answer. Yes. The Wasserstein Bird Feeder Camera Case ($50.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/wasserstein-bird-feeder-camera-case) turns the Blink or Wyze camera you already own into a bird feeder camera without a subscription: $50.99 on 2026-11-03 (list $65.99), holding 26.4 oz of seed and fitting the Blink Outdoor 4 and 3, Wyze Cam OG, v3, v3 Pro and Outdoor, and Ring Stick Up Cam. What to know: the case is a weatherproof feeder with a camera pocket, nothing more, so the picture, the clips and the alerts are whatever your camera already gives you. TechRadar found Blink's picture in the case slightly sharper than Ring's, and Bob Vila's review (7.8/10) says squirrels are not kept out. Returns are 30 days.

What recording you get with your own camera

With a Wyze Cam v3 or OG, a microSD card in the camera records continuously or on events with no plan, and the Wyze app's free tier gives 12-second event clips with a cooldown. With a Blink Outdoor 4, a Sync Module 2 saves motion clips to a USB drive (up to 256 GB) when the account has no plan; Blink's local clips skip person detection, extended live view and Moments. Either way the feeder adds nothing to the storage question and takes nothing away.

Mounting and build

A strap for small trees and poles or a metal wall mount, weatherproof plastic, and a 26.4 oz seed well. TechRadar's review found the Blink picture sharper than Ring's in the case and birds not perfectly sharp with either; CNN Underscored called it an affordable way to start, and Bob Vila scored it 7.8/10 with the note that it is not critter-proof.

When a dedicated feeder is the better buy

If you do not own a Wyze or Blink camera, a Birdfy Lite at $169.99 is within $50 of buying a camera plus the case and comes assembled with its own app; if species identification is the point, Birdfy's AI model or Bird Buddy do it and the Wasserstein case cannot.

Warranty and returns

The store page states no warranty term for the case; Wasserstein lists 3 months on its accessories. Returns are 30 days.
