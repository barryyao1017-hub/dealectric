What the differences mean

Every maker quotes its longest battery figure, and that figure assumes the lights are off. Dividing each lit figure by an 8-hour typing day shows how often you will charge with the backlight on:

  • Keychron Q1 Max: 30 h ÷ 8 = 3.75 days lit (RGB at lowest); 180 h ÷ 8 = 22.5 days dark.
  • Lofree Flow 2 84: 14 h ÷ 8 = 1.75 days at maximum backlight; 120 h ÷ 8 = 15 days dark.
  • NuPhy Air75 V3: 60–100 h ÷ 8 = 7.5 to 12.5 days lit; 1,200 h ÷ 8 = 150 days dark.
  • NuPhy Halo75 V2: 9–91 h ÷ 8 = 1 to 11 days lit; 307 h ÷ 8 = 38 days dark.
  • Logitech MX Mechanical Mini: 10 months × 30.4 = 304 days dark; 304 ÷ 15 = about 20× the 15-day lit figure.
  • Price: $209.99 − $159 = $50.99 more for the Q1 Max than the Flow 2 84.

The Q1 Max is our typing pick, and we don't earn on it. KitGuru's Mat Mynett lists “incredible typing feel” and “great acoustics and sound profile” among its pros, and its only con is “no adjustable typing angle”. Keychron's cheaper K8 Max lists at $94.99, but the US ANSI version showed as unavailable at Keychron on 2026-11-17.

Why the battery figure on the box assumes the lights are off

Each keyboard draws power for its LEDs for as long as they are lit. Reading keys and running the radio cost far less, so the backlight setting decides how long a charge lasts. All five makers rate their boards with the lights off and give a much smaller figure with them on:

  • Keychron rates the Q1 Max at “up to 180 hours” with the backlight off and “up to 30 hours (lowest brightness)” with RGB on, as reprinted in KitGuru's review. Brighter settings will run shorter than 30 h.
  • Lofree rates the Flow 2 84 at “up to 120 hours without backlighting” (Digital Trends' spec table). PCWorld's Mattias Inghe gives “up to 14 hours with the backlight set to maximum brightness” for the same 3,000 mAh cell. In his three-day test the charge fell 20 percent, and he lists “moderate battery life” as a con.
  • NuPhy rates the Air75 V3 at 60 to 100 h with all lights on and up to 1,200 h with all lights off. The Halo75 V2 (QMK/VIA) is rated at 9 to 91 h lit and 307 h dark.
  • Logitech says the MX Mechanical Mini “stays powered up to 15 days on a full charge – or up to 10 months with backlighting turned off.”

The lit figures aren't all measured the same way: Keychron's is at the lowest brightness and Lofree's 14 h is at maximum, so the Flow 2 84 at a low setting will last longer than 14 h. The battery size doesn't explain the gaps either. The Q1 Max and Air75 V3 both carry 4,000 mAh, yet NuPhy's dark rating is 1,200 ÷ 180 = 6.7× Keychron's. That difference comes from each board's power draw and each maker's test conditions. If you type with the backlight on, compare the lit column, not the headline figure.

Hot-swap sockets also tie each board to its own switch family. Gadgetoid found the Flow 2's Kailh low-profile switches are “not pin compatible with MX low profile switches”, and NuPhy lists the Air75 V3's switch type only as its own Low-profile 3.0. So choose the switch feel when you buy a low-profile board.
