Who it's for

iPhone owners who leave the phone behind for runs, gym and errands; parents or adult children setting up a watch for a kid or senior without a phone.

Caveats

  • An iPhone is required once to set it up; a GPS-only SE 3 cannot be set up for a family member.
  • Watches set up through Apple Watch For Your Kids lose Sleep, ECG, Cycle Tracking, Blood Oxygen and card payments in Wallet.
  • Owners report a long phone call can drain most of the battery; 18 hours is the rating with the iPhone nearby.
