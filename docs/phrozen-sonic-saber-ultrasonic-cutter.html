Who it's for

Resin printers who print miniatures, busts or other delicate parts and break them with flush cutters, and who work at a desk near an outlet.

Caveats

  • Corded: you work within the cord's reach of an outlet.
  • On PLA and other FDM plastics, friction heat can melt the cut instead of slicing it. One owner called PLA "almost impossible"; ABS and ASA worked.
  • Two retail owners reported a unit that died early (one within seconds). Phrozen replaced one of them.
  • One owner said thin supports didn't cut until they pushed hard. Hackster's reviewer found a light touch cut even thick supports.
  • Humans can't hear the vibration, but one reviewer's dog was upset by it.
  • The blade gets hot enough to burn. Let it cool before changing it.
