Best KVM switch for a laptop and a desktop, dual monitor (2026)

Updated 2026-11-05

Short answer. For a Windows work laptop and a desktop on two monitors, get the AV Access KD-E10 ($169.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/avaccess-kd-e10): one USB-C cable from the laptop carries both 4K@60 screens and 100 W of charging, the desktop plugs in over HDMI and DisplayPort, and EDID emulation keeps your windows from rearranging every time you switch. The three things that decide it

  • One cable, two extended screens: Windows laptops do it over USB-C MST (the KD-E10). Macs don't support MST, so a Mac needs two cables (iDock M10) or Thunderbolt (TESmart X24), and base M1/M2 MacBooks stop at one external display.
  • EDID emulation: without it, each switch makes the laptop think the monitors were unplugged, so windows pile onto one screen; the most-cited complaint in KVM threads.
  • Locked-down work laptops: no software to install, and keyboard-hotkey switching often fails with gaming or NKRO keyboards, so a front-panel button (KD-E10) is the safe choice.

Not on our list: AV Access's iDock B10 and B12 (8K desktop path, but no EDID emulation) and the older iDock C10 (60 W, and owner reports of flicker with an HP EliteBook). UGREEN's KVM-style switches carry no video.
