What the differences mean

  • KD-E10 charging: its adapter is 12 V × 3 A = 36 W, and the dock itself draws up to 33.12 W. That leaves 36 − 33.12 = 2.88 W, so the 100 W reaching the laptop comes from the laptop's own charger plugged into the dock.
  • iDock M10 charging: its adapter is 20 V × 6 A = 120 W. After the full 100 W to the laptop, 120 − 100 = 20 W is left for the dock.
  • iDock C10 on a big MacBook Pro: 60 W ÷ 140 W = 43% of a 140 W charger's rating.
  • Price per extended Mac screen: iDock M10 $289.99 ÷ 2 = $145; TESmert T722 $256.00 ÷ 2 = $128.
  • Windows laptop gap: T722 $256.00 − KD-E10 $169.99 = $86.01 more for a dock that doesn't ask you to plug in the laptop's charger.

Which MacBooks can extend two monitors through a KVM

A one-cable USB-C dock splits two monitors with DisplayPort MST. AV Access states that macOS doesn't support USB-C MST, so on a MacBook the KD-E10, iDock C10 and similar docks show the same picture on both screens. A dock gets two separate screens from a Mac in one of two ways. It takes one stream per cable over two USB-C cables (iDock M10, TESmert T722). Or it uses Thunderbolt, which Apple says carries two displays over one port on the M5 MacBook Air (TESmart HDC202-X24). Then the chip sets the ceiling:

The iDock M10 needs two USB-C or Thunderbolt ports on the MacBook. If the second computer is a Mac mini or Mac Studio, AV Access says both Macs need a Pro chip or M3 or later. Macworld's review of the M10 confirms two screens on Pro/Max chips and the lid-closed rule for base M3. It also notes that the dock's spare USB-C port charges a phone at only 7.5 W. The T722's maker guide says a Mac must use both its 'MST+USB' and 'DP Alt' ports.

Not recommended

The TESmart HDC202-P23-N costs $419.99, which is $419.99 − $169.99 = $250.00 more than the KD-E10. It charges at up to 60 W instead of 100 W, and TESmart warns that laptops without 20 V charging may get only 7.5 W. Like the KD-E10, it gives M1/M2/M3 MacBooks one extended screen. The KD-E10 itself is the wrong buy for any MacBook, because its two monitors mirror.
