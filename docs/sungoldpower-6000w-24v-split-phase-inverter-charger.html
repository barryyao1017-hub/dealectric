Who it's for

24 V banks of three or more 100 Ah batteries that charge from a 240 V generator whose voltage sags. Its 140 V lower input limit is wider than Sigineer's 160 V.

Caveats

  • Charging and pass-through need 240 V input. A 120 V-only generator won't charge it.
  • The power-saver draw is 29 W (0.70 kWh a day), almost twice the Sigineer APC6024D's 15 W.
  • Idling with power saver off draws 77 W, which is 1.85 kWh a day, or 72% of one 24 V 100 Ah battery.
  • Sun Gold's store text marks the 48 V version as UL1741. The 24 V listing makes no such claim.
  • The spec table gives no per-leg 120 V wattage.
