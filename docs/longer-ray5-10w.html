Who it's for

Suits a first-time hobbyist who wants to engrave and cut wood, leather and dark acrylic on a budget and can run it in a ventilated garage or workshop. Buyers who need an enclosure for indoor use should look at the WECREAT Vista 10W enclosed laser ($490 at WECREAT), and anyone who mainly wants thicker cuts should consider the Ray5 20W ($359.99 at LONGER).

Caveats

  • $239.99 is LONGER's Black Friday price, which the store says runs until December 2, 2026; the product page shows $329.99 as the regular price.
  • LONGER's product page says the 5 W and 10 W Ray5 come without limit switches (sold separately), so automatic homing is not included, although the page's safety list includes limit-switch protection.
  • It is an open-frame diode laser with no enclosure (LONGER does not state a laser class for the Ray5), so smoke must be vented and eye protection worn; air assist is not in the Basic Set (the $299.99 10W Cutting Kit adds air assist and a honeycomb bed).
  • LONGER's own pages disagree on speed: the description says up to 24,000 mm/min, the spec table gives 10,000 mm/min for the 10 W (24,000 only for the separate 40 W).
  • Notebookcheck's review of an earlier 5.5 W Ray5 (Marc Herter, 2021) called its noise level rather high, found the rubber feet let it slip during fast moves, and said LONGER had cut some corners to reach the price.
  • Machine weight and overall dimensions are not stated by LONGER.
