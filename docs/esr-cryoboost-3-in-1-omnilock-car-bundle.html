Who it's for

iPhone 12 or later with MagSafe, plus Apple Watch and AirPods at home; cars with bladed air vents.

Caveats

  • Plug the car charger into the included adapter's 40 W USB-C port. The car's built-in USB ports are 'probably not going to provide enough power' for Qi2.2 speed (9to5Mac, Bradley Chambers), and the adapter's 18 W USB-A port is below the 35 W minimum.
  • Does not fit bladeless, round, irregular or hidden vents.
  • Full 25 W only on iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max, 16 Plus and 16 Pro Max. iPhone 16 and 16 Pro peak at 22.5 W, iPhone Air at 20 W, iPhone 17e or iPhone 15 and older at 15 W, and iPhone 12 mini and 13 mini at 12 W (Apple).
  • The adapter wattages come from reviews of the standalone stand (Macworld) and car charger (9to5Mac). Check the bundle's in-the-box list at checkout.
