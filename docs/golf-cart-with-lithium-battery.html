What the differences mean

All Club Car range figures are the maker's estimates for non-lifted carts unless noted. The low end assumes maximum payload on moderate hills. The high end assumes only the driver, on flat ground.

  • Range kept after four years (Onward 4 Passenger). Lead-acid: 5 ÷ 20 = 25% at the low end and 15 ÷ 40 = 38% at the high end. HP lithium: 18 ÷ 20 = 90% and 30 ÷ 35 = 86%. XR lithium: 30 ÷ 35 = 86% and 50 ÷ 55 = 91%.

Why range after four years decides it

In its first year, a lead-acid Onward 4 Passenger is rated at 20–40 mi. That equals or beats the base 4.0 kWh lithium pack (20–35 mi). The difference shows up with age on Club Car's own spec sheet. By year four, lead-acid falls to 5–15 mi (5–10 mi lifted). The base lithium pack keeps 18–30 mi, and the 7.0 kWh XR pack keeps 30–50 mi. The Onward 2 Passenger shows the same pattern: lead-acid goes from 30–50 mi to 10–15 mi, and XR lithium from 45–60 mi to 40–55 mi. Lithium Onwards also get a 375 A controller instead of 250 A and a 4.7 hp (3.5 kW) rated motor instead of 3.3 hp (2.4 kW). On the Onward 2 Passenger, the 7-inch display comes only with lithium, and the dual wireless chargers come with lithium or a premium package.

The other factory option is E-Z-GO's ELiTE line. The Liberty ELiTE starts at $12,999 MSRP, and E-Z-GO's ELiTE page says 8 years of battery coverage is included with purchase. Club Car's current pages give no battery warranty term; its 2019 lithium launch release said 6 years. E-Z-GO's spec pages give no pack kWh or range in miles, so the four-year comparison above can only be made for the Onward. The Liberty spec page lists 11.7 hp (8.7 kW) without saying whether that is a rated or a peak figure, so it can't be set against the Onward's 4.7 hp rated figure.

Converting a cart you already own. Both kits ship with a matched 58.4 V lithium charger, so don't reuse the old lead-acid charger. On a Club Car Precedent or a later DS, the On-Board Computer (OBC) manages lead-acid charging. ROYPOW, which makes drop-in lithium batteries for Club Car carts, says the OBC has to be bypassed with a jumper harness for lithium. Neither kit listing says whether a bypass harness is included, so ask the seller before ordering. Not recommended

A new Onward with flooded lead-acid batteries if you drive it every day. It falls under the same starting MSRP listing as the lithium Onward and matches lithium range in year one. But Club Car rates it at 5–15 mi after four years, against 18–30 mi for the base lithium pack, and it weighs 1,070 lb against 797 lb.
