Who it's for

Lawns up to about 0.37 acre with trees that shed leaves, or owners who don't want clippings left on the grass.

Caveats

  • Loud while collecting: PCWorld measured just under 68 dB at 3.28 ft and Notebookcheck about 70 dB at 1 m, nearly as loud as a regular mower.
  • Slow: PCWorld's unit took 97 minutes for 1,200 sq ft.
  • About 3 ft long and needs room to turn; testers saw uncut strips at the edges (up to about 6 in in PCWorld's test).
  • Rainwater pools on the bag cover when it rains while docked.
  • No automatic mapping: you drive the edges by hand in the app.
  • 4G is free for a trial period, then it's a paid yearly plan.
  • Wet or matted leaves collect less well than dry ones.
