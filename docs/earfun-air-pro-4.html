Who it's for

Android owners who want the widest hi-res codec choice, and anyone who wants multipoint plus a hi-res codec: aptX Adaptive and aptX Lossless keep working with two devices connected, LDAC does not.

Caveats

  • Turning LDAC on disables multipoint; LDAC with adaptive ANC drops battery to about 6 h per charge.
  • ANC measured 7.5 out of 10 in SoundGuys' lab, below the soundcore Liberty 4 NC (8.4) and Space A40 (8.5).
  • Single-tap volume can trigger when you adjust the bud; remap or disable it in the app.
  • EarFun's support forum has repeated threads about one bud (usually the left) going silent or not pairing; forgetting and re-pairing fixed most.
  • Mic is average in noisy places; call quality is best on Snapdragon Sound phones.
