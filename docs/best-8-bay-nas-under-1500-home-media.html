What reviewers measured

  • 10GbE: NASCompares (April 2024) saw about 650–700 MB/s up and down at MTU 1500, "less than expected" for a 10GbE link; its sibling DXP6800 Pro, with the same chip and ports, later measured 9.5 Gbit/s in iPerf3 at Notebookcheck, so the gap is in the drives and file protocol, not the ports. Jumbo frames and an SSD pool help.
  • Power: UGREEN says 29 W hibernating and 70 W active; NASCompares' wall meter read 64–66 W idle and 90–94 W active with ten drives fitted. Size the UPS on the measured numbers.
  • Noise: about 49 dB at 1 m under load (AppleInsider, via StarryHope); the 6-bay sibling idles at 34.6 dB(A).
  • Plex: runs in Docker on UGOS; hardware transcoding needs a Plex Pass. No review published a stream count for the i5-1235U; the closest measurement, an Iris Xe i7-1165G7 in Jellyfin, held three 4K HDR tone-mapped transcodes at 73 fps each, so plan on about three tone-mapped 4K streams or many more plain 1080p ones.
  • Software: NASCompares scored the hardware high and UGOS 6/10 at launch (April 2024); the OS has had two years of updates since.

Eight drives: capacity and cost

RAID 6 keeps two drives for parity: eight 8 TB drives give 48 TB usable, eight 12 TB give 72 TB, eight 16 TB give 96 TB (decimal; the NAS shows about 9% less in TiB). NAS drive prices swung widely in 2026: 12 TB IronWolf Pro drives were listed from $229.99 (Newegg, Best Buy) to $429.99 (Amazon, February 2026) and 8 TB WD Red Plus drives at $345–416 in September 2026, so eight 12 TB drives run roughly $1,840 to $3,440 depending on the week. Price them on the day; the NAS is the smaller half of the bill.

Why not the others

The Synology DS1825+ is the polished choice for backups and Synology Photos, but its Ryzen cannot hardware-transcode and its PCIe slot takes a full-size 10GbE card, not Synology's mini card. QNAP's Intel 8-bay with 10GbE (TS-855X) uses an Atom with no iGPU. The Asustor Lockerstor 8 Gen3 has the best network ports of the group and no iGPU, at $1,700 or more. UniFi's UNAS Pro 8 is the cheapest 8-bay with 10GbE but is a rack unit running UniFi Drive, with no Plex or Docker.
