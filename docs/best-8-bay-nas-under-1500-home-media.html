What the differences mean

  • Free slots once Plex transcodes and 10GbE is in place: DXP8800 Plus 1 slot − 0 cards = 1 free (Quick Sync and 2× 10GbE are on board). TS-873A 2 slots − 1 NVIDIA card − 1 10GbE card = 0 free. DS1825+ 1 slot − 1 10GbE card = 0 free, and it still can't transcode.
  • Per-port bandwidth: 10 Gb/s ÷ 8 = 1.25 GB/s against 2.5 Gb/s ÷ 8 ≈ 0.31 GB/s, four times the line rate for copying large rips. The Synology E10G30-T1 card adds one 10GbE port. The DXP8800 Plus and AS6508T have two built in.
  • Power over a year, maker figures, 24/7 under access: UGREEN 70.39 W × 8.76 = 617 kWh. Asustor 66.9 W × 8.76 = 586 kWh. Synology 60.1 W × 8.76 = 526 kWh. QNAP 54.067 W × 8.76 = 474 kWh. Each maker tests differently: only QNAP states the bays were full, and UGREEN's figure is quoted by Starry Hope.
  • Big arrays on Synology: eight 20 TB drives in RAID 6 give 8 × 20 − 2 × 20 = 120 TB usable. Synology says volumes over 108 TB need 32 GB of memory, so the DS1825+ (8 GB) and DS1825neo+ (4 GB) need two 16 GB modules for one volume that size.

What Plex hardware transcoding takes on each 8-bay NAS

Every box: Plex states that hardware-accelerated streaming requires an active Plex Pass. Watching your own library away from home also needs a Plex Pass on the server or your account, or a Remote Watch Pass. Jellyfin's documentation lists both Intel Quick Sync and NVIDIA NVENC as supported acceleration methods.

UGREEN DXP8800 Plus: The Core i5-1235U has Iris Xe-eligible graphics with Quick Sync (Intel). Marius Hosting's UGREEN guide runs Plex as a Docker container and maps /dev/dri/renderD128 and /dev/dri/card0 into it. Plex says Intel hardware encoding on Linux has no artificial limit on simultaneous videos. No card goes in, so the PCIe 4.0 x4 slot and both 10GbE ports stay as they are.

QNAP TS-873A: QNAP says the TS-873A supports entry-level NVIDIA cards that need no power cables, and names the GeForce GTX 1650. The box has a 250 W supply, and QNAP notes that a wider card blocks the next slot. Plex's QNAP requirements: Plex Media Server 1.15.1.791 or newer, the card set to QTS mode in Control Panel, and only the first card is used. QNAP adds that when Virtualization Station uses the card, QTS stops hardware transcoding. Plex also warns that GeForce drivers can cap concurrent hardware encodes. Built-in networking is 2× 2.5GbE, so 10GbE needs a card in the other slot.

Synology DS1825+ and DS1825neo+: Synology lists four cards for the single Gen3 x8 (x4 link) slot: the E10G30-T1, E10G30-T2, E10G30-F2 and E25G30-F2, all network cards. There is no graphics option, so Plex on these boxes converts video in software or not at all.

Asustor AS6508T: Intel's spec page for the Atom C3538 lists no processor graphics, and Asustor lists no PCIe slot. It plays files your TV can decode and serves them fast over 2× 10GbE.

Not recommended

The QNAP TS-873A as a Plex-first box. On Amazon it currently costs more than the DXP8800 Plus, yet transcoding means buying a separate NVIDIA card and depending on QNAP's NVIDIA driver package. In a Plex forum thread from March 2025, owner thelimey reported that GTX 1650 transcoding on a TS-873A stopped after the QTS 5.2.3 update, with no fix posted. Choose it for ZFS, not for transcoding.
