Charger-powered or self-powered: where the heat comes from

Below freezing, the BMS refuses charge current to protect the cells. LiTime explains that the 0 A a charger shows on a cold battery is often the BMS actively refusing the charge. A heater only solves this if it has power to run on, and the makers split into two designs.

Charger-powered. Vatrer states it plainly: when a charger is connected at 32°F, incoming charging power goes to heating instead of the cells, and charging starts above 41°F. Shari Galiardi (Freedom in a Can) tested Renogy's DuoHeat with a 400 W solar suitcase and a 20 A AC charger, and reports that it needs a charging source of at least 10 A. Redodo's 12V 100Ah page says charging halts until the pads raise the cells above 41°F, and it lists no battery-powered mode. Redodo describes an App Pre-Heat mode from stored energy only on its Apex Mini 24V. With a charger-powered heater, a weak winter panel that never reaches the heater's current floor leaves the battery cold and charge-locked.

Self-powered. LiTime's LiHeat can warm the battery while plugged in, or pre-heat it from battery power before charging. Battle Born's heater draws from the battery itself, but it will not turn on until the heat-enable post is jumpered to the positive terminal. In a multi-battery bank, the posts are chained and fed from the highest-voltage point. Battle Born suggests an inline switch so the heater does not drain the battery in storage. Its heater turns on below about 35°F, turns off near 45°F and allows charging above about 25°F. Battle Born says warming from freezing can take 2 to 4 hours.

Measured warm-up. Shari Galiardi froze a Renogy Core Mini DuoHeat four times, and each time it reached charging temperature in 11–20 minutes. Her single-pad Renogy 100Ah PRO took just over an hour. Redodo's own figure for its 100 W pads is 30–60 minutes from 14°F to 50°F. frater secessus lists the cell data for his REPT 280 bank, which limits charging to 0.1C, up to 80% SOC, between 32°F and 50°F. Even after a heater shuts off at 41°F, a gentle charge rate is kinder to the cells.
