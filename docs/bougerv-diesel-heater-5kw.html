Caveats

  • BougeRV recommends using the diesel heater outdoors; its product page and launch release give no ventilation or carbon-monoxide guidance and list no CO sensor (not stated by BougeRV), even though the description names home, garage and RV use, so a separate CO alarm in the heated space is the buyer's call.
  • BougeRV says the exhaust muffler reaches 482°F (250°C) at 77°F ambient and must not be touched or covered with anything flammable.
  • An exhaust pipe extension is not included and is sold separately; BougeRV does not list the box contents (not stated by BougeRV).
  • It needs power to start and run: BougeRV says a power station's 12 V DC port must support a 15 A+ peak, and the spec table lists only 12 V although the title says 110V/12V.
  • No independent measurements were found; Bob Vila's hands-on review (Glenda Taylor, 2026-01-19) of a BougeRV portable diesel heater, not identified as this 110V/12V version, gives no measured figures and reports that the household power adapter received did not work while 12 V did.
  • BougeRV's checkout adds a Seel 'Worry-Free Purchase' protection charge to the order by default (about 2% of the item price); the prices listed here leave it out, so check the order summary before paying.
