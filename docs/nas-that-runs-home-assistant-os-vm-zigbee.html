The passthrough evidence, laid out

For: SmartHomeJunkie (July 2026, UGREEN-sponsored, DXP4800 GT) added a SONOFF Zigbee Dongle Plus under VM Settings → Advanced → Add USB Device and got a working ZHA network; raspberry.tips (October 2026, DXP2800) shows a USB controller toggle and device list but never completed a Zigbee test; UGREEN's 2025–2026 update plan lists "supported USB passthrough for virtual machine" as released. Against: two search indexes quote UGREEN's Hardware Passthrough article (911, firmware 1.19.0.0093+) as saying USB port passthrough is "not currently supported"; a UGREEN representative said the same in January 2025; a DXP4800 Plus owner's Z-Wave stick failed on HAOS 16.3 and worked after HAOS 17.0 with UGREEN blaming the image; ConBee II sticks were detected but had no usable serial port in a UGREEN VM in two reports; and the old virsh attach-device workaround stopped working for several owners. Nobody has published a completed Zigbee-stick setup on a DXP2800 or DXP4800 Plus on current firmware. UGREEN's support pages render only in a browser, so read article 911 yourself before relying on a stick.

Why a wired coordinator settles it

A network Zigbee coordinator such as the SMLIGHT SLZB-06 (Ethernet or PoE, Home Assistant integration, auto-discovered by ZHA; about $40–75 across EU, UK and US listings) puts the radio on the LAN, so the VM talks to it over TCP and the hypervisor's USB layer never matters; ZHA's docs warn against serial-over-IP across Wi-Fi or VPN, so wire it. The same trick works on Synology, QNAP and any Docker host, and it survives NAS reboots and firmware updates better than a passed-through stick.

UGOS VM facts and one side effect

The VM app is x86-only (DXP and iDX series), imports .img, .qcow2, .vdi and .vmdk (extract .xz first), needs UEFI set or you get a black screen and the bridge set to the live Ethernet port or you get no IP, and owners resize the HAOS disk from 32 GB to 128 GB before first boot. Installing the VM app adds libvirt dnsmasq listeners on port 53, which breaks a Pi-hole or AdGuard container on the same NAS until the VM app is removed or the container gets a macvlan address.
