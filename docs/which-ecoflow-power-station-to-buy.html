What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test units. The measurements below come from the named labs. The sums are ours, one line each.

  • Expect 85–93% of the rated capacity at the outlet. DELTA 3 Plus: 910 ÷ 1,024 Wh = 88.9% (StorageReview). DELTA 3: 870 ÷ 1,024 Wh = 85% (OutdoorGearLab). RIVER 3: 210 ÷ 245 Wh = 85.7% (StorageReview). DELTA Pro 3: 3,790 ÷ 4,096 Wh = 92.5% (OutdoorGearLab).
  • Wall to outlet. DELTA 3 Plus: 910 Wh out ÷ 1,180 Wh from the wall = 77.1% (from StorageReview's two readings).
  • Idle drain. DELTA 3 Ultra: 3,072 Wh ÷ 22 W = 140 h, about 5.8 days with nothing plugged in (The Solar Lab's idle reading). DELTA 3 Max: 2,048 Wh ÷ 27 W = 76 h, about 3.2 days.

Which EcoFlow models can add batteries later

At 2 kWh and 3 kWh, EcoFlow sells a fixed model next to an expandable one. The fixed one keeps its built-in battery and can never take more, so decide before you buy.

  • DELTA 3 Max: The Solar Lab says it 'cannot connect to extra batteries'. EcoFlow's DELTA 3 Max Plus listing claims 2–10 kWh and 3,000 W.
  • DELTA 3 Ultra: The Solar Lab lists it as non-expandable. The DELTA 3 Ultra Plus (3,072 Wh, 3,600 W, $1,599 at EcoFlow's store) claims 3–11 kWh.
  • DELTA 3000 Gen4: Popular Science notes its spec sheet 'lists no battery port'. The DELTA 3800 Gen4 ($1,699 at EcoFlow's store) claims 4–12 kWh.
  • Which DELTAs the 2,048 Wh extra battery fits: the DELTA 3 Max Plus Smart Extra Battery ($859 at EcoFlow's store) names only the DELTA 3 Max Plus, DELTA 3 Ultra Plus and DELTA Gen4 3800.
  • Generator auto start-stop: EcoFlow's Smart Generator 3000 page limits automatic start-stop to the DELTA 3, DELTA 3 Plus and DELTA 3 Ultra Plus. The DELTA 3 Max Plus listing also claims automatic start-stop with 'an EcoFlow Smart Generator' but does not say which one.
  • 1 kWh: the DELTA 3 and DELTA 3 Plus grow to 5 kWh with DELTA 3, DELTA 2, DELTA 2 Max or DELTA Pro 3 extra batteries (EcoFlow; StorageReview). The DELTA 3 Classic's page claims no expansion.
  • Small units: EcoFlow's RIVER extra battery fits only the RIVER 3 Plus, which reaches up to 858 Wh with it. EcoFlow lists no extra battery for the RIVER 3.
  • Big units: the DELTA Pro 3 grows to 48 kWh (OutdoorGearLab) and the DELTA Pro Ultra to 90 kWh (EcoFlow). On 2026-11-15, EcoFlow's US store listed the DELTA Pro 3 as unavailable until late January and the DELTA Pro Ultra as unavailable.

Not recommended

The DELTA 3 Ultra for solar-first use, for adding batteries later, or bought from EcoFlow's own store. Its solar input is rated 800 W. The Solar Lab reports that on a 1,000 W array it 'made a strange sound and then started to smoke', twice. It can't take extra batteries, it can't auto-start the Smart Generator 3000, and it idles at about 22 W. At EcoFlow's store the DELTA 3000 Gen4 costs the same, takes 1,000 W of solar and weighs 14.7 lb less. The Ultra does win in three places: The Solar Lab charged it from 0 to 100% in 2 h on a household outlet, it has a true 30 A RV outlet, and on 2026-11-15 it cost less than the DELTA 3000 Gen4 on Amazon. buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FQVBFLHL?tag=dealectric-20
