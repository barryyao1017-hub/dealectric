Stacked above vs beside the laptop

A screen above the laptop keeps both screens in one line of sight and takes no extra desk width, which suits small tables, trains and cafés. Side-mounted extenders and free-standing dual monitors need table room to the left or right.

Cable and power

On a laptop with USB-C DisplayPort Alt Mode, one cable carries video and power to the Float. Running on laptop power caps its brightness; plug a 15 W or larger USB-C charger into the monitor for full brightness (Mobile Pixels). Laptops without Alt Mode use the mini-HDMI port.
