Who it's for

Indoor movie nights, kids' rooms and dorms where the lights go off. Pair it with the Aurzen 120-inch screen for the cheapest honest 1080p projector-and-screen setup.

Caveats

  • 200 ANSI lumens: washed out with lights on or outdoors before dark.
  • The 1/4-inch tripod socket is off-center, which reviewers call a design flaw for tripod use.
  • Auto keystone handles up-and-down tilt; side-angle correction is weak and softens corners.
  • Infrared remote needs line of sight.
  • No battery; needs a wall outlet or an AC power station.
