Who it's for

Homeowners, farms and small acreages with home internet who want alerts from a camera too far from the house for normal Wi-Fi, and no monthly fee.

Caveats

  • The hub needs a wired Ethernet connection to your router. It can't join your network over Wi-Fi.
  • Trees and hills cut range a lot. One owner's camera got 3 of 5 signal bars at 202 ft behind small trees and would not connect near the rated distance.
  • One owner says live view sometimes connects right away and sometimes doesn't, and that recording can start late on fast animals.
  • The R3 2.0 Pro only works with the Link 1.0 hub. Other GardePro cameras can't join it.
  • No use on land with no internet. For that, you need a cellular camera.
  • A February 2026 owner review said live view and cloud storage needed a paid plan. GardePro's page now says they are free (seen 2026-11-10).
