The Fn key and remapping on a Mac

A third-party Fn key is usually not Apple's Fn/Globe key. QMK's documentation says the Fn key on Apple keyboards has its own keycode, and that it is not recognized unless the keyboard's VID and PID match that of a real Apple keyboard. QMK adds that official support is unlikely because of the legal issues. The Keychron K3 Max runs QMK firmware, so its Fn is a layer key handled inside the keyboard.

What that touches in macOS. Apple lists these shortcuts on the Fn key: Fn-Q (Quick Note), Fn-A (Dock), Fn-C (Control Center), Fn-N (Notification Center), Fn-D (dictation), Fn-Delete (forward delete) and Fn-arrows (Page Up/Down, Home/End). Apple gives a non-Fn route for some of them: Control-Command-Space opens the Character Viewer as well as Fn-E. On a board whose Fn never reaches macOS, the Fn-letter shortcuts are the ones to test first.

What Lofree states for the pick. The Flow 2 for Mac physically replicates the exact architecture of your MacBook keyboard, with the Fn/Globe, Control, Option, Command sequence on the lower row. That describes where the keys sit. Lofree's page does not list which macOS Globe shortcuts the key triggers, so test the ones you use when the board arrives. Paulo Vargas of Digital Trends, who tested the 84-key Mac version, reports that the Fn key still can't be customized.

Remapping tools on a Mac.

  • Keychron K3 Max: Keychron's support article (dated 9/9/2026) says to use the latest Chrome, Edge or Opera and a wired connection, and that the Launcher won't detect the keyboard over Bluetooth or 2.4G. Safari is not on that list.
  • Lofree Flow 2 84 for Mac: Lofree says keys and macros are set through VIA without needing to install extra software. Digital Trends describes it as a browser interface that did not recognize the board until Vargas loaded a JSON file from Lofree.
  • Lofree Flow Lite 2 84: the web-based Lofree Hub, which Lofree says is available on both Mac and Windows, plus VIA.
  • NuPhy Air75 V3: NuPhyIO 2.0, which RTINGS says launched alongside the keyboard.
  • Logitech MX Mechanical Mini for Mac: the Logi Options+ app, which Logitech lists for Windows and macOS.

Not recommended

The original Lofree Flow84 ($119 through our link) looks like the cheaper way into the same aluminum design, but it is the wrong buy for this question. It connects by Bluetooth and USB-C only, with no 2.4 GHz dongle, and Lofree rates its 2,000 mAh battery at 40 h. RTINGS notes that the original Flow models don't support any configuration software, so there is no way to remap it on a Mac. The Flow Lite 2 84 costs less and adds 2.4 GHz, Lofree Hub and VIA.
