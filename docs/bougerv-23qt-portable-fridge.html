Who it's for

Suits one or two people on day trips, weekend car camping or a road-trip seat-side fridge, where a 23-can interior is enough and no app control is needed.

Caveats

  • OutdoorGearLab's sample reached -3.2 °F from a 75 °F start, short of the advertised -7 °F floor, and its display averaged 3.0 °F off the measured temperature.
  • OutdoorGearLab lists interior condensation and DC power cord issues as reasons to avoid it.
  • BougeRV's page gives conflicting ECO-mode figures (45 W in the spec table, under 36 W in the FAQ) and its FAQ calls the unit dual-zone although it has one compartment.
  • No app or Bluetooth control (BougeRV lists App: N), and the 'Fridge+Battery' bundle uses a separate 266 Wh power station that BougeRV says runs a portable fridge for about 6 hours, not a built-in battery.
