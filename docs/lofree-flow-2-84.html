Who it's for

Anyone who wants the Flow 2 typing feel with a tactile (Pulse) or silent (Void) switch instead of the Mac version's linear-only option.

Caveats

  • Space Gray keycaps are hard to read with the backlight off.
  • VIA remapping needs a JSON file from Lofree support.
  • Touch strip triggers by accident; can be disabled with Fn + Space.
  • Tom's Guide found the stabilizers and case acoustics sound poor; other reviewers disagreed.
  • Battery level shows only over Bluetooth, not over the dongle.
