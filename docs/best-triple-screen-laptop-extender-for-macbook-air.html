How many external displays each MacBook Air drives

  • M1 and M2 MacBook Air: one external display (Apple).
  • M3 MacBook Air: one with the lid open; a second only with the lid closed, which defeats a laptop screen extender (Apple).
  • M4 MacBook Air: two external displays with the lid open (Apple), so a triple extender's two screens work over two video-capable USB-C cables with no driver.

Driver or two cables

A triple extender shows up as two external displays. On a Mac that drives one, the second screen needs a driver that creates a virtual display (DisplayLink or the maker's own, as with Mobile Pixels and INVZI) or a DisplayLink adapter (Xebec). On a Mac that drives two, plug each screen into its own USB-C port and skip the driver.

Fit: measure the lid

A 13-inch MacBook Air is 30.4 cm (11.97 in) wide, a 15-inch Air 34 cm (13.4 in) (Apple). Makers state fit as screen sizes or lid dimensions; the side screens clamp to the lid's back, so a lid smaller than the minimum leaves the clamp loose.
