Why the Xebec needs its adapter even on an M4 or M5 Air

Apple says the M5 MacBook Air drives up to two external displays while the built-in screen stays on, and Plugable lists two external displays for the M4 as well. The M5 Air has two Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C) ports and a MagSafe 3 charging port. Apple says both displays can run over a single Thunderbolt port, but Plugable says that two extended screens from one cable take a Thunderbolt or DisplayLink dock. Xebec's own store says the Tri-Screen 3 Mac Adapter is 'required for all Mac users' because 'Apple does not support MST, which is the technology that allows for a single cable to drive both additional screens'. The adapter gets around this with DisplayLink chips, and you install the free DisplayLink driver. Xebec's help center marks a Mac with Thunderbolt ports as 'Not compatible — Mac Adapter required' and exempts no chip. Plugable adds that on a Mac, even an M5 Max, an MST hub only mirrors the same image on both monitors.

The Trio 3 Pro avoids this by shipping two USB-C cables, one per screen. Macworld's Simon Jary writes that any M4 or M5 MacBook runs both screens without Mobile Pixels' software. The cost is that both of the Air's Thunderbolt ports are in use, which leaves MagSafe for charging. Jary also found the screens can't reach full brightness on laptop power alone. A charger plugged into one of the Trio's USB-C ports fixes that and passes up to 65 W on to the laptop.

On M1 and M2 Airs, Plugable lists one external display natively. The M3 Air drives two only with the lid closed (Plugable, Macworld), which leaves no laptop screen. So on those chips every extender here needs extra help: Mobile Pixels' free driver for the Trio, the $59 adapter and DisplayLink driver for the Xebec, or a docking station for the KEFEYA. Macworld notes that DisplayLink tops out at 60 Hz, needs macOS's Screen Recording permission, and blocks protected video on every screen once a DisplayLink display is connected.

Not recommended

UPERFECT BE14BF on a MacBook Air. UPERFECT describes a single L-shaped USB-C cable, and the listing names no Apple chip, driver or DisplayLink chip. Xebec and Plugable both say macOS won't drive two extended screens from one plain USB-C cable. Ask UPERFECT how it gives two extended screens on macOS before you buy. KEFEYA S2Pro on an M1, M2 or M3 Air. KEFEYA says those Macs need a docking station, but it doesn't name one or include one, so $299.99 isn't the full cost.
