What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test units. Every figure here is the named tester's measurement or the maker's stated figure, read 2026-11-17; the lines below are our arithmetic on those figures.

  • S5000 vs F3800 Plus, usable energy: 3,840 Wh × 0.84 = 3,226 Wh for the F3800 Plus; 4,870 − 3,226 ≈ 1,640 Wh more from the S5000. The S5000 figure is pv magazine's app reading on a full discharge; The Solar Lab's 84% comes from its own inverter test, so treat the gap as approximate.
  • S5000 vs F3800 Plus, price per usable kWh at Anker's store on 2026-11-17: $2,599.99 ÷ 4.87 kWh ≈ $534 for the S5000; $2,399.99 ÷ 3.23 kWh ≈ $743 for the F3800 Plus, which is sold out.
  • Apex 200 vs DELTA 3 Ultra: 90% − 82% = 8 points more efficient; 22 W − 12.8 W ≈ 9 W less idle draw, which is 9.2 W × 24 h ≈ 0.22 kWh a day left in the battery.
  • Apex 200 vs Elite 200 V2 at BLUETTI's store on 2026-11-17: $1,099 ÷ 2.56 kWh ≈ $429 per kWh against $899 ÷ 2.0736 kWh ≈ $434 per kWh. Same price per kWh; the extra $200 buys 3,000 W instead of 1,000 W of solar input, 3,200 W instead of 2,600 W of output, and expansion to 33.28 kWh.

Measured results for the 2026 releases

Anker SOLIX S5000. Andrew Riha (pv magazine USA, 2026-10-28) drew 4.87 kWh on a full discharge and needed 6.24 kWh from the wall to refill it at the 1,800 W setting (78% round trip). The charge held about 1.79 kW for 2 h 49 min, then about 1.43 kW for the last 50 min. With the AC output on and nothing plugged in it lost 5% in 24 h, about 10 W. Through a real utility shutoff it ran a large refrigerator plus phones for 61.5 h, from 100% to 18%. Paul Rankin (Bob Vila, 2026-10-28) ran a refrigerator for about 86 h and recharged empty to full in about 3.5 h on a 120 V outlet. Neither tested the 240 V output. Weak points: 97 lb, and new orders ship in 9–10 weeks.

BLUETTI Apex 200. The Solar Lab measured 90% efficiency, 12.8 W idle with the inverter on, and a full wall charge in about 2 h 21 min, and reached 3,000 W of solar using both inputs. TJ Donegan (Reviewed, 2026-11-05) saw 270 W peak and about 190 W average from one BLUETTI SORA 500 panel in October in New England, about 50% charged in 6 h of sun: plan on more panels than one if you want to use the 3,000 W input.

EcoFlow DELTA 3000 Gen4 and TITAN 5000. Both have maker figures only so far. The DELTA 3000 Gen4 ships in early January 2027 (EcoFlow), so buy on its specs only if the January date suits you.

Not recommended

The EcoFlow DELTA 3 Ultra on a solar array larger than 800 W. The Solar Lab fed it a 1,000 W array and it made a strange sound and started to smoke; it does not shut off when the array exceeds its input. Keep its array at 800 W or below, or choose the Apex 200 for large arrays.
