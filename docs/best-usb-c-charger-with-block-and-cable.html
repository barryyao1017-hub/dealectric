Best USB-C charger with block and cable

Short answer. The best USB-C charger with block and cable is the UGREEN Nexode 65W Charger with Retractable USB-C Cable ($39.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/ugreen-nexode-65w-retractable-cable-charger): 60 W through its built-in 2.3 ft retractable cable, a 65 W USB-C port and a 22.5 W USB-A port in a 6.9 oz cube. That 60 W cable gives an iPhone 17 the full 28 W it can take (ChargerLab measured the phone peaking at 28 W) and runs a 13-inch MacBook Air, and UGREEN rates the reel for 25,000 pulls, which answers the most common complaint about retractable chargers: the reel jamming.

If you want a longer reach, the Belkin UltraCharge Retractable 67W pulls out 3.3 ft at the same list price, but it has no USB-A port, no independent reviews yet, and was out of stock at Belkin on 2026-11-06. Why a built-in cable instead of a block plus a loose cable: the cable is the part that fails first in the Anker 20 W bundles owners complain about, and it is the part that gets left behind. A retractable charger also removes the counterfeit risk of the cheap 20 W "with cable" bundles on Amazon that Apple has sued over.

What owners complain about

Across retailer reviews, deal forums and review aggregators, five problems come up again and again. The reel jams or stops retracting: a ShopSavvy summary of a Sharge 65 W retractable attributes about 56% of its criticism to the mechanism, a Baseus retractable cable owner reported a jam within weeks, and ReTrak's retractable chargers average 2.1 of 5 at Best Buy Canada for the same reason. The cable is too short: about 33% of the Sharge complaints, and 9to5Mac's main knock on the Anker 35W. The charger delivers less than the label: the Sharge unit measured 41 W instead of 65 W, and MacSources measured the UGREEN 65W's USB-A port at about 18 W instead of 22.5 W. Cheap 20 W block-and-cable bundles die early: six separate Anker 20 W bundle owners on Best Buy, Slickdeals and Trustpilot reported the cable or block failing within a year. Finally, people are surprised when a second device slows the first; every multi-port charger here splits its total, and the UGREEN 65W drops its USB-C port to 7.5 W the moment the USB-A port is used.

Which one by need

Phone plus laptop, one block for travel: UGREEN Nexode 65W Retractable. 60 W through the reel runs a MacBook Air or a 65 W Windows laptop, and the spare 65 W port takes a long cable at a desk. Longest cable at 65 W class: Belkin UltraCharge Retractable 67W, 3.3 ft and a 2-year warranty, once it is back in stock; it gives up the USB-A port. Phone only, smallest and cheapest: Anker Nano Charger 35W, 3.9 oz with a second USB-C port; 35 W is more than an iPhone 17 or Pixel 10 can draw. Phone or iPad with a longer reach, Galaxy 45 W: ESR Xtend 45W, 3.1 ft of braided cable and PPS. 16-inch laptop or two laptops: UGREEN Nexode 100W Retractable, 100 W through the reel and 65 W + 30 W when a phone joins.

Not recommended

Unbranded 20 W "charger with cable" bundles on Amazon: Apple's lawsuit over fakes sold there and the early-failure reports above make the few dollars saved a poor bet. Sharge's 65 W retractable charger: measured 41 W and the highest share of reel complaints we found. ReTrak retractable chargers: 2.1 of 5 at Best Buy Canada with the retractor breaking soon after purchase. Anker's 20 W bundles are fine chargers, but a separate cable is the part owners report dying first, so a built-in reel from Anker, UGREEN or ESR is the better buy at this price.
