What the differences mean

Plex's HDR-to-SDR tone mapping and scaling run as shaders on the iGPU's execution units, so EUs times clock is the hardware headroom that decides how many tone-mapped 4K streams a NAS can hold. It is a proxy, not a measured stream count: no named lab has published simultaneous 4K HEVC transcode counts for any of these units.

  • Shader budget (EU × GHz): N150 24 × 1.0 = 24.0; N95 16 × 1.2 = 19.2; N100 24 × 0.75 = 18.0; N5095 16 × 0.75 = 12.0; J4125 12 × 0.75 = 9.0. Figures from Intel ARK and Notebookcheck.
  • Price for the step up: F2-425 Plus N150 $499.99 − DXP2800 $389.99 = $110.00 for 33% more iGPU clock, two 5GbE ports and a third M.2 slot, if it comes back into stock. The $399.49 F2-425 Plus is the N95 model: $9.50 more than the DXP2800 for 8 fewer execution units.
  • Price per EU·GHz: F2-425 Plus N95 $399.49 / 19.2 = $20.81; F2-425 Plus N150 $499.99 / 24.0 = $20.83; DXP2800 $389.99 / 18.0 = $21.67; F2-425 $299.99 / 12.0 = $25.00; F4-425 $386.99 / 12.0 = $32.25; DS225+ $364.29 / 9.0 = $40.48, and that engine is unreachable on stock DSM.
  • Every option needs a Plex Pass: Marius Hosting's guide states that a Plex Pass is mandatory for hardware transcoding. Budget for it alongside the drives.

Stock firmware or a script: how each NAS reaches the Quick Sync engine

Plex talks to Intel Quick Sync through VA-API, which needs the Linux i915 kernel driver to expose the iGPU as /dev/dri/renderD128. Every CPU in the table has Quick Sync on Intel's spec page; the difference is whether the NAS operating system exposes it.

UGREEN DXP2800. UGOS Pro has no native Plex package (Plex's download list covers Synology, QNAP, ASUSTOR, TerraMaster, Netgear, Unraid, Thecus, Seagate and WD, not UGREEN), so Plex runs as a Docker container. Marius Hosting's guide, updated 2026-10-31 and tested with Plex 1.43.5, maps /dev/dri/renderD128 and /dev/dri/card0 into the container and then enables hardware transcoding and HDR tone mapping in Plex's transcoder settings. The device mapping is a one-time compose entry. $389.99: https://dealectric.com/go/ugreen-nasync-dxp2800

TerraMaster F2-425 Plus, F4-425, F2-425. TerraMaster's spec sheet lists H.264 and H.265 hardware transcoding up to 4K at 60 fps. One caution: Plex's own download manifest (version 1.43.5, 2026-10-23) lists TerraMaster packages for TOS 4 and TOS 5 & 6 only. The N150 F2-425 Plus ships TOS 7 and the 425 series is moving to TOS 7, so check that the app center build installs on your TOS version before relying on it; Docker is the fallback.

Synology DS225+ and DS425+. Both use the J4125. In the Plex forum thread, a Synology Technical Support reply quoted on 2025-09-10 says hardware transcoding 'is not supported on these two models', and a reply quoted on 2025-09-17 says this applies 'starting with products released in 2025 and beyond, even if the CPU supports hardware transcoding'. Plex's ChuckPa wrote that 'without the kernel drivers included to access the ASIC via the i915, there is nothing we can do.' Luka Manestar's Blackvoid workaround (published 2025-09-23, updated 2026-07-15) removes the stock i915, drm_kms_helper and drm modules over SSH as root and inserts replacement modules built for each DSM version (7.2.x, 7.3.x and 7.4); the steps must run after each reboot, for example via Task Scheduler, and Manestar labels it unofficial and unsupported by Synology.
