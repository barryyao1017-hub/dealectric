Caveats

  • Not expandable: Anker dropped the expansion port to save size and weight. The original C1000 (1,056 Wh, 1,800 W, BP1000 expansion) is still sold on Anker's US site as the C1000X if adding capacity matters.
  • The 49-minute charge only happens with UltraFast enabled in the app and the cells above 68 °F; otherwise it charges at 1,200 W.
  • Amazon carries several near-identical C1000 Gen 2 listings (with a bag, a 200 W or 400 W panel, or the alternator charger); this is the bare unit.
