Who it's for

PC and PS5 players who talk in Discord every night, stream, or have been told their headset mic sounds muffled or compressed.

Caveats

  • You must sit about 2 to 4 inches from the capsule; lean back and your voice thins out, which is why the boom arm matters.
  • Does not work on Xbox, which blocks standard USB microphones.
  • No XLR cable in the box; USB-C is the plug-and-play route.
  • Maono's full tuning software (EQ, noise gate, compressor) is strongest on Windows.
