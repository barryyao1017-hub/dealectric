Who it's for

Men who want Whoop-style recovery tracking without a yearly fee, and lifters who want a second sensor on the upper arm that broadcasts heart rate to gym machines.

Caveats

  • Auto-detected workouts are unreliable: DC Rainmaker logged 10 workouts on a day he did 2, so start sessions manually.
  • Sleep onset was off by about 37 minutes on one night in DC Rainmaker's test; Apple, Garmin, Oura and Whoop were closer.
  • Daily strain caps at 100%, so a very hard day looks like a moderate one.
  • Max heart rate is fixed at 220 minus age, which skews zones for fit men.
  • The hook-and-loop strap is one size and may wear over time.
  • Training plans only work when paired with an Amazfit watch.
