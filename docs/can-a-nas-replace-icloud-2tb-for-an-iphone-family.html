What the differences mean

Compare the yearly bills. iCloud+ 2 TB already keeps your data off-site, in Apple's data centers. A NAS at home doesn't, so it needs a paid copy somewhere else.

  • iCloud+ 2 TB: $9.99 × 12 = $119.88 a year, shared by you and up to five family members.
  • Synology C2 for a DS225+: 2 TB × $77.99 = $155.98 a year, which is $36.10 more than iCloud+. Paid monthly, it's 2 × $7.99 × 12 = $191.76.
  • Backblaze B2 for a UGREEN NAS: 2 TB × $6.95 × 12 = $166.80 a year, which is $46.92 more than iCloud+.
  • BeeProtect for a Synology BeeStation Plus: $119.99 a year at a flat rate, $0.11 more than iCloud+.
  • A small iCloud+ 200 GB plan kept for the iPhones' own backups costs $2.99 × 12 = $35.88 a year. A NAS with C2 plus that plan comes to $155.98 + $35.88 = $191.86 a year.
  • The hardware is on top of that. The DH2300 at $239.99 costs $239.99 ÷ $119.88 = 2.0 years of iCloud+ 2 TB, before you buy drives.

The off-site copy decides the bill

Two mirrored drives keep your files if one drive fails. They don't protect against deleted files or a lost box, so moving off iCloud means paying for a second copy outside the house. Each maker handles that copy differently:

  • Synology DS225+: Hyper Backup sends the NAS to Synology C2 OneStorage, which costs $77.99 per TB a year from 22 June 2026 (NASCompares, Rob Andrews). The DS225+ has two drive bays and a 3-year warranty (Synology). buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FC2888KF?tag=dealectric-20
  • UGREEN NASync: the Cloud Drives app connects to Backblaze B2 from firmware 1.17.0.0031 and to Amazon S3 from 1.16.0.0042. UGREEN warns that Cloud Drives syncs, so 'deleted or overwritten data may also change at the destination'. Keep B2's earlier file versions turned on. Downloads from B2 are free up to 3× the data you store (Backblaze), so restoring the whole library costs nothing extra. $239.99: https://dealectric.com/go/ugreen-nasync-dh2300
  • Synology BeeStation Plus: BeeProtect is one subscription at one price 'regardless of how much storage you use'. It keeps up to 8 versions and includes a 3-month free trial if you activate it within a month of unboxing (Synology). Blackvoid (Luka Manestar) gives $119.99 a year after the trial. The BeeStation Plus can back up a Mac with Time Machine (Synology), but it holds a single drive, and Synology's US store shows no price for it today.

What a NAS can't take over from iCloud

  • iPhone backups: an iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a Mac or PC (Apple), not to a NAS. Finder writes those backups to ~/Library/Application Support/MobileSync/Backup/ on the Mac's own drive (Apple), so the Mac needs free space for every phone's backup before Time Machine copies it to the NAS. Time Machine needs a NAS that supports it over SMB, because AFP isn't supported from macOS 27 (Apple).
  • iCloud Photos with Optimize iPhone Storage: some full-resolution originals may exist only in iCloud. UGREEN says to switch to 'Download and Keep Originals' before the NAS can keep a full-quality archive, so each iPhone needs room for its whole library (UGREEN).
  • Automatic photo backup: Synology Photos backs up in the background while the phone charges, and Focused Backup runs only with the app open (Synology). UGREEN's app adds a nighttime idle backup (UGREEN).
