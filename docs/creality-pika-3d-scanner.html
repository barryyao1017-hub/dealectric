Who it's for

Small, detailed objects such as figures, mechanical parts and props, scanned away from a desk with a recent iPhone (A15 chip and 6 GB RAM or newer) or a Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 Android phone.

Caveats

  • It needs a connected phone, tablet or computer for processing. On mid-range phones (Snapdragon 695, Dimensity 900, Exynos 1480), owners report 1 to 3 fps in NIR mode and no blue-laser mode.
  • Creality says marker-free laser scanning works only in wired PC mode. On a phone, laser scans need the included marker towers or dots.
  • Black surfaces left holes in NIR mode in one review until the object was dusted with powder. Hair is hard to capture in every mode.
  • Reviewers say the included turntable wobbles, and test units arrived with flat batteries.
  • Amazon listings have been priced well above the Creality store (CNX Software saw $899).
