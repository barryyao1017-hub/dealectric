Inverter compatibility and cold charging: what each maker documents

Over CAN or RS485, the battery's BMS reports its state to the inverter. Without that link, the inverter charges by its voltage settings alone. Sun Gold lists the SGH48100T on RS485 and CAN with Growatt, Deye, Sol-Ark, SunGoldPower, Luxpower, EG4, Victron Energy, Schneider, SMA and Solis. Those are the ten brands the answer above counts. The unheated SG48100P adds RS232 and lists eight brands, including Phocos and SMK. Sun Gold's own SGR-8K10E kit runs its 8,000 W 48 V inverter with two SG48100P batteries over RS485. $1,035: https://dealectric.com/go/sungoldpower-sgh48100t-48v-100ah-self-heating $981: https://dealectric.com/go/sungoldpower-sg48100p-48v-100ah

ECO-WORTHY's Cubix100 offers CAN/RS485, Bluetooth and WiFi, scales to 32 units, and its listing names UL9540 (ETL tested), UL1973, UL9540A and a CEC listing. The Cubix 100 Pro V2 names EG4, Victron and Growatt and charges down to −4°F. WEIZE's 48V 100Ah rack is the cheapest 48 V option at $799.99 ($156/kWh). Its listing title names Bluetooth and CAN/RS485 but no UL standard.

Cold charging works three different ways. Sun Gold's SGH48100T starts heating at 41°F, which is above freezing, so it keeps the pack warm before the charge cutoff is reached. Redodo's Self-Heating battery stops charging below 32°F and runs its 100 W pads until the cells reach 41°F. Vatrer's 48V heated rack ($1,009.99) sends incoming charge power to the heater at 32°F or below and starts charging above 41°F, so on solar the first energy each cold morning goes into heating. The Solar Lab kept a Cubix100 in a deep freezer for 24 hours, until its surface was about −25 °C, and it then refused both charge and discharge, as its BMS should. $251.99 on redodopower.com, $226.80 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/redodo-12v-100ah-self-heating-lifepo4

For a 12 V system, Vatrer's Group 24 has a 150 A BMS and UL 1973, stops charging below 32°F, and charges from a LiFePO4 charger, an MPPT controller or a DC-to-DC charger. $259.99 on vatrerpower.com, $247 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/vatrer-12v-100ah-group-24-bluetooth-150a

Not recommended

Redodo 12V 100Ah Group 24 Bluetooth on an inverter that runs a pump. Redodo's own listing says it is not suitable for high-power uses such as water pumps, electric jacks or pool vacuums. Use a 48 V rack for that load. Older pre-Cubix ECO-WORTHY 48V server-rack units, including used ones: The Solar Lab measured 52 V still coming out at −4°F after an overnight freeze, when the BMS should have blocked discharge.
