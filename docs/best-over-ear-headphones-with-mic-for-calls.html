What the differences mean

  • Call audio band. Microsoft says the original Bluetooth hands-free codec sampled at 8 kHz and that wideband doubles it: 8 × 2 = 16 kHz. Sound can reach at most half the sampling rate, so the ceiling is 16 ÷ 2 = 8 kHz. Jabra rates the Evolve3 85's speak mode up to 14.5 kHz, which is 14.5 ÷ 8 = 1.8 times that ceiling.
  • Days of calls per charge. Sony rates 24 h of talk with noise cancelling on, so 24 ÷ 8 = 3.0 eight-hour call days. Jabra rates 21 h with ANC and busylight on, so 21 ÷ 8 = 2.6. soundcore rates the Space 2 Pro at 20 h of talk, so 20 ÷ 8 = 2.5.
  • Music hours are not call hours. Jabra's 55 h headline is music with ANC on. Its talk rating is 21 ÷ 55 = 38% of that. Sony's talk rating is 24 ÷ 30 = 80% of its music figure.

Why the mic changes what you hear on a computer

Standard (Classic) Bluetooth headphones switch between two modes. In Microsoft's words, A2DP “provides high quality audio but doesn't allow the microphone to be used”, while HFP “allows the microphone to be used but plays back audio in mono with lower fidelity than A2DP”. So when a meeting app opens the headset mic over a regular Bluetooth link, the stereo music stream gives way to the hands-free one.

  • Sony WH-1000XM6. For video calls on a computer, Sony's guide says to set both speaker and microphone to “Headset (WH-1000XM6 Hands-Free)”. It warns that with the music playback (A2DP, “Stereo”) connection selected, “a video call may not be available”. The cable doesn't get around this: “When using the headphone cable, the Bluetooth function cannot be used”, and Sony says to answer on the phone and talk using the phone's microphone.
  • Jabra Evolve3 85. It ships with the Jabra Link 390 Bluetooth adapter in USB-A or USB-C. Jabra rates its speak mode at 100 Hz–14.5 kHz super-wideband and lists LE Audio support. Teams certification depends on the variant, so buy the version that matches your company's meeting platform.
  • Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen). Firmware 8.2.20 (10 February 2026) added “USB voice call support for your PC”, which is two-way audio over USB-C. Bose says this is “supported only by certain VoIP applications”. Units still on the launch firmware, 5.0.20, need the update first. Firmware 10.12.12 (8 September 2026) improved wired USB compatibility with web-conferencing apps and added LE Audio (beta) with compatible Android and Windows devices.
  • soundcore Space 2 Pro. SoundGuys lists Bluetooth 6.1 with SBC, AAC and LDAC, two-device multipoint and no USB audio, so on a computer it takes calls over Bluetooth like the Sony. On Windows, Microsoft's way to keep stereo with the mic on over Bluetooth is LE Audio. That needs four things: a headset that supports LE Audio, a Windows 11 PC that supports LE Audio, the latest Windows 11 version 24H2 updates and an updated Bluetooth audio driver from the PC's maker.

Not recommended

The Sony WH-1000XM6 on its cable for laptop meetings. Plugging in the supplied cable turns Bluetooth off, and Sony's own guide says to talk through the phone's microphone instead, so the headset's six voice mics take no part in the call. Use it over Bluetooth Hands-Free, or choose the Jabra or the updated Bose for wired desk calls.
