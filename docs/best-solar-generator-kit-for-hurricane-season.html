How long hurricane outages last

Days, not hours. The EIA found that Beryl, Helene and Milton caused 80% of US outage hours in 2024, and South Carolina customers averaged about 53 hours without power that year. After Ian (landfall Sept 28, 2022) FPL had restored 99% of customers by Oct 7, while Lee County Electric Cooperative still had 27,500 out on Oct 10. Plan for three days or more.

What the panel gives under storm clouds

Little while the storm is overhead: heavy overcast cuts a panel to about 10–25% of its rating, and one owner measured 8 W from a 100 W panel on an overcast day. Once skies clear, a 200 W panel puts back about 600–700 Wh a day, enough for a fridge's lower days or a CPAP and a router; size the battery to carry the first day or two alone.

Gas generator or battery

CPSC and CDC say to run a gas generator outdoors at least 20 feet from the house, never in a garage, basement, crawlspace or shed, because of carbon monoxide. A battery power station has no exhaust. FEMA warns against wiring any generator into the house without a transfer switch.

Storing it between seasons

Jackery's guidance for its stations is to store at about half charge for anything over three months, at 32–104 °F and low humidity; check the charge every few months. Keep the kit on a shelf above any floodable floor and charge it fully when a storm is named.
