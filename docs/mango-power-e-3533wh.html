Who it's for

Home backup where you want 240 V now or later, or a 30 A RV hookup, at about $368 per kWh for one unit.

Caveats

  • 100.1 lb per unit. The Gadgeteer called it a beast to move around, even with wheels.
  • The included cable charges at up to 1,800 W. The 3,000 W rate needs the 30 A cable, sold separately.
  • Solar needs at least 60 V, so small 18 V panels have to be wired four or more in series.
  • Mango doesn't publish a UPS switchover time.
  • Reviewers found the app thin: few settings, and some usage screens showed no data.
