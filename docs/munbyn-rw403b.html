Who it's for

Suits first-time online sellers who want a cheap, wireless 4 x 6 shipping-label printer that also prints product and circle labels. Sellers whose labels carry small text or fine logos should consider MUNBYN's 300 dpi RW941BP ($125.99), and anyone who only needs small 2-inch labels can spend less on a NIIMBOT B1 ($23.99 at niimbot.com/us).
