What owners complain about

  • No wireless charging. This came up most often (5 of the sources we read). The Wallet Slayer, Scooch Wingmate and most built-in card-slot cases are cable-only, because the cards sit between the phone and the charger. One reviewer takes the case off every night to charge on a pad.
  • Loose cards. Also 5 mentions. Underfilled slots let cards slide out. Smartish says the Vol. 2 fabric pouch stretches over about 72 hours to fit what you carry, so load three cards while it settles.
  • Bulk with 4 or more cards (3 mentions). The pouch sits above the back of the case and catches in tight pockets.
  • Too tight when packed (2 mentions). With a full slot, you may have to pull out several cards to reach one.

Which one by need

  • Phone plus 3 cards, cable charging is fine: Smartish Wallet Slayer Vol. 1 ($29.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/smartish-wallet-slayer-vol-1-iphone-17).
  • You want a stand for video: Smartish Wallet Slayer Vol. 2 ($34.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/smartish-wallet-slayer-vol-2-iphone-17).
  • You charge on MagSafe every night and want the cards to stay on: Nomad Modern Leather Folio ($99 at Nomad). The cover goes over the screen. One owner of the Pro Max leather version said not to use it on a wireless charger, so test yours early.
  • You want MagSafe in a slim case and don't mind pulling the cards off to charge: Smartish Gripmunk with MagSafe ($19.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/smartish-gripmunk-magsafe-iphone-17) plus a snap-on wallet like the ESR Geo Wallet Boost ($36.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/esr-geo-wallet-boost-halolock), which also works with Find My if it falls off.
  • You want the cards out of sight: Scooch Wingmate. Its bottom-loading slot is covered when closed and has RFID blocking. It is also cable-only, and Scooch says it can't promise cards stay in on every impact.

Not recommended

  • Stick-on card pockets. In one owner's report, the sides split after a few weeks with three cards and the cards started falling out.
  • Leaving a full card case on a wireless charger. It won't charge through the cards. Use a cable or a folio.
