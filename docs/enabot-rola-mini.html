Who it's for

Suits pet owners who are away for days at a time and want to roll around the home to check on a pet, with no dock to place. Those who want the robot to recharge itself and play laser chase should take the EBO Air 2 ($139 on Enabot's store).

Caveats

  • No charging dock or automatic recharging: it has to be picked up and charged by cable (Enabot FAQ).
  • No laser pointer, no patrol mode, and the eye lights cannot be customized (Enabot FAQ).
  • Enabot does not say what use the 25-day battery figure assumes; The AU Review's Shantelle Santos (March 2025) got about three weeks before the first charge.
  • The AU Review found it got stuck in front of raised surfaces a few times, and her dogs knocked it over, though it proved sturdy.
  • Enabot's own spec and comparison tables disagree on climbing slope (15° vs 6°) and noise (55 vs 48.5 dB).
  • Enabot sells only an 'International Version' and also lists a 'ROLA Mini FamilyBot' at the same $129.99 without stating a difference. $129.99 is the Black Friday price (Nov 19–30) against $169.
