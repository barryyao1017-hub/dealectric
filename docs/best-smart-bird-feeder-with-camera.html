How free bird ID differs between Birdfy and Birdbuddy

Both brands advertise AI bird identification, but the free plans work differently. Birdbuddy's plan table lists AI+ Recognition as Manual on the free plan: you pick a postcard and ask for an ID. Automatic identification of every visitor needs the Individual or Family plan. On the Birdbuddy Home shop page, Family Premium is $98.99 a year after a free first month. Birdbuddy's Individual and Family plans also add 2K Ultra resolution.

On 2 November 2026 Birdfy made automatic species identification free for life, covering more than 6,000 species. Digital Camera World reported that it comes with no daily limits, no manual identification requests and no trial period, and that it applies to existing owners too. T3 reported the same change. Birdfy's own membership page now lists Unlimited Bird Species Recognition on the free tier for videos from your Birdfy devices. The free tier also includes 10 Photo IDs for photos you upload, 3 AI Reasoning uses a day, Moment clips of up to 20 seconds kept for 30 days, and 5 GB of Collection storage. Plus (shown at $69.99 a year) and Pro still charge for estimates of a bird's sex and age and for health indicators. You don't need either plan to have birds named.

Reviewed's guide still says Birdfy sells AI either by subscription or included for life, and it mentions a 7-day trial on the Feeder 2 Pro. Birdfy's membership page and both 2 November news reports describe free ID with no trial.

Not recommended

Birdfy Feeder 2 at $169.99. Birdfy's store lists it with a 1080p camera. The Birdfy Feeder with Solar Panel costs $119.99, has a 2K camera and includes the panel. That makes the Feeder 2 $50 more for a lower-resolution camera, and both now get the same free species ID.
