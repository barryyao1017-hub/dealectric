What owners complain about

Across reviews at MacRumors, PopSci, Tom's Guide, Trustpilot and retailer Q&A, the same five problems repeat. Squirrels come up most: MacRumors' tree-strapped Birdfy was emptied and its solar cable chewed, Tom's Guide watched squirrels drain a Birdbuddy in a day or two through its large bottom opening, and the fix in every case was a pole with a baffle about 8 ft from trees. Battery in shade is second: MacRumors' Birdfy sat near 50 percent in a wooded spot and needed a winter charge, while a sunny unit never dropped below 90 percent. Wi-Fi is third: most camera feeders are 2.4 GHz only and struggle with mesh routers and long live streams, which is why the Metal 2 4K's 5 GHz support matters. Bird ID misses: in MacRumors' 47-visit sample the Birdfy app named 38 correctly, left 14 unknown and got 2 wrong. And subscriptions: Birdbuddy moved automatic species ID behind Premium in spring 2026, while Birdfy went the other way and made it free for life for new and existing owners on November 1, 2026. Two smaller gripes: Birdfy sends an alert for every visit, often 50 or more a day, and PopSci's Birdbuddy collected water and molded seed in humid Florida.

Which one, by need

Squirrels, raccoons or a shady yard: Birdfy Feeder Metal 2 4K. Metal body, 9,000 mAh battery, built-in solar, dual-band Wi-Fi, and nothing more to pay for ID. First feeder or a gift under $150: Birdfy Feeder with Solar Panel. 2K, solar included, free ID, 2-year warranty. Close-ups and slow motion: Birdfy Feeder 2 Pro. Not recommended

Birdfy Feeder 2 (non-Pro): 1080p and no solar panel in the box for more than the 2K Feeder with Solar Panel. Any plastic feeder on a tree strap in a yard with squirrels: every tester who tried it lost seed and, in two cases, the cable.
