What the differences mean

  • Price. The F2-425 costs $30.00 more than the DH2300 ($269.99 − $239.99) and buys 2.5GbE, an x86 CPU and Docker. The DXP2800 at UGREEN's own store is $89.99 over a $300 budget ($389.99 − $300).
  • Copy speed. Hardware Busters measured about 270 MB/s from the F2-425 over 2.5GbE, and StorageReview measured 115 MB/s from the DS223j over gigabit. That is 270 ÷ 115 ≈ 2.3× faster. A 100 GB photo library takes 100,000 MB ÷ 270 ≈ 6.2 minutes instead of 100,000 ÷ 115 ≈ 14.5 minutes. Hardware Busters tested with SSDs and notes that drive type makes little difference at 2.5GbE speeds.
  • Room for containers. The F2-425 has 4 GB and the DS223j has 1 GB: 4 ÷ 1 = 4× the memory for apps. Only the F2-425 and DXP2800 take a RAM upgrade, both to 16 GB.
  • Electricity. At rated access power around the clock and the EIA's August 2026 US average of 18.16 ¢/kWh, the F2-425 (22 W) costs 22 × 8.76 × 0.1816 ≈ $35.00 a year. The DH2300 (13.72 W) costs ≈ $21.83 a year. Over five years that is about $66 more, so box plus power comes to $444.98 vs $349.12. These ratings are worst cases.
  • Cheapest 2.5GbE. The UNAS 2 is $70.99 under the F2-425 ($269.99 − $199.00) but runs no apps.

Which of these run Docker, and how much they can run

Synology DS223j and DS223: yes, through Synology's own package. Synology's download center for the DS223j on DSM 7.3.2 (build 86009) offers Container Manager 24.0.2-1606 as an armv8 build. Container Manager is the successor to Synology's Docker package, per Synology's release notes. The DS223 has had it since Container Manager 20.10.23-1405 (2023-05-22), per Synology's release notes. The release notes also list a newer version, 24.0.2-1707, dated 2026-11-05. Because it is the ARM build, each container needs an ARM64 image. The real limit is memory: the DS223j has 1 GB that can't be upgraded, enough for a backup or DNS container next to Synology Photos, not a media stack. With no Quick Sync, Plex has to direct-play.

TerraMaster F2-425 and UGREEN DXP2800: yes, on x86. NASCompares says the F2-425 handles containers and services. Starry Hope notes that UGREEN added Docker to UGOS Pro and that Plex and Jellyfin install as containers on the DXP2800. Both have Intel Quick Sync, which ComputingForGeeks calls the transcoding path Plex and Jellyfin support best. ComputingForGeeks also notes that installing another OS on the F2-425 voids its 2-year warranty.

UGREEN DH2300 and UniFi UNAS 2: no. ComputingForGeeks reports no Docker and no VM support on the DH2300, which is built around photo backup and AI albums instead. NASCompares says the UNAS 2 isn't made for VMs or containers; it is a file server with a 2.5GbE port.
