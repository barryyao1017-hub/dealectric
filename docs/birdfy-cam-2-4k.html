Who it's for

Shoppers who like their current feeder (or bird bath, or nest box) and want a bird-ID camera without replacing it. Also the pick for a 5 GHz-only network, since the cheaper Cam 2K is 2.4 GHz only.

Caveats

  • Birdfy recommends mounting about 12 in from the feeding area; one owner reports the PIR trigger and focus weaken past about 3 ft.
  • Live view is not full 4K; 4K detail appears in saved Moments clips.
  • No microSD recording; clips live in Birdfy's free cloud for 30 days, then are deleted unless saved.
  • Owners report crackly or missing audio on the Cam line and short Wi-Fi range at the far end of a yard.
  • The 2 W solar panel maintains charge; it is not sized to recharge a drained battery, so charge fully by cable before mounting.
