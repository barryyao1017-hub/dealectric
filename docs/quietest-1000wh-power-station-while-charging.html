What the differences mean

  • DJI Power 1000 V2, full speed: 1,024 Wh charged in under 53 min, so 1,024 ÷ (53/60) ≈ 1,160 W on average, with fan noise The Technology Man could barely separate from his room's background at 1 m. Its Standard mode charges at about 600 W, so 1,024 ÷ 600 ≈ 1.7 h to full. The Technology Man did not take a dB reading in Standard mode.
  • Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2, full speed: 42 dB against the DJI's 37 dB from the same reviewer is 5 dB, or 10^(5/10) ≈ 3.2 times the sound energy. Notebookcheck's 45 dB is 8 dB above the DJI, or about 6.3 times.
  • Anker at 200 W, fans off: 1,024 Wh ÷ 200 W ≈ 5.1 h before charging losses, which fits inside one night.
  • BLUETTI AC180 in Silent: 1,152 Wh ÷ 283 W ≈ 4.1 h with the fan running at about 37 dB. The DJI reaches that level for under an hour per full charge, so the AC180 is audible about 4.6 times as long.
  • Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 in quiet mode: 1,070 Wh ÷ 260 W ≈ 4.1 h at nextpit's measured 30 dB.

Why 37 dB is the test room's floor, not a tie

The Technology Man measured the BLUETTI AC180 in Silent at about 37 dB at 1 m, "just 1dB above background noise". His room was about 36 dB, so no unit could read much lower there. His DJI Power 1000 V2 reading, also about 37 dB, was taken while it charged at over 1,400 W, and he wrote that his meter "barely picked up anything over and above background noise". The two readings therefore don't make a tie. Both units were about as quiet as the room, and the DJI got there while charging roughly four times faster. Both DJI and Anker figures were taken on UK mains, around 20–21 °C.

Maker figures don't fill these gaps. Anker's "20 dB" is for discharging under 200 W, not charging. BLUETTI's own page says "as low as 45dB while charging", which is louder than what The Technology Man measured in Silent. For the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus, the only published dB figure is Power Station Geek's 41 dB under sustained load, with no charging condition stated, so it isn't in the table.

Set the quiet rate before bed. The DJI's Standard mode is a physical switch, and The Technology Man notes that it automatically charges at the lower speed after five consecutive fast charges. On the AC180, Silent is turned on in the BLUETTI app. On the Anker, the AC rate is set in the Anker app in 100 W steps, and UltraFast 1,600 W also has to be turned on there (otherwise it charges at up to 1,200 W). On the Jackery, the quiet charging mode is in the Jackery app.
