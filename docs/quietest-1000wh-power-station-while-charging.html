How the numbers were taken

The Technology Man measured each unit with a sound meter at one metre in the UK: the DJI Power 1000 V2 at about 37 dB charging at full speed ("one of the quietest power stations I've tested"), the AC180 at about 47 dB in Turbo and 37 dB in Silent, and the C1000 Gen 2 at about 42 dB in UltraFast. StorageReview measured the AC180's Silent input at 283 W, Turbo at 1,458 W. For scale, 37 dB is about the level of a quiet bedroom at night, and each 10 dB sounds roughly twice as loud.

Keeping any unit quiet overnight

Fans run to cool the charger and the battery, so the slower the charge, the quieter the unit: set the lowest AC charge rate in the app before bed (on the C1000 Gen 2 the reviewer found 200 W ran with the fans off), keep it out of direct sun and away from a wall that traps heat, and avoid charging and powering a heavy load at the same time. Fan noise also rises with what you run from it, which is a separate question from charging.

What we could not find

No measured dB while charging for the EcoFlow DELTA 3 or DELTA 3 Plus, the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2, the BLUETTI Elite 100 V2 or the ALLPOWERS P1800, and no maker publishes how long its quiet mode takes to fill the battery; the times in the table are capacity divided by the charge rate.
