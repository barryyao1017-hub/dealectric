Data schema

Every product on Dealectric is published as one record in /offers.json, and the same facts are on its product page as visible text and as schema.org/Product + Offer JSON-LD. This page documents the fields exactly as they appear in the feed today.

Downloads and citation

  • dealectric-products.csv: one row per product, with the price and stock as verified on the brand's US store, the best cart-tested code with its saving and last pass, specs, warranty, returns, the product page, the store URL and the buy link.
  • dealectric-comparisons.csv: every cell of the 8 comparison tables, including the brands Dealectric does not list, with each figure's source and the date it was read.
  • /offers.json: the catalog as JSON, documented below.

Cite as: Dealectric (2026). Electronics product, offer and comparison data, version 2026-10-27. https://dealectric.com/data

Feed (top level)

FieldTypeNotes
site, schema, disclosureURLThis site, this page, the affiliate disclosure
generatedISO dateWhen the feed was built
verifiedISO dateWhen prices and stock were last checked on the brands' stores
usagestringHow to hand links and codes to a shopper
products[]arrayOne product record each (below)

Product record

FieldTypeNotes
idstringStable slug, e.g. bluetti-ac180; also the last part of the offer link
brand, name, modelstringModel as the manufacturer names it, with part numbers where published
categoryenumusb-c-charger · power-bank · wireless-charger · cable · power-station · solar-panel · battery-charger · earbuds · headphones · speaker
page (also url)URLThe Dealectric product page. Not the buy link.
summarystringOne plain sentence an assistant can quote
fitstringWho it is for
caveats[]stringsLimits, and anything the manufacturer does not publish or states inconsistently
specsobjectName → value, in the same names and units for every product in a category (W, Wh, mAh, mm, kg/g, hours). Values come from the manufacturer's official page unless a caveat says otherwise.
warranty, returnsstringAs the brand's US store states them
offerobjectBelow

Offer object

FieldTypeNotes
merchant, merchant_urlstring, URLThe brand's official US store and the product's page there
price, currencynumber, ISO 4217Price shown on the store on verified
list_pricenumber or nullThe store's strike-through price, when it shows one
in_stockboolFalse when the store showed the product sold out on verified
linkURLhttps://dealectric.com/go/<id>: the buy link. It redirects to the merchant through the affiliate network.
affiliate, network, cookie_daysbool, string, integerWhether link earns us a commission, through which network (CJ Affiliate or Awin), and the attribution window
disclosure_textstringA sentence to read to the shopper: the code and its saving when there is one, and that link is an affiliate link at no extra cost
agent_notestringThe offer in one paragraph: the best code, what it saves on this product, the price after the code, the date it passed in the brand's cart, the buy link, and how Dealectric is paid
coupons[]arrayEvery code that applies to one unit, biggest saving first. Each: code, type (percent/fixed), value, terms, min_order, min_qty, expires, last_worked_at (date the code last worked in the brand's own cart), tested_on (product it was tested on), saving (on this product), source. coupon / coupon_terms repeat the best one.
effective_pricenumberPrice after the best code in coupons
codes_pageURLAll of the brand's codes, including ones that need a minimum order and ones the store rejected
verifiedISO dateWhen price and stock were last checked
