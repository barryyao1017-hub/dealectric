Who it's for

Suits someone who wants one small charger for a laptop, a phone and earbuds, and who wants to see what each port delivers; Smartzone says its AVS support charges the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max at full power. Anyone who needs USB-A or a larger laptop share with several devices plugged in is better served by the UGREEN Nexode 100W 4-Port GaN Charger ($54.99). Anyone who wants a display and more than 100 W should look at the UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W 5-Port ($56.99 with the code on our link), which has two 140 W ports.

Caveats

  • Total output is 100 W shared, and reviewers' tables disagree: with two devices the laptop port gets 67 W in Smart Mode or 80 W in Turbo Mode per Smartzone, while techtest.org lists 45 W + 45 W.
  • Smartzone found that USB-C2 and USB-C3 used together share 15 W at 5 V, so a laptop belongs on USB-C1.
  • It does not hold 100 W indefinitely: techtest.org saw 80 W after about 40 minutes, and Smartzone saw 80 W after 45 minutes with the housing at 67 °C.
  • There is no USB-A port and no built-in cable. Android Authority's reviewer found the included USB-C cable too short and bought longer ones.
  • Device recognition and the charge-to-95% power-off feature work only with iPhone 15 and newer, and the temperature page shows a bar rather than degrees (Smartzone).
  • The techtest.org and Smartzone figures come from German reviews, so their units were probably the EU-plug version; neither figure set was checked against Baseus's US page.
  • No discount at Baseus's store today: price and compare-at price are both $59.99. The Dark Cherry Edition is a separate listing at the same price.
  • Returns are short: 7 business days without a reason, buyer pays shipping, 30 business days for a defect; warranty is 18–24 months (Baseus US pages, read 2026-11-27).
