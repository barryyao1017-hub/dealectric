Which hardwire kit fits which camera

A dash cam records while parked only if it has power with the engine off. Digital Camera World's review of the N5S says the camera “will need to be hard-wired to your vehicle's 12 volt battery” and lists parking mode as “Yes, optional hardwire kit required”. The kit also cuts power at a set battery voltage so the camera cannot flatten the battery. Each maker names its own kit, and they are not interchangeable in the makers' listings.

Two fit details matter. Vantrue's and Rexing's cabin-facing or inward-turned cameras use infrared light, which helps inside the car; on the N5S, Ben Andrews found that “at night, very little will be visible through the car's side windows” and that “exterior coverage from the cabin cameras isn't great”. And the Rexing 360° Motion kit is for gasoline cars only, so a hybrid or electric car owner who wants outward side lenses with parking mode should look at the R4-RD or R4 instead of the C4.

Not recommended

Either outward side-lens camera, if the footage has to read plates at night. Both drew poor image verdicts from named testers. The Smart Home Hookup, in its 13-camera test published 2025-12-19, said of the R4-RD: “The Rexing was my least favorite image and was both oversaturated and overexposed”, and at night with both cars moving “the Rexing was just a blurry mess”. Vortex Radar, reviewing the 4-channel Botslab G980H on DashCamTalk on 2026-04-23, wrote “No HDR, so really struggles at night”, called the rear camera's video “subpar”, and said the parking G-sensor “has really low sensitivity”. The summary was “a crummy half-baked dashcam with a pair of side cams slapped onto the side”. The review also raised security concerns, which Botslab disputed in an update to the same post. Buy either for where its lenses point and what is in the box, not for image quality.

The Rexing R4 for parking mode. With its kit it costs $289.98, against $279.99 for the R4-RD, which adds a motion sensor and has the kit in the box. The R4 makes sense only if the camera will never be hardwired.
