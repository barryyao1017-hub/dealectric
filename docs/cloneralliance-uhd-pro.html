Who it's for

Anyone recording a PS5, Xbox or PC that sends 4K60 who wants 4K files rather than 1080p, and anyone who needs very long recordings on a large hard drive.

Caveats

  • Records 4K at 30 fps at most. A 4K60 source is recorded at 4K30.
  • Captures in SDR. ClonerAlliance says to set a PS5 or Xbox to SDR output.
  • Smart TVs and TV antennas are not supported as input sources (maker).
  • The maker page has no HDCP statement. Turn HDCP off on a PS5 before recording.
