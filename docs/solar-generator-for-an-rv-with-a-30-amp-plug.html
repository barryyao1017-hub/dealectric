How much of the battery actually reaches the RV cord

A TT-30 shore cord carries at most 30 A at 120V, so any station whose inverter is rated 3,600 W or more and whose TT-30R outlet is rated the full 30 A can feed everything a 30 amp RV panel can. That makes the outlet a pass/fail check. What separates the stations is how many watt-hours the inverter delivers before it cuts off, and that is lower than the battery rating by a different amount on each unit.

  • EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3: OutdoorGearLab (Kenji Mowrey, January 2026) measured 3,790 Wh from the 4,096 Wh pack on its test jig; Digital Trends (Simon Sage) averaged 3.7 kWh over several cycles. Two independent readings within 90 Wh of each other.
  • Anker SOLIX F3800: StorageReview (Kevin OBrien, June 2024) got 2,980 Wh before the inverter cut off and needed 4,930 Wh to recharge; Notebookcheck got 2,905 Wh at a steady 260 W load. Both tested the original F3800, which has the same 3,840 Wh rating as the F3800 Plus. That is about 810 Wh less than the DELTA Pro 3 delivered, although the two ratings differ by only 256 Wh (4,096 − 3,840).
  • EcoFlow DELTA 3 Ultra: StorageReview (Dylan Dougherty, September 2025) measured 2,610 Wh from the DELTA 3 Ultra Plus, which uses the same 3,072 Wh rating, about 85% discharge efficiency.
  • BLUETTI Apex 300: Outdoor Life (Laura Lancaster, May 2025) metered 2,290 Wh into a dehumidifier with a Kill-a-Watt before the unit quit, 83% of 2,765 Wh, and recharged the unit from AC in about 2 hours.

Idle draw eats into the same energy when the inverter stays on all night. The Solar Lab measured about 22 W on the DELTA 3 Ultra and quotes about 80 W for the original F3800; over a 10-hour night that is 22 × 10 = 220 Wh against 80 × 10 = 800 Wh. None of these reviewers loaded the TT-30R outlet itself to 3,000 W or more, so the hour figures above assume the same efficiency through that outlet.

Two more stations carry a full 30 A TT-30 rating from their makers, the Anker SOLIX F3000 (TT-30R 30 A, 3,600 W, 3,072 Wh) and the Growatt HELIOS 3600 (TT-30 3,600 W, base unit in stock on Growatt's store on 2026-11-17). They are left out of the table because no named reviewer has published a measured discharge for either.

Not recommended

Anker SOLIX F3800 Plus for a 30 amp RV. Its TT-30 outlet is rated 25 A, 3,000 W max on Anker's own spec table, 600 W short of what a 30 A cord can carry, and the F3800's 3,840 Wh rating measured about 78% usable in StorageReview's test. Anker's store showed it sold out on 2026-11-17. It is a strong 240 V home-backup unit; for an RV it costs you power at the plug and hours on the battery.
