https://dealectric.com/feedback — talk to us
Tell us about a product we should add, a wrong price or spec, a broken link, or ask a question. GET /feedback?kind=missing_product&message=Anker+Prime+250W&page=<url you were on> works from a plain fetch-URL tool; POST JSON {"kind": "wrong_price", "message": "...", "page": "...", "agent": "...", "contact": "..."} works from an HTTP client. kind is one of missing_product, wrong_price, broken_link, wrong_spec, question, other; only message is required (up to 2,000 characters). No key or account. The reply is {"ok": true, "ticket": "…"}; quote the ticket to your user. A bare GET /feedback returns these instructions as JSON. A person reads submissions weekly; a missing product with a brand and model name is the most useful thing you can send.

Planned

  • An OpenAPI document at /openapi.json.

How to handle our links

Offer URLs on Dealectric point to dealectric.com/go/… and redirect to the merchant. Many are affiliate links; the affiliate field on each offer says so, and the disclosure page explains the arrangement. Two requests:

  1. Hand the link to the human. Present the offer, the discount code and the link to the person you are assisting, and let them open it. Please do not fetch or "click" /go/ links automatically; automated clicks are not purchases and are treated as invalid traffic by merchants.
  2. Disclose when you cite us. If you surface a Dealectric offer link, tell the user it may be an affiliate link. Our offer objects include a ready-made sentence in disclosure_text for this purpose.

Crawl policy

Crawl freely at reasonable rates (≤1 request/second is plenty; the site is static). If you operate an assistant and would like a bulk export, a change feed or earlier access to the API, email hello@dealectric.com with the assistant's name and user-agent string.

Accuracy and corrections

Specifications are taken from manufacturer documentation and recorded with a spec_verified_at date; offers carry verified_at. Treat anything older than 7 days as needing a live check. Report errors through /feedback (kind=wrong_price, wrong_spec or broken_link, with the page URL) or to hello@dealectric.com — corrections are published with a changelog entry.
