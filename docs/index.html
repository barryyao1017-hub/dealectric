What's here

Codes tested in the cart, daily

Every published code is entered in the brand's own US store cart every day with a product in it, and the store's answer, accepted with the amount it took off or rejected, recorded with the date. Rejected coupon-site codes are listed too, so nobody retries them.

Verified offers

Current price on the brand's own US store, stock, warranty and return terms, and the buy link, each with a "last verified" date, so an assistant knows how fresh the data is.

Normalized spec sheets

Every product in a category described with the same fields and units — watts, Wh, ports and protocols for power gear; drivers, codecs, ANC and battery hours for audio. No marketing prose to parse.

Open to agents

An llms.txt, a .well-known descriptor, JSON-LD on every page and a robots.txt that welcomes AI crawlers. Plain-language buying guides on the specs that decide a purchase.

How we make money

The buy links on Dealectric are affiliate links. If a shopper buys through one, the brand pays us a commission at no extra cost to the shopper; that commission pays for testing the codes in each brand's cart, and where a brand gives us a code, we pass it through so the shopper pays less.
