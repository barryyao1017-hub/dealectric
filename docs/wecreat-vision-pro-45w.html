Who it's for

Small sellers and serious hobbyists who cut thick plywood, fill A3-size sheets or engrave tall objects and want a camera and an enclosure, including 3D-printer owners who find a printer's laser module too small. Anyone who needs to cut clear acrylic should skip diode lasers, since WECREAT lists only dark opaque acrylic as cuttable, and buyers who mainly want a larger bed should compare the xTool S1 40W (23.93 × 15.16 in bed, $1,448 list at xtool.com, out of stock there on 2026-11-18).

Caveats

  • TechRadar's reviewer found MakeIt! dropped its connection when the computer slept and sometimes needed both machine and computer restarted, more often on a Mac.
  • The camera was significantly out of alignment out of the box in TechRadar's test and took about 15 minutes to recalibrate with the supplied wood squares.
  • Owner reviews on WECREAT's own product page report that venting through the hose does not remove the smell and that the software froze and needed a restart.
  • The Basic Pack has no rotary, honeycomb panel or filter: the Rotary Pack is $200 more, the honeycomb panel $109 and the AirGuard Ultra 2.0 extractor $1,099.
  • WECREAT lists only "some dark opaque acrylic" as cuttable, so clear acrylic is out, and TechRadar noted the optional infrared head is 2 W, enough for marking metal but not more.
