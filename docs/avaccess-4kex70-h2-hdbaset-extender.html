Who it's for

Cable routes from the booth to the projector longer than 49 ft and up to 197 ft on Cat5e/6 or 230 ft on Cat6a.

Caveats

  • AV Access says it works only over a direct cable from transmitter to receiver, not through an Ethernet switch.
  • One transmitter works with only one receiver. Plug the receiver into the projector's HDMI input. AV Access doesn't say the transmitter works with a projector's built-in HDBaseT port.
  • At 4K the reach drops to 115 ft on Cat5e/6.
  • The Cat cable isn't included. Use T568B straight-through cable, as AV Access recommends.
