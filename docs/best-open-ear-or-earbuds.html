How long each pair lasts in the mode you'll use

Battery ratings are measured in each pair's most economical mode. The Liberty 4 Pro's '10 hours from one charge' is with ANC off. Headphonecheck measured 10 h in basic mode and 6.5 h with noise cancelling at maximum, and SoundGuys measured 5 h 42 min with ANC enabled. Bose tests at a playback loudness of 75 dBA and rates 9 h, or 5 h once Immersive Audio or Auto Volume is on. soundcore's own table rates the AeroClip 2 at 8 h for music and 5 h in call mode.

Noise cancelling is what costs earbuds their runtime, and it only works with a sealed ear canal. Bose says of its open design that 'the earbuds do not include active noise cancelling'. SoundGuys measured about 0 dB of midrange isolation on the open-ear AeroFit 2, against about 30 dB of low-frequency reduction from the sealed Liberty 4 Pro. An open-ear pair can't make a train quieter. A sealed pair can, but with ANC on it won't last a full workday on one charge.

Not recommended

soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro ($179.99) as a both-in-one pair. Moving its ear hooks switches it from open-ear to noise cancelling. Andy White at Expert Reviews found the ANC 'fell some way short of my expectations' and behind the Shokz OpenFit Pro, and the pair is rated five hours with ANC against seven without. If you need quiet on a commute, the sealed Liberty 4 Pro cancels more noise for $30 less. If you don't, the AeroFit 2 lasts longer for $50 less.
