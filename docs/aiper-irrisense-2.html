Who it's for

Lawns and gardens up to about 4,800 sq ft with a hose bib and an outdoor outlet near the edge of the yard, for owners who don't want to dig for an in-ground system.

Caveats

  • It needs an outdoor power outlet. There is no battery or solar option.
  • Reach depends on water pressure and the hose. Aiper says a reach under 26 ft usually means the supply is restricted, and suggests keeping the hose under 50 ft and not using an expandable hose.
  • In The Smart Home Hookup's test it put down less than half the depth set in the app, so check with catch cups and raise the setting.
  • WIRED saw wet and dry stripes, with edges getting more water than the center. The tester's gusty wind also tripped the rain sensor and stopped runs.
  • It only saves one map, and the app has no Alexa, Google Home, HomeKit or SmartThings support.
  • The 40% water saving is Aiper's own claim, and reviewers did not measure it.
