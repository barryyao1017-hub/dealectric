What the differences mean

  • Redodo's 5,000 W is about 98 A. 5,000 W ÷ 51.2 V = 97.7 A. That is the same class as LiTime's 1C pack: 1 × 100 Ah = 100 A.
  • WEIZE gives twice the current for $13 more. Redodo battery plus charger: $836.99 + $149.99 = $986.98. WEIZE kit: $999.99 − $986.98 = $13.01 more, for 200 A instead of about 98 A.
  • LiTime's 200 A pack costs more and has no charger. $1,097.99 − $999.99 = $98 more than the WEIZE kit, and LiTime rates its 200 A for 2 minutes: 2C × 100 Ah = 200 A.
  • The Redodo kit's extra is the monitor. $1,149.99 − $986.98 = $163.01 more than battery plus charger, for the 500 A shunt monitor.

Continuous amps vs. burst seconds: what each maker actually rates

The BMS cuts power when current goes over its rating for longer than its time window. Two numbers matter: the current the pack holds with no time limit, and how many seconds a burst lasts. Most 48V budget packs are 100 Ah. Their makers word the same limit as '1C', as watts, or as '200A BMS', and those labels are hard to compare until you convert them.

WEIZE's 35-second window at 400 A lasts through a long climb. LiTime's 600 A lasts 1 second, which covers a start from standstill. On cold days, note that Golf Cart Gear Lab reports the WEIZE pack has no heater and charges from 32°F. WEIZE's store listed it as available on 2026-11-13, but Golf Cart Gear Lab reported on 2026-11-11 that it was a pre-order shipping around November 23.

NewtiPower's Amazon listing (ASIN B0CPSRQCGW) says '200A BMS' in its title. The title gives no burst duration and doesn't mention a charger, and NewtiPower has no maker spec page we could open. That is why it is left out of the table.

Club Car Precedent owners: ROYPOW's conversion guide says the Precedent (2004 on) has an On-Board Computer that runs lead-acid charging. The guide calls that charging profile 'incompatible with lithium chemistry' and says a bypass harness is 'mandatory on most 48V Club Car models'. Budget for that harness whichever pack you choose.

Not recommended
