What 288 Wh covers on a camping weekend

A phone charge is about 12–15 Wh, a string of LED lights 5–10 W, a tent fan 10–40 W and a CPAP without humidifier 20–30 W. So 288 Wh covers two nights of lights, phones, a fan and a CPAP for one night, with the panel topping it up by day. It will not run a kettle, a hair dryer or an electric cooler all weekend; a 12 V compressor cooler needs about 250–450 Wh a day, which is AC180 territory.

How long the 100 W panel takes

A folding panel gives about 70–80% of its rating, so 100 W puts back about 75 W in full sun: roughly 4 hours to refill 288 Wh, most of a day under broken cloud, and next to nothing under trees. Set it in open sun and move it as the shadows move.
