What owners complain about

Across the reviews and owner comments we read, the same five issues come up for 2 kWh stations, and the Growatt is not immune to any of them.

  • Fan noise under load. Every tester who ran the INFINITY 2000 or 2000 PRO above about 1,500 W heard the fan, and one measured 45 dB at 3 ft. Quiet-charge mode cuts AC input to roughly 400 W to keep it silent at night.
  • App pairing. Three reviewers had the MyGro app fail to find the unit the first time; toggling Bluetooth and Wi-Fi fixed it. The runtime estimate is rough and one owner needed a full charge cycle to recalibrate the percentage.
  • Warranty follow-through. Growatt's own store carries a one-star review about an approved claim with no replacement sent, and Trustpilot reviews of the parent company are split between fast fixes and unanswered emails. Buy from the store, register within 30 days and keep the invoice.
  • Weight. At about 51 lb with no wheels the Growatt, like the 53-lb BLUETTI, is a two-hand carry. Only the Jackery and Anker drop near 40 lb.
  • Returns. Lithium units are often non-returnable on marketplaces. Growatt's store allows 30 days but you pay return freight and about 5%.
