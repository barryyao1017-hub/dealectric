Who it's for

Cabin, homestead or home-backup buyers who want solar plus a wind top-up at night, 120 V loads only, and room to add Power Pods later.

Caveats

  • Wind adds at most 150 W, and only in a 27 mph wind; at a 10 mph site expect a few watts, so size the solar for the real load.
  • The wind turbine, its adapter and a mounting pole are sold separately.
  • 120 V output only; a 240 V well pump or dryer needs the L6000 or MyGrid 10K.
  • Warranty is 1 year, against 5 years on BLUETTI's Elite 200 V2.
  • Weight is not listed on the store page; a retailer spec sheet shows about 97 lb, so plan on two people or the maker's cart.
