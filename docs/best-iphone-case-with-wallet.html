Charging an iPhone case with a wallet: Apple's card rule

Apple's MagSafe Charger support page says: "Don't place credit cards, security badges, passports, or key fobs between your iPhone and MagSafe Charger," because this "might damage magnetic strips or RFID chips in those items." It also says: "If you have a case that holds any of these sensitive items, remove them before charging." Tap-to-pay bank cards have RFID chips, so the rule covers them as well as magnetic-stripe cards.

  • One-piece card case (Wallet Slayer Vol. 1). Smartish says the case is not compatible with MagSafe or wireless chargers ("Cable charging works faster anyways"). Your cards stay in and you plug in a cable. Smartish's current Vol. 1 listings say it holds one to four cards plus cash, and it is sold separately for the iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, 17e, Air, 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max. Smartish says the case was drop-tested 50 times from 6 feet onto concrete. TechGearLab (Kat Elliott) named it Top Pick, Best Wallet Case to Stash Everything In One Place.
  • Detachable MagSafe wallet (ESR, Smartish Side Hustle, Nomad). The wallet comes off before you charge. ESR's FAQ answers "No, you must take the wallet off first before charging your phone wirelessly." Nomad's wallet charges its own Find My battery on a Qi or MagSafe pad, which Nomad rates at 5 months per charge. That battery is separate from your phone's.
  • Any one-piece case with a card pocket on the back, even one sold as MagSafe compatible. The magnets still work for mounts and accessories, but under Apple's rule the cards come out before wireless charging.

So the real choice is between plugging in a cable (Wallet Slayer) and pulling the wallet off (MagSafe). Find My also differs. ESR says its Geo wallets have a built-in module that tracks "in real time". Nomad says its wallet shows the last known location, has a range of up to 150 ft and has no Precision Finding. For the ESR Geo Magnetic Wallet Stand, ESR's store lists Find My, but 9to5Mac's review of an ESR 5-card wallet says it has none. Check the listing for the exact model before you buy. ESR's Geo Wallet Boost fits caseless MagSafe iPhones from the 12 series on, including the 17e but not the 12 mini or 13 mini. It also works on MagSafe or HaloLock cases, or on a standard case with ESR's HaloLock Universal Ring.

Not recommended

ESR Geo Wallet Stand (HaloLock). It holds 1–3 cards and ESR publishes no holding-force figure for it. At ESR's own store it costs more than the Geo Wallet Boost, which holds 4 cards and 3 bills with a stated 1,800 g hold and the same Find My module. MacRumors also found it 2.6 times as heavy as Apple's wallet. If you want a stand, the ESR Geo Magnetic Wallet Stand holds five cards with a stated 3,200 g hold.
