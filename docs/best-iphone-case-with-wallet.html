One-piece case or MagSafe wallet?

A one-piece case can't fall off, but cards sit between the phone and the charger, so most block wireless charging (Smartish says so outright). A MagSafe wallet keeps the case slim and comes off for charging or tapping, but it can slide off in tight pockets. No product does cash, ID and wireless charging in one piece.

Will a magnetic wallet wipe my cards?

Apple warns that hotel key cards and other magnetic-stripe cards can be damaged by MagSafe magnets. Chip bank cards and ID cards aren't affected. Apple Pay still works with the wallet on; a few readers pick up a physical card first.

Not recommended

MOFT-style thin wallets on plain cases (slide off), Peak Design's wallet on cases that aren't Peak Design's (weaker hold), and Spigen Slim Armor CS for new iPhones (discontinued from iPhone 16).
