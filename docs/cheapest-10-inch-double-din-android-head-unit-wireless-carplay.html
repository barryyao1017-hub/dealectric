Samsung phones and wireless projection on cheap Android head units

On a budget Android head unit, wireless CarPlay and Android Auto usually come from an app on the unit, not from the screen itself. Binize's 10-inch Android 12 listings say so directly: 'The zlink app is designed for wireless apple CarPlay.' The same spec tables read 'Autoplay Yes, Both for iPhone and Android (not for Samsung)', and the Mirrorlink line carries the same exclusion. In a customer answer on its Honda Civic unit, Binize writes that 'approximately 85% of Samsung mobile phones may not be compatible' and says it updated its listings because of that.

Binize's cheapest 10-inch double-DIN listing doesn't print the Samsung line, but it is the same brand. Its spec table also mentions only Mirrorlink, not a wireless CarPlay or Android Auto row. The Haudio listing and Eonon's UA13 Plus and UA12S Plus-6G pages print no phone-brand limit.

Google's requirement for wireless Android Auto applies to every unit here. You need an Android phone running Android 11 or later, with 5 GHz Wi-Fi and an active data plan, plus a compatible stereo. The head unit has to talk 5 GHz too. Binize states '2.4G+5G dual-band WiFi' only on its Corolla-specific Android 13 unit. Its universal 10-inch listings just say 'Built-In WIFI Receiver Yes', and the Haudio listing says only 'Wi-Fi connectivity'.
