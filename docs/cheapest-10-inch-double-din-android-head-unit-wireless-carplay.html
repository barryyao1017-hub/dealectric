What $230 gives up

Two things. The hardware: 2 GB of RAM and a quad-core chip run CarPlay and Android Auto, which do the work on your phone, but Android apps installed on the unit itself will stutter, and the 1280 × 720 panel is sharp enough at arm's length and no more. And the track record: Trustpilot and ResellerRatings reviews of Eonon include early failures, freezes and slow support next to owners who got free mainboard repairs and quick replacements. A July 2025 r/CarAV thread warns against generic Chinese head units as a class; ATOTO gets the most consistent praise of the budget Android brands, and its 10-inch units cost $180 or more above the UA13 Plus.

Fit, wiring and CAN bus

Both Eonon units are universal double-DIN chassis. Budget for the vehicle-specific harness, a dash kit and, on cars with steering-wheel controls or a factory amplifier, a CAN-bus adapter; Eonon sells wiring options on the product page and vehicle-specific units for BMW, Mazda, Volkswagen and GM models at higher prices.

Coupon pricing

Eonon's product pages show a base price and one or more coupon prices that change with its sales calendar (on 2026-11-03 the UA13 Plus page read $229.99 with a $209.99 coupon price and the UA12S Plus-6G $319.99 with $289.99). The price you pay is the one the page offers that day, not a code you type.

Returns and warranty

Eonon gives Android car stereos bought on eonon.com a 2-year warranty: full refund or exchange within 3 months for a product issue, exchange within 6 months, replacement accessories or a discounted new unit within 2 years. Returns for any other reason are accepted within 3 months at your return-shipping cost, plus 20% depreciation if the unit was opened or installed.
