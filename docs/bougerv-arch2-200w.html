Who it's for

Suits owners of curved or weight-limited roofs (vans, trailers, boats) who want to mount a panel without a rack. BougeRV's FAQ says Arch and Arch 2.0 panels should not be mixed in one system, so it fits new installs better than add-ons to an older Arch array.

Caveats

  • BougeRV lists one bypass diode and makes no partial-shade claim for this panel (shade behaviour not stated by BougeRV).
  • The listed upper operating limit is +140°F, lower than the +185°F BougeRV lists for its rigid ShadePower panel; BougeRV's Canadian page lists +185°F for the same SKU, so the figures disagree.
  • BougeRV's US product page does not state an IP rating, connector type, cable length or certifications (not stated by BougeRV), and lists the Isc temperature coefficient as +0.048%/K in one table and -0.048%/K in another.
  • No independent test of the Arch 2.0 was found; OutdoorGearLab's test (Sam Schild, published 2026-07-13: 166 W, 83% of rating) covers the earlier Arch 200W, listed there with 23% cells and 6.6 lb, not this N-type 2.0 model.
  • Materials and workmanship coverage is 5 years, half the 10 years BougeRV lists for its rigid ShadePower 200W.
