Who it's for

Kayak and canoe anglers running a 30 to 40 lb motor for a few hours, or a 55 lb motor mostly at low speed. Not enough for a full day of full-throttle running.

Caveats

  • About 1 hour at full throttle on a 55 lb motor (50 A draw); 2 to 3 hours at half speed.
  • The 60 A limit is rated for 30 minutes; a 55 lb motor at full throttle sits close to it.
  • Needs a 14.6 V LiFePO4 charger; a lead-acid charger will not fill it.
  • Will not accept a charge below 32°F, so charge it indoors in winter.
