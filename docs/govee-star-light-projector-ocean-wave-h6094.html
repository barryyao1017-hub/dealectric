Who it's for

For bedrooms and kids' rooms when you want to schedule the ocean-and-stars effect or switch it on by voice, rather than use a remote.

Caveats

  • No power adapter in the box: you need a 5 V / 2 A (10 W) USB adapter.
  • Over Matter (Apple Home) you get on/off, brightness and scenes. MacRumors found colour changes from the Home app did not work. Govee's Matter FAQ says a Matter hub is required.
  • The laser stars are fixed green (MacRumors); they can be switched off.
