Battery: who timed what

Built-in storage matters because Bluetooth stops at the water's surface. H2O Audio's own product page says "Bluetooth does NOT work underwater", so the music has to live on the headset. The figure that decides how often you charge is runtime, and only one model here has been put on a clock by a named tester:

  • MOJAWA Run Plus: Headphones Addict (Peter Susic, 2023-08-01) played music at 50% volume until the battery ran out at 8 h 43 min, then recharged it from 0 to 100% in 50 minutes. MOJAWA's own rating is 8 h at 75% volume. The review doesn't say whether the test ran in MP3 or Bluetooth mode. GamingTrend (Ron Burke) saw roughly 1 h from empty to full but repeated MOJAWA's runtime claim without testing it.
  • Jabees 7Seven: New Atlas (Bronwyn Thompson, 2025-10-19) writes that "we didn't track it exactly" against Jabees' 5 h Bluetooth / 6 h MP3 claim at 60% volume, and calls that claim "probably a stretch". The review puts a full charge at about 2 h.
  • H2O Audio STREAM 3 PRO: 7 h, H2O Audio's figure. FINIS Duo: up to 7 h and 4 GB according to FINIS's own manual. We couldn't confirm a current US price for the Duo on 2026-11-08, so it isn't in the table.

All of these players need music files you own, copied over from a computer. H2O Audio's STREAM DM page states that an Apple Music subscription doesn't let you download the files to its players. If you can spend more than $100, MOJAWA's Purra Swim is $169.99, has 32 GB and is rated for 15 h, which works out to 15 ÷ 0.75 = 20 swims per charge. $169.99: https://dealectric.com/go/mojawa-purra-swim-32gb-bone-conduction
