Why storage, not Bluetooth

Radio at Bluetooth frequencies dies within an inch of water, so a swim headphone is an MP3 player on your cheekbones. 32 GB holds a library; 4 or 8 GB holds a few playlists. Loading is drag-and-drop over USB, and MOJAWA's and Shokz's units both switch to Bluetooth out of the pool. Use the included earplugs: bone conduction underwater sounds clearer with the ear canal blocked.

The sound, honestly

RTINGS measured the Run Plus's frequency response consistency as bad and calls its sound poor even for bone conduction, recommending the Shokz OpenRun Pro for most people and reserving the Run Plus for swimmers who need the storage. Tom's Guide found it light, comfortable and "up there with" the OpenSwim in the water; Headphones Addict liked the natural mids but heard the bass distort, and GamingTrend called the underwater performance excellent. Buy it for the pool, not as your only pair.

Battery and charging

8 hours rated and 8 h 43 min measured at 50% volume, with a 5-minute charge good for about 1.5 hours; the OpenSwim Pro gets 6 hours in MP3 mode and the Purra Swim 15 hours on a magnetic USB charger in 2 hours.

Fit

Open-ear loops that sit on the cheekbones; some wearers report top-of-ear pressure after an hour or so, and the loops can press on glasses frames, so goggles with thin straps fit better than thick ones.

Returns and warranty

A 30-day money-back guarantee and a 2-year warranty on purchases through MOJAWA's official channels. A refurbished Run Plus was $59.99 on mojawa.com on 2026-11-03.
