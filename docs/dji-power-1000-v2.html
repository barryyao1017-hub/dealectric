Who it's for

Buyers who want the most 1 kWh power for the money, need to run 1,500–1,800 W appliances or tools, or use DJI drones and cameras. Not for solar-first or weight-sensitive camping.

Caveats

  • Heaviest of OutdoorGearLab's five ~1 kWh units at 31.5 lb (OutdoorGearLab)
  • Solar and 12 V charging need DJI's proprietary adapters; 400 W solar through the MPPT module (DJI FAQ; OutdoorGearLab)
  • About 23 W idle draw with the inverter on (The Technology Man)
  • Could not start an induction-motor table saw (The Technology Man)
  • Not sold on DJI's US store, so US warranty and support need confirming (DronEDJ; us.store.dji.com 404)
