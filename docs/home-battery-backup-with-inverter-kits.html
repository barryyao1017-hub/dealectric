What the differences mean

  • Battery current. 6,500 W ÷ 51.2 V ≈ 127 A before inverter losses, so the real draw is a little higher. Two packs: 127 ÷ 2 ≈ 64 A each. Four packs: 127 ÷ 4 ≈ 32 A each. The SG48100P manual rates each pack at 100 A continuous.
  • Charging current. The SPH6548P charges at up to 140 A. One pack is rated 100 A continuous charge; two in parallel take 2 × 100 = 200 A.
  • Stored energy. 2 × 5.12 = 10.24 kWh; 4 × 5.12 = 20.48 kWh; 2 × 2,764.8 = 5,529.6 Wh for the BLUETTI pair. An average 1 kW load uses 1 kWh an hour, so 10.24 kWh lasts at most about 10 hours at 1 kW, and less after inverter losses. StorageReview got 7.38 kWh out of an Apex 300 with two B300K packs (8,294.4 Wh rated), which is 89%.
  • Cost per kWh, maker prices on 2026-11-13. $3,166 ÷ 10.24 ≈ $309/kWh; $5,747 ÷ 20.48 ≈ $281/kWh; $3,297 ÷ 5.53 ≈ $596/kWh.
  • Power. 6,500 W against 3,840 W. On the Apex 300 all outlets, including the 240 V NEMA 14-50R, share one 3,840 W budget (StorageReview).

Why one 48 V battery can't feed a 6.5 kW inverter

A separate inverter and battery set is limited by battery current as well as kWh. The inverter turns 48 V DC into 120/240 V AC, so every AC watt is drawn from the batteries as DC current. One SG48100P is rated 100 A continuous discharge and 100 A continuous charge (SG48100P user manual). The SPH6548P at its rated 6,500 W needs about 127 A and can charge at 140 A. With one pack, heavy loads or full-rate charging go past the battery's rating, and the BMS shuts the battery off. Two packs on a 250 A bus bar raise the ceiling to 200 A. A 160 A DC breaker then sits between the bus bar and the inverter. Sun Gold Power's own SGM-6510M kit uses that same 160 A breaker and 250 A bus bar, plus 1/0 AWG battery cables.

Setup details that decide whether the pair works together:

  • The SPH6548P ships with BMS communication off (setting 33 = Disable). Set 32 to 485 or CAN and setting 33 to SGP so the inverter reads the Sun Gold batteries' charge limits (SPH6548P manual).
  • Give each battery in parallel its own address with the DIP switches on its board (SG48100P manual).
  • The SG48100P lists CAN/RS485/RS232. The $35 RS232 cable is for PC monitoring, not the inverter link.
  • Not in any cart: a transfer switch or interlock for the Sun Gold sets, and the licensed electrician who connects any of these kits to the home panel.

Not recommended

The SPH6548P with a single SG48100P costs $2,016, which looks like the cheap start. It puts about 127 A on a battery rated for 100 A, and the 140 A charger can exceed the battery's 100 A charge rating. If you must start with one pack, keep the AC load under about 100 A × 51.2 V = 5,120 W and set the inverter's charge current (adjustable from 0 to 140 A) to 100 A or less. Then add the second pack.
