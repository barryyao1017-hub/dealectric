Who it's for

Drivers who want 3840 × 2160 from the rear camera as well as the front, and will add their own microSD card.

Caveats

  • The 4K rear stream carries about 24 Mb/s, close to what a 2K rear gets on the VIOFO A229 Pro (25 Mbps per DashCamTalk), so it has fewer bits per pixel.
  • DashCamTalk's spec table lists no HDR on the rear camera; TonyM's July 2025 review says the rear supports HDR but not PlatePix.
  • Parking modes need a hardwire kit that is sold separately.
  • EricSan measured 54 °C on the rear camera's back panel in a 22 °C room.
