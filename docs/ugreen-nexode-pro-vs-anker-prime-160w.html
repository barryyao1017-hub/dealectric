Who held 140 W, and how each charger really splits power

Michael Barton of techtest.org ran the same long-load test and efficiency sweep on both chargers and on three related models. The UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W 5-Port gave 140 W from one port for 4 hours with no throttling (review dated 2026-10-20). The Anker Prime 160W held a constant 140 W at room temperature. Barton notes that a constant 160 W can trigger thermal throttling (review dated 2025-11-28). By contrast, the Anker Charger 140W 4-Port (A2697) dropped to 120 W after about half an hour. The UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W with Retractable Cable (X776) dropped to 120 W after about 40 minutes.

Anker's own US page states a limit: “At 77°F, single-port charging runs without derating in 220 - 240V areas, and for at least 1 hour in 100 - 130V areas.” US outlets are 120 V, so Anker promises full single-port output for at least an hour, not indefinitely.

UGREEN split (measured, fixed by port): USB-C1 + USB-C2 gives 60 W + 100 W. So when the laptop shares with one other device, plug it into C2. C1 + C2 + C3 gives 65 + 65 + 30 W, and C1 to C4 gives 45 + 65 + 30 + 20 W (techtest.org). Barton lists the fixed split as a con. Notebookcheck's US launch report says one port keeps 100 W with up to three devices and 65 W with four or five. Barton's three-port reading shows the 100 W does not hold for every three-port combination. With five devices attached, Notebookcheck measured 56 to 58 W on C1 over an hour. Only C1 and C2 reach 140 W. C3 tops out at 100 W, C4 at 30 W and USB-A at 22.5 W (Notebookcheck).

Anker split (set by mode, not published per port): Anker lists “160W Max” for two or three ports and gives no per-port figures. Dynamic distribution works only in AI Mode 2.0. With three devices on the default profile, How-To Geek's Bertel King saw about 50 W each. Holger Becker (smartzone.de) could not get a constant 160 W across two loads: after just over 40 minutes the charger throttled them to 100 W + 50 W. Anker's FAQ says the 150 W mode needs its A110A power bank and named Anker cables. It also says DJI charging hubs will not charge in AI Mode, so use USB-C1 Priority or Custom Mode for them.

Neither charger comes with a cable, and 140 W over USB-C needs a 240 W (EPR) cable. Anker's product page says the cable “is not included with the charger”, and Notebookcheck found only the charger and a manual in the UGREEN box. buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F9NK4M7Q?tag=dealectric-20

Not recommended

The older UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W 4-Port (X763) has the same name but costs $119.99 at UGREEN US (2026-11-14). That is 30 dollars more than the 5-port, for one fewer port. Its C1 + C2 split is 100 + 60 W. Barton weighed it at 312.5 g, so it saves almost nothing over the 5-port's 330 g. The retractable X776 costs the same $149.99 as the Anker Prime 160W but weighs 319 g against 205 g, and it throttled to 120 W after about 40 minutes.
