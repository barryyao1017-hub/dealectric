Does the laptop cut out when a phone is plugged in?

Any multi-port charger re-splits its power when a device is added, and the laptop can pause for a second while it renegotiates. Owners report this more often on Anker's multi-port line (Slickdeals, Framework forum); Notebookcheck's one-hour, five-device test of the UGREEN 5-port held the laptop at a steady 56–58 W with four other devices attached. Neither maker documents it.

Which one for a 16-inch MacBook Pro

Full-speed charging needs 140 W: on 2021 models only over MagSafe 3, on 2023 and later also over USB-C with a 240 W cable (MacRumors). Either charger gives 140 W to one port when nothing else is plugged in; neither ships with that cable. Anker's 100 W Prime (A2688) was sold out on anker.com on 2026-11-05.
