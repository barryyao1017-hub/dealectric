What owners complain about

No foot pedal or regulator in the box. This came up most. The base TIG200, YesWelder's TIG-205P PRO and both AC/DC machines ship without a pedal, and YesWelder's base boxes have no gas regulator. Price the pedal in before you compare: $79.99 from ARCCAPTAIN, or $79 more for YesWelder's pedal bundle. DC-only machines can't do aluminum. Reviews often catch buyers who expected a cheap DC TIG to weld aluminum. Reduced power on 120 V. YesWelder's TIG-205P drops from 200 A on 220 V to 160 A on 110 V. Plan on a 240 V outlet for thicker steel. Long-term reliability. Forum threads warn that budget HF starts can fail and cheap parts may not last. Use the return window hard, and pick the longer warranty if the welder will see regular use.

Which one by need

Learning TIG on steel and stainless for the least money: ARCCAPTAIN TIG200, which has HF start for $199.99. Thin sheet, pulse, longest warranty under $300: ARCCAPTAIN TIG205P Pro with 3 years of coverage. Pulse at the lowest price: YesWelder TIG-205P PRO at $249 with 2 years. Aluminum: you need AC. YesWelder's TIG-200P ACDC PRO with pedal cost less on its own store than ARCCAPTAIN's TIG200P AC/DC pedal kit ($729.99) on 2026-11-14. Welding forums also often recommend the PrimeWeld TIG225X as the step up for aluminum. We did not check its price.

Not recommended

Any DC-only TIG if aluminum is on your list, including both ARCCAPTAIN picks above. ARCCAPTAIN's TIG200P AC/DC if price is your main concern: it weighs 26.5 lb and needs the $729.99 kit to get a pedal.
