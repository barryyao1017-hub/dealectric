Which foot pedal fits which welder

In TIG welding one hand holds the torch, the other feeds the filler rod, and the foot pedal sets the current. PrimeWeld's pedal page calls it needing three hands. Each of these makers sells its pedals for its own welders only, and the plugs differ:

  • LOTOS: the TIG200ACDC comes with its pedal. LOTOS's separately sold FP05P ($89, 16 ft cable) is listed as fitting only the brown TIG200ACDCP. The listing says it is not compatible with the TIG200 or with any other brand. The pulse TIG200ACDCP ($449.98) ships with no pedal and no regulator. Adding the FP05P and the $28.99 AR03 regulator brings it to $449.98 + $89 + $28.99 = $567.97, against $399.98 for the TIG200ACDC kit.
  • YesWelder: the FP-5P-B is a 5-pin pedal, and the WP-26 torch uses a 5-pin control cable. Choose the '& Foot Pedal' option at checkout.
  • ARCCAPTAIN: one $79.99 pedal serves several models. It has a 5-pin (2+3) plug, a 10 ft cable and a 0–200 A range. The maker's compatibility table says yes for the TIG200, TIG200P AC/DC and TIG205 Pro, and no for the MIG205MP.
  • AHP: the NOVA pedal ($99) has a 7-pin plug and fits the AlphaTIG 203Xi and 225Xi. The NOVA wireless pedal is $429 on its own, or the 203Xi with a wireless pedal is $870.
  • PrimeWeld: the TIG225X pedal costs $125 and has a 7-pin plug and a 25 ft cable. PrimeWeld's $69 5-pin pedal is for the CT520D and CT520DP only. The AirLink wireless pedal is $295.

One more thing for aluminum beginners. LOTOS's spare tungsten kit for these machines (DTE10) is 2% thoriated and listed for DC TIG welding on steel. For aluminum, buy tungsten suited to AC welding.

Not recommended

Skip the bare YesWelder TIG-200P ACDC PRO option at $649.00. It is the same machine as the $599.15 '& Foot Pedal' option, just without the pedal. For steel and aluminum, also skip any DC-only welder, including the ARCCAPTAIN TIG200 linked above. It has no AC TIG, so it cannot weld aluminum, and it only makes sense if steel and stainless are all you plan to weld. PrimeWeld's TIG225X ($849) shows as unavailable on PrimeWeld's store today.
