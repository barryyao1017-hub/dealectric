Who it's for

Makers who want a 300 mm cube enclosed printer mainly for PLA, PETG and TPU with occasional ABS, and who value the LiDAR first-layer check. On the same store the K2 Pro without CFS was also $549 and adds a 60 °C heated chamber and CFS support, so it is the better buy for ABS, ASA and nylon; the QIDI Plus5 ($699) goes further with a 65 °C chamber, a 370 °C nozzle and 320 × 320 × 300 mm.

Caveats

  • There is no chamber heater; HowToMechatronics notes the K1 series relies on its enclosure alone and needs the lid off to keep PLA cool.
  • Tom's Hardware says it is not a quiet machine and not one to keep in a bedroom or beside a TV.
  • Tom's Hardware notes the smooth PEI plate needs glue stick and the rear-mounted spool holder means turning the printer around to load filament.
  • AI failure alerts work only through Creality Cloud; in LAN-only use Tom's Hardware found no automatic failure warning.
  • Bed flatness varies: Creality's own leveling guide treats a high-to-low mesh difference under 3 mm as normal, and one owner on Creality's forum measured 0.35 mm at 50 °C rising to 0.44 mm at 100 °C.
