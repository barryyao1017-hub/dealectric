What the differences mean

  • An 8-hour call day: the Shokz OpenComm2 (2025) finishes with 8 h of talk time left (16 h − 8 h). The Nuroum OpenEar Pro 2 finishes with 4 h left (12 h − 8 h). The Soundcore AeroClip 2 runs out 3 h early (8 h − 5 h) and has to go back in its case.
  • Boom headset vs clip-on: the OpenComm2 (2025) gives 3.2 times as many call hours per charge as the AeroClip 2 (16 h ÷ 5 h = 3.2).
  • The AeroClip 2's case: the 20 h call rating with the case adds up to 4 call-length charges (20 h ÷ 5 h = 4): one from the earbuds and 3 refills from the case ((20 h − 5 h) ÷ 5 h = 3).
  • Price per rated talk hour, one charge: OpenComm2 (2025) $10.00 ($159.95 ÷ 16 h). Nuroum $10.75 ($129.00 ÷ 12 h). OpenComm2 UC $12.50 ($199.95 ÷ 16 h). AeroClip 2 $34.00 ($169.99 ÷ 5 h), or $8.50 when you count the case ($169.99 ÷ 20 h).
  • What the UC's extra cost buys: $40.00 more ($199.95 − $159.95) gets you the Loop110 USB adapter. The talk rating is the same 16 h.
  • Cheapest boom headset with a dongle: the Nuroum costs $70.95 less than the OpenComm2 UC ($199.95 − $129.00).

Call hours are not music hours

Earbud battery claims are usually music playback. Soundcore's own comparison table does give a call figure for the AeroClip 2: 5 h per charge in call mode against 8 h in music mode, and 20 h against 32 h with the case. On calls you get 62.5% of the music rating (5 ÷ 8). The same Soundcore post mentions 10 to 11 h in another section. The table's 8 h / 32 h matches the rating Andy White quotes in his Expert Reviews test, so this page uses the table.

Boom headsets work the other way round. Shokz rates both the OpenComm2 (2025) and the OpenComm2 UC (2025) at 16 h of talk time. Nuroum rates the OpenEar Pro 2 at 12 h of talk and 15 h of music at 50% volume. In SoundGuys' test, Jasper Lastoria measured 17 h 59 min of playback on one charge from the earlier 2023 OpenComm2 UC (firmware 409-27). He did not test the 2025 model.

Makers rate the other earbuds in music or listening hours only. Shokz gives the OpenFit 2 48 h and the OpenDots 2 40 h, both counting the case. Soundcore gives the AeroFit 2 10 h of music per charge. Bose gives the Ultra Open Earbuds (2nd Gen) 9 h, or 5 h with Immersive Audio or Auto Volume. Treat each figure as a ceiling for calls: the AeroClip 2, the only clip-on here with a published call-mode figure, loses 37.5% of its music time on calls.

Microphone results point the same way. In SoundGuys' listener polls, 79% of 1,079 voters rated the 2023 OpenComm2 UC boom mic Good or Perfect, against 58% of 923 for the Soundcore AeroFit 2. Where the AeroClip 2 earns its place is the call itself. It uses 4 mics plus 2 voice pickup units on Anker's Thus AI chip. Guinness World Records certified it on 2026-08-31 for the highest objective G-MOS speech-quality score among open-ear clip-on earbuds. Andy White at Expert Reviews calls it "head and shoulders above their rivals in the call quality department". If your calls add up to less than 5 hours a day, it is the clip-on to buy: $169.99: https://dealectric.com/go/soundcore-aeroclip-2

Not recommended

The first-generation Soundcore AeroClip at the AeroClip 2's price. Soundcore's US store lists the original AeroClip at $169.99, the same as the AeroClip 2. Its listing describes "4 beamforming mics" and doesn't claim the Thus chip or the voice pickup units that the AeroClip 2's call claims depend on. Check that the listing says AeroClip 2 before you buy.
