Who it's for

Hobbyists who want to copy household parts, figures and medium objects for 3D printing without a $1,000 budget.

Caveats

  • Laser mode runs only in Revo Metro on a Windows PC; Revopoint lists an Nvidia RTX 3060 class GPU and 16 GB RAM, and phone scanning is structured light only.
  • Handheld tracking drops on plain or symmetrical shapes; reviewers call it marker-hungry on small, awkward parts and marker removal can leave holes.
  • Chrome, mirror finishes and clear plastic still need scanning spray even in laser mode.
  • Plan on 15 to 30 minutes of cleanup per scan before a mesh is printable.
