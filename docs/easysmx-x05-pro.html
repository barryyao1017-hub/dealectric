Who it's for

Anyone gaming on PC, Steam Deck or Switch in a shared room, dorm or next to a sleeping partner, and anyone with sound sensitivity who finds Xbox-style clicks grating.

Caveats

  • Does not work on Xbox or PlayStation consoles; PC, Steam Deck, Switch, Android and iOS only.
  • Battery is the most common complaint: 6 hours (HLPlanet) to 8 to 9 hours (The Outer Haven, Final Weapon) per charge.
  • GamingOnLinux (Nov 2025) found the triggers can misread light presses as full pulls after fast repeated presses in long-travel mode until pulled fully; a firmware tool exists on the support page, and no release notes confirm a fix.
  • Short trigger mode still travels about twice as far as a true hair trigger (Wccftech); bumpers feel mushy (Retro Handhelds).
  • Stick latency over the 2.4 GHz dongle measured about 36 ms (buttons about 10 ms) in Gamepadla's test; wired is faster for competitive shooters.
