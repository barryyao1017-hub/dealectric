What you pay for the panels in each kit

Each maker here also sells the station alone on the same store, so the kit price minus that bare price is what the panels cost you. Results on 2026-11-21, from cheapest to dearest per panel watt:

  • ALLPOWERS, $0.97/W. $649 + $379.05 = $1,028.05: the station plus the SP037 panel bought on its own costs $10 less than the $1,038.05 kit, so the bundle saves nothing at the store's own prices. The panel is still the cheapest per rated watt here. One caveat: the SP037 is polycrystalline, and ALLPOWERS's own S2000 Pro + SP037 listing says a 100 W polycrystalline panel 'can output about 55W-65W'. At 55%, the 400 W panel gives 220 W. That works out to $379.05 ÷ 220 = $1.72 per delivered watt and 1,920 ÷ 220 = 8.7 h of sun to refill. The kit is still the cheapest here, by $81.94 against the Anker SOLIX S2000 + PS400 ($1,119.99 − $1,038.05).
  • Anker, $1.18/W. S2000 + PS400: ($1,119.99 − $649.99) ÷ 400 W. C2000 Gen 2 + 400 W: ($1,319.99 − $849.99) ÷ 400 W. On 2026-11-08 these kits cost $1,699.99 and $1,999.99, which was $1.75 and $2.00/W. The S2000 kit price comes from a sale code that Anker's page applies by itself. If the PS400 delivers 75% of its rating (300 W), its $470 works out to $1.57 per delivered watt, less than the SP037's $1.72 at 55%. Amazon's price for the C2000 Gen 2 kit can differ from Anker's store, so compare the two before buying.
  • EcoFlow, $1.25–$1.35/W. $550 buys two 220 W bifacial panels, but that kit was sold out. The kit in stock adds two 100 W rigid panels for $270 ($1.35/W); rigid panels are framed panels for a roof or rack, not folding ones. Note the warranties EcoFlow lists for the 2 × 220 W kit: 12 months on the panels and 5 years on the DELTA 2 Max.
  • BLUETTI, $1.50/W. The SP200L costs $329.99 on its own, so two would be $659.98. The 2 × 200 W kit charges $600 for them, a $59.98 saving. The Elite 200 V2's 1,000 W rating applies to a single 12–60 V, 20 A input. With SP200L panels, three in series reach 3 × 24.6 V = 73.8 V, over the 60 V limit, so a series string tops out at two panels and 49.2 V. Even at the open-circuit voltage, 49.2 V × 20 A = 984 W, so these panels can't reach the full 1,000 W.
  • Jackery, $1.63–$1.90/W. The 2 × 200 W kit: ($1,649 − $999) ÷ 400 W = $1.63/W. The kit with 2 × 100 W Air panels rose from $1,239 on 2026-11-08 to $1,379: ($1,379 − $999) ÷ 200 W = $1.90/W.

Not recommended

Avoid the Jackery Solar Generator 2000 v2 with 2 × 100 W Air panels on Jackery's store. It costs $380 more than the bare Explorer 2000 v2 for 200 W of panels ($1.90/W, the dearest panel watt on this page), and it is a pre-order that ships by Dec. 20. At $1,379 it costs $340.95 more than the ALLPOWERS kit and carries half the panel watts. The station itself is sound (2,042 Wh, 2,200 W); the problem is the panel premium in this kit.
