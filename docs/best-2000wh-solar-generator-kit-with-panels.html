Panel watts decide how fast 2 kWh comes back

A folding panel gives about 70–80% of its rating, so one 200 W panel puts back about 150 W in full sun: roughly 14 hours of strong sun for 2,073.6 Wh, which is 2–3 days. Two 200 W panels halve that. The Elite 200 V2's 1,000 W solar input leaves room to add panels later; Jackery's 2000 v2 is listed by retailers at 400 W of input, so it stays near a full day of sun however many panels you add.

What 2 kWh runs

A full-size fridge (1.0–1.6 kWh a day) for about a day and a half on its own, or a fridge and a CPAP through a day and a night. A 2,600 W inverter starts most kitchen appliances and a window air conditioner, though the battery drains fast at those loads.
