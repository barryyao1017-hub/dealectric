Who it's for

Hobbyists and small shops whose main output is ABS, ASA or nylon parts up to about 10.5 inches square, on a budget under $500.

Caveats

  • PLA needs the lid and door open: QIDI says keep the chamber at or below 45 °C for PLA, and reviewers who printed PLA closed up got heat-creep clogs.
  • The optional QIDI Box jammed repeatedly for Tom's Hardware and Thomas Sanladerer; buy the printer alone unless you need multi-color.
  • Louder than a Bambu P1S: one reviewer measured 56 dB printing, and the idle fan is audible.
  • The camera runs at 640 × 480 and the QIDI Link app is poorly rated; the stock slicer profiles need tuning for PETG overhangs.
