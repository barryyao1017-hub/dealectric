Who it's for

Small to mid-size in-ground pools with a sunny, flat edge for the dock, owned by someone who doesn't want to carry a wet robot to an outlet.

Caveats

  • The sun charges it slowly. PCWorld measured more than 2.5 days from 19% to 100% in a Texas summer, and Android Authority saw 5-7% per hour of sun.
  • In PCWorld's test, a full floor-and-wall cycle ran out of battery before the floor was done. Once, the robot stopped in a corner and had to be pulled out with a pole.
  • WYBOT sells a dock power adapter ($19.99 on 2026-11-17) for charging from an outlet. We couldn't confirm whether one comes in the box.
  • The app drops its connection at times. To empty the basket at the dock you reach about a foot into the water.
  • The dock needs a flat, sunny pool edge where it sits flush with the wall.
