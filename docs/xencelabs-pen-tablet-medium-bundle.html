Who it's for

Photographers and retouchers editing on a 24 to 32 in monitor who want a dial and shortcut keys without paying Wacom Intuos Pro money.

Caveats

  • Wireless uses a USB dongle that needs a free USB-A port (adapter included), not Bluetooth.
  • The tablet itself has only 3 buttons; the 8 keys and dial live on the separate Quick Keys remote.
  • Several reviewers hit a Quick Keys dial that stopped responding in Photoshop or needed a driver reinstall; the driver also conflicts with an installed Wacom driver.
  • No touch input; plan to keep a mouse or trackpad for scrolling.
  • The v2 bundle sells for more and adds v2 pens and HP Anyware support only; the original bundle is the better buy for photo editing.
