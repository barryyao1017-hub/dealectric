Who it's for

Phone-and-laptop travelers who want one block that charges a 13-inch MacBook Air or similar laptop through the built-in cable and still has two spare ports.

Caveats

  • The built-in cable tops out at 60 W; the full 65 W only comes from the separate USB-C port with your own 5 A cable.
  • Power sharing is not dynamic: plugging anything into the USB-A port drops the USB-C port to 7.5 W (Macworld).
  • At 2.3 ft the cable reaches a nightstand, not a sofa; the Belkin 67W and ESR Xtend 45W reel out about a foot more.
  • The reel cannot be replaced if it fails; the two standard ports still work.
  • About 6.9 oz and cube-shaped, so it is heavier than UGREEN's slim Nexode Pro 65W and can sit loosely in worn outlets.
