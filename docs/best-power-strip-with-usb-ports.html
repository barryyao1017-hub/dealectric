What owners complain about

Across retailer reviews, deal-forum threads and brand support pages, the same four problems repeat.

  • USB ports die while the outlets keep working. The most frequent failure, reported on Anker, UGREEN, Baseus and store-brand strips, often within a few months to two years. Anker even has a support article for it. Buy from a maker that states a warranty term and keep the receipt.
  • USB is slower than the box implies. Many strips label a 5 V, 3 A port as USB-C fast charging. That is 15 W. Others share one budget across ports, so the 20 W or 35 W headline drops to 12 to 15 W when a second device plugs in. Look for a per-port Power Delivery rating.
  • Warranty claims go unanswered. One UGREEN strip reviewer and several owners said port failures inside the 12-month term got no support. TESSAN's 12 to 24 month coverage and 30 business day defect window are at least written down.
  • Surge protection expires silently. MOVs wear out with each spike. Pick a strip with a protection indicator light and replace the strip when it goes dark.

Which one by need

  • Desk or nightstand, phones and tablets: the TESSAN PS07. Two 20 W USB-C ports, 2,100 J, flat plug that hides behind furniture.
  • Entertainment center or many wall-wart plugs: the TESSAN 14-in-1 Tower. Eleven outlets on a small footprint and a cETLus listing. Accept the lower 1,500 J rating and one 20 W port.
  • Laptop without a brick, or travel: the TESSAN 65 W GaN Desktop Hub for one laptop at $29.99 with no surge protection. For two laptops, the Amazon-only Anker Prime 6-in-1 at 140 W. Cruise lines generally ban surge-protected strips, and some ban any corded strip, so check your line before packing either.
  • Twelve outlets and USB is an afterthought: the Anker 351 is a fine surge strip, but its single USB-C drops to a 15 W shared budget when either USB-A port is in use.

Not recommended

  • Any strip whose USB-C spec reads 5 V, 3 A or omits the wattage. That is 15 W at best and often shared.
  • A non-surge hub as the only protection for a desktop PC or TV. The 65 W hub and both Anker charging stations are outlet expanders, not surge protectors.
  • Strips with no stated warranty term. USB port failure is the number one complaint, and the term decides whether you get a replacement.
