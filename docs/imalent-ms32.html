Who it's for

Search and rescue, large properties, farms, shorelines and anyone who needs to light a whole field for half an hour. It is not a carry light.

Caveats

  • The 200,000 lm burst lasts about 45 seconds. The Gadgeteer measured the bezel going from 70 °F to 138 °F in about 40 seconds.
  • It weighs 6.83 lb, and a Budget Light Forum reviewer found the strap attachment too weak for that weight.
  • Rated IP56: fine in rain, not for dunking. The fans can't be serviced by the user and the battery pack is proprietary.
  • Battery and charger warranty is 1 year. Trustpilot reviewers of the IMALENT store report slow support on battery replacements.
  • Color rendering is low (about 70 CRI, per ZeroAir).
