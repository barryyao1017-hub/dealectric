What parking mode adds to the price

A dash cam on the 12 V socket records while the car powers that socket. For recording while parked, the makers here tell you to hardwire. Vantrue's Element 1 Lite listing says: "The dash cam should be hardwired to use the parking mode. (Hardwire kit sold separately)". 70mai's A400 listing says parking surveillance needs a "70mai hardwire kit". For the S1 Pro's parking power, BlackboxMyCar says to use a compatible Vantrue ACC hardwire kit or another manufacturer-supported continuous supply. The kit feeds the camera from the car battery and cuts power before the battery runs low: Vantrue's $29.99 Voltage Display kit cuts off at 11.6, 12.0 or 12.4 V as you set it, and its $24.99 universal kit at 11.6 V.

  • Vantrue: kit sold separately. The Voltage Display Hardwire Kit is $29.99 (list $34.99) and names the S1 Pro and the E1 Lite. The Universal 11.5ft ACC Hardwire Kit is $24.99 (list $29.99); its listing says it is for Vantrue USB-C dash cams and does not name the S1 Pro.
  • DDPAI: sells camera-plus-kit variants. Its Hardwire Kit with Fuse Tap, "For All DDPAI Dash Cams (Excluding Z100 Master)", is $29.99 on its own.
  • 70mai: the A400 has a micro-USB power port, so it takes the UP02 kit ($28.99). 70mai's USB-C kit, the UP03, does not fit it.

On footage, The Hook Up compared the 2-channel S1 Pro with the VIOFO A119 Mini 2 in 2024: by day the two were "almost identical", the Vantrue was "ever so slightly better" on a passing car's plate, and at night "the Viofo's HDR seemed to be better tuned for capturing plates". The unit in that test was the 2-channel kit; the camera in this table is the front-only 1CH.

Where the line was drawn. A camera is in the table if its price paid was $100.00 or less on 2026-11-21. The VIOFO A119 Mini 2 was left out: it was $159.99 on VIOFO's US store that day, the only seller we read for it. The Hook Up listed it at $98.99 in December 2024 and at $85 in December 2025, so it is worth a look when a seller has it at $100 or less. DDPAI's N3 Pro GPS (Upgraded) was $99.00 alone and $109.00 with its hardwire kit, so its kit variant was left out for being just over.

Not recommended for parked recording: 70mai A400

At $39.99 (70mai.store, 2026-11-21) the A400 is the lowest-priced camera in this table and suits driving footage: 2560 × 1440 at the front, and the $19.99 RC09 adds a 1080p rear camera. For parked recording it is a trap on three counts. Its only hardwire kit, the micro-USB UP02 ($28.99), was shown as unavailable on 70mai's store on 2026-11-21. It keeps a 500 mAh battery, where the Vantrue S1 Pro 1CH and the DDPAI N2 use a supercapacitor. And its card limit is 128 GB, against 512 GB for the two Vantrue cameras and 256 GB for the DDPAI N2. 70mai's spec page also lists no built-in GPS.
