Privacy policy

Dealectric is a static informational website. We do not require accounts, we do not sell personal information, and we collect as little as the service needs. Effective October 21, 2026.

Who we are

Dealectric (https://dealectric.com) is operated by Barry Yao, Sunnyvale, California, USA. Contact: hello@dealectric.com.

What we collect

Server and hosting logs
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. Both record the IP address, user-agent string, referring page, requested URL and timestamp of each request, as all web servers do. We use these in aggregate to understand traffic (including which AI assistants read the site) and to detect abuse. Logs are retained for up to 30 days.
Outbound link clicks
When you follow a /go/ link we record the destination, the page you came from, the link's query string (for example utm_source), a timestamp, your user-agent string, your country and a random click identifier that is passed to the merchant's affiliate network so a resulting purchase can be attributed to Dealectric. We do not store your IP address. We do not receive your name, address or payment details from the merchant — only that a commission-eligible order occurred.
Arrivals from tagged links
When you open one of our pages through a link whose address carries a utm_source tag (ChatGPT adds utm_source=chatgpt.com to the links it shows), we record the page, the link's query string, a timestamp, the referring page, your user-agent string and your country, so we can tell which assistants send readers and whether those readers find what they came for. We do not store your IP address, and the page you get is the same as without the tag.
Feedback
If you (or an assistant acting for you) send something to /feedback, we keep what was sent — the message, the optional page, assistant name and contact fields — with a timestamp, user-agent string and country, for as long as it is useful to act on. Nothing you send is published.
Email
If you email us we keep the correspondence to respond to it.
Analytics
We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-free service that reports aggregate page views without identifying individuals.

What we do not collect

No accounts, no newsletter list, no advertising trackers, no cross-site profiling, no sale or sharing of personal information for advertising.

Cookies

Dealectric itself sets no cookies. After you click an affiliate link, the merchant and its affiliate network (CJ Affiliate or Awin) may set cookies on their domains to attribute your purchase; those are governed by their privacy policies.

Your rights

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you may request to know what personal information we hold about you and to have it deleted. We hold only server logs, the click, arrival and feedback records described above (none stores your IP address) and email correspondence, so requests are typically fulfilled by deleting any email thread or feedback message that identifies you. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CPRA, so there is no opt-out to exercise. EEA/UK residents (GDPR): our legal basis for log processing is legitimate interest (site operation and security); you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection. To exercise any right, email hello@dealectric.com.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new effective date.
