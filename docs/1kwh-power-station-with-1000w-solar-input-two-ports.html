What the differences mean

  • Refill time in full sun. At 1,000 W of solar: 1,024 Wh ÷ 1,000 W = 1.02 h before losses. StorageReview measured 1,180 Wh going in for a 0–100% charge of the DELTA 3 Plus: 1,180 Wh ÷ 1,000 W ≈ 71 min, in line with EcoFlow's 70-minute solar claim. BLUETTI also claims 70 minutes for the Elite 100 V2 at 1,000 W.
  • 650 W input. ALLPOWERS P1800: 1,024 Wh ÷ 650 W = 1.58 h before losses.
  • 500 W input. EcoFlow DELTA 3: one 500 W input, which EcoFlow says 'recharges in 2 hours'.
  • Voltage floor. DELTA 3 Plus, per input: 500 W ÷ 15 A = 33.3 V, which is 56% of the 60 V ceiling. Elite 100 V2: 1,000 W ÷ 20 A = 50 V, which is 83% of 60 V and leaves a 10 V window.
  • Below the floor. Take a string working at 40 V. On the Elite 100 V2 it delivers at most 20 A × 40 V = 800 W. Each DELTA 3 Plus input could take 15 A × 40 V = 600 W, so it still reaches its 500 W cap.

Which panels fill each input: the 33.3 V and 50 V floors

The DELTA 3 Plus manual rates each solar input at 11–60 V, 15 A and 500 W, with 1,000 W across both ports. It also says to keep total short-circuit current within 15 A. EcoFlow's support FAQ sets the rule for using a panel: 'All three conditions must be met; exceeding any one of them means the solar panels cannot be used.'

EcoFlow's FAQ works through two 400 W panels rated 37.1 V open-circuit and 12.9 A. Wired in series they reach 74.2 V, past the 60 V ceiling. Wired in parallel they reach 25.8 A, past 15 A. So each input takes one panel of that class, and 1,000 W in total needs a roughly 500 W panel or string on each input, working at 33.3 V or more.

The Solar Lab tested the DELTA 3 Plus with Serious 415 W bifacial panels on both 500 W solar plugs and reports 'really close to' 1,000 W combined. buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DCC2BVFW?tag=dealectric-20

The XT60i ports also take car charging (12 V, 8 A: 12 × 8 = 96 W, per the manual). With a car cable plugged in, only one 500 W solar input is free. When AC and solar are connected together, the station uses solar first by default.

The Elite 100 V2 has one XT input rated 12–60 V and 20 A (BLUETTI, Notebookcheck). Notebookcheck writes that the single input 'makes reaching the advertised 1,000 W input difficult in practice'. Notebookcheck notes that a BLUETTI PV350 panel's 46.5 V open-circuit voltage makes series wiring impractical under the 60 V limit (two in series: 93 V). It puts a two-panel setup at around 700 W and finds that five 200 W panels don't reach 1,000 W either. BLUETTI caps AC and DC charging together at 1,200 W.

Not recommended

EcoFlow DELTA 3, if you want 1,000 W of solar. It shares the DELTA 3 Plus's name but has one input of 11–60 V, 15 A and 500 W (EcoFlow FAQ). That is half the Plus's solar input, and EcoFlow's own refill claim is 2 hours instead of 70 minutes.
