What the differences mean

All four banks charge at up to 25 W wirelessly and carry a 10,000 mAh label. The measurements separate them more than the labels do.

  • What the fan buys. Macworld timed the Anker MagGo 2 Pro on an iPhone 16 Pro at 30 minutes to 50% with the fan running and 33 minutes in Silent Mode: 33 − 30 = 3 minutes. Against the fanless Baseus on the same phone and tester: 36 − 30 = 6 minutes.
  • What those minutes cost. $109.99 − $55.99 (Baseus with built-in cable, Baseus US store, 2026-11-11) = $54.00 more for the Anker, or $54.00 ÷ 6 = $9.00 per minute saved to 50%. The Baseus without the cable was $59.99 the same day.
  • Why the Baseus scores lower on capacity. Macworld lists it at 36 Wh against 39.2 Wh for the other three. Score per Wh is level: 182 ÷ 36 = 5.06 for the Baseus and 199 ÷ 39.2 = 5.08 for the Anker. The gap is the smaller cell (36 ÷ 39.2 = 0.92), not a less efficient pack.
  • Price per full iPhone refill (maker price ÷ Macworld score): Baseus $55.99 ÷ 1.82 = $30.76; INIU SnapGo Air $54.99 ÷ 1.85 = $29.72; Anker $109.99 ÷ 1.99 = $55.27; UGREEN MagFlow Pro $149.99 ÷ 2.08 = $72.11.
  • Heat. Notebookcheck measured a 36.4 °C peak on the fanless Baseus. Anker's own claim for its fan-cooled pack is below 36 °C (96.8 °F). Techlicious recorded the INIU SnapGo Air never above 95 °F (35 °C). These are three different testers and rooms, and Notebookcheck does not state its room temperature, so read them as a range of 35 to 36.4 °C and not as a ranking.

Measured speed and heat, collected

  • Macworld (Simon Jary), iPhone 16 Pro, wireless 0–50%: Baseus PicoGo AM52 36 minutes (review of 2026-03-02); Anker MagGo 2 Pro about 30 minutes with active cooling and 33 minutes in Silent Mode (review of 2026-09-25). The review says the fan is audible close up and gives no decibel or temperature reading.
  • Macworld, UGREEN MagFlow Pro (2026-09-29): 30 minutes to 50% at 25 W against 45 minutes at 15 W, and a 208% recharge score, the highest it has recorded from a 10,000 mAh bank. The review does not name the test iPhone.
  • Notebookcheck (Benedikt Winkel), Baseus AM52: 10% to 80% in 40 minutes on both an iPhone 16 and an iPhone 17 Pro; Baseus's Qi2 PicoGo AM41 needed 90 minutes for the same charge on the iPhone 16. Peak temperature 36.4 °C. Refilling the bank took about 100 minutes.
  • Macwelt (Thomas Bergbold, 2026-04-22), Baseus AM52, iPhone 17 Pro, 30 minutes: 62% over USB-C, and between about 21% and 44% over MagSafe. Macwelt attributes the swing to the protection circuit cutting power early, which it also credits for the low heat. This is the caution on the pick: its wireless speed varied from run to run in that test, and the cable is the consistent route when time matters.
  • Techlicious (2026-10-02), INIU SnapGo Air: about 30 to 32 Wh reached the phone from a 39.2 Wh battery (30 ÷ 39.2 = 77%, 32 ÷ 39.2 = 82%), surface never above 95 °F, bank refill about 1 hour 48 minutes. Instruments: ChargerLAB POWER-Z KM003C, FNIRSI FNB58 and an infrared thermometer.
  • Maker claims, for contrast: Anker says the MagGo 2 Pro takes an iPhone 17 Pro from 0 to 50% in about 25 minutes. INIU says its $44.99 SnapGo Compact (INIU store, 2026-11-11) takes an iPhone 17 Pro to 50% in 33 minutes and runs under 104 °F. Macworld's independent Anker result was 30 minutes on the older iPhone 16 Pro. We read no named-reviewer timing for the SnapGo Compact, so it stays out of the table.

Not recommended

UGREEN MagFlow Pro at $149.99. It has the best Macworld score (208%) and matches the Anker's 30 minutes to 50%, but at UGREEN's US store price on 2026-11-11 it costs $149.99 − $109.99 = $40.00 more than the Anker for that same 30 minutes, and $149.99 − $55.99 = $94.00 more than the Baseus for 208 − 182 = 26 more points of an iPhone. It also refills at 30 W, like the Baseus. It becomes a fair buy only at a much lower price.

UGREEN MagFlow 10000mAh Qi2 25W (non-Pro) at $79.99. UGREEN's US store listed it at $79.99 on 2026-11-11. Cult of Mac (David Snow) gives the MagFlow with the 30 W built-in cable as 254 g and 21 mm, heavier and thicker than all four banks in the table, and for 0–50% wireless it gives only UGREEN's claim of about 30 minutes on an iPhone 16 Pro Max.

Any 25 W bank for a phone that takes 15 W. By Anker's and INIU's own statements above, Android phones, the iPhone 15 and earlier, and the iPhone 16e and 17e do not get 25 W from these banks, so the fan and the Qi2.2 premium add nothing for them.
