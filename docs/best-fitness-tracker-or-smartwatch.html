Fitness tracker or smartwatch? The honest pick for each kind of buyer

Updated 2026-11-06

Short answer. The best fitness tracker or watch for most people is the Amazfit Active 2 (Round) ($99.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/amazfit-active-2-round): a 44 mm watch rated for up to 10 days of battery on a charge, 21 hours of continuous GPS, 5 ATM water resistance and a 1.32-inch AMOLED screen, with the Zepp app free and no subscription. It sits between a band and a full smartwatch, which is where most people asking this question end up. The complaint that decides this choice is charging: owners who moved from a band to a full smartwatch report 1 to 2 days per charge and say they regret it, and the Apple Watch SE 3 is rated for 18 hours. A band or an Amazfit lasts a week or more.

If you only want steps, heart rate and sleep in the smallest thing you can wear overnight, the Fitbit Inspire 3 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B5F9SZW7?tag=dealectric-20) is the honest band pick: rated for up to 10 days, but it has no GPS of its own and locks some insights behind Fitbit Premium. If you want to reply to texts, pay from your wrist and run apps, you need a true smartwatch: the Apple Watch SE 3 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HJB73N19?tag=dealectric-20) for iPhone owners, accepting a daily charge. Cheapest route to GPS and a big screen is the Amazfit Bip 6 ($79.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/amazfit-bip-6), rated for up to 14 days.

What owners complain about

Daily charging is the top complaint in every tracker-versus-smartwatch thread we read. On a Blind thread, two of four replies picked a band or fitness watch because it needs charging every 10 to 15 days, and one owner said a smartwatch at about 2 days per charge was a downgrade they regretted. A Digital Trends writer dropped a Galaxy Watch that lasted 1.5 days for a sports watch that lasts a week. Second is unreliable notifications: a Reddit thread on the Amazfit Active 2 had owners on iPhone and Android losing notifications until a Bluetooth toggle, with Amazfit promising a firmware fix. Third is Fitbit firmware trouble: we counted at least 8 separate Fitbit community threads from 2025 and 2026 on the Charge 6 losing sync or dropping from about 6 days of battery to about 1 after update 220.31. Fourth is paywalls: three Inspire 3 reviews flag that some sleep and readiness insights need Fitbit Premium at $9.99 a month. Fifth, for the Active 2, two reviewers called GPS accuracy so-so against a Garmin Forerunner while three others found it matched other watches; it lacks topographic maps and runs breadcrumb navigation only.

Which one by need

Week-long battery plus GPS, any phone: Amazfit Active 2 (Round). Rated 10 days typical, 19 days in saver mode, 21 hours of GPS, 29.5 g without strap, 5 ATM. Calls work over Bluetooth. The US standard model has no NFC, so no wrist payments. Smallest band for steps and sleep: Fitbit Inspire 3. Up to 10 days, water resistant to 50 m, but no GPS and a tiny screen. Band with GPS and Google Wallet: Fitbit Charge 6, but read the firmware section above before buying. Full smartwatch, iPhone: Apple Watch SE 3. Apple lists it from $249 and rates it at 18 hours, so plan on charging every night or every other night; reviewers who track workouts saw about a day and a half. Full smartwatch, Android: price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F7PX6RSB?tag=dealectric-20 if you want replies and payments; a Garmin vívoactive 6 if you want 11 days of battery and Garmin Pay and can live without typed replies on iPhone. Biggest screen for the least money: Amazfit Bip 6 at 14 days rated, with built-in GPS.

Not recommended

No-name bands from marketplace sellers: Which? found some put your data at risk and over- or under-count steps. Fitbit Charge 6 until Google settles the 220.31 firmware sync and battery reports. An Amazfit Active 2 Premium bought for wrist payments in the US: Zepp Pay has not launched here as of 2026-11-06. Any full smartwatch if your real reason for buying is sleep tracking: you will have to take it off to charge at exactly the time you want it on.
