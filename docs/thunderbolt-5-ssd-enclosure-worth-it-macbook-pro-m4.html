Which Macs have Thunderbolt 5

Apple's M4 Pro and M4 Max announcement (October 2024) says those chips bring Thunderbolt 5 to the Mac; the 14-inch MacBook Pro with the base M4 lists three Thunderbolt 4 ports at 40 Gb/s. The M4 Pro and M4 Max MacBook Pro, the Mac mini with M4 Pro and the Mac Studio with M4 Max have Thunderbolt 5 at "up to 120 Gb/s": 80 Gb/s each way for data, with the 120 Gb/s Bandwidth Boost reserved for displays. So storage sees 80 Gb/s at most, about 6,000 MB/s after overhead, against about 3,000–4,000 MB/s on Thunderbolt 4 and USB4. The M5 generation keeps the same split.

Heat and the fan question

A Thunderbolt 5 bridge plus a Gen4 SSD runs hot in a hand-sized shell. Macwelt measured UGREEN's unit at 34 °C idle and 40 °C under a stress test with no throttling and a fan it called quiet but not silent; Neowin's fanless TerraMaster peaked at 39 °C; a reviewer of the fanless ORICO saw the drive at 58–68 °C over a 13-minute write. No head-to-head shows fan or fins winning; a fan model is the safer buy for copies of hundreds of gigabytes, which is why the fan UGREEN is the pick here at the same $159.99 after the code.
