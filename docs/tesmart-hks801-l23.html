Who it's for

A homelab rack of servers or mini PCs used at 1080p or 1440p, especially with a PiKVM in front for remote access.

Caveats

  • TESmart's title rates it at 4K 30 Hz. Buy the HKS801-P23 if you need 4K 60 Hz.
  • Behind a PiKVM, 3 of 3 owners in one Techno Tim thread hit EDID or black-screen problems. They fixed them with an EDID adapter, an HDMI splitter, or by removing the 4K emulators.
  • The default IP is 192.168.1.10 with no DHCP. You change it with TESmart's Windows utility, and the change takes effect only after a power cycle (PiKVM docs).
  • USB 2.0 only. Fine for keyboards and mice, slow for drives.
  • The price is a sale price; TESmart's list price is higher.
