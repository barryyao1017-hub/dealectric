How many charges a 20,000mAh power bank really gives

mAh is rated at the cells' 3.7 V: 20,000 mAh is 74 Wh and 25,000 mAh is 91 Wh. About 80–85% reaches the device after conversion, so a 20,000 mAh bank gives roughly 4 charges of a phone with a 15 Wh battery and a 25,000 mAh bank about 5.

Watts: what 30, 45, 65 and 140 W buy you

  • 20–30 W: full-speed charging for any phone; a laptop only slowly.
  • 45–65 W: a MacBook Air, a Steam Deck at full speed, most 13–14 in laptops.
  • 100–140 W: a MacBook Pro at fast-charge speed, or a laptop and a phone at once.
