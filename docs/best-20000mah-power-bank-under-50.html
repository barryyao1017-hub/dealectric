What happens under sustained load

A 20,000 mAh label describes the cells at 3.7 V (about 74 Wh). Converting that to USB voltage loses some energy, and heat makes a small bank cut its output. A wattage on the box tells you the peak, not how long the bank holds it.

  • INIU Carry 45W: Techtest charged a Framework 16 laptop from it. It started at about 40 W and dropped to 17 W after about 30 minutes (near 60% remaining). It then climbed back to 40 W and dropped again. Techtest puts the bank's throttled output at about 27 W, depending on the device. Barton rates it unsuitable, or only partly suitable, for laptops. A full recharge of the bank took about 4 hours at up to 27 W.
  • INIU Cougar 65W: Macworld's Simon Jary got 87% into a flat M2 MacBook Pro. In the same test the Baseus EnerCore CR11 reached 95% and a Cuktech 45 W bank 78%. With both USB-C ports in use the Cougar splits 45 W + 20 W, and USB-C 2 plus USB-A share 15 W. It recharges at 45 W in under two hours (Macworld).
  • Baseus EnerFill FC11: Baseus says the output is shared when several devices charge. Notebookcheck notes that Baseus has not said how power is split across outputs, and no bench test of its capacity has been published yet.

No 20,000 mAh bank from UGREEN's US store comes in under $50. The cheapest was a 100 W bundle at $60.22, and the cheapest single unit was the Nexode 20K 130W at $67.99 (checked 2026-11-08). INIU's own 22.5 W Carry (P512) costs $32.99, more than the 45 W Carry, and has no built-in cable.

Not recommended

Anker Zolo 20K 30W, built-in USB-C (A1689). In Techtest's bench test it delivered 58.83–68.12 Wh (80–92% of rated), the lowest here. It dropped from 30 W to 20 W after about 25% of discharge, with the case above 70 °C. As soon as a second port is used it turns off fast charging and shares 5 V × 3 A = 15 W across all ports, half its rated 30 W. It took about 5 hours to refill. The A1689 is also part of Anker's recall of about 481,000 power banks over fire and burn hazards, which covers units sold from August 2023 to June 2025 (Notebookcheck). The UK notice covers A1689 units made June 2024 to February 2025. If you own one, check the serial number at anker.com/rc2506 before using it.
