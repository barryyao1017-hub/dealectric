Who it's for

Suits solo creators with a light mirrorless body and a short zoom who also want one gimbal for a phone or action camera. Anyone with a full-frame body and an f/2.8 zoom should look at FeiyuTech's SCORP 3 ($299 on FeiyuTech's store, 3.5 kg payload) instead.

Caveats

  • Digital Camera World's George Cairns (July 2025, tested with iPhones only) found the AI tracker left too much headroom and overrode his own joystick framing.
  • With a camera mounted it tracks faces only, started by hand gesture; picking another object to track works only with a phone in the Feiyu ON app, and only one subject at a time (FeiyuTech FAQ).
  • FeiyuTech limits lenses to 90 mm long and 75 mm wide for proper balance; Insta360 Ace Pro 2 can be stabilized but not controlled.
  • Store reviewers report a learning curve for balancing and thin setup instructions, and one says the phone mount clashes with iPhone 18 Pro buttons.
