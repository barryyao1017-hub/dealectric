Who it's for

Caveats

  • Smartzone measured 4-7 days at 8 hours a day with the screen and RGB on; up to 20 days with both off.
  • Plate flex can cause phantom releases with rapid trigger below about 0.3 mm; HLPlanet suggests 0.4 mm.
  • Turn rapid trigger off on any key fitted with a mechanical switch, or it can double-type.
  • The software is English only and less polished than Wooting's (HLPlanet, Smartzone).
