Who it's for

Homes with a working 120 V primary sump pump that need cover for both outages and a failed main pump.

Caveats

  • The battery is sold separately. For its ESP25 system, Wayne recommends its WSB1275 75 Ah battery.
  • Wayne's ESP25 manual says the system will not function without check valves on both pump discharge lines. The check valve is sold separately.
  • The charger needs a separately fused, GFCI-protected 115 VAC outlet, and the battery must sit within six feet of the sump.
