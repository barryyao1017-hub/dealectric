What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test units. Every measured figure above comes from the named reviewer or lab; maker figures are labelled as such, and the sums below are our arithmetic.

  • Standby loss per day: Apex 300: 20 W × 24 h = 0.48 kWh. Anker SOLIX E10: 40 W × 24 h = 0.96 kWh. Jackery 5000 Plus: 58 W × 24 h = 1.39 kWh. Original F3800: 80 W × 24 h = 1.92 kWh. Without solar, that energy is bought from the grid every day the inverter stays on, and during an outage it comes out of the reserve.
  • Days on idle before empty: Apex 300: 2,765 Wh ÷ 20 W = 138 h, about 5.8 days (The Solar Lab says about 6). Jackery 5000 Plus: 5,040 Wh ÷ 58 W = 87 h, about 3.6 days.

Standby drain and delivered energy: why they decide a no-solar backup

A grid-charged battery fills up while the power is on, then sits ready. Two things eat into it before and during an outage: the inverter's own idle draw, and the losses between the label capacity and what reaches the outlets. With solar, a sunny day hides both. Without solar, nothing refills the battery until the grid returns, so every watt of idle draw and every percent of conversion loss is runtime gone.

The Solar Lab measured about 20 W idle for the BLUETTI Apex 300, about 40 W for the Anker SOLIX E10, about 58 W for the Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus and 80 W for the original Anker F3800; ESS News (Tristan Rayner, 2025-09-17) measured 19.5 W for the Apex 300 with its inverters on. On delivered energy, OutdoorGearLab (Kenji Mowrey, 2026-01-13) got 3,790 Wh out of the DELTA Pro 3, and Outdoor Life (Laura Lancaster, 2025-05-20) got 2,290 Wh out of the Apex 300 under a dehumidifier load. For the installed systems, The Solar Lab's 40 W idle and 87% efficiency on the E10 are the only measurements so far: no reviewer has published a discharge test for the E10, or a standby or discharge measurement for the FranklinWH aPower 2 or Tesla Powerwall 3.

Two practical checks before ordering: FranklinWH states its system charges from the grid off-peak and switches over automatically with no solar attached; for a Powerwall 3, ask the installer whether grid charging will be permitted on your utility, since it can be set to restricted.
