What the differences mean

  • Cost per rated Wh: AC180 $499 ÷ 1,152 Wh = $0.43; DELTA 3 Classic $459 ÷ 1,024 Wh = $0.45; Elite 30 V2 $289.99 ÷ 288 Wh = $1.01; Explorer 1500 Ultra $1,079 ÷ 1,536 Wh = $0.70.
  • Idle draw costs the most when the load is small: The Solar Lab measured 17 W of idle draw on the DELTA 3 Classic. A 20 W LED string on it runs 1,024 ÷ (20 + 17) = 27.7 h, not the 1,024 × 0.83 ÷ 20 = 42.5 h that capacity times efficiency suggests.

Why string lights go dark halfway through the night

Power stations save battery by turning off outlets that carry little or no load, and LED lights draw very little.

  • BLUETTI AC180: the manual says that in ECO mode “the AC/DC output will automatically turn off if the AC180 is bearing low or no load for a while.” You set the AC threshold to 10–30 W (DC: 5–10 W) and the timer to 1, 2, 3 or 4 hours. smartzone.de reports that ECO is on by default. To switch it off, hold the AC and DC buttons together for 2 seconds while the screen is on and turn ECO off in the menu that opens, or turn it off in the BLUETTI app. With ECO off the outlets stay on until you switch them off. $499 on bluettipower.com, $474.05 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/bluetti-ac180
  • Jackery Explorer 600 Plus: the manual says Energy Saving Mode is on by default. An AC load of 25 W or less counts as low power, and the outputs turn off after 12 hours. Separately, the whole unit shuts down after 2 hours with no charging or discharging; you can change that time in the Jackery app. To turn Energy Saving Mode off, hold the AC and Main Power buttons until its icon disappears.
  • Plug-in timers and photocells: a timer that cuts the lights during the day leaves the outlet with no load for hours. On the manuals' wording, that zero load can run out the auto-off countdown before dusk. Turn auto-off off before relying on a timer.
  • Cold nights: the AC180 is rated to discharge from -4°F to 104°F, and the Explorer 600 Plus from 14°F to 113°F.
  • Rain: Jackery's 600 Plus manual says not to keep the unit where rain or moisture can reach it. The Explorer 1500 Ultra is the IP65-rated exception.

For a single small string, the Elite 30 V2's 4.5 W standby wastes less than a large unit's inverter. $289.99 on bluettipower.com, $275.50 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/bluetti-elite-30-v2

Not recommended

A 286 Wh station such as the EcoFlow RIVER 3 Plus is too small for 50 W of lights over a 6-hour night. The Solar Lab measured its inverter at 87% efficiency, so 286 × 0.87 = 249 Wh, and 249 ÷ 50 = 5.0 h: the lights go out an hour before the night is over.
