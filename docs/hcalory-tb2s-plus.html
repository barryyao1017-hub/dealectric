Who it's for

Rooftop, wall and truck-bed tents where the heater sits outside, overnight use with a set temperature, and camps up to 18,000 ft.

Caveats

  • The case has mesh side vents and no water-resistance rating, so cover it in rain or snow.
  • The 8 kW rating is more heat than a small two-person tent needs, so expect it to cycle or run on low.
  • One tester got false CO shutdowns (error E11) from the remote and found its CO sensor slower than a standalone alarm.
  • The pump still ticks audibly, according to two reviewers, even though Hcalory calls it a silent pump.
  • On 2026-11-14 the US version could be ordered, but the page also showed a pre-order notice. Hcalory says US pre-orders ship in 15 to 30 business days.
