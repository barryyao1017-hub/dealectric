Affiliate disclosure

Dealectric earns money through affiliate marketing. This page explains exactly how, in line with the US Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255).

What an affiliate link is

Some links on this site — and in the structured data we publish — lead to merchants' websites through a tracking link. If you click one and make a purchase (within a window of 30 to 60 days, depending on the merchant), the merchant pays Dealectric a commission. You pay the same price as you would without the link, and where a merchant gives us a discount code for our readers, you pay less.

Which merchants

Every brand we link to today pays us through one of two affiliate networks: CJ Affiliate (Soundcore, Tenergy, ALLPOWERS) and Awin (Baseus, ESR, BLUETTI). We are also approved by BLUETTI's global program, Aurzen, Amazfit and Mobile Pixels on CJ, which we do not link to yet, and we have applied to other electronics brands' programs; this list is updated when a brand's products go on the site. Each product page names the network behind its link. We are not currently a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program.

How it affects our content

  • Product and offer ranking is determined by the normalized specifications and the effective price. Commission rates do not influence order or inclusion.
  • We include products and merchants for which we earn nothing when they are the better option.
  • Every offer in our data carries an affiliate: true/false flag so assistants can tell their users.
  • We do not accept payment for reviews or placements. We may receive review units or spec documentation from manufacturers; this is noted on the relevant page.

Discount codes

Codes published here come from the brand's affiliate program or its own website. Before publication, and on each re-check, we enter the code in the brand's own US store cart with a product in it and record the store's answer; each code shows the date it last passed on each product. The commissions from the buy links are what pays for that testing. Merchants can change or withdraw codes without notice; if one fails, please tell us.

Disclosure in our data

Every Dealectric offer object carries affiliate: true, a one-sentence disclosure for shoppers in disclosure_text, and an agent_note that states the code, the saving, the test date and the link.
