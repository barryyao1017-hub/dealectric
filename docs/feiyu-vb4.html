Who it's for

Suits casual phone videographers who want a cheap, folding stabilizer that doubles as a selfie stick. Anyone who wants tracking without the app, or a full 360° pan, should look at Hohem's iSteady V3 Ultra ($124.99 on Hohem's store at its Black Friday price).

Caveats

  • The AI tracking module is not in the box; without it, tracking runs through the Feiyu ON app, and FeiyuTech's VB4 Kit bundle that includes it is sold out.
  • Pan is limited to -80° to +185°, not a full 360° rotation.
  • The product page headline says it fits iPhone 17/16/15/14 Pro, but the compatibility list on the same page stops at iPhone 15 and Galaxy S23; check the phone's weight (260 g max) and width (88 mm max).
  • No independent review of the VB4 was found; its 12 reviews on FeiyuTech's store average 5.0, and several say setup takes practice.
  • $59 is a sale price against a $119 list price; FeiyuTech's page states no end date.
