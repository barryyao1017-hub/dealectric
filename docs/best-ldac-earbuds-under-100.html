What the differences mean

Every headline battery figure on these boxes is measured without LDAC. Switch LDAC on and the earbuds draw more power, but the loss differs a lot between models:

  • Soundcore Liberty 4 NC: 5 h with LDAC and ANC ÷ 8 h with ANC alone = 62.5%. Against the 10 h headline it is 5 ÷ 10 = 50%, and the total with the case falls from 50 h to 25 h (Soundcore).
  • Nothing Ear (3a): 4.5 h ÷ 6 h on AAC with ANC = 75%. Against its 10 h headline, 4.5 ÷ 10 = 45% (Nothing).
  • EarFun Air Pro 4: 6 h ÷ 7.5 h = 80% (Simon Cohen, Digital Trends).
  • EarFun Air Pro 4+: about 6 h ÷ 7.75 h on aptX (the midpoint of HiFi Oasis's 7.5–8 h) ≈ 77%.
  • Noise: SoundGuys measured 84% for the Liberty 4 NC against 80% for the Air Pro 4+ and 75% for the Air Pro 4, so the Liberty removes 4 points more outside noise than the 4+.

The Soundcore and Nothing figures are maker ratings at 50% volume. The EarFun figures come from reviews, so treat half an hour either way as a tie. On that basis the EarFun pair leads on LDAC runtime, the Liberty 4 NC leads on noise cancelling, and the Ear (3a) is the cheapest at Nothing's own store today.

What LDAC costs in battery and multipoint

Soundcore explains the battery loss itself: "In LDAC mode, high-speed transmission leads to increased power consumption." The same applies to every pair here. The makers that publish an LDAC figure show runtime with LDAC and ANC on falling to 39–50% of the headline. For the CMF Buds 2 Plus, Nothing rates 5.5 h per charge and 25 h with the case with LDAC and ANC on, against a 61.5 h headline that is AAC with ANC off: 25 ÷ 61.5 = 41%.

Multipoint. On the Liberty 4 NC, Soundcore says: "Once LDAC is enabled, the earbuds will automatically exit the multipoint connection." Simon Cohen at Digital Trends found the same on the EarFun Air Pro 4: "To use it, though, you'll need to disable LDAC." aptX Adaptive and aptX Lossless do work with multipoint there. The Air Pro 4+ is the exception in this group. Björn Alsborger at senses.se called "support for LDAC in Multipoint mode" a welcome addition, and Matthew Tjen at Loud and Wireless had LDAC "work even with multipoint pairing turned on". senses.se tested on preview firmware that received several updates, so install the latest firmware in the EarFun app after pairing.

Setup on the Liberty 4 NC. Soundcore says the factory firmware does not include LDAC. You add it in the soundcore app under Settings > Sound Mode > Preferred Audio Quality, which triggers a firmware update. It needs Android 8.0 or later, and on Samsung phones you may have to turn on the LDAC switch in Bluetooth settings.

Bitrate. SoundGuys notes that most Android phones default to LDAC's 660 kbps Best Effort mode, not the 990 kbps maximum. To get 990 kbps, set it in Android's developer options.

Not recommended

The SoundPEATS Capsule3 Pro+ is a poor fit for someone buying earbuds for LDAC. SoundGuys measured 3 h 53 min with ANC on, the shortest runtime among the LDAC pairs here, and that is before LDAC's extra drain. SoundGuys does rate its sound highly (4.9/5 MDAQS), so it suits short listening sessions, not a full workday.
