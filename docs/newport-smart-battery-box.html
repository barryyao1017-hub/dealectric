Who it's for

A single 12 V lead-acid or AGM battery powering a Newport 62 lb or Minn Kota 50–55 lb motor, which match the 60 A breaker. Smaller motors also need their own smaller breaker.

Caveats

  • Newport's store listing says it is not compatible with lithium batteries. Its FAQ says the LED meter is the part that cannot read lithium.
  • Newport specifies 40 A for its 36 lb motor and 50 A for its 40, 46, 55 and 86 lb motors, so those motors need their own breaker in addition to the box's 60 A one.
  • The USB port is rated 0.5 A at 5 V (2.5 W).
