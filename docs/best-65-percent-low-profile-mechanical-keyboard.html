What the differences mean

A 65% board has about 68 keys: it keeps the arrow keys and drops the function row, one step below the 84-key 75% layout. NuPhy's own table shows what that step buys and costs inside one family: the Air65 V3 (68 keys) is 109.0 mm deep against 128.9 mm for the Air75 V3 (84 keys) at the same 318.9 mm width, and 635 g against 724 g, but its battery falls from 4,000 to 2,500 mAh. Every row above is a low-profile board its maker sells as 65% or 68 keys. Dealectric does not test units; the ranking uses each maker's stated figures, read on 2026-11-26.

  • What the 65% size saves (NuPhy): 128.9 − 109.0 = 19.9 mm of desk depth; 724 − 635 = 89 g.
  • What it costs in battery (NuPhy): 2,500 / 4,000 = 62.5% of the 75% model's capacity; rated lights-off hours 761 against 1,200 (63%).
  • Same step at Lofree: 2,000 mAh in the Flow 2 68 against 3,000 mAh in the Flow 2 84 (67%); 90 h against 120 h backlight off (75%).

Battery ratings on the same footing

The two leading boards do not quote battery life on the same basis, so the headline numbers need reading with care.

  • NuPhy Air65 V3: 2,500 mAh; up to 761 h lights off and 15–67 h lights on. NuPhy labels these lab results, "measured under laboratory conditions with RGB lighting turned off", and does not name the connection mode. Its page also carries a "1000 hours" battery headline; its spec table gives the Air65 V3 761 hours, the figure used here.
  • Lofree Flow 2 68: 2,000 mAh; up to 90 h with the backlight off, stated for Bluetooth mode, and up to 13 h with the backlight on. Lofree gives no figure for the 2.4 GHz dongle.
  • Keychron K7 Pro: Keychron's US listing states the capacity (1,550 mAh) and no hours, so it has no rated figure to set beside the other two.

The lights-on figures are the closest to a like-for-like comparison, and there the gap narrows from 8.5 times to 15–67 h against 13 h. The loads still differ: RGB under non-shine-through keycaps on the NuPhy, a white backlight on the Lofree.

Measured results exist only for larger or different boards:

  • RTINGS tested the 75% Air75 V3 and the 100-key Flow 2, not the 68-key sizes. It found the NuPhy has "slightly better gaming performance overall" and the Lofree "somewhat better and more premium-feeling build quality". RTINGS expects its Air75 V3 results to apply to each size, but battery capacity is one thing that does not carry: the 65% has 62.5% of the cell.
  • Philipp Briel (basic-tutorials) got about 21 hours with the backlight on from the 100-key Flow 2, against Lofree's 17-hour claim for that size. That unit has a 3,000 mAh battery, not the 68's 2,000 mAh.
  • Notebookcheck measured drain on the Lofree Flow Lite84, a different 75% board with a 2,000 mAh battery: 1% per hour on Bluetooth with the backlight off (about 100 hours), 6% per hour on Bluetooth at maximum backlight (about 16.6 hours) and 10% per hour on 2.4 GHz at maximum backlight (about 10 hours). On that board the dongle drew 10 / 6 = 1.7 times as much as Bluetooth, which is why a Bluetooth-only rating should be read as the best case for dongle use.

Thickness is also stated on different bases: NuPhy gives 13.2 mm as overall thickness, and Lofree lists 21.5 mm as the third dimension without saying where it is taken, so the 8.3 mm difference is each maker's own figure, not a common measurement.

Not recommended

  • Keychron K7 Pro for dongle use or switch swapping. Keychron's spec line reads "Connectivity: Bluetooth" with no 2.4 GHz mode, and hot-swap is "for RGB Backlight Hot-Swappable Version only". It suits Bluetooth typing at a low price; check the variant before ordering.
  • Battery numbers borrowed from a 75% review. The Air75 V3's 4,000 mAh and 1,200 h, or the Flow 2 84's 3,000 mAh and 120 h, overstate the 68-key boards by a third or more.
  • Left out for missing the 65% line: the NuPhy Air60 V2 has a 64-key layout as NuPhy states it ($109.95 at NuPhy, 2026-11-26), and the NuPhy Air60 HE is wired only. The Keychron K7 Max did not come up as a keyboard listing in two searches of Keychron's US store on 2026-11-26 (only keycap sets naming it), so it has no row.
