Who it's for

For people who already have an AV receiver or stereo amp with a sub out and want to add or revive a passive subwoofer for the least money.

Caveats

  • Real output is lower than the box number, and the included 32 V supply limits peak power.
  • RCA line input only: a stereo amp with no sub out needs a different amp, such as one with speaker-level inputs.
  • Fosi's page lists no auto-on, so you switch it on and off by hand or from a switched outlet.
  • Rated for 4–8 Ω; check the impedance on older 3 Ω home-theater-in-a-box subs.
  • Audiophonics in France lists it as discontinued. The US Fosi store showed it in stock on 2026-11-10.
