What is the lightest 1,000 Wh LiFePO4 power station to carry for car camping? (2026)

Short answer. The lightest 1,000 Wh LiFePO4 power station for car camping is the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D7PPG25F?tag=dealectric-20): 23.8 lb for 1,070 Wh, the most capacity per pound of any current 1 kWh unit (45 Wh/lb), with a fold-flat handle and two solar inputs taking 400 W. What to know: these are the makers' weights; no reviewer has published a scale reading (OutdoorGearLab gives 24.4 lb for the Explorer 1000 v2). If 1.1 lb more is fine, the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FN7MSY4L?tag=dealectric-20, 24.9 lb) brings 2,000 W and a 49-minute charge. We found no lighter 1 kWh unit with NMC cells; Jackery's NMC Explorer 1000 Pro weighs more.

Why Wh per pound

For carrying from the car to a campsite, what matters is how much energy each pound buys. The Explorer 1000 v2 carries 1,070 Wh in 23.8 lb (45 Wh/lb); the 1,024 Wh units from Anker and BLUETTI land at 41, EcoFlow's DELTA 3 pair at 37 because of their larger inverters and chargers, and DJI and Dabbsson near 30. The spread between the lightest and heaviest 1 kWh unit is over 10 lb.

What the lightest gives up

The Explorer 1000 v2 has the lowest AC output here (1,500 W), takes the least solar (400 W, so OutdoorGearLab estimates 3–4 hours of good sun to refill), and needs its Emergency Charging mode switched on in the app to reach about an hour on the wall. It is not expandable. For camping loads (a 12 V fridge, lights, phones, a laptop, a CPAP) none of that matters; for a microwave, a kettle or fast solar refills, the C1000 Gen 2 or DELTA 3 Plus is worth the extra pound or four.

Weights we could not check

Every weight in the table is the maker's; no outlet published a scale reading of a 1 kWh unit. OutdoorGearLab's review quotes 24.4 lb for the Explorer 1000 v2, and BLUETTI's AC180 page gives 37 lb in its copy and 35.3 lb in its spec table. The Anker SOLIX C1000X (the original 1,056 Wh model) is left out: we could not confirm its weight or that it is still sold new.
