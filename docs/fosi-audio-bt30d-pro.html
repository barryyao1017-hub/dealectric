Who it's for

For people building or rescuing a 2.1 system (passive bookshelf speakers plus a passive sub, or the passive parts of an old home-theater-in-a-box) who want one amp with Bluetooth.

Caveats

  • Power ratings are inflated: AudioReviews.org estimated about 100 W to the sub on the stock 32 V supply.
  • The SUB FREQ knob has no Hz markings, so you set the crossover by ear.
  • It runs warm and makes a small click at power-off (AudioReviews.org).
  • Bluetooth has to be reconnected by hand after each power-up. No aptX, and no optical input.
  • Not enough for a large 12–15 inch home-theater sub in a big room.
