Why the N100 encodes out of the box and the N5095 may not

Jellyfin's Intel guide says Gen 11 Jasper Lake graphics (the Celeron N5095) "only support LP encoding mode." It says Low-Power encoding relies on the HuC firmware, and that the HuC setup "is not necessary unless you are using an Intel Jasper Lake or Elkhart Lake processor." The same guide says "Linux supports two modes only on Gen 12 ADL+ by default." The N100 and N95 are Alder Lake-N, per Intel.

The Linux i915 graphics driver explains the difference. In its default-options code, the comment "Don't enable GuC/HuC on pre-Gen12" is followed by enable_guc = 0. Gen12 parts other than Tiger Lake, Rocket Lake and Alder Lake-S get the default "enable HuC authentication and GuC submission." Jellyfin's fix for Jasper Lake is an i915 enable_guc=2 kernel option followed by a reboot. That change is made on the NAS operating system itself, not in the Jellyfin container. On the DXP2800 there is nothing to change: UGREEN's Jellyfin guide maps /dev/dri/renderD128 and selects Intel QuickSync (QSV) on its Intel DXP models. To confirm it works, check that the FFmpeg log shows qsv.

There are two cautions. First, TerraMaster rates the N5095 for 4K60 H.264 and H.265 transcoding, as ComputingForGeeks reports. Notebookcheck's Enrico Frahn found that the N5095 in a TerraMaster F4-423 "did not have any issues with the hardware transcoding" in Plex. That was Plex, not Jellyfin, so an N5095 box you already own is worth testing before you replace it. Second, Jellyfin warns that Jasper Lake and older chips "are losing support for QSV on Linux" and will need VA-API. Its hardware guide also advises avoiding Intel J/M/N/Y series low-power CPUs up to 11th gen in a Jellyfin server.

Not recommended

UGREEN DXP2800 GT. It shares the name but uses an AMD Ryzen Embedded R2514 (Starry Hope). UGREEN's Jellyfin guide says the GT models "have no Intel Quick Sync path" and need VA-API instead, so Quick Sync setup steps won't apply. Synology DS225+. Starry Hope reports that hardware transcoding does not work on stock DSM, and its J4125 is in the low-power class Jellyfin advises against. UGREEN DH2300. UGREEN says it "cannot host Jellyfin because it lacks Docker support."
