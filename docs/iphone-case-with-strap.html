Which Apple cases take Apple's Crossbody Strap

Apple's strap never clips onto the iPhone itself. Apple's support page says to start with the case off the phone and thread each connector's cord loop from the outside through one of the small openings in the case's bottom corners. The strap then snaps onto those connectors. A case without the two openings can't take the strap, and Apple says only "select Apple iPhone cases" are compatible. Apple also warns that the strap contains magnets that may interfere with medical devices.

The Clear Case is where people get caught. When the iPhone 17 Pro launched, Apple paired the strap with the TechWoven and Silicone cases only. For the iPhone 18 Pro, Apple says the Clear Case "features an updated design and is now compatible" with the Crossbody Strap and Wrist Strap. iPhone 16 and older models aren't on Apple's list at all. Owners of those phones need a case that comes with its own straps, like Bandolier's, or a Peak Design strap on a case with strap loops.
