What the differences mean

  • Load headroom. Notebookcheck measured a two-bay UGREEN DXP2800 GT with two WD Red drives at 14.84–20.14 W idle and 35.9 W flat out. The US3000's 120 W covers that peak 3.3 times (120 ÷ 35.9 = 3.3), and the US2000's 72 W covers it twice (72 ÷ 35.9 = 2.0).
  • Battery. UGREEN lists 43.2 Wh in the US3000 and 22.2 Wh in the US2000. The US3000 holds 1.9 times the energy (43.2 ÷ 22.2 = 1.95) for $40 more at UGREEN's store on 2026-11-17 ($109.99 − $69.99).

Which UPS your NAS can hear, and how to make it come back on

UGREEN's DC units fit only UGREEN NAS. The US3000 has no power adapter of its own. The NAS's original adapter plugs into its DC IN, and its DC OUT cable goes to the NAS. A USB data cable to a rear NAS USB port carries the outage signal. NASCompares notes that it works only with UGREEN NAS models that take external DC input, powers one NAS, and can't run a router or switch. UGREEN's guide lists the US3000 for the DH2300, DH4300 Plus, DXP2800, DXP4800 Plus, DXP4800 Pro and DXP480T Plus, and says it is not compatible with the DXP6800 or DXP8800 series. The plain DXP4800 (non-Plus) is not on that list. The US2000 is listed for the DH2300, DH4300 Plus, DXP2800 and DXP2800 GT, but UGREEN's own store menu describes it as DH-series only, so check the listing for your model before you buy. UGREEN also says a unit may arrive in Energy-Saving Mode; switch it to Normal Mode before you rely on it.

AC UPS on a UGREEN NAS. UGREEN lists the APC BE600M1, APC BK650M2-CH and SANTAK TG-BOX850 as passing its basic compatibility test. It also warns that with the BE600M1 or BK650M2-CH, if Auto Shutdown is set to 'Immediately' and power returns within the UPS's roughly two-minute delayed cutoff, the NAS may not restart on its own. Use a timed or low-battery shutdown instead.

Synology Standby Mode. Synology says that in Standby Mode the NAS 'stops all services and unmounts volumes in order to prevent data loss'. It turns itself back on when power returns only if it actually shut down and 'Restart automatically after a power failure' is ticked (Control Panel > Hardware & Power > General). The option 'Shut down UPS when the system enters Standby Mode' makes the UPS cut power, but Synology warns that 'Some UPS devices do not support shutting down by DSM'. Run a simulated outage once to check yours.

The power race. The Network UPS Tools FAQ describes power that 'came back during the shutdown, but before the UPS power off', leaving the computer off. A UPS that can switch its output off and back on avoids this.

Not recommended

CyberPower CP850AVRLCD. Cable Plus USA lists its battery output as a simulated sine wave, not a pure sine wave. It was $164.95 there on 2026-11-17.
