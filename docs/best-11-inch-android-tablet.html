What the differences mean

  • Yoga Tab vs Tab S11, battery: 17.7 h ÷ 11.2 h = 1.58, so the Yoga Tab browses about 58% longer, or 6.5 h more per charge.
  • Tab S10 FE vs Tab S11: 14.8 h − 11.2 h = 3.6 h more browsing from the cheaper Samsung. Samsung's US store listed the S10 FE at $549.99 and the Tab S11 128 GB at $999.99 on 2026-11-11, a gap of $999.99 − $549.99 = $450.
  • Tab A11+ vs Tab S11: 14.9 h − 11.2 h = 3.7 h more, from a smaller 7,040 mAh battery. At Samsung US prices that day, $999.99 − $299.99 = $700 less.

Battery life measured in one lab

All four runtimes come from Notebookcheck's Wi-Fi browsing test, which loads web pages in a loop until the battery runs out. For the Tab S11 and the Yoga Tab, Notebookcheck states it set the screen to 150 cd/m². Because the method is the same, the hours can be compared directly, unlike the makers' 'up to' video ratings.

Battery size doesn't explain the gap. The Yoga Tab's 8,860 mAh cell is only 8,860 ÷ 8,400 = 1.05, or about 5%, larger than the Tab S11's, yet it lasts 58% longer. The A11+ has 16% less capacity than the S11 (7,040 vs 8,400 mAh) and still lasts 3.7 h longer.

So the Tab S11 earns its place on the screen and the pen, not on endurance. buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FGKTLFGD?tag=dealectric-20 The S Pen in its box has no Bluetooth, so it can't act as a camera remote or use Air Actions, and Samsung says buying an S Pen Pro doesn't add them on this tablet. Notebookcheck also measured 480 Hz PWM flicker on the S11's panel and found none on the Yoga Tab or the A11+. If you're sensitive to flicker, that matters. Samsung promises up to 7 Android upgrades and 7 years of security updates, counted from global launch rather than from your purchase date.

If you want a Samsung with the pen included and longer battery life for less money, the Tab S10 FE ran 14.8 h in the same test. It has a 90 Hz LCD instead of OLED. buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DVZWKCDV?tag=dealectric-20

Before buying a Yoga Tab, check which generation it is. The tested 2025 model (Snapdragon 8 Gen 3) shipped with Android 15 and gets 3 Android upgrades, so up to Android 18, and 4 years of security patches, per MyNextTablet. It also has no microSD slot. The Yoga Tab Gen 2 launched in September 2026. It has the same 11.1-inch size, an 11,000 mAh battery, Android upgrades through Android 24 and security updates through 2033, at a $729.99 launch price (9to5Google). Notebookcheck hasn't published a battery test for it yet.
