Who it's for

Suits owners of stock 48 V Club Car, EZGO, ICON or Yamaha carts on mostly flat courses and neighborhoods who already have, or will buy, a 58.4 V lithium charger. Carts that draw more than 100 A for long stretches (lifted carts, large tires, steep hills) are better served by a 200 A continuous pack such as Vatrer's 48V 105Ah Club Car kit ($1,499.99 with charger at Vatrer, checked 2026-11-21); the Redodo 48V 100Ah battery is a near match at $836.99 (Redodo store, checked 2026-11-21).
