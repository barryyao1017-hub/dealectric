Home backup and camping for someone who wants the fastest wall charging in the 1 kWh class, a 4,000-cycle pack and the option to add a battery later, without needing 140 W laptop charging or two solar inputs.

Caveats

  • Amazon sells the whole DELTA 3 family under one listing: the Style menu switches between DELTA 3, DELTA 3 Plus, DELTA 3 Classic, DELTA 3 1500, DELTA 3 Max and bundles, and the old DELTA 2 listing now resolves to the DELTA 3. Check that 'DELTA 3' is the selected style; the DELTA 3 Classic is a cheaper, different model that the DELTA 3 extra battery does not fit.
  • Successor to the discontinued DELTA 2 (same 1,024 Wh / 1,800 W class): it adds the 4,000-cycle rating, 1,500 W AC input, the IP65 pack and the 10 ms UPS, and still accepts the DELTA 2 extra battery.
  • The DELTA 3 Plus is the same battery, inverter, chassis and expansion port with 140 W USB-C, 36 W USB-A, a second 500 W solar input and a wired USB-C UPS link to a PC or NAS.
