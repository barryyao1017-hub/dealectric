Who it's for

RV, van and cabin owners whose battery sits in an unheated bay and who need to charge from solar, shore power or a DC-DC charger when it is below freezing.

Caveats

  • No Bluetooth; you cannot watch cell temperature or state of charge from a phone.
  • Redodo lists a minimum charge current of about 10 A to start the heater; a small solar panel on a cloudy morning may not trigger it.
  • Redodo says this model must not be wired in series or parallel with its non-heated 12V 100Ah batteries.
  • Heating from -4°F to charging temperature takes well over an hour by Redodo's own figures.
  • Not a starter battery and not rated for golf carts; trolling motors of 30 to 70 lb thrust only.
