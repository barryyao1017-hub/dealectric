Who it's for

Makers who want four-color printing plus ABS, ASA or polycarbonate capability in one enclosed machine, without the size and 35 kg weight of the K2 Plus. For single-color engineering parts the QIDI Plus5 ($699) does more for $50 less: a 65 °C chamber, a 370 °C nozzle and 320 × 320 × 300 mm, with no color unit at that price.

Caveats

  • Tom's Hardware could not get a clean TPU print: parts came out under-extruded even from the side spool holder, and the CFS itself does not feed flexible TPU per Creality's spec table.
  • Creality's chamber guide says the chamber may not reach its target on 110 V power in a room below 15 °C, and warns that long heater runs can shorten the machine's life.
  • Tom's Hardware found the CFS cables too short to place the unit beside the printer, so it sits on the lid (850 mm tall in total) and must be powered down and unplugged before the lid comes off.
  • The long high-flow hotend purges a lot of filament at each color change, per Tom's Hardware, and Fabbaloo notes there is no purge bin in the box.
  • Owner reports on Creality's forum are mixed: one July 2026 poster had two units bought the month before both out of service with motion and CFS errors, while another reported about 3,000 hours with only occasional clogs.
