Who it's for

Backup or off-grid power for a 240 V well pump. That covers a Franklin SubDrive20 or SubDrive30 Connect from one unit, and a plain across-the-line pump within the maker's 6 HP motor rating.

Caveats

  • The 16,000 W peak and the 6 HP motor rating are Sun Gold's own figures. No named reviewer has measured them, and the listing gives no peak hold time.
  • Franklin publishes its 1.5× sizing rule for generators. Applying it to an inverter's continuous rating is our proxy.
  • A SubDrive50 (11,000 W bar) needs two units in parallel.
  • Two-wire pump motors get no soft start, so with one of those the start relies on the peak rating.
