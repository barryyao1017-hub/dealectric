What the differences mean

Every figure here is a maker's or a named tester's; Dealectric does not test units. Prices were read on 2026-11-21.

  • Front pixels. 4K is 3840 × 2160 = 8.29 MP. A 1944p frame of 2592 × 1944 is 5.04 MP. Botslab's 3K is 2880 × 1620 = 4.67 MP. The A810S front has 8.29 ÷ 5.04 = 1.65 times the pixels of the S1 Pro's.
  • Rear pixels. Every rear camera in the table is 1920 × 1080 = 2.07 MP. A 1440p rear is 2560 × 1440 = 3.69 MP, which is 3.69 ÷ 2.07 = 1.78 times as many.
  • Frame rate. At 60 mph a car covers 88 ft a second. That is 88 ÷ 30 = 2.9 ft between frames at 30 fps and 88 ÷ 25 = 3.5 ft at 25 fps.
  • Card life. DashCamTalk tester rcg530 added up the A810S's video files at 5.14 MB/s with front and rear recording. 5.14 × 3,600 = 18.5 GB an hour, so a 128 GB card loops after about 6.9 hours and a 256 GB card after about 13.8.
  • Parking mode. A hardwire kit gives the camera constant power while parked. 70mai's Road Trip Kit has one in the price. Vantrue's Voltage Display Hardwire Kit, which names the S1 Pro, was $29.99 at vantrue.com, with cut-off at 11.6, 12 or 12.4 V.

Megapixels per camera, with both recording

A 2-channel dash cam encodes two video streams at once, and makers headline the front one. What the rear camera records is the part that differs least in this table and matters most in a rear-end hit.

  • 70mai A810S. 70mai states both rear options use a Sony IMX662 at 1080p HDR, 30 fps; the exterior RC23 was marked 'not available yet', which leaves the in-car RC24. DashCamTalk tester Augustus recorded front and rear together: 2160p at 30 fps and 29.4 Mb/s in front, 1080p at 30 fps and 10.1 Mb/s behind. The rear stream gets 10.1 ÷ 29.4 = 34% of the front's data rate. In a heat test in the same thread the card slot reached 82.4 °C after about 30 minutes parked in sun at 28.1 °C outside, and the camera did not shut down.
  • Vantrue S1 Pro 2CH. Vantrue states 2.5K (1944p) front and 1080p rear, with 158° and 165° lenses, a supercapacitor and up to 15 seconds of pre-event recording. Product Bible lists both at 30 fps.
  • VIOFO A229 Plus 2CH (over the line). VIOFO states 2560 × 1440 on both cameras, each on a Sony STARVIS 2 IMX675 with HDR. BlackboxMyCar's settings guide says turning on front HDR changes a 60 fps selection to 30 fps, so 60 fps and HDR are separate modes. knowledgelib.io's price check logged this kit at $159.99 on Amazon (seller VIOFO LTD) on 2026-11-21; Amazon's listing did not load for us that day, so it has no row. If you see it under $200, it is the one to buy for the rear view: 3.69 MP against 2.07 MP.

Not recommended

70mai A810 Lite as a front and rear set. DashCamTalk lists it with 'limited 24/25fps dual-channel recording' and 'no rear HDR'. $109.99 is the listing's starting price, 70mai describes the rear camera as optional, and its parking modes carry the note 'Hardwire Kit required'. The A810S kit records both cameras at 30 fps with HDR on each.
