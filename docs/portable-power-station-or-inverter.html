Why a car's lighter socket changes the answer

An inverter makes no power of its own. In a car it draws from the 12 V system, and the lighter socket is the weak link. Cobra's CPI 400 manual says: "Do not use the cigarette cord for loads higher than 150 watts." Above that, it says a direct connection to the battery is recommended. That limits a 400 W inverter to less than half its rating when it is plugged in. The Directed VDC301 manual explains why: "Most cigarette lighter currents are fused between 10-15 amps". That works out to 10–15 A × 12 V = 120–180 W before conversion losses. The same manual says to connect directly to the power source above 100 W.

A real 1,000 W-class inverter therefore has no plug at all. The Xantrex PROwatt SW 1000 is hardwire-only and is rated 900 W continuous and 2,000 W peak. Xantrex recommends a 150 A DC fuse and lists under 0.6 A no-load draw and 90% efficiency. At 900 W ÷ 12 V ≈ 75 A, it pulls five to seven times what a lighter-socket fuse allows, and it draws that from the car's own battery.

A power station turns this around. Its 1,800 W comes from its own battery, so the car socket doesn't limit what it can run. The socket only limits how fast it refills: BLUETTI's Charger 1 FAQ says "a standard cigarette lighter port in a 12V vehicle outputs around 100W". That is why BLUETTI sells the 560 W Charger 1, which wires to the car battery and charges from the alternator while the engine runs. If you take one box to a campsite and refill it at home, the Elite 100 V2 needs no car wiring at all: $499 on bluettipower.com, $474.05 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/bluetti-elite-100-v2.

Not recommended

The Sun Gold Power 2000W 12V inverter/charger is $657 on Sun Gold's store, and both its new and refurbished versions were sold out on 2026-11-13. Sun Gold lists 28 W idle draw (13 W in power-saver mode). 1,280 Wh ÷ 28 W ≈ 46 h, so with nothing plugged in it empties a 1,280 Wh battery such as the Vatrer 12V 100Ah in under two days. Its 2,000 W rating is also more than the Vatrer battery's 150 A BMS can feed (1,920 W).
