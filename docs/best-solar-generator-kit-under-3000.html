Price per Wh, sun hours and what the batteries actually delivered

Price per Wh is what you pay for each watt-hour of storage. Sun hours tell you whether the included panels can refill that storage in a day. Read together, they explain the pick. The R3500 + 600 W kit costs $0.55 per Wh, about 35% less than the F3000 kit's $0.85. Its 600 W panel is also the largest in this table relative to its battery, so it needs 5.3 h of rated sun against the F3000 kit's 7.7 h. ALLPOWERS rates the R3500 for over 3,500 charging cycles and up to 2,000 W of solar input, so you can add panels later. $1,993.10 on iallpowers.com, $1,753.93 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/allpowers-r3500-sp039-solar-kit

Rated Wh is not what reaches your appliances, because the inverter uses some of it. Notebookcheck (Marcus Schwarten) drained three of these stations at a light, steady load with the same method. The Anker SOLIX F3000 gave about 2,500 Wh at 200 W, 81% of 3,072 Wh. The BLUETTI Elite 300 gave about 2,650 Wh at just over 200 W, just under 88% of 3,014.4 Wh. The BLUETTI Elite 400 gave about 3,130 Wh at about 250 W, just under 82% of 3,840 Wh. StorageReview measured 1.91 kWh from the Elite 200 V2, about 92% of 2,073.6 Wh. Not recommended

BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 or Elite 400 with 2× 350 W panels. Here is the arithmetic from BLUETTI's own figures:

  • BLUETTI lists its 350 W panel at 33 V open-circuit. Two in series make 66 V, which is over the 12–60 V solar input on both stations, and cold weather pushes the voltage higher. So the two panels can only be wired in parallel.
  • Parallel wiring needs an MC4 Y-branch cable that isn't in the box. BLUETTI's own Y-branch ($39) showed sold out on 2026-11-17.
  • Even in parallel, the 20 A input cap holds the pair under 20 A × 33 V = 660 W, so the 700 W of panels can't all be used.

The 2× 200 W versions of the same stations cost less and can be wired in series within the 60 V limit. If you want about 700 W of panels on a BLUETTI station, look at one with two solar inputs, such as the Apex 300.

The Anker SOLIX F3000's 3,600 W figure is the TT-30R RV port and the total across outlets. Each household NEMA 5-20R outlet is rated 2,400 W max.
