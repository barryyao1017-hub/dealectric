What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test dash cams. The figures here come from the makers' US stores and from named DashCamTalk and Mighty Gadget testers, all read 2026-11-21. For a rideshare driver, three figures decide the pick. First is the cabin camera's resolution and data rate with every channel recording, because that footage is the evidence in a passenger dispute. Second is whether the cabin camera has infrared light for night rides. Third is the front camera in the same mode, which covers crash and plate evidence.

  • Cabin pixels: 2560 × 1440 = 3.69 million pixels and 1920 × 1080 = 2.07 million, and 3.69 ÷ 2.07 = 1.8. The 2K cabin cameras on the T330 3CH, A329S 3CH and N4S therefore have 1.8 times the pixels of the cabin cameras on the N4 Pro S and N5S.
  • Cabin data: 23.8 ÷ 9.9 = 2.4, so the T330's cabin channel stores 2.4 times as much picture per second as the N4 Pro S's. For the N4S, 14.8 ÷ 9.9 = 1.5 times. Data rate stands in for detail; it is not a sharpness test.
  • Card use on the N4 Pro S: EricSan's files total 417,790 KB a minute with all three cameras on. That is 417,790 × 60 = 25.1 GB an hour, so a 256 GB card holds at most 256 ÷ 25.1 = 10.2 hours, which is barely one 10-hour shift. A 512 GB card holds 20.4 hours and a 1 TB card 39.8 hours. Vantrue's FAQ lets locked event clips use up to 30% of the card, so the loop keeps less than these totals.
  • Parking-ready cost of the pick: $499.99 + $25.99 for the HK6-F kit = $525.98 at VIOFO on 2026-11-21. The three Vantrue cameras take the $29.99 Voltage Display kit, which Vantrue lists for the N4S, N4 Pro S and N5S.

Why the cabin camera's data rate decides a rideshare dash cam

A rideshare complaint, such as an argument, a damage claim or an accusation, happens in the cabin, often at night, and usually with no impact. Vantrue's FAQ says a clip is locked into the event folder only when the G-sensor feels a hit. Otherwise it goes to the normal folder, and only event-folder files are protected from being overwritten. That makes the cabin channel's detail and the card's hours the evidence you actually keep. Lock a disputed ride by hand: on the N4 Pro S, a short press of the power button locks the current video, and EricSan notes the button sits behind the side cover with the card slot.
