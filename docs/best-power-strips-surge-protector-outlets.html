What the joule figure leaves out

The joule figure is the total surge energy a strip's metal-oxide varistors (MOVs) are rated to absorb. Techlicious explains that MOVs wear down with every spike, that nothing shows how much life they have left, and that a strip which cuts power to its outlets when the MOVs are spent is better than one that only turns off a light. Techlicious also advises a UL 1449 voltage protection rating (VPR) of 400 V or lower on all three lines: line-neutral, line-ground and neutral-ground.

  • Belkin SRA009P12TT8: Belkin says it is UL 1449 certified. Its clamping voltage is 400 V line-neutral and 500 V on both line-ground and neutral-ground. The spec table lists 'Power Safety Shut Down: Yes', a $300,000 connected-equipment warranty and EMI/RFI filtering up to 75 dB. The two 500 V figures are above Techlicious's 400 V bar; that is what you give up for the lowest price per joule. One image description on the page says 3,940 J, but the spec table and the summary both say 3,780 J, which is the figure used here.
  • Belkin PivotPlug BP112230-08: Belkin's page lists 400 V clamping and 'Power Safety Shut Down: Yes'. Techlicious lists it at 330/400/400 V with auto-shutoff, which meets the 400 V bar on all three lines. It also carries a $300,000 connected-equipment warranty, a 2-year product warranty and EMI/RFI filtering up to 50 dB. Pay the extra $20 for it if lower clamping or pivoting outlets matter more to you than price per joule.
  • Anker 351: Anker's certification line reads 'TUV / CP65 / FCC / ICES', and the page gives no clamping voltage. If you require a UL 1449 surge protector, the Belkin SRA009P12TT8 is the one whose maker page states it. Its single USB-C port gives 20 W only when both USB-A ports are empty, and the two USB-A ports share 5 V/3 A. That is enough for phones but not for a laptop.
  • TESSAN PS07 and 14-in-1 Tower: TESSAN rates the PS07 at 2,100 J, with an automatic cutoff on overload. The Tower is rated 1,500 J, with overload protection, an LED that shows protection status, and cETLus certification.

Stock on 2026-11-08: belkin.com shows both Belkins as 'Out of Stock', with the SRA009P12TT8 due by Nov 11 and the PivotPlug by Nov 19. Anker's store shows the Anker as in stock, and TESSAN's store offers both TESSANs with an add-to-cart button and no option marked sold out.

If you use a UPS battery backup, plug the strip into the wall and not into the UPS. APC says a non-APC surge strip on the output of a Back-UPS or Smart-UPS can void its Equipment Protection Policy.

Not recommended

Don't use the TESSAN PS07 for a space heater, hair dryer or anything else drawing close to 1,500 W. TESSAN caps its eight outlets at 1,250 W in total, so a single 1,500 W appliance goes over the limit. It works for phones, lamps, a TV and chargers. For high-draw appliances, use a 1,875 W strip or, better, plug straight into the wall.
