Who it's for

Campers and emergency kits that want the lightest 1 kWh class unit with a long-lived battery and no need to add capacity later.

Caveats

  • Not expandable, and not compatible with the Explorer 1000 Plus battery pack; Jackery's own page now points buyers to the newer HomePower 1000 v2 (1,024 Wh, 1,500 W).
  • 1,500 W AC is the lowest of the 1 kWh class (the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 gives 2,000 W); the 1-hour charge needs Emergency Charging enabled in the app before each charge.
  • Amazon mixes the bare Explorer 1000 v2 (AC cable included, no panel) with Solar Generator 1000 v2 panel bundles and a Dune White + charger kit; the cycle rating is to 70% capacity, not 80%.
