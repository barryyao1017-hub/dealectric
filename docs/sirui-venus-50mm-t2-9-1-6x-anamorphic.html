Who it's for

Full-frame Sony, Canon RF or L-Mount shooters who want a real anamorphic look on a tripod or a strong gimbal.

Caveats

  • Heavy at 2.27 lb and long; not a run-and-gun lens.
  • 1.6x is not in most cameras' de-squeeze menus (Sony FX3/FX30 have it on recent firmware; Panasonic offers 1.5x and 1.8x), so you may preview squeezed or use a monitor.
  • Reviewers note focus breathing and slight distortion, and a warm color cast.
  • Manual focus only.
