What the differences mean

  • How far away a face stays readable: 6.7 m ÷ 2.3 m = 2.9× the distance for the eufy E340. On the SwitchBot, a visitor standing back from the door, or at the bottom of the steps, falls below LinkdHOME's legibility line.
  • What the indoor screen can show: 1,280 × 800 = 1,024,000 pixels on the eufy E10 against 480 × 272 = 130,560 on SwitchBot's monitor (PCWorld's figure), so the eufy screen has 7.8× as many pixels.
  • Wait before you see the door: 5.3 s − 1.04 s = 4.3 s longer for SwitchBot's average live view. PCWorld's Christopher Null also found that SwitchBot's monitor doesn't start video when someone rings: you press play and wait. eufy says the E10 lights up with the live feed on a ring.
  • Who gets flagged: 76% ÷ 46% = 1.65× the person-detection rate for the eufy. LinkdHOME's SwitchBot unit missed 25% of night detections (6 of 24).
  • Charging the doorbell: 78 ÷ 47 = 1.66× the days between charges for the eufy on battery. The E340 can also run on 16–24 V doorbell wiring, which removes charging altogether.

One caveat applies to the screen side. LinkdHOME tested the SwitchBot with its own monitor (two-way talk on the monitor gave 'the exact same results'). It tested the E340 with a HomeBase 2 and an Echo Show, not the E10. The doorbell figures carry over, but none of them is a timing taken on the E10 itself.

Before you install the eufy, check two things. eufy's spec sheet says the E340 runs on existing doorbell wiring at 16–24 V, 10 VA, and keeps a mechanical chime ringing but not a digital one. eufy also says the E10 ends live view from a battery-powered camera after 3 minutes, while wired cameras have no limit. If you plan to leave the screen watching the porch, wire the doorbell.

Not recommended

Skip the SwitchBot kit if you want to watch the visitor on the indoor screen. Its 4.3-inch monitor plugs into an outlet. It shows 480 × 272 pixels, and PCWorld lists 'Display has very low resolution' as a con. You press play to start video. And the camera kept a chart legible only out to 2.3 m in LinkdHOME's daytime test. It works as a chime with a small preview and two-way talk, at a much lower price.
