Why non-Apple watches last weeks

Amazfit, Garmin and COROS watches run their own low-power software instead of watchOS, so a charge lasts days or weeks instead of a day. Battery figures are the makers' "typical use" numbers; always-on display, GPS workouts and heavy notifications shorten them (Active Max: 10 days always-on, 13 days heavy use).

What works with an iPhone

  • Works: notifications, calls on models with a speaker (both Amazfit picks), and health and sleep data in the maker's app.
  • Doesn't: replying to messages from the watch (Android only on Amazfit and Garmin), Apple Pay, iMessage.
