Cell chemistry and cycle ratings: why a 1,500 Wh kit costs $613 or $1,279

The kits at this size split by cell type. Jackery's 1500 v2 and 1500 Ultra use LiFePO4 cells. Jackery rates the 1500 v2 at 6,000+ cycles; its listing gives no end capacity, and BackupPowerHub's spec table says the cycles run to 80%. Jackery rates the 1500 Ultra at 4,000 cycles to 70%. ALLPOWERS's S2000 and S2000 Pro use ternary (NCM) lithium cells. Matt Gregersen of The Gadgeteer lists a “ternary lithium battery” for the S2000. StorageReview named NCM cells for the S2000 Pro and quoted ALLPOWERS's rating of 2,500+ cycles to 80%. ALLPOWERS's S2000 kit listings claim “1000+ life cycles” and give no end capacity.

StorageReview tested a 2023 S2000 Pro sold as 1,500 Wh. Today's listing gives 1,451.5 Wh at 43.2 V, 33.6 Ah. 43.2 V is 12 cells × 3.6 V, the nominal voltage of a ternary cell; a LiFePO4 cell is 3.2 V nominal.

The end capacity matters when you compare ratings. A rating “to 70%” counts cycles until the battery holds 70% of its label. So the Ultra's 4,000 cycles end at 1,536 × 0.7 = 1,075 Wh. The 1500 v2's ratings end at 80%: 1,536 × 0.8 = 1,229 Wh. The S2000 Pro's end at 1,451.5 × 0.8 = 1,161 Wh.

Usable energy is the second gap. StorageReview drew 920 Wh at a 350 W load from its S2000 Pro, 61% of the 1,500 Wh rating at the time. The unit shut off with 22% still showing, which StorageReview put down to calibration. At that figure, the S2000 Pro + SE200 costs $759.24 ÷ 920 = $0.83 per delivered Wh, level with the Jackery 1500 v2 kit's $0.83 per rated Wh.

Solar input limits set how far each kit can grow. The Jackery 1500 v2 takes up to 400 W (BackupPowerHub), so its 200 W kit can add one more 200 W of panels. The 1500 Ultra takes 800 W (Jackery). ALLPOWERS's S2000 Pro station listing and current manual give 1,000 W (150 V, 13 A). The S2000 Pro + SP037 kit listing still says 650 W max, so check the input rating on the unit's label before adding panels. The S2000 takes 500 W (12–70 V, 18 A), per The Gadgeteer. Wellbots' own spec text for the Jackery 1500 v2 kit lists 1,488 Wh, 1,800 W and “lithium-ion”. Jackery's store gives 1,536 Wh LiFePO4 and 2,000 W, and this page uses Jackery's figures.
