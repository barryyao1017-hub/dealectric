Who it's for

Drivers who want side coverage for sideswipes and parking-lot dings on the road, or rideshare drivers who turn the side cameras inward, at a mid-range price.

Caveats

  • No HDR: the front camera struggles with headlight glare at night, and the side and rear cameras blur in the dark.
  • Parking motion detection triggers only from the front camera; side cameras do not wake it.
  • Every parking mode needs the separate hardwire kit (the $275.99 bundle on the Botslab store adds the kit and a CPL filter).
  • Wide body: mounted under the mirror, the mirror can block one side camera; most owners mount it high and centered.
  • All channels record at 25 fps, which looks slightly jerky.
  • Botslab is the overseas brand of Qihoo 360; Botslab says US customer data is stored on servers in Virginia.
