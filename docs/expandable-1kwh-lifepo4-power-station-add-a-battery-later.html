Which 1 kWh LiFePO4 power station can I start with and expand with an extra battery later? (2026)

Short answer. To start with a 1 kWh LiFePO4 power station and add an extra battery later in small steps, the EcoFlow DELTA 3 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DCBYMC9D?tag=dealectric-20) takes 1,024 Wh extra batteries (21.1 lb each) up to 5 kWh and also accepts older DELTA 2, DELTA 2 Max and DELTA Max extra batteries. The plain DELTA 3 has the same expansion port as the Plus. If you may need more than 5 kWh, the Jackery HomePower 1000 Plus v2 and DJI Power 1000 V2 reach about 11 kWh with 2 kWh packs.

What to know: several popular 1 kWh LiFePO4 power stations can't be expanded with an extra battery later at all: the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 (Anker dropped the port), the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 and EcoFlow's cheaper DELTA 3 Classic. An extra battery costs about as much as a second station; makers that publish prices list $578–1,399 for 2–3 kWh packs.

Small steps or big ones

EcoFlow's DELTA 3 grows in 1 kWh steps: one extra battery doubles it, four reach 5 kWh, each at 21.1 lb so one person can carry each piece. DJI, Jackery's HomePower 1000 Plus v2, OUPES and Dabbsson add 2 kWh at a time, which reaches further (DJI and Jackery to about 11 kWh) but means a 2 kWh, roughly 40 lb purchase for each step. For most homes, one step (2–3 kWh) covers a fridge and lights overnight, which any of these do.

Older batteries that fit

EcoFlow's compatibility table for the DELTA 3 series lists the DELTA 3, DELTA Pro 3, DELTA 2, DELTA 2 Max and DELTA Max extra batteries on the same XT150 port, which matters if you already own one from an older EcoFlow. Jackery's HomePower 1000 Plus v2 takes only its new Battery Pack 2000 Plus v2, not the older Explorer packs.

Before you add one later

Only DJI documents a firmware step (the original Power 1000 needs a wired update before its first expansion battery; the V2 does not). EcoFlow's extra battery shows a firmware update by its LED pattern, and one owner reported needing the app for it. Dabbsson and OUPES packs take solar on their own, so they can charge while the station is elsewhere. Buy the station whose port you will use: the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2, Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 and DELTA 3 Classic have none, and Anker's older C1000X, which expanded to 2,112 Wh with a BP1000, could not be confirmed as still sold new.
