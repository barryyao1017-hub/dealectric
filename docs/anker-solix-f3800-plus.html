Who it's for

Houses that need 240 V for a well pump, central air or a dryer, and want the battery wired to a transfer sub-panel rather than run on extension cords.

Caveats

  • Idle draw is the weak spot: a reviewer measured 112 W with the inverter on and nothing plugged in, about 34 hours to empty. Anker's June 2025 firmware claims to halve that; no independent after-update measurement was found.
  • The 240 V output cannot be used while the unit charges from a 120 V wall outlet (Anker's own listing).
  • Reaching 6,000 W of 240 V charging needs two expansion batteries; one unit alone charges at about 3,500 W from a generator.
  • The '30 A' outlet is a 25 A outlet, which can trip appliances that expect a full 30 A.
  • At 136.7 lb it is a two-person lift up stairs even with the wheels.
