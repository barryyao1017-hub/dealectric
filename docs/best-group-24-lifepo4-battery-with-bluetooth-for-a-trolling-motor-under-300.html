Why the BMS rating matters on a boat

A 12 V trolling motor on high draws 40–60 A, a 24 V setup on two batteries less per battery; add a fish finder, livewell pump and lights and a 100 A BMS is still within limits but closer to them, and a BMS that trips under a surge shuts the whole battery off until it resets. Vatrer's 150 A rating is the headroom. None of the packs here is a starter battery, so an outboard's starter stays on its own lead-acid or dual-purpose battery.
