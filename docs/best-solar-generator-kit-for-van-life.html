How much battery a 12 V fridge needs

A 30–50 quart compressor fridge uses about 250–450 Wh a day in mild weather and more in summer heat (Dometic's own figure for a CFX3 35 works out to about 280 Wh a day at 90 °F outside). Add 50–100 Wh for a laptop and lights. A 2 kWh battery carries that for about four days with no sun; 1 kWh about two.

Charging while you drive

A car's 12 V socket sits on a 10–20 A fuse, and stations draw about 100 W from it (BLUETTI lists 96 W for the Elite 200 V2 and about 100 W for the AC180), so a 1–2 kWh station needs 10–20 hours of driving to refill. An alternator charger is the fast route: EcoFlow's 800 W unit refills about 1 kWh in 1.3 hours of driving but works only with EcoFlow stations; BLUETTI sells its Charger 1 with the AC180 + 200 W kit for $1,018.

Panel on the roof or on the ground

A folding panel set out at camp in full sun gives the most; a flexible panel glued to the roof works while parked or driving but runs hotter and flatter, so it gives less per watt. Either way, a 200 W panel adds about 600–700 Wh on a clear day, enough to keep a fridge going indefinitely in good weather.
