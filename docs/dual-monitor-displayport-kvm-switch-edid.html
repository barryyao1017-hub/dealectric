  • 4K 144 Hz is a DSC figure: VESA puts DisplayPort 1.4's video payload at 25.92 Gbps. 3840 × 2160 × 144 Hz × 24 bits = 28.67 Gbps before blanking, so it doesn't fit without Display Stream Compression (or 4:2:2 colour, per Cable Matters). 4K 120 Hz works out to 23.89 Gbps and fits, which matches Level1Techs' own 4K 120 Hz rating. Why EDID emulation decides a dual-monitor KVM

When a KVM switches away from a PC, a switch without emulation disconnects that PC from its monitors. The operating system sees both displays disappear and reconnect, so it moves windows and can reset the layout. CKL states this directly for the 622DP-4: 'This DisplayPort KVM switch does not support EDID emulation so the desktop locations may change upon switching and boot-up.' AV Access says the 8KSW21DP-DM 'does not support EDID emulation at present', and Level1Techs says its switch 'does not support monitor emulation'. A switch with EDID emulation keeps presenting each monitor to the PC you left. TESmart calls this 'Smart EDID emulation' on the DKS202-M24, and CKL says the 622DP-5's emulated EDID 'retains your monitor resolution, refreshrate and display configuration when switching between PCs.'

Know the limits before you buy the $329.90: https://dealectric.com/go/tesmart-dks202-m24. TESmart's FAQ says each computer needs two video connections for dual monitors. One owner on TESmart's community forum (2026-03-25) reported windows still moving after a Windows lock and unlock.

Mac owners: Apple's specs list one external display for the M2 MacBook Air, so only one of each computer's two KVM inputs gets a picture. An M3 MacBook Air or Pro drives two displays only with the lid closed, and the second display runs 4K at up to 100 Hz (or 5K at 60 Hz). Apple lists the M4 and M5 MacBook Air at two displays of up to 4K 144 Hz each.

Not recommended

CKL 622DP-4, if you want windows to stay put. CKL's own listing says it has no EDID emulation. It also ships without the two monitor cables, so a complete setup costs $175.99 at $13 a cable, more than the $159.99: https://dealectric.com/go/avaccess-8ksw21dp-dm at $159.99 with all six DP cables in the box.
