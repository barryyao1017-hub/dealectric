What makes a battery heater run in an RV

A heated LiFePO4 battery does not warm itself just because it is cold. Each maker sets its own condition, and in a parked RV that condition decides whether the battery can take a charge in the morning.

  • LiTime LiHeat (320Ah Mini, 165Ah): LiTime's listing says the battery can warm itself while a charger is plugged in, or preheat with battery power before charging. So it can warm up even when no charger is running.
  • LiTime's standard self-heating series: heating starts only with a charger supplying 14.2–14.6 V and more than 15 A (100Ah) or more than 20 A (200Ah). It heats between −4°F and 41°F, stops at 50°F and does not charge while heating. LiTime gives warm-up from 14°F as 30–60 minutes in one section of its guide and about 90 minutes in another. On the 200Ah, a solar controller or converter that delivers less than 20 A never starts the heater, so the battery stays cold and does not charge.
  • Epoch Essential (105Ah, 300Ah): the heater runs on incoming charge current only, never on stored energy, and needs at least 0.5 A. Charging is blocked below 32°F and resumes at 41°F. With heating, Epoch rates charging from −4°F to 131°F. A trickle of solar is enough to start it, but a parked RV with nothing charging gets no heat.
  • Battle Born heated (BB10012H): the heater draws about 28 W from the battery's own cells and switches on near 35°F, but only while a charge source is present. An hour of heating uses 28 Wh, about 2.3% of the 1,200 Wh pack (28 ÷ 1,200).
  • Redodo 12V 100Ah Self-Heating: 100 W of heating pads switch on below 32°F and off at 41°F, and then charging resumes. Discharge stops below −4°F.
  • LiTime 12V 230Ah Bluetooth: no heater. Charging stops at 32°F and resumes at 41°F, and discharge stops at −4°F, so in freezing weather it needs to be in a warm space before it will charge.

Warranty terms matter for an RV stored over winter. LiTime's 5-year repair-or-replace warranty covers the cells only if the battery is fully charged within 7 days of purchase and at least once every 3 months. Epoch's 11-year warranty gives free replacement in years 1–3 and 40% off MSRP on a replacement in years 4–11.
