What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test units. Every figure below comes from the maker's page or from the reviewer named next to it.

  • Pixels per side view. On the G980H each side camera is its own channel: 1920 × 1080 = 2,073,600 px. On Thinkware's multiplexer the side views are tiles in one frame: (1920 × 1080) ÷ 4 tiles = 960 × 540 = 518,400 px. That gives 2,073,600 ÷ 518,400 = 4× more recorded pixels per side on the G980H.

How each system records its side cameras

Thinkware. The Multiplexer Box takes 'up to 4 Analog Full HD cameras' and outputs one composite video at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps. Thinkware's multi-channel page says the recordings from the extra cameras 'are merged together and saved into one video file' and 'cannot be saved separately'. BlackboxMyCar's MB-100 review says the dash cam stores that file as its rear-channel recording and that the views 'share the frame'. Thinkware gives no tile size. On the DashCamTalk forum, commenter TonyM describes the four-view output as an array of 960 × 540 videos, which is exactly a quarter of the frame. Each side camera has its own 1920 × 1080 sensor (129°, IP68), but it records only into its tile. Thinkware's install article recommends mounting the cameras on the front fenders. They record as a left view by default, and you cut the white wire on the camera you want to be the right one. For parking mode the box's yellow wire goes to constant power and the dash cam has to be hardwired. Compatibility is the catch: the Multiplexer Box page and the support article list the ARC 800, but the side-camera pack page lists only the U1000 Plus, Q850, Q200, F200 PRO, F790, X800 and X700. Confirm with Thinkware before you buy the side cameras for an ARC 800.

Botslab G980H. Botslab lists '3K+1080P+1080P+1080P' with fields of view of 170°, 120°, 120° and 150°, and says all four cameras record simultaneously. On DashCamTalk, Vortex Radar (posted 2026-04-23, edited 2026-10-16) reports the front at 1620p, 25 fps in what they call 3CH mode, and the rear and both side cameras at 1080p, 25 fps. They name the front sensor as a 5 MP SmartSens SC501AI. Botslab lists a 4K front only in 2- and 3-channel modes, after you detach side cameras, and Vortex Radar calls that 2160p, 25 fps mode 'fake 4K'. The kit includes a 128 GB card (cards up to 512 GB), a supercapacitor rated -20 to 70 °C, built-in GPS and 5.8 GHz Wi-Fi. It loses in two places. First, Vortex Radar found that in parking mode 'motion detection only works on front cam, side cams won't trigger'. Second, they wrote 'No HDR, so really struggles at night'. If parked door dings are your main worry, Thinkware's outside-mounted cameras are the stronger choice. For driving footage, take the G980H: $234.99 on botslab.com, $192.70 through this link: https://dealectric.com/go/botslab-g980h-4-camera-dash-cam

Not recommended for side coverage

The REDTIGER F17 Plus and the Vantrue Nexus 5S are sold as 4-channel cameras, but DashCamTalk lists their cameras as front, rear, interior and interior rear. Neither has a camera aimed at the left or right of the car. The N5S suits rideshare drivers who need the cabin on video. It is not a side-camera dash cam.
