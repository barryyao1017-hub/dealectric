What the differences mean

  • Pro vs Plus: both are rated at 1.2 GPM, so the Pro's extra cost buys pressure and a quieter motor, not more rinse water. $594.99 − $359.99 = $235.00 for 2,700 − 2,100 = 600 PSI more rated pressure. Giraffe's published noise figure drops from 90–93 dB to 78–80 dB, which is 12–13 dB quieter.
  • Lite Pro vs Pro: $594.99 − $439.99 = $155.00 less for the same 100 ft auto-retracting hose and an induction motor. Giraffe publishes only a 3,000 PSI maximum for it.
  • Pro in use: in Bob Vila's month-long test, the hose sometimes had to be guided by hand to retract fully. Mounting took a drill and some planning to hit the studs.

Winter in an unheated garage: what Giraffe's manuals say

On a wall-mounted all-in-one, the pump hangs on the wall with the reel, so it gets as cold as the garage does. Giraffe's manual for the Grandfalls G30A says: “Always store your pressure washer in a location where the temperature will not fall below 32°F (0°C).” It adds: “The pump in this machine is susceptible to permanent damage if it freezes,” and “FREEZE DAMAGE IS NOT COVERED BY THE WARRANTY.”

The same page tells owners to empty the water from all hoses and the pump before storage. Before winter, they should run a non-corrosive, non-toxic antifreeze, a pump saver or a pump protector through the machine. It also asks them to “dismantle the pressure washer” and “store it indoors during winter.” The G20 manual gives the same take-down instruction and adds that if the hose is partly frozen, you should not pull it out but wait for it to thaw. Follow the manual that ships with your own unit.

In a garage that freezes, then, a Grandfalls doesn't stay up all year. Plan to take it down each fall and rehang it in spring, and mount it where you can reach the bracket safely.

If that won't work for you, split the setup. A separate washer such as the Active 2.3, sold as a tool-only unit with a handle, comes inside for the winter, and a pressure hose reel stays on the wall. Giraffe's 1/4 in retractable pressure reel lists a hose temperature range of -40 to 150 °F. It comes in two ratings: 2,600 PSI with 65 ft of hose at $129.99, or 4,000 PSI with 60 ft at $169.99 ($40 more). Choose a rating above your washer's maximum pressure. The listing doesn't say the reel tolerates frozen water inside it, so drain it too.
