What owners complain about

  • Hose rewind (5 reports): the hose winds unevenly, the guide misaligns, or it needs a hand to go all the way in. Bob Vila saw this on the Pro.
  • Pump or motor failure within 1 to 2 years (5 reports): one owner's motor failed just after the warranty ended.
  • Leaks (4 reports): at the hose base, the connector to the reel, or the nozzle joint.
  • Slow support: 5 of the 6 recent 1-3 star Trustpilot reviews say calls or emails went unanswered. Others say Giraffe shipped replacement parts quickly.
  • Install: you need studs or masonry, a GFCI outlet within reach of the 6 ft cord, and a water hookup.

Which one by need

  • Most homes: Grandfalls Lite Pro ($439.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/giraffe-tools-grandfalls-lite-pro). You get the 100 ft hose and the quieter induction motor without the Pro's price.
  • You want the longest review record: Grandfalls Pro ($594.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/giraffe-tools-grandfalls-pro). It has the same 100 ft reach and more owner and press reviews.
  • Tight budget or a short driveway: Grandfalls G20 ($224.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/giraffe-tools-grandfalls-g20).
  • You already own a pressure washer: add Giraffe's 1/4 in retractable pressure hose reel ($129.99 on 2026-11-14) and keep your machine on a shelf.
  • Weekly car detailing: a Kränzle K1322TS on a wall shelf. It gives steadier flow and a serviceable brass pump, at about three times the cost of the Lite Pro and with no built-in reel.

Not recommended

  • Unheated garages that freeze, unless you will drain the pump every fall.
  • Renters who cannot drill into studs or masonry.
  • Shopping by the biggest PSI number. Max PSI is a peak rating, and for rinsing cars, flow matters more.
