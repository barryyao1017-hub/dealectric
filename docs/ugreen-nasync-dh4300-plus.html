Who it's for

A home that wants to start with two drives and add two more later, or needs RAID 5 and 2.5GbE on a quiet ARM box.

Caveats

  • No drives in the box; budget for NAS-rated drives.
  • 8 GB of memory is soldered and cannot be upgraded.
  • ARM processor: Docker works but many x86 containers are unavailable, and heavy video transcoding is beyond it.
  • Uses an external power brick, unlike the DH2300.
