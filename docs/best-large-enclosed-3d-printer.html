What the differences mean

  • QIDI Max4: 39.0 × 39.0 × 34.0 cm = 51.7 L. $1,049 ÷ 51.7 L = $20.29 per litre.
  • Anycubic Kobra S1 Max and Creality K2 Plus: 35 × 35 × 35 cm = 42.9 L each. $699 ÷ 42.9 L = $16.29 per litre and $899 ÷ 42.9 L = $20.96 per litre.
  • QIDI Plus5: 32 × 32 × 30 cm = 30.7 L. $699 ÷ 30.7 L = $22.77 per litre.
  • Prusa CORE One L+: $1,850.93 ÷ 29.7 L = $62.32 per litre, about three times the others.
  • Max4 over K2 Plus: ($1,049 − $899) ÷ (51.7 − 42.9 L) = $150 ÷ 8.8 L, or about $17 for each extra litre. That is cheaper than the K2 Plus's own average of $20.96 per litre.
  • Chamber heater power per litre: on the Max4, 500 W ÷ 51.7 L = 9.7 W per litre. On the Plus5, 550 W ÷ 30.7 L = 17.9 W per litre. Both are rated up to 65 °C, but the Max4's heater has about half as much power for each litre of build volume.

Price per litre at today's maker-store prices

A large printer costs what it does for its build volume, so price per litre is the fairest way to compare them. At the makers' US store prices on 14 November 2026, the QIDI Max4 costs $1,049, which is $250 below its $1,299 compare-at price. That makes it the largest heated-chamber printer here and the second-cheapest per litre. Only the Anycubic Kobra S1 Max ($699) costs less per litre, but it holds 8.8 L less. The Creality K2 Plus has the same 42.9 L as the Kobra for $200 more, and its rated chamber temperature is 5 °C lower (60 °C against 65 °C). The Bambu Lab H2S builds in 340 × 320 × 340 mm (37.0 L) with a 65 °C heated chamber, according to Bambu. That is smaller than either 350 mm machine and 14.7 L smaller than the Max4.

Multicolour adds to the cost. The QIDI Box is $228, so a Max4 with a Box comes to $1,277. The Kobra S1 Max Combo is $899 and the K2 Plus Combo is $1,099. Purge waste can be high: on a three-part multicolour print, HowToMechatronics (Dejan) weighed 350 g of K2 Plus purge waste for 83 g of finished parts.

The Max4 has costs beyond its price. Its chamber heater has about half the power per litre of the Plus5's. It is also louder: AndroidPCTV (Kim) measured 56 dB in normal printing, 62 dB with the Polar Cooler and 65 dB with the QIDI Box running. Not recommended for size per dollar

The Prusa CORE One L+ costs $62.32 per litre and has the smallest build volume here (29.7 L). It has no separate chamber heater. Its 60 °C chamber comes from two fans blowing air across the heated bed. Prusa lists ABS, ASA, HIPS and PA for it only with the Advanced Filtration add-on. Buy it for Prusa's serviceability, not as a large enclosed printer for engineering filaments.
