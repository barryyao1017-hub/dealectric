One battery, two jobs: why half the charge is spoken for

In a jump starter with an air compressor, the compressor and the clamps draw from the same pack. WOLFBOX's MegaVolt16 Air manual, which also covers the MegaVolt 24 Air, heads its jump-start section “Ensure Battery Level >50%”. GOOLOO's A3 guide says not to jump-start a vehicle if the battery is below 2 of its 4 bars. It also says to make no more than 3 jump attempts in a row, wait 2 minutes between them, and let the unit cool for 30 minutes before recharging. So the half above that line is the compressor's budget, and any tire you inflate beyond it uses charge you need for the jump. If you need both at the roadside, jump the car first while the pack is above the line, then inflate the tires.

WOLFBOX's own how-to guide says to check the display for remaining charge before a jump and to watch the battery when airing up more than one or two tires in a row.

If you inflate often, two batteries avoid the trade-off. A separate cordless inflator such as the $89.99: https://dealectric.com/go/fanttik-x8-apex-tire-inflator ($59.99 at fanttik.com today) leaves your jump pack full.
