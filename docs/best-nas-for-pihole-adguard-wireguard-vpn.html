The port-53 problem on UGOS, in detail

Owners found UGOS Pro 1.6 and later binding dnsmasq to 127.0.0.1:53, with the Virtual Machines app adding two more listeners, so Pi-hole's own dnsmasq cannot start. The Pi-hole forum's solution (uninstall the VM app, mask dnsmasq, restart the container) works but may break UGREENlink and VMs, and one commenter says it has to be redone after reboots; UGREEN support said it does not recommend disabling or reassigning port 53. The clean route is Docker macvlan: the container gets its own LAN IP, keeps per-device statistics, and leaves UGOS alone. AdGuard Home has the same port-53 need and the same fix.

The VPN half

WireGuard in Docker (wg-easy or linuxserver/wireguard) needs the host kernel's wireguard module; the UGREEN Docker guide reports kernel 6.12 with the module, an ip6_tables error after UGOS or Docker updates that needs a one-line SSH fix, and UDP 51820 forwarded on the router. Tailscale as a Compose project needs no open port and works behind CGNAT (free plan, 6 users), at the cost of a Tailscale account and an occasional relay hop. UGREEN's own UGREENlink relay reaches the NAS but not your whole LAN. Throughput is not the question on an N100: the OpenWrt WireGuard table puts the chip in the multi-gigabit range, so your internet uplink is the limit.

What the DNS box needs

Pi-hole asks for 512 MB of RAM and 2 GB of disk and "does not require much processing power"; AdGuard Home publishes no minimum. Either runs on the oldest Pi you own, which is the point: a 3 W box that never reboots for a NAS update is better DNS than a 40 W NAS that does. Clients do not reliably fall back to a secondary DNS, so two resolvers (Pi-hole on the NAS plus a Pi, synced) is the setup that survives updates.
