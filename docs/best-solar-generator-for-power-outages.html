One box versus the headline number

Several of these stations advertise a power figure that one box delivers only under certain conditions. Anker's F3800 Plus page carries a footnote: "3,300W for single unit, 6,000W when working with 1 or more expansion batteries." The same page says the 240 V output cannot be used while the unit charges from 120 V AC. Jackery's 7,200 W comes only from the L14-30R/14-50 outlet. The four 120 V outlets top out at 3,600 W. The DELTA Pro 3 gives 4,000 W, but StormWatts reports that its 120 V and 240 V outputs work one at a time. BLUETTI says one Apex 300 gives 3,840 W and can power 120 V and 240 V devices at the same time. Anker's SOLIX S5000 gives more power from one box than the Apex 300 + B300K, 4,500 W against 3,840 W. It stores less, 5,024 Wh against 5,529.6 Wh. Its 10,000-cycle rating also ends at 60% capacity, while BLUETTI's 6,000 cycles end at 80%.

The cycle ratings also end at different points. BLUETTI rates the Apex 300 for 6,000 cycles to 80% capacity. EcoFlow rates the DELTA Pro 3 for 4,000 cycles to 80%. Jackery's spec gives 4,000 cycles to 70% for the Explorer 5000 Plus. Anker states "3,000+ cycles" with no end capacity.

Measured results: OutdoorGearLab's Kenji Mowrey got 3,790 Wh out of the DELTA Pro 3 (review dated 2026-01-13). Outdoor Life's Laura Lancaster got 2,290 Wh out of the Apex 300 running a dehumidifier. The Solar Lab measured about 20 W idle on the Apex 300 and about 58 W on the Explorer 5000 Plus with the inverter on. The Solar Lab's Explorer 5000 Plus also struggled to start an air conditioner with a 10,000 W surge. We could not find a named test of delivered energy for the Explorer 5000 Plus or the F3800 Plus, or a named idle measurement with a stated method for the DELTA Pro 3 or the F3800 Plus. For those, use the rated figures and the arithmetic above.

Availability on 2026-11-15: BLUETTI's Apex 300 page says the unit is expected to ship in late January, though the BLUETTI store listed the Apex 300 + B300K bundle as available. Jackery lists the Explorer 5000 Plus as a pre-order shipping Nov 28. The DELTA Pro 3 did not appear in EcoFlow's US store search.

Not recommended

Don't buy a single Anker SOLIX F3800 Plus for its 6,000 W. On its own it gives 3,300 W, which is less than an Apex 300 or a DELTA Pro 3. It also drops its 240 V output while charging from a 120 V outlet. On 2026-11-15 Anker listed the station alone as sold out and offered it only in a $4,799.99 Smart Home Power Kit.
