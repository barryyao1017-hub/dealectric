What the differences mean

  • Price gap: $119.99 − $59.99 = $60 more for the FLEX-DARK than the FLEX-M, and $79.99 − $59.99 = $20 more for the FLEX-M2. All three get the same 100 free SD photos a month, so the extra money pays for the camera, not the service.
  • Recovery: 65.7 ÷ 3.6 ≈ 18× longer between pictures on the FLEX-M. An animal that stays in front of the camera for one minute gets 1 picture on the FLEX-M and at most 60 ÷ 3.6 ≈ 16 on the FLEX-DARK.
  • Batteries per year (Trailcampro's daily day-and-night test on Energizer Ultimate Lithium): FLEX-DARK 12 ÷ 3.9 ≈ 3.1 sets × 8 = about 25 AA; FLEX-M 12 ÷ 3.3 ≈ 3.6 sets × 8 = about 29 AA. Outdoor Life's FLEX-M2 on Duracell AA: 12 ÷ 1.5 = 8 sets × 8 = 64 AA.
  • Free plan per day: 100 ÷ 30 ≈ 3.3 photos a day reach your phone. SPYPOINT says photos past the limit are still saved to the SD card.
  • If you want HD on the phone: Standard billed yearly is $7 × 12 = $84 a year for 1,000 HD photos a month. A Full-HD pack costs $5 ÷ 50 = $0.10 a photo.

Trailcampro's overall score is higher for the FLEX-M, mostly because of its detection score (93 vs 86) and its 120 ft detection range. The FLEX-DARK wins on recovery, night flash, battery life and picture quality, and those are what count once the camera sits on a free plan saving most of its photos to the SD card. SPYPOINT's FLEX-M page says the camera placed first in Trailcampro's 2024 flash-range shootout. In the 2026 shootout, where every camera was shot on the same night with fresh batteries and ranked on illumination out to 130 ft, it placed #33 of 40.

Pixel count doesn't change what reaches your phone. SPYPOINT's plan grid labels Free and Basic photos 'SD' and saves HD for the Standard and Premium plans. Hook & Barrel's Kenneth Piper Jr. writes that even Full-HD requests from the FLEX-M2 arrive compressed, not as the 32 MP originals. The FLEX-M2 has no Trailcampro lab review. In Outdoor Life's field test, Alex Robinson's unit caught him at 10 and 60 ft but not at 110 ft, sometimes took a few hours to send a photo, and ran through a set of Duracell AAs in about a month and a half.

Check coverage at your stand before buying. Game & Fish's Drew Warden lists the FLEX-M's two SIMs as AT&T and Verizon, and TechGearLab's Chris Maxcer says the FLEX-M needs at least 1–2 steady bars at the camera site.

Not recommended

SPYPOINT FLEX-RANGE, if the point is no subscription: SPYPOINT's plan grid marks the Free plan 'Not available on FLEX-RANGE'. Moultrie cellular cameras: Moultrie's plans page states that all cellular connected devices require a paid subscription, starting at $8.99 a month for 1,000 images.
