Who it's for

iPhone 18 Pro and 17-series owners who want the fastest wired charge from a small charger.

Caveats

  • The AVS and iPhone 18 Pro 60 W figures are Anker's claims. No named reviewer has measured them on an iPhone.
  • Anker's spec sheet lists PD3.1. Apple's main text names USB-PD 3.2, but its iPhone 18 Pro footnote accepts USB PD 3.1 or better with AVS.
  • Anker says output lowers after about 20 minutes at 65 W and does not say to what level.
  • PPS stops at 11 V, so the 20 V PPS mode of the Galaxy S26 Ultra and Pixel Pro XL is out of reach.
  • Anker's own US store marks the current price as a sale with no end date.
  • The A121D Nano Charger (45W, Smart Display) is a different model. Match A120M.
