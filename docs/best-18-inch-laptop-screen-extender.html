What the differences mean

Where the line was drawn: a row is in the table if its screens are 18 to 18.5 inches, or if its maker states a laptop fit range that reaches 18 inches. "Triple" on the Aura and FOPO rows means three added screens (four with the laptop's own); "dual" means two added screens. Dealectric does not test units: every figure here is a maker's or a named reviewer's, read 2026-11-21.

  • Sharpness. 18.5-inch 1920×1080: √(1920² + 1080²) = 2,203 pixels on the diagonal ÷ 18.5 in = 119 ppi. 16-inch 1920×1200: 2,264 ÷ 16 = 142 ppi. Per screen that is 2,073,600 pixels against 2,304,000 (2,304,000 ÷ 2,073,600 = 1.11), so a 16-inch panel shows 11% more on 21% less glass (115.1 sq in ÷ 146.2 sq in = 0.79).
  • Power per screen. Aura Triple Boost: 45 W ÷ 3 = 15 W. Aura Dual Boost: 30 W ÷ 2 = 15 W. Aura Triple Aero: 100 W − 65 W for the laptop = 35 W left for three screens.
  • Outlets. The Triple Boost does not charge the laptop (Aura), so it takes two outlets: its adapter and the laptop's charger. The Triple Aero takes one if the laptop is content with 65 W; a laptop whose own charger is rated above 65 W charges more slowly from it or keeps its own charger plugged in.
  • Price per added screen at Aura's US store (sale prices, lists $729.00, $799.00 and $599.00). Triple Boost $579.00 ÷ 3 = $193.00; Triple Aero $629.00 ÷ 3 = $209.67; Dual Boost $489.00 ÷ 2 = $244.50.
  • Desk width. An 18.5-inch 16:9 panel is 16.12 in wide. Two side screens around an 18-inch 16:10 laptop panel (15.26 in wide) come to 2 × 16.12 + 15.26 = 47.5 in of glass before bezels and hinges. This is geometry from the diagonals, not a maker's dimension.
  • Weight per screen. FOPO S999: 4 kg ÷ 3 = 1.33 kg (FOPO). UPERFECT Delta Max Touch: 2.36 kg ÷ 2 = 1.18 kg (TechRadar).

Driver or native video: what each 18.5-inch extender asks of your laptop

Every 18.5-inch extender with two or three screens that was in stock at its maker's store on 2026-11-21 is a driver product with its own power supply. Aura writes "One quick driver install on first use" for the Triple Boost and Dual Boost, which also connect over USB-A, a port that carries no native video. FOPO's S999 data says it is not driver-free and that macOS must allow its UsbDisplay driver. The driver has to be installed before the screens show anything, so a laptop you cannot install software on rules these rows out.

Native video needs one video output per added desktop. The FOPO S11 takes one full-featured USB-C cable per screen, or USB-C for one screen and HDMI for the other, and FOPO says it does not support base M1, M2 or M3 MacBooks. Apple's specification for the M1 MacBook Air lists one external display. The UPERFECT Delta Max needs no driver (Julie Strietelmeier, The Gadgeteer), but on one USB-C cable Alastair Jennings of TechRadar saw both panels "mirroring the contents of the MacBook" and wrote that "you will need three cables to provide two separate video feeds and additional power".

Power is the second limit. Sarfraz Khan of Wccftech saw the Delta Max's panels cycle on and off above 50% brightness on a single USB-C cable until the included 45 W adapter was connected, and The Gadgeteer's unit shut down above 50% the same way. Aura sizes its adapters at 30 W for two 18.5-inch screens and 45 W for three, and its 65 W charger listing says the Triple Aero Pro Studio 18.5 "needs 100W". These are desk screens on mains power.

Left out for missing the size line: the Mobile Pixels Trio 3 Pro and Duex Float 2 Pro (Mobile Pixels states 15–16-inch laptops) and the FOPO S10 and S7 (FOPO states up to 17.3 inches). Aura's Triple Go Studio 18.5 ($609.00 at Aura's US store, 2026-11-21) is a fourth 18.5-inch driver model that also requires a video-capable USB-C port and lists macOS and Windows only. The INVZI MagHub Quad Max has three 18.5-inch 1080p screens, a Racertech driver that macOS needs installed by hand once, a recommended 45 W adapter and a stated fit of 12 to 18-inch laptops (Monica J. White, New Atlas); it has no row because we have no INVZI store price dated today.

Not recommended

The UPERFECT Delta Max 18.5 as a one-cable, two-desktop extender. It is the only driver-free 18.5-inch pair here, but one cable gives the same picture on both panels (TechRadar), two desktops take two video outputs on the laptop plus the power adapter, and TechRadar measured 89% sRGB on the Touch version. On 2026-11-21 UPERFECT's own store showed the touch listing at $499.99 as sold out, with the note "Pre-orders will ship at the end of October". Aura's driver-free Single Aero Pro Studio 18.5 was also sold out at Aura's US store on the same date.
