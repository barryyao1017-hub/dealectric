Who it's for

Full-time or long-stay boondockers who want the coach's own outlets, lights, pump and fans running from solar.

Caveats

  • The 12 V output is 100 A. BLUETTI says leveling systems that draw more than 100 A at startup need a B1210 or the coach's existing 12 V lead-acid battery, and neither is in the box.
  • Panels must be wired in parallel (PV100 FX 41.4 V Voc against the 50 V limit). The box list includes no Y-branch connectors, and BLUETTI's own Y-branch pair was out of stock on 2026-11-13.
  • The kit page does not name the 100 W panel model, so PV100 FX figures are inferred from it being BLUETTI's RV panel.
  • PV2 is shared with the alternator charger, so with alternator charging fitted, only PV1 takes solar.
