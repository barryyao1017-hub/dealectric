What the differences mean

  • The fridge: 363 kWh/yr ÷ 365 = 0.995 kWh a day, and 995 Wh ÷ 24 h = 41.4 W on average. That is ENERGY STAR's figure for an 18 cu ft top-freezer model such as the Crown CRNREF18WT. Other 18–20.5 cu ft top-freezer models on the same results page use 363–390 kWh/yr, so redo the sums with your own model's figure.
  • Fridge hours: rated Wh × 0.80 ÷ 41.4 W. For the Elite 200 V2 that is 2,073.6 × 0.80 = 1,659 Wh, and 1,659 ÷ 41.4 = 40.1 h. For the Elite 100 V2 it is 1,024 × 0.80 = 819 Wh, and 819 ÷ 41.4 = 19.8 h. For the Apex 300 it is 2,764.8 × 0.80 = 2,212 Wh, and 2,212 ÷ 41.4 = 53.4 h.
  • Rated size you need: hours × 41.4 W ÷ 0.80. That gives 24 h → 1,242 Wh, 48 h → 2,484 Wh and 72 h → 3,726 Wh.
  • Cost per usable Wh: the Elite 200 V2 costs $899 ÷ 1,659 Wh = $0.54. The Jackery Explorer 2000 v2 costs $999 ÷ 1,634 Wh = $0.61, and it is on pre-order. The Elite 100 V2 costs $499 ÷ 819 Wh = $0.61.
  • Cost per fridge-day: the Elite 200 V2 costs $899 ÷ 1.67 days = $538. The Elite 100 V2 costs $499 ÷ 0.82 days = $609. The Apex 300 costs $1,499 ÷ 2.22 days = $675, but it is the one that can grow with an add-on battery.

Why a fridge drains a power station faster than its label says

A power station's inverter uses some power just by being switched on, whatever you plug in. At a 500 W load that overhead hardly shows: Techreviewer (Timo Altmeyer) drew 946 Wh from the 1,024 Wh BLUETTI Elite 100 V2. At loads in the tens of watts, the same review found usable capacity fell to about 80%. The Solar Lab also lists 80% efficiency for that unit and calculates a 16 W idle draw. That is about 39% of the 41.4 W an efficient fridge averages, which is why fridge backup gets the low figure. OutdoorGearLab measured 1 kWh-class stations at 85–87% of their rating, but its pages don't state the test load, so those figures don't show what a station gives at fridge-level loads.

Plan on the low-load figure, not the high-load test results: the 98% APH Networks measured at 987 W would overstate the Elite 200 V2's fridge runtime by about 9 hours (2,028 ÷ 41.4 = 49 h, against 40 h).
