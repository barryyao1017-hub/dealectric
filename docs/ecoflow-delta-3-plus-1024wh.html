Who it's for

Buyers who want the DELTA 3 platform with faster laptop charging, double the solar input or a USB-connected UPS for a NAS or desktop; otherwise the plain DELTA 3 is the same battery and inverter.

Caveats

  • Same shared Amazon listing as the DELTA 3: the Style menu also offers DELTA 3, DELTA 3 Classic, DELTA 3 1500, DELTA 3 Max and bundles. Check that 'DELTA 3 Plus' is the selected style; the DELTA 3 Classic and DELTA 3 1500 are different models.
  • The expansion port and the list of compatible extra batteries are the same as the plain DELTA 3's; what the Plus adds is the 140 W USB-C, 36 W USB-A, the second 500 W solar input and the wired UPS link. The PC/NAS link needs a USB-C data cable and the Power Manager software.
  • The DELTA 3 series replaces the discontinued DELTA 2 (same 1,024 Wh / 1,800 W class); the DELTA 2 extra battery still works with it.
