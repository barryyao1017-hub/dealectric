Free course maps vs Garmin Golf

Every Garmin Approach includes preloaded maps for 43,000+ courses with distances to the green and hazards. Garmin Golf ($9.99 a month or $99.99 a year) adds full-color CourseView maps with touch targeting, green contours and PlaysLike distances on watches without them built in (Garmin). Amazfit's golf maps on the Balance 2 are free in the Zepp app.

Fitness tracking the rest of the week

Wrist heart rate is what makes sleep tracking, training load and recovery scores possible. Among Garmin's Approach watches, the S50, S70 and S72 have it; the S12, S42 and S44 count steps only (Garmin). The Balance 2 also has HYROX race modes, offline hiking maps and 10 ATM water resistance.
