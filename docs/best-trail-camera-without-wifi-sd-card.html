What the differences mean

  • Night range for the money: the T5NG Pro placed 29 spots above the DS-4K (31 − 2 = 29) and costs $80 less ($179.95 − $99.95 = $80).
  • Detection: Trailcampro measured 120 ft on the T5NG Pro against 80 ft on the DS-4K, so it detects 50% farther away (120 ÷ 80 = 1.5).
  • Trigger: the T5NG Pro fires 0.43 s later than the S-4K (0.51 − 0.08 = 0.43 s). Where animals cross a narrow trail quickly, the Bushnells (0.08 to 0.15 s) and the Browning (0.18 s) catch them sooner.
  • AA cells a year, photo mode (35 day + 35 night photos a day): T5NG Pro 8 × 12 ÷ 29.8 months = 3.2; DS-4K 6 × 12 ÷ 25 = 2.9; Browning 6 × 12 ÷ 22.5 = 3.2; S-4K 6 × 12 ÷ 21.5 = 3.3.
  • AA cells a year, video mode (15 day + 15 night ten-second clips a day): T5NG Pro 8 × 12 ÷ 6 = 16; DS-4K 6 × 12 ÷ 3.7 = 19.5; Browning 6 × 12 ÷ 2.8 = 25.7; S-4K 6 × 12 ÷ 2.4 = 30.

How the 2026 night flash test ranks these cameras

Trailcampro shot all 40 cameras on the same night, under the same moon, sky and tree canopy, with fresh batteries. It ranked the photos first on illumination across the whole frame out to 130 ft, then on clarity and contrast, then on hot spots. Red-glow, low-glow and no-glow cameras share one list. Trailcampro calls this one of the few tests it runs where human judgment comes in, and it publishes positions, not distances.

Bushnell's manual gives the S-4K 110 ft and the DS-4K 120 ft of night range only in its 'Long Range' illumination mode, and in that mode night videos stop at 15 s. Trailcampro lists 'limited flash range' as a con on both. GardePro rates the E5 2.0 at 100 ft of 940 nm no-glow night vision with a 0.1 s trigger, but no named lab has published a test of it. GardePro's A60 listing calls that camera the '2026 Non-Cellular Flash Range Winner'. Trailcampro's shootout page names the T5NG Pro for that category and does not include the A60 among its 40 entries.

No-Wi-Fi check: Trailcampro files the T5NG Pro as Non-Cellular, separate from GardePro's WiFi T6LRW. GardePro titles the E5S 'Non-Cellular, Non-WiFi'. The E5 2.0 title says only 'Non-Cellular'. Security box: the T5NG Pro and E5 2.0 take GardePro's MB300 box. The MB200 fits the E5S and the original E5, not the 2.0. Trailcampro notes the T5NG Pro has no hole for locking its latch.

Not recommended

Bushnell Core DS-4K for night use. On 2026-11-14 it was $179.95 and out of stock at Bushnell, the most expensive camera here. It placed 31st of 40 in the night flash test, and Trailcampro found it records only 14-second videos in the dark. It does have the best Bushnell battery figures (25 months on photos), so it suits buyers who want dual-sensor 4K mostly in daylight and can find it in stock.
