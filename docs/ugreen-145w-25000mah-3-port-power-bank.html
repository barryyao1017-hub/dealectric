Who it's for

Anyone with a USB-C laptop from a 13-inch MacBook Air to a 16-inch MacBook Pro who wants one full top-up and flies with it.

Caveats

  • Your laptop sets the real charge rate: a MacBook Air draws about 70 W no matter what the port can give.
  • Reviewers saw the laptop port slow to about 65 W once the bank fell near 25%.
  • With all three ports in use the two secondary ports drop to about 20 W each.
  • 65 W input means roughly 2 hours to refill; the Anker Prime 26K refills about three times faster.
  • No built-in cable. Bring a 5 A-rated USB-C cable or you will get 100 W at most.
