What a panel on a balcony really produces

Less than on a roof. A panel hung flat against a railing loses roughly 30% against one tilted toward the sun (Jackery's estimate), and a north-facing balcony gets a fraction of that; no one we found has measured a railing-hung folding panel over a day. Our estimate: 30–60 Wh a day from 100 W on a south or west balcony in good weather: a phone and a laptop's worth, or most of a 288 Wh battery over several sunny days.

Balcony solar that plugs into a wall outlet is a different product

Plug-in "balcony solar" feeds the apartment's wiring through an outlet. Utah's HB 340 (2025) allows systems up to 1,200 W without a utility agreement, and Utah Clean Energy counts nine more states with similar laws by August 2026; UL's standard for them (UL 3700) launched in January 2026. A power station and panel never connect to the building's wiring, so none of that applies to it.

Lease and safety

Read the lease's clause on balcony items and railings, and tie the panel down against wind. The FDNY's lithium battery guidance applies indoors: use certified equipment, don't run it through extension cords or power strips, and don't charge it unattended overnight.
