What the differences mean

  • Battery-only reserve, Eco Ultra: 21.6 Wh × 1% ≈ 0.22 Wh a day, so a full battery lasts about 100 days. Michael Barton (techtest.org) puts it at 2–3 months, or 1–2 months at a busy spot.
  • Battery-only reserve, Argus 4 Pro: Barton measured about one month without the solar panel at a lightly to moderately busy spot, against Reolink's rating of about 2 months at 300 s of recording a day.
  • A three-week freeze: 21 days × 1% = 21% of the Eco Ultra's battery. The same stretch uses most of the Argus 4 Pro's measured reserve of about one month.
  • Panel size: 6 W ÷ 2.2 W = 2.7 times the S340's panel rating. 6 W ÷ 2.5 W = 2.4 times the most the C660 accepts from its panel.
  • Refill after a freeze: 21.6 Wh ÷ 6 W = 3.6 hours of full sun to refill the Eco Ultra from empty, before charging losses.
  • Tapo's 45-minute claim: 0.75 h × 2.5 W ≤ 1.9 Wh. Tapo measured it at 1,000 W/m² and 77°F (25°C), which is clear-sky noon, not a winter day.

Why solar cameras stop charging in the cold

Lithium batteries are damaged by charging below freezing, so the makers block it. Reolink states that with its Solar Panel (3 W), Solar Panel 2 (6 W) or Solar Panel 3 (12 W), the charging temperature is 32°F to 113°F. Below that, charging pauses to protect the battery and resumes once it warms up. Tapo's C660 spec sheet gives a 32°F to 113°F charging range and a -4°F to 113°F operating range. eufy's cold-weather support article says its battery devices work from -4°F to 122°F but "will fail to recharge" at 32°F.

So each camera keeps recording below the point where its panel can charge it: 18°F lower on the Reolink models (operating to 14°F) and 36°F lower on the Tapo and eufy models (to -4°F). On days that stay below freezing, the panel adds nothing, and the battery's reserve decides whether the camera is still recording when the thaw comes. That is why the Eco Ultra is the pick. Of the four, it has the largest published battery (21.6 Wh), and it showed the slowest drain in a named reviewer's test. Before a forecast freeze, top the battery up to 100% over the camera's USB-C port. Both Reolink cameras charge over USB-C. For long sub-freezing spells, Reolink sells the Solar Panel 2S. Its own 5,900 mAh (42.48 Wh) battery powers the camera directly from 32°F down to -4°F (-20°C), and the panel recharges that battery from 14°F. Reolink lists the 2S for its battery cameras except the Reolink Argus, Keen and Doorbell (Battery), so ask Reolink to confirm your Argus-series model is covered before you buy one.

Not recommended for long freezes: Tapo C660 Kit

Tapo rates the C660 at 120 days, but Michael Barton (techtest.org) measured a runtime of about one month in his March 2026 test. The camera also accepts at most 2.5 W from its panel. That combination leaves the least margin once charging stops at 32°F. In a mild climate it remains a capable pan-tilt camera. eufy's S340 also has a small panel (2.2 W). Its 3-month rating is eufy's own figure at 300 seconds of recording a day; no reviewer has measured it without sun. On 2026-11-10 Abt listed the S340 at $159.99, against $219.99 on eufy's store.
