Who it's for

Tabletop and miniature printers who want fine detail on a small footprint and don't need a large build plate.

Caveats

  • The 6.5 × 2.8 in plate is small. Batch printing or large busts need a 10-inch or 15-inch printer.
  • It has a single linear rail; the older Sonic Mini 8K used dual rails.
  • The LCD is a consumable. A replacement screen costs $90 at Phrozen (2026-11-11).
