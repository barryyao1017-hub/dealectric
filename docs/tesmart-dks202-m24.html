Who it's for

Two DisplayPort monitors shared by two PCs, where windows should stay put when you switch. Gaming at 4K 120–144 Hz works if your graphics card and monitors support DSC.

Caveats

  • 4K 144 Hz over DisplayPort 1.4 needs Display Stream Compression (DSC) from the graphics card and the monitor. Without it, expect about 4K 120 Hz.
  • Each PC needs two video connections (TESmart FAQ).
  • One owner (TESmart Community, 2026-03-25) reported windows moving after a Windows lock and unlock despite EDID emulation.
