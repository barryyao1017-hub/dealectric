What the labs measured, and where they disagree

SoundGuys plays music through each pair with noise cancelling on, peaking at 75 dB SPL, until the battery runs out. RTINGS runs its own continuous playback test (Test Bench 2.3). For three of these headphones the two labs roughly agree: AirPods Max 2 at 21.0 h and 22 h, Momentum 4 at 56.4 h and 62.3 h, and the Sony at 37.2 h and 31.75 h. For the Bose they don't. SoundGuys stopped at 27 h 12 min, RTINGS reached 37 h, and Bose's own footnote says 24 h. The Sony is the only flagship here that both labs measured above 31 hours with noise cancelling on. That is why it wins when the two noise cancelling scores are tied.

On noise cancelling, SoundGuys gives the Sony and the Bose the same 8.7/10, so battery, sound and price decide between them. The AirPods Max 2 measured highest: 89.4% of outside noise removed by perceived loudness and a 9.0/10 score, which beats 98% of over-ears SoundGuys has tested. The Marshall Monitor III A.N.C. cut noise by 83% on average (8.3/10). The soundcore Space One makes noise sound about one-quarter as loud at its strongest setting (8.0/10). The Momentum 4 cut low-frequency noise by about 75% (7.6/10), the weakest of the six. On SoundGuys' predicted sound score, the Sony leads at 4.8, ahead of the Bose at 4.7 and the Momentum 4 at 4.6.
