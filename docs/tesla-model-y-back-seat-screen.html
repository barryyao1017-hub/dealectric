What the differences mean

  • The factory screen comes with a trim, not as an option. Premium RWD $45,990 − Standard RWD $39,990 = $6,000. Premium AWD $49,990 − Standard AWD $41,990 = $8,000 (The Charge Port MSRPs). That step also pays for the rest of the Premium equipment. We don't sell this screen; it comes with the car.
  • An add-on screen costs far less, but only fits older cars. Hansshow H86 $359 ÷ $6,000 = 6% of the cheapest factory step. Hansshow lists it for Model Y builds through 2025.02 only.
  • The projector route. AutoCinema $119.99 + ZIP Pro $629.99 = $749.98. With the ZIP instead: $119.99 + $259.99 = $379.98.
  • Battery. ZIP Pro: 2 h 10 min = 130 min of movie in Normal mode (Notebookcheck). ZIP: just over 60 min in Eco (Gizmodo), so about half as long.
  • Picture size per metre. Notebookcheck got 57 in (16:9, about 1.26 m wide) from 1.59 m. 1.59 ÷ 1.26 ≈ 1.26 throw ratio, which works out to about 36 in of diagonal per metre between projector and screen.

What an add-on back-seat screen can and can't control

Tesla's manual lists what the factory rear touchscreen controls: climate power, rear fan speed and direction, rear seat heaters, front passenger seat movement, media, video, games and volume. It also pairs up to two sets of Bluetooth headphones. Changing media or volume from the back also changes it for the front cabin. The front seats can lock the rear screen under Controls > Display > Lock Rear Display.

An add-on screen can only switch hardware the car already has. Hansshow's H86 listing says its seat-heat module “requires original car to have heating”. A 2025+ Standard has no rear seat heaters for it to switch on. InsideEVs (Iulian Dnistran) reports that “only the front seats are heated” and that the 8-inch rear passenger touchscreen “has been removed”. InsideEVs' drive review (Andrei Nedelea) adds that the Standard has “no separate climate zone” and that you adjust the air vents by hand. So in a Standard, any rear screen is a video screen only: its comfort controls have nothing to work.

Fit is the other limit. Basenor's fitment guide says the 2025+ rear console is closed around a display, where the older Model Y had an open cubby. Console screens built for 2020–2025.02 cars therefore don't carry over. To check your car, look at the 10th character of the VIN: S means 2025 and T means 2026 (Basenor). A projector screen avoids both limits. Not recommended

Buying an add-on screen for rear seat heat in a 2025+ Standard. The car has no rear seat heaters, and Hansshow's own listing says its module needs them.

The Aurzen ZIP for full-length films on battery. Gizmodo (Jorge Jimenez) got about 45 min in Turbo and just over an hour in Eco. Keep it on USB-C power, or step up to the ZIP Pro, which Notebookcheck timed at 2 h 10 min.
