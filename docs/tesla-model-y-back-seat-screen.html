What fits depends on the model year. Most add-on rear displays are made for the 2020 to early-2025 Model Y; the refreshed 2025–2026 Model Y (Juniper) has far fewer options, because most trims already come with Tesla's own 8-inch rear touchscreen and the add-on displays below were made for the older console.

Short answer. On a 2025–2026 Model Y, for a big picture while parked (camping, charging stops), the Aurzen TravelPlay AutoCinema screen clips into the roof rim and turns the panoramic roof or the rear window into a 47–50 inch screen for any portable projector ($119.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/aurzen-travelplay-autocinema-tesla-model-y, code CJ10 saves $11.99); pair it with the Aurzen ZIP ($199.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/aurzen-zip-tri-fold-mini-projector) or the 1080p ZIP Pro ($579.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/aurzen-zip-pro-tri-fold-1080p-projector, code CJ10 saves $57.99). For a screen that works while driving, a Juniper Standard without a rear screen can take Hansshow's H86 ($399.99), and any Juniper except the Standard can take Hansshow's 18.5-inch flip-down ($1,049). The cheapest fix on any year is a seat-back tablet mount: TESERY's fits the seat gap for $19.99. On a 2020 to early-2025 Model Y, the plug-in rear displays are the usual choice: Teslahubs Rear Display Ultra ($269.99, 2019–2024), TESERY 8.66-inch ($359, to 2025.03) or Tlyard H86 ($359, to 2025.02).

A projector screen and a rear display solve different problems: the projector setup is for parked movies (the screen covers the roof or rear window, and Aurzen says not to use it while driving); a rear display or tablet works on the road.

Which one, by situation

  • Kids watching on the road, 2020–2024 car: a plug-in rear display. Teslahubs is cheapest ($269.99); TESERY and Tlyard add CarPlay or Android Auto. Check the year range before buying; none of these three fits the 2025 refresh.
  • 2025–2026 Juniper with Tesla's rear screen: you already have a rear screen; for a bigger or second screen on the road, a $19.99 seat-back tablet mount is the simple add-on (check your trim against TESERY's list).
  • Juniper Standard (no rear screen): Hansshow's H86 for the Standard ($399.99), or the tablet mount.
  • Movie nights while parked or camping in the car: the Aurzen TravelPlay screen ($119.99; buy: https://dealectric.com/go/aurzen-travelplay-autocinema-tesla-model-y, code CJ10 saves $11.99) with a portable projector; sound goes to the car's speakers over Bluetooth from either Aurzen projector. It needs no wiring and doubles as a roof sunshade.

Aurzen's MagPlay suction mount, which Aurzen suggests for rear-seat projection, showed as unavailable in its US store when we checked; a small tripod on the console or the rear-seat floor works with either ZIP model.
