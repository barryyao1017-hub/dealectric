Who it's for

Buyers who need a small, 12.4 kg enclosed printer for PLA, PETG, TPU and carbon-fiber PLA on a desk 355 mm square. For ABS, ASA or nylon the K2 Pro without CFS ($549 on the same store) is the better pick, with a 300 mm cube volume and a 60 °C heated chamber for $50 more, and the QIDI Plus5 ($699) goes further with a 65 °C chamber and a 370 °C nozzle.

Caveats

  • There is no chamber heater: Clever Creations needed 30 minutes to bring the chamber from 25 °C to its 38 °C maximum, and found a fan that cannot be switched off starts at that point.
  • Clever Creations found the carbon filter to be loose granules with wide gaps and noticed no difference in smell with it on or off, so ABS fumes are better vented outdoors.
  • Clever Creations found plastic extruder gears on its unit and had to partly take the print head apart to swap the nozzle, which is a Creality-only design.
  • Tom's Hardware notes the smooth PEI plate needs glue stick and the rear spool holder makes loading awkward; Creality supplies files for a printable side mount.
  • The 220 × 220 × 250 mm build volume is less than half that of a 300 mm cube printer, and color printing needs a separately sold upgrade kit.
