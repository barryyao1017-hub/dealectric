What the differences mean

Refill hours are the battery's capacity divided by the panel wattage that comes in the kit. This is the best case: full rated sun and no charging loss. A railing-mounted panel will take longer.

  • ALLPOWERS R2500 V2 + 400 W: 1,920 Wh ÷ 400 W = 4.8 h
  • Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 + 400 W: 2,048 Wh ÷ 400 W = 5.1 h
  • BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 + 2× 200 W: 2,080 Wh ÷ 400 W = 5.2 h
  • BLUETTI AC180 + 2× 200 W: 1,152 Wh ÷ 400 W = 2.9 h
  • BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 + 200 W: 2,080 Wh ÷ 200 W = 10.4 h

Why a plug-in balcony battery isn't the US buy yet

A European-style balcony system plugs into a wall outlet and feeds the home's circuits. In the US, the battery sold that way is the EcoFlow STREAM Ultra: 1.92 kWh per unit, expandable to 11.52 kWh. Footnote 6 on EcoFlow's own US page says it "is only available for sale and plug-and-play installation in Utah," under Utah's H.B. 340. The page lists the unit as "UL1741-Certified & IEEE1547-Certified." A single Ultra feeds at most 800 W to the grid; the 1,200 W headline figure needs a STREAM Microinverter or two to six Ultra units. EcoFlow also says DELTA power stations will be able to join a STREAM system "soon," so a DELTA you already own can't be added yet.

California's SB 868 takes effect on January 1, 2027. It allows up to 1,200 W of AC output per home through a standard receptacle. The device must be certified as a plug-in photovoltaic system by UL or an equivalent lab, and must carry a certified feature that isolates it from the building's wiring during an outage. The law says nothing about batteries. UL's plug-in standard is UL 3700, launched January 8, 2026. EcoFlow's own blog calls it "distinct from UL 1741" and doesn't claim it for STREAM. The first plug-in microinverter Hoymiles announced against UL 3700, the HiFlow Pro, has no battery, and Hoymiles calls it "compliant" rather than certified.

Outage backup works the same way on either route. EcoFlow says STREAM "immediately ceases grid-tied output" when the grid fails, and backup power goes only to what is plugged into its own two outlets. A power station does that job without touching the building's wiring: lamps, the router and the fridge plug straight into it, and ALLPOWERS lists a switchover of 15 ms or less for the R2500 V2. Keep any panel you add within the station's input limits. The C2000 Gen 2, for example, accepts 11–60 V on one XT-60i port. If your goal is to cut the bill by feeding the wall, wait for a UL 3700-certified plug-in kit after January 1, 2027.

Not recommended

BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 + one 200 W panel. At $1,199 it has the second-lowest cost per Wh here, but refilling 2,080 Wh from 200 W takes 10.4 hours of full rated sun, more than a balcony gets in a day. The second panel costs $300 more ($1,499 − $1,199) and cuts the refill to 5.2 h. BLUETTI says its 200 W panel ships from November 18.
