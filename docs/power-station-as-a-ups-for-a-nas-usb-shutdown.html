What the differences mean

The runtimes below assume a 30 W NAS. Apollomaniacs measured a 2-bay QNAP TS-269L v2 at 27 to 31 W. They leave out the station's own standby draw, so treat them as ceilings.

  • RIVER 3 Plus, about 8.5 h: 286 Wh × 0.889 = 254 Wh; 254 ÷ 30 W = 8.5 h. The 0.889 is the DELTA 3 Plus's measured output ratio, borrowed. With a PC, display and network gear on it as well (about 340 W at most, by the author's estimate), Apollomaniacs got about 25 minutes from an 85% charge.
  • DELTA 3 Plus, about 30 h: StorageReview measured 910 Wh delivered; 910 ÷ 30 W = 30 h. A DELTA 3 Plus owner in NUT issue #2735 found that NUT's low-battery point matched the app's discharge limit. With a 30% limit, 910 × 0.7 = 637 Wh, and 637 ÷ 30 W = 21 h before the shutdown starts.
  • DELTA 3 Ultra, about 91 h: 3,072 Wh × 0.889 = 2,731 Wh; 2,731 ÷ 30 W = 91 h, using the same borrowed ratio. Capacity cost: $1,299 ÷ 3,072 Wh = $0.42 per Wh.
  • UGREEN US3000, about 1.4 h at most: 43.2 Wh ÷ 30 W = 1.4 h. It is built to carry the NAS to a clean shutdown, not through a long outage; NASCompares quotes up to around ten minutes depending on workload.

Why the NAS shuts down but doesn't come back on

A conventional UPS handles an outage in two steps. First the NAS shuts down, then the UPS switches its outlets off and back on. The Network UPS Tools manual explains why: “the UPS load should be turned off so that all devices that are attached to it are forcibly rebooted”, and “If your UPS just sits there and never resets the load, you are vulnerable to a power race”. An APC Back-UPS ES 650G2 gives NUT the commands for the second step: load.off, load.off.delay and shutdown.reboot.

The EcoFlow stations only do the first step. In NUT issue #2735 the RIVER 3 Plus offers only driver commands: driver.killpower and the three driver.reload variants. On 2026-10-29 an owner running firmware V1.33.81.61 with NUT 2.8.4 reported that the low-battery shutdown ran, but “Shutdown does nothing” and “the AC output stays live for 60+ seconds afterwards”. The same thread calls the DELTA 3 Plus “almost identical”. The issue is still open, with its milestone set to NUT 2.8.6. If mains power returns before the battery runs out, the NAS never loses power, so its “power on when power returns” setting never triggers and it stays off until someone presses the button. If you need to reach the NAS remotely, plan a way to power it on remotely, or use a UPS that resets its outlets.

How the signal reaches the NAS differs by model:

  • RIVER 3 Plus: EcoFlow's US download center lists it as the only device Power Manager supports; on 2026-11-08 the page showed “Coming soon” instead of a download. On Synology, EcoFlow's NUT package serves on port 3496 as device “nutdev1”, while DSM's UPS client expects port 3493 and “ups”. Sandcomp's guide therefore edits the package's files and sets DSM to Synology UPS Server mode. Commenters report a standby boot loop or a NAS that never shut down, while one DS423+ owner on DSM 7.3.2 got it working after a upsmon.conf change. On a QNAP TS-269L v2, Apollomaniacs saw “no reaction on the NAS side”.
  • DELTA 3 Plus: NUT 2.8.3 added “basic support” for it and the RIVER 3 Plus, and NUT's hardware list rates it support level 3 on the usbhid-ups driver. Power Manager doesn't list it, so the NAS has to run NUT 2.8.3 or later itself, or poll a separate machine that does. buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DCC2BVFW?tag=dealectric-20
  • DELTA 3 Ultra / Ultra Plus: EcoFlow sends the UPS data over the USB-C 140 W output port (USB 2.0) and says to “make sure the device (e.g., NAS device, PC, or laptop) supports UPS functionality”. NUT's hardware list does not include it.
  • UGREEN US3000: a DC UPS for UGREEN NAS models with external DC input. In NASCompares' test the NAS “immediately began its shutdown process”, and “After power was restored, the UPS automatically triggered a restart of the NAS”. $109.99: https://dealectric.com/go/ugreen-nas-ups-us3000-120w

Not recommended

BLUETTI AC180 and Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 for this job. Both can keep a NAS running through a brief outage: Anker rates the C1000 Gen 2 under 10 ms, and BLUETTI rates the AC180 at 20 ms or less with Grid Enhancement off. But ComputingForGeeks lists no data port for either brand's stations, so nothing tells the NAS to shut down, and it loses power without warning when the battery runs out.
