Buy the Xbox version: why the same headset name can fail on an Xbox

Turtle Beach explains that Xbox consoles 'do not support Bluetooth audio for game sound or chat'. The console uses its own Xbox Wireless protocol instead. So a wireless headset works on an Xbox only if it has that radio built in, like Microsoft's own Xbox Wireless Headset, or comes with a dongle made for Xbox. Several popular headsets are sold in two versions that look almost the same, and only one of them carries the Xbox link.

  • Turtle Beach Stealth 600 Gen 3: Turtle Beach Support says 'The Stealth 600 Gen 3 for PlayStation will NOT work on any Xbox console', and that the Xbox version 'will work on both Xbox and PlayStation'. If you have both consoles, buy the Xbox version. It doesn't work the other way round.
  • SteelSeries Arctis Nova 5 family: SteelSeries says 'The Arctis Nova 5X is the only Nova 5 model built for Xbox' and 'The Nova 5 and Nova 5P won't work with Xbox—even if you'd purchase the Nova 5X dongle separately.' A spare or second-hand 5X dongle won't convert a Nova 5. On the 5X, set the dongle's switch to Xbox for Xbox Series X|S or Xbox One, and to USB for PC or PlayStation. If the switch is in the wrong position, the headset won't connect to the console.
  • Xbox Wireless Headset: it pairs directly with the console. Microsoft lists Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Windows 10/11 and Bluetooth phones, and PlayStation is not on that list. Microsoft also notes that 'some voice chat features may require XBOX Game Pass'.
  • Wired headsets: these plug into the controller's 3.5 mm jack. Game audio and chat both run through the controller (Turtle Beach). Microsoft's Xbox Wire post says any 3.5 mm stereo headset that uses the CTIA plug standard gets stereo game audio through its adapter. EKSA's E1000 WT manual adds that the original Xbox One controller needs that Microsoft adapter, and the manual's only Xbox route is the 3.5 mm cable.

Not recommended

  • Stealth 600 Gen 3 for PlayStation for an Xbox owner: same name and price, but Turtle Beach Support says it will not work on any Xbox console.
  • Arctis Nova 5 or Nova 5P for an Xbox owner: SteelSeries says neither works on Xbox, even with a 5X dongle.
  • HyperX Cloud III Wireless as a wireless upgrade from the wired Cloud III: HyperX lists it for 'PC, PS5, PS4, Nintendo Switch' only, with no Xbox. It was also out of stock at HyperX's US store on 2026-11-17, where it is marked discontinued.
