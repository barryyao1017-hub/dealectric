What the differences mean

Dealectric does not test units. Every figure on this page is either a named reviewer's measurement or the maker's rating. The derived figures below are our arithmetic.

Idle loss per day = idle draw × 24 h ÷ energy delivered. Days to drain = energy delivered ÷ that daily loss, with the inverter on and nothing plugged in.

  • Anker SOLIX S5000: 10 W × 24 h = 240 Wh; 240 ÷ 4,870 = 4.9% a day; 4,870 ÷ 240 = about 20 days.
  • EcoFlow DELTA Pro Ultra X: 36 W × 24 h = 864 Wh; 864 ÷ 12,288 Wh rated = 7.0% a day; about 14 days.
  • BLUETTI Apex 300: 19.5 W × 24 h = 468 Wh; 468 ÷ 2,290 = 20% a day; about 4.9 days.
  • EcoFlow DELTA 3 Ultra: 3,072 Wh × 0.82 = 2,519 Wh delivered; 22 W × 24 h = 528 Wh; 528 ÷ 2,519 = 21% a day; about 4.8 days.
  • Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus: 58 W × 24 h = 1,392 Wh; 1,392 ÷ 4,450 = 31% a day; about 3.2 days. On HOBOTECH's figure of 2,060 Wh a day, it is 46%, about 2.2 days.
  • OUPES Mega 1: 1,779 W × 27 ÷ 60 h = 800 Wh delivered at a heavy load.
  • Tesla Powerwall 3: 1.5 to 2.64 kWh a day (110 W × 24 h = 2.64 kWh) ÷ 13.5 kWh = 11–20% a day, from owner logs.

Cost per kWh for the two context rows: DELTA Pro Ultra X, $7,899 ÷ 12.288 kWh = $643/kWh before a transfer panel. Powerwall 3, about $15,000 installed ÷ 13.5 kWh = about $1,110/kWh.

Why idle draw decides a long outage

A plug-in battery's inverter uses power whenever it is switched on, even when nothing is plugged in. In a two-hour outage that hardly matters. In a three-day outage, with a fridge cycling on and off, the inverter's own draw can be one of the biggest loads in the house. Andrew Riha (pv magazine) left the S5000's 120 V output on with nothing connected, and it lost 5% in 24 hours, close to Anker's 8.8 W claim. The Solar Lab measured 58 W on the Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus. HOBOTECH logged 515 Wh in 6 hours on the same model, which it put at 85 W. Tristan Rayner (ESS News) watched an Apex 300 draw about 19.5 W with its inverters on, in line with BLUETTI's 20 W figure. The Solar Lab measured about 36 W on the DELTA Pro Ultra X and about 22 W on the DELTA 3 Ultra.

On a wired system, the efficiency figure to use is Tesla's 89% round trip for Powerwall 3 (25°C, beginning of life, 3.3 kW). The 97.5% figure Tesla also publishes is solar-to-home efficiency, which skips the battery.

The fix costs nothing: switch the AC inverter off when nothing needs it. The OUPES Mega 1 does this itself after 6 hours with no load, and you can change that timer in the app.
