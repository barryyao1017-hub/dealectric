Who it's for

MacBook Pro M4 Pro or M4 Max owners who want a silent enclosure for a PCIe 4.0 drive such as the Samsung 990 Pro.

Caveats

  • On a base M4 MacBook Pro the Thunderbolt 4 port caps it near 3,800 MB/s.
  • The included cable is short, about 20 cm.
  • PCIe 5.0 SSDs must average 11 W or less; OWC names the Samsung 9100 Pro as a passing example.
  • No USB 3.x fallback is claimed; PCs need a Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 5 or USB4 port.
