What the differences mean

Each figure uses the maker's own ratings for a day of 6 hours on the most accurate GPS mode plus 18 hours of normal wear: days per charge = 1 ÷ (6 ÷ GPS hours + 18 ÷ smartwatch hours).

  • Amazfit T-Rex 3: 1 ÷ (6/42 + 18/648) = 5.9 days.
  • Amazfit T-Rex 3 Pro 48 mm: 1 ÷ (6/38 + 18/600) = 5.3 days.
  • Amazfit T-Rex 3 Pro 44 mm: 1 ÷ (6/26 + 18/408) = 3.6 days.
  • Garmin Instinct 3 Solar 45 mm, no sun: 1 ÷ (6/24 + 18/672) = 3.6 days. With Garmin's solar ratings: 1 ÷ (6/40) = 6.7 days.
  • Garmin Instinct 3 Solar 50 mm, no sun: 1 ÷ (6/34 + 18/960) = 5.1 days. With Garmin's solar ratings: 1 ÷ (6/60) = 10 days.
  • Garmin fēnix 8 Pro 51 mm AMOLED, gesture display: 1 ÷ (6/53 + 18/648) = 7.1 days, the longest here.
  • Apple Watch Ultra 4: 1 ÷ (6/18 + 18/50) = 1.4 days. With Max Extended Workout (45 h): 1 ÷ (6/45 + 18/50) = 2.0 days.

Why the Instinct 3 Solar's battery depends on the sun

Garmin's manual rates the Instinct 3 Solar 'unlimited' in smartwatch mode, 'assuming all-day wear with 3 hours per day outside in 50,000 lux conditions'. Its with-solar multi-band GPS figures (40 h for the 45 mm, 60 h for the 50 mm) assume 'use outside in 50,000 lux conditions'. Without that light, the 45 mm is rated 28 days and 24 h of multi-band GPS, close to the T-Rex 3's 27 days but well short of its 42 h. Garmin's own blog says solar is 'not intended as the primary charging source' and advises not to cover the watch with clothing.

DC Rainmaker measured what the sun actually adds. A 50 mm Instinct 3 Solar went from 20% to 40% in about 3 h 45 min on a mostly sunny winter day (328k lux-hours), and a second unit went from 20% to 38%. That is a real gain for someone working outdoors. An office wearer, or anyone with the watch under a cuff, should plan on the no-sun figures.

The Amazfit figures hold up in testing with the screen kept quiet. OutdoorGearLab got about 40 hours of GPS recording from the T-Rex 3 with the always-on display off and no maps, against its 42-hour rating. Notebookcheck measured 4% per hour in high-accuracy GPS on a bike ride, about 25 hours. On that drain the same day pattern gives 1 ÷ (6/25 + 18/648) = 3.7 days. Even then it matches the Instinct 3 Solar 45 mm's no-sun 3.6 days.

Not recommended

The Amazfit T-Rex 3 Pro 44 mm is a poor choice for multi-day trips. It costs $399.99 on Amazfit's store against $279.99 for the T-Rex 3, but it is rated for 26 h of accurate GPS and 17 days of typical use. That gives 3.6 days per charge at 6 h of GPS a day, against 5.9 for the cheaper T-Rex 3. Pick it only for a smaller wrist on day trips.
