How long 400 W of folding panels takes to refill 1 kWh

About 3.5–4.5 hours of strong midday sun. Folding panels give 70–80% of their rating: one test of Anker's 400 W panel peaked at 368 W and averaged 285 W over a clear June day, and a review refilled a 1,056 Wh station from a 400 W panel in 2 h 40 min at 350–380 W. Under overcast skies expect 80–120 W, so most of a day.

One 400 W panel or two 200 W panels

One panel means one cable and one thing to aim, but ALLPOWERS' SP037 is a two-hand panel at 18.5 kg (a 2025 revision sold under the same model is 13.8 kg with a 36.5 V open-circuit voltage; buyers report receiving either). Two 200 W panels split the weight and can face the sun separately; on the AC180 they go in parallel inside its 500 W input.

Why the input limit matters

A station only takes what its solar input allows. The P1800 (650 W), Anker C1000 Gen 2 (600 W), AC180 (500 W) and DELTA 3 Plus (1,000 W) take a full 400 W; the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 tops out at 400 W, so a larger panel adds nothing there.
