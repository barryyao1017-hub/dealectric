What a HYROX race mode does

A HYROX race is eight 1 km runs, each followed by a workout station, with the Roxzone transitions in between. A HYROX race mode tracks the runs and stations as one race session; with a generic interval or strength mode you sort the laps out afterwards.

Battery and phones

Both Amazfit watches work with iPhone and Android in the Zepp app with no subscription for workout data. On iPhone you get notifications and calls but can't reply to messages from the watch.
