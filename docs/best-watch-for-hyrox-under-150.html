Manual or automatic: what 'HYROX race mode' means on each watch

A HYROX race is eight 1 km runs, each followed by a workout station. A race mode keeps each run and each station as its own split. On every watch here under $150, the mode moves to the next segment only when you press a button. Miss a press and that run and station are recorded as one split. The5krunner reports that Zepp's roadmap brings automatic switching to the flagships (Balance 3, Balance Ultra, Cheetah 2 Pro and Ultra, the T-Rex 3 range, T-Rex Ultra 2, Active 3 Premium) in October 2026. The Balance 2, Balance XT and Active Max follow in November, and the Bip Max in December 2026. The Active 2 (Round and Square) and Bip 6 get swimming improvements in September and no HYROX transition date at all. the5krunner writes that automatic switching 'should end the need to press lap between every run and station'. This is a published schedule, not a shipped update, so check the Zepp app's release notes before you count on it.

COROS's Hybrid Fitness mode on the PACE 3 already offers both modes. With Auto-Transition, COROS says 'your watch will automatically detect when you've completed a run segment or training station'. With Manual Transition you press the Back/Lap button. The mode is not flawless either. the5krunner tested it on an APEX 2 Pro and wrote 'I press the lap/advance button correctly'. Some leg times were still recorded wrongly, and every rep count read zero.

Pace is the other limit. HYROX runs are indoors, and Amazfit's own blog says that there 'real time GPS pace is not reliable on any watch'. Amazfit's fix is a Stryd foot pod. the5krunner lists Stryd indoor pace only for the Cheetah 2 Pro, Cheetah 2 Ultra, T-Rex 3 and Active Max, none of them under $150. On the Bip Max, Bip 6 and Active 2, judge your runs by lap time, not by the pace on screen.

Not recommended

Amazfit Helio Strap Pro as an add-on for these watches. It is Amazfit's HYROX-branded sensor. Amazfit's support page says 'Currently, only Balance 3 and Balance Ultra watches are supported', and its waist sensor works only in HYROX Race and HYROX Simulation modes. Bought for a Bip Max, Bip 6 or Active 2, it adds nothing to the race mode.
