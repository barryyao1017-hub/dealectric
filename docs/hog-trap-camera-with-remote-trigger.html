How each camera reaches the gate

JAGER PRO (radio to its own control box). JAGER PRO's pairing guide says all M.I.N.E. cameras have an internal transmitter and that the camera should be no more than 50 yards from the gate. New control-box receivers must be set to frequency channel 5 before they will pair, and one camera can run several gates. The $599 camera listing says its transmitter signals "the M.I.N.E. gate to drop" and names no other gate. The $1,800 conversion kit adds the parts that make another brand's trap work: a control box with a 12 V battery, an electronic latch rated for gates up to 1,100 lb, an 8 ft power cable and a hand-held remote with a range of 300+ yards.

HogEye (wired). The Memphis Net listing for the HogEye Mini includes a 30 ft gate cable, a latch and a latch adapter: "Every camera comes with a latch and latch adapter to make any trap a smart trap!" With a wired link there is no radio to pair, but the camera has to sit within 30 ft of the gate. The kit runs on a 75 W solar panel.

AnywhereCam Trapper Editions (9 V relay). You watch live video in the AnywhereCam app and press a button. That closes a 9 V relay on low-voltage leads built into the camera (battery not included). The cable is printed as 220 V / 3 A / IP65, 2-pin with a 15 mm nut. Neither Trapper page lists a latch, solenoid or actuator. A trap with only a root or trip-wire gate therefore needs an electric release added before the button can drop it. The Wireless edition adds a transmitter and receiver so the camera doesn't have to be cabled to the gate. AnywhereCam gives no radio range, so test the distance at your trap before you rely on it. The wired edition lasts up to 14 days without direct sun and the wireless one up to 7. Both are built to order in Colorado and ship in about 2 weeks. $457: https://dealectric.com/go/anywherecam-eagle-4g-trapper-edition $557: https://dealectric.com/go/anywherecam-eagle-4g-wireless-trapper-edition

Hog Trap Trigger (its own cellular link). The trigger is a GSM controller in an ammo can. You call it or text it a code, and it releases a 12 V rotary latch clipped to the door. It runs on a 10 W panel and a 12 V 8 Ah battery. The camera is separate, so the camera can sit anywhere, but you pay for two cellular services and use two apps. A camera such as the AnywhereCam Eagle 4G Pro covers the live view. $245: https://dealectric.com/go/anywherecam-eagle-4g-pro

Why a remote trigger is worth it. In Oklahoma field trials (Gaskamp et al., Animals, 2021), suspended traps watched by a cellular camera and closed remotely by the trapper removed 88.1% of the estimated wild-pig population, at 0.64 person-hours per pig. Trip-wire corral traps removed 48.5%, at 2.3 person-hours per pig.

Not recommended

JAGER PRO M.I.N.E. Live Stream Camera on its own, for a trap that isn't a JAGER PRO M.I.N.E. trap. The $599 listing's transmitter signals a M.I.N.E. gate, and without a M.I.N.E. control box within 50 yards it cannot release anything. The cheapest JAGER PRO listing we found that adds the control box and latch is the $1,800 DIY Conversion Kit.
