What reviewers measured on the DXP6800 Pro

  • 10GbE: 9.5 Gbit/s steady in iPerf3 both ways (Notebookcheck, 2026); 8.9 Gbit/s sequential read and 6 Gbit/s write over a mapped share with four WD Red Pro drives in RAID 5 (ITPro, 2024); 2.26 GB/s read and 1.4 GB/s write over CIFS (StorageReview).
  • Noise: 34.6 dB(A) idle with hard drives, up to 43 dB(A) under load (Notebookcheck); "remarkably quiet", two 9 cm rear fans (ITPro).
  • Power: 22.4 W with no drives, 36.6–41.9 W idle with four IronWolf 4 TB drives (Notebookcheck); about 33 W averaged over a day (Dong Knows Tech). UGREEN's own 24.9 W hibernating figure is lower than any review's idle.
  • Plex: not in UGOS's App Center, so it runs in Docker; hardware transcoding needs a Plex Pass. No review published a transcode-stream count for this box; the i5-1235U's Iris Xe decodes HEVC, VP9 and AV1 in hardware (Quick Sync).

Thunderbolt 4 on a NAS

UGREEN's Thunderbolt 4 ports are a point-to-point network link to one computer (a "Thunderbolt" interface with its own IP), not a direct-attached disk, and need a certified cable; UGREEN says Macs run it faster than Windows. No review measured it on this model; treat it as a second fast link for one editing machine, not a 40 Gb/s drive.

Pro or Ultra

The Ultra's Core 5 120U is the same 10-core, 80-EU Iris Xe design with a higher turbo (5.0 vs 4.4 GHz), RAM to 96 GB and a PCIe Gen4 slot; its media engine is the same, so transcoding gains nothing. Six 12 TB drives in RAID 6 give 48 TB usable (about 43.7 TiB) on either.
