Caveats

  • ALLPOWERS has a 2025 monocrystalline revision (Voc 36.5 V, 13.8 kg) under the same model number; the US page still lists the older 45.3 V, 18.5 kg panel, and buyers report receiving the new one. Don't mix the two in one string.
  • Too high-voltage (Voc 45.3 V) for the Tenergy T320 (5–24 V) and the BLUETTI Elite 30 V2 (12–28 V).
  • Heavy for a folding panel at 18.5 kg (the manual confirms it).
  • The page claims 22% efficiency; the manual says 19–23%.
  • Not meant to stay outdoors for weeks (manual).
  • Kickstands are the most common owner complaint.
