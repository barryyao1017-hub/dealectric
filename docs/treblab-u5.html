Caveats

  • IPX4 covers sweat and splashes only. Wipe them down after a workout and don't rinse them.
  • One reviewer found the touch controls can trigger by accident when you adjust the headphones mid-workout.
  • The noise cancelling cuts steady noise like treadmills and fans but still lets voices through. There's no app to adjust it.
  • The headband wires are exposed and could snag (The Gadgeteer).
  • Like any over-ear model, they can slide during bench presses, burpees and running.
