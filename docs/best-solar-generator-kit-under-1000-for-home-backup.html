What a 1 kWh kit keeps running in an outage

A full-size fridge uses about 1.0–1.6 kWh a day (350–600 kWh a year), so 1 kWh keeps one cold for roughly 15–24 hours before the panel adds anything. A CPAP without its humidifier draws about 20–30 W (160–240 Wh a night); with the humidifier and heated tube, 40–60 W. A Wi-Fi router and modem together draw about 15–35 W, so 1 kWh runs them for more than a day. Size up to 2 kWh if the fridge and a CPAP must both run through a night and a day.

What the panel adds on a cloudy outage day

A folding panel gives about 70–80% of its rating in full sun, so 400 W puts back 280–320 W at midday: a 1 kWh battery refills in about 3.5–4.5 hours of strong sun. Under heavy overcast expect 10–25% of the rating; one owner measured 8 W from a 100 W panel on an overcast day against 90 W in sun. On a grey day a 400 W panel covers the router and phones, not the fridge.
