Who it's for

RV owners who want app or voice control of one or two small appliances over the coach's 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or a hotspot.

Caveats

  • 15 A / 1,875 W is shared by both outlets, about half of a 30 A RV service: never use it on the shore-power feed or the rooftop A/C.
  • Works only on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, and must plug into a GFCI receptacle outdoors or in wet locations.
  • No surge protection or voltage cutoff is listed.
