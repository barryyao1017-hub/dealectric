The booth's loads

The fridge is the only load that runs all day. EPA's ENERGY STAR data for compact refrigerators under 2.5 cu ft lists 106 kWh a year for Dometic's 1.1 cu ft C40S absorption units, 121 for an Insignia 1.5 cu ft, 190–210 for Danby, Ikea and Smeg 1.2–1.5 cu ft units, and 241–247 for Danby, Absocold and Avanti 1.0–1.6 cu ft compressor fridges: 290–680 Wh a day in a lab at room temperature, which a sunny booth with a door that keeps opening will exceed. Lights: a string of LED bulbs is 10–40 W. Card reader and tablet: their own batteries, topped up from a USB port. Blender: Vitamix lists the Explorian E310 at 2 HP (about 1,500 W electrical) at 110–120 V, which an 1,800 W inverter holds for a 30–60 second blend but a 600 W one trips on; a personal blender in the 600–900 W class fits the Elite 30 V2 and R600. A hot plate or espresso machine is a 1,000–1,500 W continuous load and a different page.

The arithmetic

Fridge 400–600 Wh, lights 60–240 Wh, reader and phones about 60 Wh, ten blends about 125 Wh, station idle about 90 Wh: 0.7–1.1 kWh for six hours. The AC180's measured 950–1,030 Wh covers it with little margin on a hot day; the Elite 200 V2 or S2000 Pro gives the margin, two days, or a second fridge. The 300 Wh units cover lights and a reader for the day and nothing cold. Between markets the AC180 refills to 80 % in 45 minutes from a wall socket; a 200 W panel on the stall adds 300–600 Wh over a sunny day if you have the space, and the car socket adds about 100 Wh an hour on the drive.

Carrying it and keeping it cool

Weight from the car: Elite 30 V2 4.3 kg, S2000 Pro and P1800 13 kg, AC180 16 kg, Elite 200 V2 24.2 kg. Shade the station: the ALLPOWERS R4000's rating tops out at 40 °C and BLUETTI publishes no range on these pages, so under the table out of the sun is the rule. Fan noise rises under a blender load and settles after. Rivals: EcoFlow's DELTA 3 (1,024 Wh, 12.5 kg) is lighter than the AC180 at $579, about $0.57 per watt-hour against the AC180's $0.39–0.43; Jackery and Anker pages could not be read today.
