Headline watts against the outlet you will use

A station's big AC number is often the total across all its outlets, or the rating of its 240 V outlet. A 120 V appliance in a regular outlet gets that outlet's own limit.

  • Anker SOLIX F3800 Plus: Anker's spec list rates the standard outlets at 120 V, 20 A, 2,400 W max, and the L14-30R twist-lock at 120/240 V, 25 A, 6,000 W max. Any one 120 V outlet therefore gives 2,400 ÷ 6,000 = 40% of the headline figure. Anker also states that the 240 V output cannot be used while the unit charges from 120 V AC. Its cycle rating is “3,000+ cycles” with no capacity end point. On 2026-11-17 the station showed Sold Out, and 400 W panels ordered on or after Oct 30 ship in early December. Anker's Smart Home Power Kit, which wires the unit to house circuits, says professional installation by a licensed electrician is required.
  • Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus: 7,200 W rated (14,400 W surge) at 120 V and 240 V. It is sold out, the pre-order ships Dec 2 and the 500X panels ship by Dec 5. Jackery's listing starts at $3,499.
  • BLUETTI Apex 300: 3,840 W output, powering 120 V and 240 V devices at the same time. BLUETTI calls its 7,680 W figure “Lifting Power”, not surge. Rated for up to 6,000 cycles to 80% capacity.
  • ALLPOWERS R3500: 3,200 W continuous, 7,000 W peak. The listing's 7,000 W continuous figure needs two units in parallel, and it names no 240 V output. Rated for “over 3,500 charging cycles” with no end point given.
  • EcoFlow DELTA 3800 Gen4: 3,600 W continuous, 7,200 W surge.
  • BLUETTI 5kWh RV Kit: 5,000 W continuous, 10,000 W surge. It is mounted in the RV with the supplied screws and template, and BLUETTI's FAQ says most owners can install it with basic DIY skills.

Cycle counts are only comparable when they share an end point. BLUETTI rates the Apex 300 to 80% of original capacity and the Apex 200 to 70%; Anker and ALLPOWERS give counts with no end point. If your loads are 120 V plug-in appliances, the F3800 Plus's 6,000 W is not the figure to compare. Its 2,400 W per outlet is.

Not recommended: the Apex 300 + 2 × 500 W bundle

At store prices this bundle ($3,299) costs $102 more than buying the Apex 300 ($1,499) and two SORA 500 panels ($849 each) separately: 1,499 + 2 × 849 = 3,197. The kit page's box list includes no MC4-to-XT60 solar cable, and BLUETTI's own 20 A MC4-to-XT60 cable ($29) is marked Discontinued and Out of Stock. The bundle also ships in late January 2027. If you want the Apex 300 for 240 V, buy the station and panels separately and make sure the cart includes a way to connect MC4 panels to its XT60 inputs.
