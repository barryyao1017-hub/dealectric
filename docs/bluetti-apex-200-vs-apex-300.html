Which one, by what you run in an outage

  • Fridge, freezer, furnace blower, lights, Wi-Fi, CPAP (all 120 V): Apex 200. A fridge averages 100–200 W and a gas-furnace blower 300–800 W while running, well inside 3,200 W; 2.56 kWh covers a fridge and the basics for about a day, and each B300K adds 2.76 kWh.
  • Anything 240 V (well pump, central AC, electric dryer or range, a 240 V sub-panel): Apex 300, wired in by an electrician. The Apex 200 cannot run these with any number of batteries.
  • Big motor starts (1 HP well pump, 4-ton AC): look at 6,000–8,000 W units such as Anker's F3800 Plus or EcoFlow's DELTA Pro 3 as well; both cost about $1,000 more.

Apex 200 + B300K $2,199; Apex 200 + B500K + 30 A cable $2,799. Apex 300 + B300K $2,599; Apex 300 + B300K + Home Integration Kit $3,399. Owner complaints worth knowing

Apex 300 owners on BLUETTI's forum report the fans running in UPS mode even with no load; a 30 A generator inlet doesn't match the Apex 300's 50 A outlet without an adapter cable; and BLUETTI's support asks detailed grounding and bonding questions before a panel hookup.
