What the differences mean

  • Ceiling light on a UST screen. The Hook Up tested 100-inch units in March 2023, before the current S PRO Plus came out. It measured the VIVIDSTORM S Pro rejecting 60% of light coming from 15° and 80% from 45°. The Elite Kestrel CLR3 rejected 49% and 70%. So the Elite lets through 51% ÷ 40% = 1.28× as much light at 15°, and 30% ÷ 20% = 1.5× as much at 45°.

Making a floor rising screen go up and down with the projector

Every screen here comes with a remote. To make it rise when the projector turns on and lower when it turns off, you need a trigger, and which trigger works depends on the screen.

  • Elite Screens Kestrel. These screens take a 12 V trigger, wired through the wall box or sent wirelessly through the RF remote. Many UST projectors have no 12 V output. For those, Elite sells the ZUSBV-RC1, which "uses the projector's USB 5V Type A power supply to send a radio frequency signal" and has a 49 ft range. Elite names the Kestrel Tab-Tension CLR 5, Tab-Tension 3 CineGrey 4D and Tab-Tension 2 CLR 3 as compatible. It lists "All Non-RC1 versions" as incompatible. The Tab-Tension 3 CineWhite is not on the list, so ask Elite before you count on it.
  • Elite's older ZU12V trigger won't work as a backup. Elite's own page says it is "Not compatible with Floor-Rising Up/Down Screens" and marks it discontinued.
  • VIVIDSTORM. The S PRO ships with a projector trigger dongle for automatic sync. VIVIDSTORM's S ALR listing says its dongle is "Not compatible with all projectors". If the dongle doesn't work with your projector, the RF remote, app and voice control still do.
  • AWOL Vision. The USB Synchronized Trigger plugs into the projector's USB port. The screen's control box also remembers a rise limit, so the screen stops at the height you set.

A USB trigger runs on the port's 5 V, so the screen follows the power on that port, not the picture. Before you rely on one, check that your projector's USB port loses power when the projector turns off.

Not recommended

Elite Kestrel Tab-Tension 3 CineGrey 4D with a UST projector. It shares the Kestrel Tab-Tension 3 name with the CineWhite model, which suits every throw type. Elite lists the CineGrey 4D (1.1 gain, 70°) as "Not Compatible With Ultra-short/Short Throw or Triple Laser Projectors", so buy it only for a standard-throw projector. VIVIDSTORM's S White Cinema has the same limit: the maker says it is "Not compatible Ultra short throw Laser projector".
