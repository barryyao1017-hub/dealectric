Proxmox on a UGREEN NASync: what owners report

  • Works: Proxmox VE installed on a DXP4800 Plus and a DXP8800 Plus for months, with the SATA controller passed through from the Proxmox GUI to a TrueNAS VM (16 GB, ballooning off) and a ZFS mirror on the two M.2 slots for VMs and containers (Proxmox forum, December 2025); "runs stably" on a DXP6800 Pro (February 2026). On the 8800 Plus only one of the two SATA controllers passes through; the rest of the drives go through one by one.
  • Watchdog: the standard it87_wdt kernel module drives the hardware watchdog, so no out-of-tree driver is needed, but a wrong timeout can cause a reboot loop; disabling it in the BIOS is the simple route.
  • Boot: Ctrl+F12 (or Ctrl+F2) at power-on for the BIOS; the 4-bay Plus, 6-bay and 8-bay models boot UGOS from a factory M.2 SSD (the DXP2800 from eMMC, which one owner warns would wear under Proxmox logging). Install to a second M.2 or swap the factory SSD out and keep it; "Force UEFI installation" is needed for an NVMe install.
  • Fans and sensors: chassis fan monitoring on the IT8613E needs custom kernel drivers (TrueNAS owners); no Proxmox-specific fan-control report found.
  • iGPU: the one DXP4800 Plus passthrough report ("99% success", Windows Server 2025) needed intel_iommu=on, vfio-pci, legacy-igd and a custom GOP ROM and still boots to a blank display first time; a TrueNAS VM owner found hardware acceleration did not work inside the VM. Keep transcoding on the host or in an LXC container.
  • NICs: Marvell/Aquantia AQC113 (10GbE) and Intel I226-V (2.5GbE) on the DXP4800 Plus; both have in-kernel drivers, with no owner complaint found.

RAM: spec versus verified

Intel lists the Pentium Gold 8505 at 64 GB maximum (DDR5-4800, two channels, no ECC) and third-party databases list the i5-1235U at 64 GB and the Core 5 120U at 96 GB. Owner verification is thinner: a single 48 GB module ran in a DXP4800 Plus (UGOS showed 32 GB, the guest OS the full amount) and a single 32 GB module in a DXP6800 Pro; no one has posted a 2× 32 GB kit, so buy memory from a vendor that names the model (OWC and NEMIX list 32 GB modules for these units) and expect UGOS to misreport the total.

Proxmox's own requirements

A 64-bit CPU with VT-x or AMD-V, at least 2 GB of RAM for the host plus the guests' memory, about 1 GB per TB of used ZFS storage, an IOMMU (VT-d) enabled in the BIOS for passthrough, fast redundant SSDs for VM storage (consumer SSDs are discouraged; ZFS does not sit on hardware RAID) and multi-gigabit NICs. Every Intel unit here meets the CPU side; the ZFS rule of thumb is why 32 GB or more matters once the array is large.
