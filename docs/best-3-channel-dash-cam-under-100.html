What the differences mean

  • What "4K" is worth with three cameras on. The N4's front camera records 2560 × 1440 = 3,686,400 pixels per frame with all three on (DashCamTalk's spec table), against 3840 × 2160 = 8,294,400 for 4K: 3,686,400 ÷ 8,294,400 = 44%.
  • Price gap. $99.99 − $72.74 = $27.25 more for the N4 than the Redtiger A3.
  • Parking mode. A3: $72.74 + $35.99 = $108.73. N4: $99.99 + $29.99 = $129.98. Neither stays under $100 once its maker's hardwire kit is added.
  • GPS. N4 with Vantrue's mount: $99.99 + $21.99 = $121.98. The 70mai T800E has GPS built in at $99.99.
  • Hours on a card (N4). DashCamTalk lists 15.3 + 8.3 + 6.7 = 30.3 Mb/s across front, rear and cabin (2020 review). 30.3 ÷ 8 × 3,600 = 13,635 MB, about 13.6 GB per hour. 256 ÷ 13.6 = 18.8 hours on its 256 GB maximum; 64 ÷ 13.6 = 4.7 hours on a 64 GB card. An 8-hour shift writes 8 × 13.6 = 109 GB, so it needs a 128 GB card.
  • Frame rate. The N4's three-camera mode runs at 30 fps and 70mai gives 24 fps for the T800E's 4K front: 30 ÷ 24 = 1.25 times the frames each second.

What each camera records with all three cameras on

A 3-channel camera encodes three video streams at once, so the resolution on the box is often the front camera working alone. Vantrue's own listing shows it: the headline reads "Single Front 4K | 3 Channel 4K", and the text below gives "1440P+1080P+1080P at 30fps" for front, cabin and rear together and "Single Front 4K (2160P@25fps)" for the front alone.

The N4 manual's menu page lists every mode:

  • Front + cabin + rear: 1440P + 1440P + 1080P, 1440P + 1080P + 1080P, 1080P + 1080P + 1080P, 1080P + 1080P + 720P or 720P + 720P + 720P, all at 30 fps.
  • Front + rear with the cabin camera off: 2160P + 1080P.
  • Front alone: 3840 × 2160.

4K never appears with the cabin camera on. Redtiger says the same of its F17 ($129.99, over the line): "Turn off the inside camera to enable 4K front and 1080P rear recording." 70mai's T800E listing says the system records front, interior and rear simultaneously and gives the front as 4K at 24 fps; that is the front camera's figure as 70mai states it. Redtiger and Kingslim give "2.5K" as a label, with no pixel dimensions or frame rate in the listing text read.

The N4 is the only row with named tests read for this page:

  • DashCamTalk (2020-09-22): day video 7/10, night video 5/10, reliability 8/10. Front "good video quality during the day and average quality at night"; rear "below average" at night; interior "very good in the day and average at night". Supercapacitor, −10 °C to 70 °C, cards up to 256 GB, and a separate hardwire kit for parking mode.
  • mybest: 4.27/5, third of eight 3-channel cameras. Low light 4.47, interior camera 3.97. "We were able to clearly record the numbers on license plates in both good and bad conditions." It measured a 100° horizontal front view against the 155° Vantrue lists, and notes there is no app.

Redtiger states a supercapacitor rated −20 °C to 70 °C for the A3.
