Who it's for

One person can carry it. Runs a coffee maker, electric cooler, CPAP, router and laptops; keeps a full-size fridge going for roughly 8 to 10 hours.

Caveats

  • AC charging runs slower than rated below about 20% charge; a full wall charge took about 1 hr 40 min in The Solar Lab's test.
  • Eco mode shuts off very low loads; turn it off for a fridge or CPAP.
  • Turbo charging and some UPS settings need the app, which reviewers call glitchy.
  • No solar or car cable in the box.
