Who it's for

Buyers who want a new, warrantied home deck to play, copy and digitize cassettes, or to record mixes, karaoke or meeting audio with a mic.

Caveats

  • Rated wow and flutter is 0.25% W.RMS; bench tests of this mechanism range from 0.085% to 0.52%, so pitch is less stable than a serviced 3-head vintage deck.
  • Noise reduction works only on playback, so recordings you make are not Dolby-encoded.
  • No auto-reverse, no high-speed dubbing, no recording on metal (Type IV) tape, and the pitch control works only on deck 1 during playback.
  • Timer recording needs a separate outlet timer.
  • TEAC USA ships to the lower 48 states only.
