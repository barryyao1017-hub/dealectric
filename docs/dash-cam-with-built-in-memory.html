What the differences mean

  • 70mai M800, about 7.2 h front + rear: 128 GB × 8,000 Mbit/GB ÷ (29.4 + 10.1) Mbps ÷ 3,600 s = 7.2 h. This uses the full 128 GB, so real loop time is a little shorter. Front only: 1,024,000 ÷ 29.4 ÷ 3,600 = 9.7 h.
  • REDTIGER F77, about 4.1 h: DashCamTalk reviewer DT MI estimated about 4.1 hours of front + rear video in the 114 GB of usable eMMC. A bench test left on loop until old files began to be overwritten came out very close to that. Bitrate check: 114 × 8,000 ÷ (31 + 24) Mbps ÷ 3,600 = 4.6 h before file overhead.
  • M800 against F77: 7.2 ÷ 4.1 = 1.76, so the M800 holds about 75% more driving in the same 128 GB. The reason is its rear stream: a 1080p rear at 10.1 Mbps against a 4K rear at about 24 Mbps.
  • DDPAI Z90 Master, about 2.0 h on its eMMC alone: 64 × 8,000 ÷ (42.1 + 30.1) Mbps ÷ 3,600 = 1.97 h front + rear. With the interior camera on: 512,000 ÷ 90.0 ÷ 3,600 = 1.6 h.
  • Same storage design, different price: Z90 Master $299 − Z90 Pro $199 = $100. Both have 64 GB of eMMC plus a microSD slot for cards up to 512 GB.

How long built-in memory lasts before it overwrites

Loop recording fills the memory, then writes over the oldest clips. Files locked by an impact are kept separately: InCarTec's M500 listing says 'Protected emergency files are stored separately.' So the hours above are how far back you can go in normal footage, for example after a scrape you only notice later.

How much you get is set by the camera, not by a card. According to DashCamTalk, the M800 has no microSD slot at all. The F77 has a slot, but REDTIGER's own store says 'For F77, the SD card can only be used for video backup and transfer, not for direct recording.' DDPAI's D²save cameras work differently. DDPAI_Official explained on DashCamTalk (24 Aug 2026, describing the Z90 Master) that the newest recordings go to the internal eMMC first. Older clips move to the microSD card only once the eMMC is full and a card is inserted. Emergency recordings stay on the eMMC.

Slow copying is part of having built-in memory. You can't pull the card out, so footage comes off over a cable or Wi-Fi. At rcg530's 18.5 MB/s, copying a full 128 GB takes 128,000 ÷ 18.5 = 6,919 s, about 1.9 hours.

Not recommended

DDPAI Z90 Master, if built-in memory is the point. Its eMMC holds about 2.0 hours of front + rear footage at DashCamTalk's bitrates, so in practice it needs a microSD card. DashCamTalk's own page lists the eMMC as 64 GB in one place and 32 GB in another. The Z90 Pro uses the same 64 GB eMMC + 512 GB card design for $100 less.
