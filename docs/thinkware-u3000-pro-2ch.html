Who it's for

Cars left parked all day or overnight where the worry is a bumper hit at the front or rear.

Caveats

  • It has no side cameras, so a door ding outside the front and rear views can be missed.
  • Smart Parking switches to impact-only recording when the cabin gets too hot. Thinkware states no before-impact footage for that mode.
  • Thinkware's US box list has the OBD-II cable but no hardwiring cable.
  • Mile Witness gives the radar range as 3 to 16 ft, for movement under 12 mph.
