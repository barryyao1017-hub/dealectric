Starlink Mini power consumption in watts

Starlink: the Mini consumes 20 to 40 W on average and 15 W idle (its spec sheet says 25–40 W average); it peaks higher while melting snow or reconnecting. Hours ≈ battery Wh × 0.85 ÷ watts: a 288 Wh station at 30 W runs about 8 hours, a 1,024 Wh station about 29.

Why a 65W power bank won't run a Starlink Mini

Starlink's USB power article: use a 100 W, 20 V at 5 A USB Power Delivery source; 100 W is the minimum, and a 65 W source or anything from 66 to 99 W won't run the Mini. The Mini doesn't draw 100 W, but the source must offer the 20 V / 5 A profile. Starlink's 5 m USB-C cable replaces the 15 m power cable and needs that 100 W source.
