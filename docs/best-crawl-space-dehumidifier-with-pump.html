Built-in pump vs add-on pump kit: how high the water can go

A built-in condensate pump sits inside the cabinet. Water collects in an internal reservoir, a float starts the pump, and the pump pushes the water up the drain hose to a sink, a sump or a line leaving the house. CrawlSpaceDehumidifiers.org notes that the float also shuts the dehumidifier off if the pump fails. A clogged pump or a drain set too high stops the drying, not just the draining. The same source notes that any horizontal run and elbows reduce the lift you actually get.

Santa Fe's crawl-space units drain by gravity. Santa Fe sells a separate Condensate Pump Kit, part 4022220, which it lists for the Compact70, Ultra70, Ultra98, Ultra120, Ultra155, Ultra205 and others. Santa Fe states that the kit moves water up to 12 ft vertically, with a 20 ft total run and a 2-quart reservoir.
