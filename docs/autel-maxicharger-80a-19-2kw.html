Who it's for

Owners of EVs with a 19.2 kW onboard charger whose garage Wi-Fi is weak, or two-EV homes that need two chargers on limited electrical capacity.

Caveats

  • 80 A only helps cars with a 19.2 kW onboard charger; most EVs stop at 48 A (11.5 kW).
  • Needs a dedicated 100 A circuit; a load calculation may show the panel needs an upgrade.
  • J1772 plug only; NACS cars need a separate adapter.
  • Some owners of earlier units in a Lucid owners thread reported Wi-Fi trouble and used Ethernet instead.
  • The whole-home load limit (ALM) needs add-on hardware the store page does not price.
