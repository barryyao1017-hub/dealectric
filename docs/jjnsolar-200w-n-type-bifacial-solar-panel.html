Who it's for

Best for a permanent mount: RV or van roof, roof rack, shed, or a ground stand where light can also reach the back. Not for carrying to a campsite.

Caveats

  • OutdoorGearLab's 182 W result is for JJN's earlier bifacial 200W. We found no independent test of the N-type version sold now.
  • Rigid glass panel, about 25 lb. It needs brackets or a rack.
  • Buyers report panels from JJN and other brands arriving with cracked glass. Open the box and test the panel the day it arrives.
  • The MC4 leads are short and OutdoorGearLab found them hard to unplug. Plan on an MC4 extension cable.
  • Measure the space between your roof vents first. The tested panel is about 54 × 30 in.
