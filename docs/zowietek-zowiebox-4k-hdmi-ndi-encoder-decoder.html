Who it's for

Churches, schools and small studios that want to bring HDMI cameras into an NDI or SRT workflow without a capture card per camera.

Caveats

  • NDI|HX only; it does not encode or decode Full NDI, so sources from OBS or vMix in Full NDI need NDI Bridge conversion.
  • The USB capture-card (UVC to PC) function is not supported.
  • Some owners report intermittent NDI-to-HDMI decoding; keep firmware current.
