Who it's for

Snorkelers and shallow divers who want sharp photos of fish, coral and tiny reef life, and a camera that also survives hikes, beaches and kids.

Caveats

  • Small 1/2.3-inch sensor: noisy below 20 ft or in murky water without a light.
  • Battery is rated for about 330 shots and drains fast with GPS on or in cold water; carry a spare.
  • Cannot shoot while charging over USB-C.
  • Video is behind GoPro and DJI action cameras; 4K tops out at 30 fps.
  • Screen is not touch-sensitive and is hard to read in direct sun.
  • Scuba past 50 ft needs the optional PT-059 housing.
