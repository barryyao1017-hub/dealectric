What owners complain about

We read 17 generator-related Trustpilot reviews of 4Patriots posted in 2026. Ten describe a unit that will not charge, will not hold a charge or died within about 15 months. Twelve mention slow or unhelpful support, refused returns or partial refunds. Only two were positive. None complained about price, which suggests buyers accept the premium going in and are let down later by durability and service. The Better Business Bureau lists 288 complaints against 4Patriots in the last three years alongside an A+ rating, and the company sells food and water filters as well as power stations. Independent reviewers add three criticisms: the model names overstate capacity (the old 1800 held 768 Wh, the 2000X about 1,612 Wh), the 2200X has 4 AC outlets despite a 10-outlet headline, and 4Patriots does not publish a maximum solar input, so you cannot tell how many panels it will accept. In 2016 the CPSC recalled about 2,200 units of the earlier Patriot Power Generator 1500 for a battery fire hazard; the current models are LiFePO4 and are not part of that recall.

Which one by need

Keeping a fridge and a few essentials running for days. The BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 + 200 W kit. 4Patriots claims the 2200X runs a fridge for 75 hours, which assumes a fridge averaging about 30 W. A typical full-size fridge averages 100 to 150 W, so expect roughly 13 to 19 hours from either 2,000 Wh-class unit before you need sun or an outlet. The BLUETTI accepts up to 1,000 W of solar, so adding panels later actually shortens recharge time. The 2200X ships with one 200 W panel, which reviewers estimate at 12 to 14 hours of good sun to fill.

A lighter unit for one night, a CPAP or camping. The BLUETTI AC180 + 200 W kit. It beats the 4Patriots 1200 on capacity, output, panel size and warranty, and it is a 35 lb unit against the 1200's 28 lb, which is the only spec the 4Patriots unit wins.

Already own panels, want the most watt-hours per dollar. The ALLPOWERS R2500 V2 or the Growatt INFINITY 2000 PRO. Both take 1,000 W or more of solar, recharge from the wall in about 2 hours and cost under $0.40 per Wh. Growatt adds a 30 A TT-30 RV outlet.

You want 4Patriots specifically. Buy the 2200X without the panel for $2,999 and add a cheaper 200 W MC4 panel, not 4Patriots' own $499 panel. Note the date the 365-day coverage ends and test the unit under a real load in the first week, since the complaints cluster around units that fail after coverage ends.

Not recommended

The refurbished and pre-owned 4Patriots units (older 1800 and 2000X, Sidekick) are Final Sale with the same 1-year coverage, and the old 1800 holds only 768 Wh. The 2200X expansion pack at $1,299 for 2,240 Wh costs more than a complete Growatt or ALLPOWERS station with its own inverter. Skip any solar generator, 4Patriots or otherwise, that does not publish its maximum solar input wattage.
