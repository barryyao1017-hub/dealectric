Which 1,000 Wh portable power station fully charges from a wall outlet in under an hour? (2026)

Updated 2026-11-08

Short answer. The Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 (price on the Amazon page; buy on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FN7MSY4L?tag=dealectric-20) is the 1,000 Wh power station measured fastest from a wall outlet: full in 47 minutes and 80 % in 36 in a reviewer's test, with its UltraFast mode switched on in the app. That mode needs the battery above 68 °F, or it charges far slower, so before a storm switch it on and keep the unit indoors.

What to know: the under-an-hour charge also needs one free wall outlet: UltraFast draws about 1,600 W, around 13 A at 120 V, so a space heater or microwave on the same circuit can trip the breaker. Anker and Jackery both say to keep their fast modes for when it's needed, to spare the battery. The other measured sub-hour units are the DJI Power 1000 V2 (under 53 min), EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus (55 min), the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 in Emergency mode and the BLUETTI AC180 in Turbo (about an hour each).

Before a storm

Charge where the battery is warm. Anker's FAQ says UltraFast only engages above 20 °C (68 °F) and charging is "significantly" slower between 0 and 20 °C; BLUETTI's AC180 took about two hours on a first charge while it was still warm from use and about one hour once it had cooled. Plug the station into an outlet with nothing else heavy on the circuit: 1,500–1,600 W is close to the 1,800 W a 15 A, 120 V circuit carries in total. No reviewer reported a tripped breaker, but none tested a shared circuit.

What fast charging costs

Every maker here slows itself or warns against routine use. Anker asks that UltraFast be used only when necessary to prolong battery life; Jackery's manual says Emergency Charging is not recommended for regular, long-term use; DJI's Power 1000 V2 falls back to its slower rate after five fast charges in a row; OutdoorGearLab warns that frequent turbo charging on the BLUETTI Elite 100 V2 degrades the battery. For storm prep, charge fast once and keep it topped up at the normal rate.

Measured, and where

The Anker, DJI and AC180 times come from The Technology Man in the UK on 230 V mains; the DELTA 3 Plus and Jackery times from The Solar Lab; the AC180's input watts from StorageReview. No source measured wall watts on a US 120 V outlet for any of these, so treat the minutes as the best case.
