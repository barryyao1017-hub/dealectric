Nextcloud's requirements

  • Server: 64-bit CPU strongly recommended; PHP 8.3–8.5; MariaDB, MySQL or PostgreSQL (SQLite for testing only); 128 MB minimum and 512 MB recommended per PHP process; SSD storage recommended and SD cards discouraged.
  • All-in-One (AIO): the official Docker bundle for amd64 and arm64 with optional Nextcloud Office (Collabora), EuroOffice, Talk with TURN, Imaginary previews, full-text search (Java, 512 MB by default), ClamAV and Whiteboard. By default it publishes 80, 8080 (admin page) and 8443 and Talk needs 3478; behind a reverse proxy it listens on 11000 bound to localhost and needs only the proxy's 443.
  • RAM in practice: a bare Nextcloud with MariaDB runs in about 500 MB on an N100 box; Office adds about 1 GB plus 100 MB per user; search and previews add more. 8 GB covers a household; 16 GB is comfortable with everything on.

Remote access

Three routes, from Nextcloud's own AIO documentation: your domain with DDNS and ports 80 and 443 forwarded (AIO issues the Let's Encrypt certificate itself); a Cloudflare Tunnel with no open ports (set SKIP_DOMAIN_VALIDATION, turn off Rocket Loader, allow Cloudflare's IP ranges for Office, and live with the free plan's 100 MB upload limit via chunking and 100-second request timeout); or Tailscale, which the docs say typically performs better with fewer trade-offs (Serve for the tailnet, Funnel for the public). UGREEN's own UGREENlink relay carries UGREEN's apps and, per UGREEN's FAQ, reaches containers only when logged in with a UGREENlink ID; it is not a replacement for a domain and certificate.

Why the DXP2800 over the usual picks

Synology ships the DS225+ with 2 GB and no M.2 slots, so Nextcloud with Office needs a RAM upgrade and lives on hard drives; its advantage is the documented Container Manager route. QNAP's TS-264 has 8 GB but an older Celeron and QTS's own claim on ports 8080 and 443. TerraMaster's F2-425 Plus matches the DXP2800 on RAM with a newer N150 and two 5GbE ports at about the same price, and its App Center lists simple Nextcloud apps. The DXP2800 wins on price for 8 GB plus two M.2 slots; it loses on documentation, so budget an evening.
