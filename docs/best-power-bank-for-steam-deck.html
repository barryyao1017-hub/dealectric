How many Steam Deck charges a power bank gives

A power bank's mAh is rated at its cells' 3.7 V, so 25,000 mAh is 91 Wh; after conversion losses about 85% reaches the Deck. The Steam Deck OLED has a 50 Wh battery and the LCD model 40 Wh (Valve), and the Deck draws about 15–25 W in demanding games. A 20,000 mAh (73–74 Wh) bank gives about 1.2 OLED charges, a 25,000 mAh (91 Wh) bank about 1.5.

What 45W USB-C PD means for a Steam Deck

Valve rates both Steam Decks at 45 W USB-C PD. A 45 W-or-higher bank charges it while you play; a 20–30 W bank charges it when idle or asleep but only slows the drain in heavy games. More than 45 W doesn't charge the Deck faster, but a 100–140 W output also charges a laptop.

For these 65 W handhelds, use a bank with 65 W or more on one port to charge them while you play.

Airline limits

Power banks up to 100 Wh (about 27,000 mAh) go in carry-on without approval; 101–160 Wh needs the airline's approval; never in checked bags. Since 27 March 2026, ICAO rules for international flights allow two power banks per passenger and ban recharging them in flight; many airlines also ban using them on board.
