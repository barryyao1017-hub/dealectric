Who it's for

Single-DIN dash, owner adding a full amp and sub setup, or anyone who wants NFC tap-to-pair.

Caveats

  • Bluetooth 3.0 and no FLAC on disc; FLAC plays from USB only.
  • Preouts are not high-voltage, so long RCA runs are more noise-prone than with the Pioneer's 4 V outputs.
  • Fewer EQ bands (10) than the Pioneer or Kenwood (13).
