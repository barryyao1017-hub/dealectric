Who it's for

A 4 Ω or 8 Ω passive home subwoofer fed from an AV receiver. Use one channel up to 350 W, or bridge it for a bigger single sub.

Caveats

  • Crown's page lists THD < 0.5% with no power level or frequency band, so the 350 W figure has no stated test conditions.
  • Audio Science Review measured one channel much worse than the other. That matters little for a sub but does for full-range use.
  • It is a rack box with forced-air fans, though Audio Science Review heard none during testing.
