Who it's for

Renters and apartment owners who want one plug-in camera on a window that watches the driveway or walkway outside and the room inside, with continuous recording to a 512 GB card and no required subscription.

Caveats

  • The inward lens is live view only: it does not record and has no motion detection.
  • The outward lens needs some outdoor light at night (porch light, streetlight); an unlit yard goes dark.
  • No tilt: the view is fixed straight out, so a window above a driveway shows mostly the far side.
  • A reviewer could not get Alexa or Google Assistant control working; there is no HomeKit.
  • Botslab is the overseas brand of Qihoo 360; Botslab says US customer footage is stored on servers in Virginia.
  • Digital zoom only; detail degrades past about 4x.
