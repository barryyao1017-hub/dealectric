What the differences mean

  • Current at 240 V: the Apex 300's 3,840 W total ÷ 240 V = 16 A, which is 32% of its 50 A outlet rating (16 ÷ 50). The OSCAL PowerMax 6000 and Anker F3800 Plus are rated at 6,000 W: 6,000 ÷ 240 = 25 A.
  • Runtime at the full 16 A: StorageReview measured 2.39 kWh delivered, and 2,390 Wh ÷ 3,840 W = 0.62 h, about 37 minutes. At 1,500 W it is 2,390 ÷ 1,500 = 1.6 h.
  • Solar refill: Impulse Gamer measured about 400 W from each SORA 500 in an Australian winter, so 2 × 400 W = 800 W, and 2,390 Wh ÷ 800 W ≈ 3.0 h of full sun. That is before charge losses: StorageReview measured about 72% round trip on AC charging.
  • Kit vs bundle: Apex 300 at $1,499 plus the SORA 500 2-pack at $1,698 comes to $3,197. BLUETTI's own Apex 300 + 2 × 500W listing is $3,299, so the bundle costs $102 more.

How much the 240 V outlet really gives, and how many panels fit

The Apex 300 has one 240 V outlet, a NEMA 14-50R on the side panel. StorageReview's Kevin OBrien found that flipping the voltage selector next to the display puts both lines, neutral and ground on that outlet, while the four front outlets stay at 120 V. The outlet's 50 A rating describes the plug, not the inverter: everything the unit powers shares 3,840 W, so a 240 V load has at most 16 A. Check the running amps on the nameplate of the pump, tool or appliance before you buy. Anything over 16 A at 240 V needs a 6,000 W unit.

On the solar side, StorageReview and Notebookcheck's Christian Hintze both list two built-in inputs at 12–60 V and 20 A, and StorageReview rates each at 1,200 W. Notebookcheck identifies them as XT60. The SORA 500 measures Voc 49.1 V and Isc 13.31 A (Impulse Gamer). One panel per input stays inside both limits. Two panels in series reach 98.2 V, over the 60 V limit. Two in parallel on one input reach 2 × 13.31 = 26.6 A of short-circuit current, over the 20 A input rating. So with these panels, the most the Apex 300 takes without the SolarX 4K is two, or 1,000 W of panels.

Not recommended

  • The Apex 300 + 2 × 500W bundle listing: it is the same unit and panels for $102 more than adding the Apex 300 and the SORA 500 2-pack to the cart separately (prices on 2026-11-13).
  • Adding a SolarX 4K to a two-panel kit: it accepts only 150–500 V panel strings (BLUETTI). Two SORA 500s in series give 98.2 V, so it can't use them. Count on at least four panels in series.
