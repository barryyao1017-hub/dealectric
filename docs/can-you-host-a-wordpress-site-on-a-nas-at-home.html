What the differences mean

  • Power bill, DXP2800 with drives in use: 16.38 W × 8,760 h = 143.5 kWh × $0.1816 = $26.06 a year.
  • Same box in its low-power state: 5.24 W × 8,760 h = 45.9 kWh × $0.1816 = $8.34 a year.
  • Extra cost if the site keeps the drives from sleeping: (16.38 − 5.24) W = 11.14 W × 8,760 h = 97.6 kWh × $0.1816 = $17.72 a year. That is the real extra cost of adding a site to a NAS you already run 24/7.
  • Hostinger Premium WordPress: $95.52 ÷ 4 years = $23.88 a year, then $10.99 × 12 = $131.88 a year at renewal.
  • When a NAS bought for the site overtakes Hostinger: after 7 years the NAS has cost $389.99 + 7 × $26.06 = $572.41 and Hostinger $95.52 + 3 × $131.88 = $491.16. After 8 years it is $598.47 vs $623.04, so the NAS only pulls ahead in year 8, and that is before drives.
  • Against Bluehost Starter at renewal: $389.99 ÷ ($119.88 − $26.06) = 4.2 years to pay back.
  • Against a $4 DigitalOcean Droplet: $389.99 ÷ ($48.00 − $26.06) = 17.8 years.
  • QNAP TS-264-8G vs DXP2800: 254.8 kWh ÷ 143.5 kWh = 1.78× the yearly energy at each maker's busy-drive figure.

What a home WordPress server costs to run, model by model

A NAS has no hosting bill, but its electricity is a running cost. Each maker publishes two power figures, one with the drives in use and one with them resting, and they test differently. QNAP tested the TS-264 with every bay filled, so its disk-standby figure (18.288 W) is higher than Synology's drives-in-use figure for the DS225+ (16.98 W). Synology's spec page doesn't name the drives it used. UGREEN publishes 16.38 W with drives in use and 5.24 W in its low-power state for the DXP2800, and costs them at $0.18/kWh. No maker says whether a WordPress database on hard drives lets them sleep between visits, so the table shows both ends of each range.

Not recommended

UGREEN NASync DH2300. At $239.99 it is the cheapest UGREEN NAS and the cheapest to run (13.72 W with drives in use). UGREEN's own FAQ says 'The DH2300 does not officially support Docker,' and it has no virtual machines either. The usual home setup, WordPress and MariaDB in containers, therefore has no supported way to run on it. If you want a UGREEN to host a site, the DH4300 Plus is the cheapest model that runs Docker.
