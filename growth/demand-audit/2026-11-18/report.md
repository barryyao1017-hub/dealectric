**Verdict: the bar is not met.** The pre-set rule was "2 of the 5 target queries in the top 10 = build more head
pages; none = move effort to Reddit and YouTube mentions". On day 11 one target is in the top 10 (the 4Patriots
alternative page, #1 on both re-reads). The panels page, #8 at baseline and on day 7, is not in today's top 10; the
set is led by T3, TechRadar, AP, EnergySage and Popular Science. The four other targets (home use, home backup,
home battery backup system, BLUETTI review) never entered.

What follows from it:
- No more pages aimed at the high-volume head queries. Editorial and news domains hold those result sets, and an
  on-site rewrite did not move us in eleven days.
- The one win is a narrow, named-alternative query. The 11-18 scoreboard says the same thing from the traffic side:
  ChatGPT landings and buy clicks come from kit, size and budget questions (solar-kit clusters: 56 landings, 40
  clicks in 8 days), not from head queries.
- The pre-registered next lever for head queries is off-site mentions: the Reddit answers (DLC-38) and the YouTube
  pilot (DLC-187), both waiting on Barry.
