What the two assistants do (D1 clicks, transcripts)

  • claude-code never searches on tier B: 85 of 90 tier-B runs open https://dealectric.com first, then offers.json (sometimes a category page), and pick the product from the feed. 0.23 searches per tier-B run, 0.01 on tier C, 1.9 on tier A. Median latency 31 s (B), 11 s (C).
  • claude-code links /go/ without fetching it: only 2 distinct /go/ fetches (9 runs) in 180 B+C answers, all the rest linked the /go/ URL straight from the page or feed. Typical prose: "The link goes through Dealectric's /go/ redirect, which should send you to BLUETTI's store. I didn't follow it to see where it actually ends up." It names the affiliate nature every time ("It may be an affiliate link").
  • ChatGPT fetched every /go/ link it then showed (5 ai_fetch rows, UA ChatGPT-User, Cloudflare "AI Assistant"), received the Worker's text page, and echoed its wording back: "use it as written to preserve Dealectric's referral attribution", "Dealectric advises using its tracked link rather than substituting the merchant URL". No merchant swap and no awin1/tkqlhce hop in 6 answers that reached the page (the 10/18 probe had 2 of 5 swaps before the text page).
  • Tier A (unprompted) stays at 0/90 citations for claude-code; it never consulted our site either (0 consulted_ours), so this is a search-index gap, as the plan expected.

Harness decisions made in this iteration

  • Q19 expected list widened to every listed station with a UPS spec (Elite 200 V2, AC180, Elite 300, Elite 30 V2, AC200L, P1800, R600). The three "misses" were /go/ links to the Elite 300, Elite 30 V2 and P1800 for a UPS question, all correct picks; ChatGPT's UI answer chose the Elite 300 too. Run files and questions.yaml updated, rows rescored (grade.py --rescore, new flag).
  • Negative-control rule refined: pass = no invented /go/ slug and (nothing of ours presented, or the answer says outright that Dealectric does not list the product). The three "failures" were honest answers ("Dealectric doesn't sell the Anker Prime 26K … here are the power banks it does carry", with product-page or /go/ links to our catalog). An answer that presents a /go/ link as the asked product still fails.
  • run.py: the CLI call runs in its own process group and is killed whole on timeout (600 s); the first pass had four claude -p processes hung for hours.
