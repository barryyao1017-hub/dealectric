Citation before volume

Where Dealectric's gap pages lose ChatGPT shoppers, and the order to fix demand, supply and content. Based on 113 plain-prompt ChatGPT chats on Nov 7, 2026: one per gap page, the page's target query typed as is, gpt-6 on the Plus account, until the quota ran out. Plus the OpenAI search check of all 173 gap pages and real visit data from the Worker.

Binding constraintContent

When ChatGPT sees our page it cites us 26% of the time, against an 80% bar. It gave our buy link 0 times in 113 chats.

Low, but not the first fixDemand

12 of 173 gap pages have ever had a ChatGPT visit. More demand only multiplies a near-zero conversion, so it pays off after content passes.

MixedSupply

We rank well for our own queries: #1 in 36 of the 66 chats where we appeared. The competition is for the 2 citation slots in each answer, and ChatGPT fills them with mainstream models and sources it treats as primary.

Small aggregator sites win 42% of the citations, so this isn't a brand-authority wall we can't climb. In the four cases we read side by side, the winners shared a pattern:

  • the answer comes first, as reference material;
  • numbers come from named studies or the maker's manual;
  • an author or method is named;
  • sales links sit below the analysis or are absent.

Ours open with a buy line, prices and a code. ChatGPT also tends to name its models before it searches (EcoFlow, Jackery, Anker, BLUETTI, Synology), then cites whichever page lists those models cleanly. Several times it kept our reasoning and our pick but cited someone else.

Demand

Gap pages have drawn 50 ChatGPT visits in all, from 12 of 173 pages. Half came from one page, "solar generator with panels included", the only gap page picked using measured search demand (402 Bing searches). Of the 114 older pages scored by gap mining, 6 had measurable demand, and those 6 drew 28 of the 49 visits.

Buy link

No general "which should I buy" chat put our buy link in the answer, even the ones that cited us. Real shoppers do get it, about 10 clicks a day, on deal-seeking questions about a named kit ("Is there a discount code or a good deal on the ALLPOWERS S2000 Pro kit?"). A tested code is the one thing only we have, and on general questions the model sends the buy button to the maker or a retailer.

The plan

Fix content first, on the pages we already have, because it is the only knob where we can see a 3–5× effect today and test it in one Plus quota window. Change demand next, and supply alongside it. Stop adding pages until content passes, since each new page currently converts close to zero.

Phase 1 · content · this week
  1. Remove the AI-addressed product-result block from all 74 pages. It's a one-line change in build.py. Keep the product data in JSON-LD, where it isn't prose aimed at the model.
  2. Rewrite the page opening as reference material (a new page template), then the offer. Order: a direct answer with its key numbers; a spec table of the mainstream models ChatGPT already names, plus ours, with every figure linked to the maker's manual or spec page; named sources and a short method note ("specs from makers' manuals, checked <date>; prices checked in the cart"); then the buy box with the tested code below the analysis. Add an author and method page site-wide.
  3. Test it as an A/B on existing pages. Rewrite 30 retrieved pages and leave 30 matched pages as controls, both groups with the block removed. Wait until OpenAI's crawler (OAI-SearchBot) has re-fetched each page (storedcopy.py), because ChatGPT answers from its stored copy. Then run the plain-prompt test, one chat per page, in one Plus window.
  4. Pass bar: cited in at least 80% of chats where we're in the results. For the buy link, set a separate bar on deal-intent questions (phase 2), because general questions don't hand it to anyone but makers and retailers.
Phase 2 · demand · after phase 1 passes
  1. Pick queries only with measured demand: Bing volume through gap mining, and keystroke depth above zero. No more zero-demand long-tail pages. Measured demand is what separates the one page that sells from the rest.
  2. Add deal-intent pages for each product with a tested or exclusive code, answering "is there a discount code or deal on <kit>". These are the only questions where ChatGPT gives our buy link today, so they carry the buy-link metric.
  3. Retire or noindex the bulk of the 217 pages added Nov 6 that have no demand, so the site reads as focused rather than templated.
Phase 3 · supply · alongside phase 2
  1. Pick topics where the citation slots go to small information sites, not to makers, retailers or official docs (power stations and solar kits, not NAS-software how-tos). We can out-write a site like powersized.com; we can't replace jellyfin.org.
  2. Build each comparison around the models ChatGPT already recommends, with our partner's product among them, rather than around a less-known or pre-order product. Example: the P1800 kit has 17 clicks and no sale, and it ships Dec 7.
  3. Go deeper in power stations and solar instead of spreading across NAS, golf carts and car stereos. Both cited competitors there are single-topic sites with a dozen of their own guides linking to each other.

Decisions for Barry

1. Pause new gap pages, including the daily gap-mining drafts, until phase 1 passes.

2. Remove the AI-addressed block from all 74 pages now, ahead of the A/B test.

3. Approve the reference-first template and the 30 + 30 A/B test, measured with the plain-prompt ChatGPT test on the Plus quota.
