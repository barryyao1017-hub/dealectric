# Which power banks support Qi2.2 25W?

**Of the two records we have, one fits: the Baseus PicoGo AM52 (10,000 mAh) supports Qi2.2 magnetic wireless charging at 25 W, and costs $59.99, or $53.99 with code AM52.** The 25 W speed needs an iPhone 16 or later (not the 16e) on iOS 26; older iPhones get 15 W. The Baseus Blade H1 (20,000 mAh, 100 W) is a wired laptop bank and does not list Qi2.2 or any wireless charging.

Note on the search query: our records do not include a release date or a manufacturer announcement for the AM52, so we can't confirm "released 2026" from them. Its Qi2.2 certification is listed as WPC.

## Baseus Blade H1 Digital Display Power Bank, 20,000 mAh, 100 W: does not fit
