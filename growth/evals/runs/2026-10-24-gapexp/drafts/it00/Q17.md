# LiFePO4 vs NMC portable power station: which is better?

**Short answer:** Among the stations we've verified, LiFePO4 is the better default. The BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 is rated for 6,000+ cycles to 80%, against 2,500+ for the only NMC unit in our records, the ALLPOWERS S2000 Pro. NMC's advantage is weight: the S2000 Pro delivers 1,451 Wh and 2,400 W in 13 kg. That makes it worth considering only if you carry the unit often and use it occasionally.

## ALLPOWERS S2000 Pro (NMC): best if weight matters most
- **Chemistry and cycles:** NMC, 2,500+ cycles. Our records list LiFePO4 rivals at 3,000-6,000.
- **Capacity and output:** 1,451 Wh, 2,400 W continuous, 4,000 W peak, 4 outlets.
- **Weight:** 13 kg, about 112 Wh per kg. The Elite 200 V2 works out to about 86 Wh per kg (2,073.6 Wh at 24.2 kg).
- **Solar:** The spec table and manual say 1,000 W at 12-150 V, 13 A, but the FAQ says 650 W. Panels would go in series.
- **Does not fit:** Daily cycling or long-term backup, where cycle life matters more than weight.

## How to choose
- **Pick LiFePO4** for home backup or frequent cycling. The Elite 200 V2's 6,000+ cycles is more than double the S2000 Pro's 2,500+.
- **Pick NMC** (the S2000 Pro) only if you carry it often and cycle it rarely. It offers more energy and power per kilogram than the 16 kg AC180 and the 24.2 kg Elite 200 V2.
- **Price per Wh:** After codes, the S2000 Pro and the Elite 200 V2 both come to about $0.41 per Wh, so the NMC unit is no cheaper per Wh.
