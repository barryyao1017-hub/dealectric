# Apple MacBook Air recommended power adapter wattage (M4, M5, official Apple support)

**Short answer:** Our product records don't include Apple's official wattage figure, so check Apple's support page for your exact model. What the records do say is that 45 W is enough to charge a MacBook Air, and a 65 W charger gives more headroom. The Baseus GaN5 Pro 65W (2-pack, $49.99) delivers 65 W from either USB-C port and still gives a MacBook Air 45 W when several devices are plugged in. The ESR 100W station also fits, but it costs more and suits an Apple-heavy desk.
