# Best travel charger with multiple USB-C ports

**Short answer:** Of the records we have, the Baseus GaN5 Pro 65W (2× USB-C + USB-A, $49.99 for a 2-pack) is the one that fits a travel bag. It gives 65 W from either USB-C port on its own, and 45 W + 20 W when two devices are plugged in, with foldable prongs. The ESR 100W 6-in-1 and the Baseus Nomos 140 W are desk stations, not travel chargers. We have no Anker or UGREEN products in our records, so we can't compare them.

## Baseus GaN5 Pro 65W Fast Charger, 2× USB-C + USB-A (2-pack): fits

- **Ports:** 2× USB-C, 1× USB-A; USB PD 3.0, PPS, QC 3.0 (PPS fast-charges Samsung Galaxy phones)
- **One device:** 65 W from either USB-C port, 60 W from USB-A
- **Two devices:** C1 + C2 gives 45 + 20 W; C1 + A gives 45 + 18 W
- **All three ports:** C1 gets 45 W, while C2 and USB-A share 15 W
- **Travel features:** foldable prongs, GaN design (92% efficiency claimed)
- **Caveat:** with two or three devices connected, the laptop port drops to 45 W. That is enough for a MacBook Air but not full speed for a 65 W laptop. Use C1 for the device that matters most.
- **Price:** $49.99 at Baseus (official US store), sold as a 2-pack, so you get one for home and one for the bag.

## ESR 100W 6-in-1 Charging Station with MagSafe + CryoBoost: not a travel pick

This is a desktop station with MagSafe, Apple Watch and AirPods pads, plus 2× USB-C and 1× USB-A. It is aimed at Apple-heavy desks. It does have two USB-C ports, but it is not designed for a bag. With the pads charging, a laptop gets at most 65 W (45 W if the second USB-C is also in use). The full 100 W needs the pads idle and one port in use.
