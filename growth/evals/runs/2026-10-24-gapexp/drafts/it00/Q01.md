Both power banks in our records are listed as carry-on legal. The best pick for most flyers is the **Baseus Blade H1 (20,000 mAh, 100 W)** at $89.99. Power banks go in carry-on bags, not checked bags. Our records only state each bank's airline status and watt-hours. They do not cover the specific rules of any airline, TSA or FAA, so check your airline before you fly.

## Baseus Blade H1 Digital Display Power Bank, 20,000 mAh, 100 W

**Best for:** laptop users who want one bank for laptop plus phone and need it to stay airline-legal.

- Capacity: 20,000 mAh (about 74 Wh nominal). Airline carry-on: yes, under 100 Wh.
- Output: 100 W USB-C PD. Baseus says it takes a MacBook Pro to 50% in 30 minutes.
- Ports: 2× USB-C (in/out), 2× USB-A (out). Pass-through charging: yes.
- Recharge: 60 W max input, about 1.5 hours to full. A 100 W USB-C cable is in the box.
- Size and weight: 134 × 134 × 18 mm, about 451 g. An image on the page says 1.08 lb (about 490 g).
- Display: digital.
- Caveats: it replaces the Blade HD, which Baseus no longer sells. Per-port wattage and the split when several devices charge at once are not published.

- **Laptop on the plane:** the Blade H1. The PicoGo's 45 W output is lower than the Blade's 100 W, and our records don't say it is meant for laptops.
