**Yes, a 20,000 mAh power bank can go on a flight if it is under 100 Wh, and the Baseus Blade H1 is listed at about 74 Wh nominal, so it falls under that limit.** The Blade H1 (20,000 mAh, 100 W output) is marked airline carry-on legal in its record and costs $89.99. These records only cover Baseus products and the specs listed. They don't include the TSA or FAA rules themselves, so check your airline's current policy before you fly. Power banks are spare lithium batteries, so keep them in your carry-on, not checked bags.
