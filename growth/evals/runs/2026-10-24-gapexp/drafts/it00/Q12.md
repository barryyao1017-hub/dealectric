# What's the difference between Qi2, Qi2.2 and MagSafe?

**Short answer:** In these product records, the practical difference is speed and labeling. Qi2.2 products are rated up to 25 W wireless (the ESR CryoBoost 3-in-1 stand and the Baseus PicoGo AM52 power bank). The Qi2 product here, the ESR HaloLock car charger, is rated 15 W. "MagSafe" appears as a compatibility or style label on magnetic 15 W products, and one of them doesn't state Qi2 certification at all. The records don't cover the full technical standards, so this page sticks to what the listed products show.

## How the labels show up in practice

- **Qi2.2:** 25 W magnetic charging, but only on newer iPhones. The ESR stand needs an iPhone 17 series, 16 Pro Max or 16 Plus on iOS 26 for 25 W. The Baseus bank needs an iPhone 16 or later (not the 16e).
- **Qi2:** the ESR HaloLock car charger lists a WPC Qi2 certificate (Qi-ID 21376) and 15 W.
- **MagSafe-compatible / MagSafe-style:** magnetic charging at 15 W. ESR's basic pad is described this way, and its page doesn't state Qi2 certification.
