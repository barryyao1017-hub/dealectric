## soundcore Boom 2 Plus Outdoor Speaker

- **Water:** IPX7, floats.

## soundcore Motion X600: not the right pick

The Motion X600 is IPX7 and sounds great (50 W, 5 drivers, LDAC, spatial audio), but it is built for indoor listening and patio use where fidelity matters more than loudness. It has the shortest battery life of the three (12 h at ¼ power with Bass Mode off) and a slow ~6 h charge. It is also not listed as floating, and it lacks the rugged rating of the Boom 3i. For the pool or beach, choose one of the Booms instead. If you want it for the patio, it is $199.99, or $160 with AFSC20 (last tested 2026-10-21): https://dealectric.com/go/soundcore-motion-x600
