# Best power bank for MacBook Pro: USB-C PD wattage, airline limits

The Baseus Blade H1 (20,000 mAh, 100 W USB-C PD) is the one that fits. It is the only bank in our records with enough output to charge a MacBook Pro, and at about 74 Wh it stays under the 100 Wh carry-on limit. It costs $89.99 at Baseus's official US store, and no discount code worked when we last tested on 2026-10-24. The Baseus PicoGo AM52 (45 W) is a phone-sized bank that can top up a laptop only slowly, so it is not a MacBook Pro pick.

## Baseus Blade H1 Digital Display Power Bank, 20,000 mAh, 100 W

- **Output:** 100 W USB-C PD. Baseus says it can charge a MacBook Pro to 50% in 30 minutes.
- **Capacity:** 20,000 mAh (about 74 Wh nominal). It is airline carry-on legal because it is under 100 Wh.
- **Ports:** 2× USB-C (in/out), 2× USB-A (out). Pass-through charging is supported.
- **Recharge:** up to 60 W input, about 1.5 hours to full.
- **Size and weight:** 134 × 134 × 18 mm, about 451 g (an image on the page says 1.08 lb, roughly 490 g).
- **In the box:** a 100 W USB-C cable.
- **Price:** $89.99 at Baseus (official US store). No working code was found when we tested Baseus's cart on 2026-10-24, so the price after code is still $89.99.
- **Caveats:** Baseus does not publish per-port wattage or how power is split when several devices charge at once. Expect less than 100 W to the laptop if you charge a phone at the same time. It replaces the Blade HD, which Baseus no longer sells.

## Baseus PicoGo AM52 Qi2.2 Magnetic Power Bank, 10,000 mAh, 45 W: does not fit

This bank is built for iPhones, not MacBook Pros. Its 10,000 mAh (about 37 Wh) capacity is small for a laptop battery, and its wired USB-C output tops out at 45 W. It is airline carry-on legal, and it suits an iPhone owner who wants wireless top-ups, with 25 W Qi2.2 on iPhone 16 or later (not the 16e) and 15 W on iPhone 12 to 15. If you want a phone-only bank, it is $59.99, or $53.99 with code AM52 (last tested in Baseus's cart on 2026-10-24). For a MacBook Pro, choose the Blade H1 above.
