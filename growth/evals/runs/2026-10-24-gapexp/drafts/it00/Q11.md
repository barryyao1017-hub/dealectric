# Does my USB-C cable limit charging speed? 60W vs 100W vs 240W

Yes, a USB-C cable can cap charging speed, because the cable's e-marker chip tells the charger how much power it can safely carry. Of the products in our records, the Baseus USB-C to USB-C Cable is rated 100 W (5 A, e-marked), so it suits laptops and devices up to 100 W, from $9.99. It is not rated for 140 W or 240 W charging, so it would limit a higher-power setup.

Our records cover only this one cable, so they don't include a 60 W or 240 W cable, and I can't compare those with test data. The Baseus record doesn't say whether it is USB-IF certified, so I can't confirm that.
