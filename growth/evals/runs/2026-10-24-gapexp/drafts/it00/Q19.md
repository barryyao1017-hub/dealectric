# Can a portable power station work as a UPS for a computer?

**Short answer:** Some can. The records list a UPS switchover time for six stations: 10 ms on the BLUETTI Elite 300, ALLPOWERS P1800 and BLUETTI Elite 30 V2, up to 15 ms on the BLUETTI Elite 200 V2, and 20 ms on the BLUETTI AC180 and AC200L. For a laptop or small desktop, the Elite 30 V2 ($275.50 after code) is the cheapest option with a 10 ms rating. The ALLPOWERS P1800 ($395.10 after code) gives 1,024 Wh and 1,800 W at the same 10 ms rating.

**What we can't tell you:** We have no EcoFlow or Anker records, so we can't compare them. Our records also don't include any manufacturer statement on whether a given PC tolerates these switchover times, so check your computer's power supply specs and the station maker's guidance before relying on one. A station that charges from the wall while powering your computer is also a different use than a standard UPS, so confirm that pass-through use is supported.
