# BLUETTI AC180 review: price in 2026, battery capacity and limitations

**Short answer:** For weekend trips, power-tool charging and short outages, the BLUETTI AC180 is a reasonable buy at $499 ($474.05 with code AFF5OFF). It has 1,152 Wh of LiFePO4 capacity rated for 3,500+ cycles, and an 1,800 W inverter that runs most kitchen appliances. Its main limits are that it can't be expanded with a regular expansion port and that $499 is the station alone. If you want similar output for less, the ALLPOWERS P1800 is $395.10 after code, with trade-offs noted below.
