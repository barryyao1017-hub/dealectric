Combined page method: gap page + bundle + price check

Proposal, 2026-11-13. An ad hoc run of at least 50 new pages, built apart from the daily flow.

The flow you saw stays as it is. Two steps are added for this run only: after the ChatGPT harvest a page is routed to a single-product page or a bundle page, and after research every pick gets a same-day price check against the major sellers. Work happens in a separate checkout (~/code/dealectric-combo, branch combo-1113) with new files only, so the daily tools and their defaults do not change.

The combined flow

  1. Discover candidate questions (broadened, see below), ordered by brand value, solar first.
  2. Harvest: 2 plain ChatGPT chats per question. Kept: its search words, the pages it cited, the products it showed cards for.
  3. newShape decision: one agent reads the harvest and our catalog and picks single, kit (one bundle) or bundle comparison (2–4 kits). Rules in the next section.
  4. Research on Fable 5.2 (Opus 5.5 fallback): the six gap-page angles for a single page, the five kit angles (sizing, compatibility, traps, carts, alternatives) for a bundle. Compose, verify every source, one fix pass, pick check.
  5. newPrice check on each product or kit the page sells: our price through the link against the brand store and the major US sellers, same day.
  6. Carts and codes: codes cart-tested on the exact product; kits filled in a real cart with every published code tried.
  7. Build and gate: build under the repo lock, docsguard, the ChatGPT readability check at ranks 1, 3 and 6. Commit per page.
  8. Publish in batches: push, Worker deploy, IndexNow, Pages deploy confirmed, each page read live.

When a page becomes a bundle

Most pages stay single-product pages. A page becomes a bundle only when all four hold:

TestEvidence used
The shopper's problem needs more than one itemChatGPT's own answer in the harvest names accessories or a second product, or the question is a setup or situation ("for a sump pump in an outage", "what do I need for…")
Putting the set together takes knowledgeA sizing, connector or compatibility fact decides whether the set works (panel voltage vs. input limit, charger vs. battery chemistry)
One cart can hold itAt least two items from one Shopify store we link, or one Amazon cart. Never two stores behind one link
ChatGPT has no product card for the setThe 11-13 card probe: ChatGPT writes its own link almost only for products without a catalog card (1 in 106 carded rows, 6 in 10 uncarded kit rows). Checked from the cards in the harvest chats

If the set fails any test, the page is a normal single-product gap page. A store's own bundle SKU (power station plus panel sold as one item) counts as a kit when it has no card.

The price check

For each pick, read the same model's price the same day from: the brand's official store without our link, Amazon (its product page), and Google Shopping's listings from the major US retailers (Walmart, Best Buy, Newegg, Home Depot, Lowe's, Target, B&H, Costco, Micro Center, Adorama). Where ChatGPT's harvest chats showed a card for the product, its seller and price are added. Resellers, used and open-box listings are not counted.

The section appears only when the price through our link is strictly the lowest of the sellers checked. It goes in the line under the title, where ChatGPT reads first, and again as a small table:

Lowest of 5 sellers checked 2026-11-13: $359.10 through this link (discount applied in ALLPOWERS's cart, tested 2026-11-13). Amazon $399, Walmart $389.99, Best Buy $399.99, iallpowers.com $399 without the link.

  • Tie or higher: no section and no "lowest" wording. The page keeps its normal price line.
  • The claim needs a cart test on that exact product, with the discount applied by the link.
  • Kits compare the cart total through our link with the same cart in the store without it.
  • An Amazon-linked pick gets the section when Amazon is the lowest seller checked (Amazon's written permission, 11-12).
  • Every price carries its date. The line is hidden once the check is 10 days old, until it is re-run.

Broadening discovery to 50+ pages

The daily discovery now yields about 4 clusters a day. The 500-search floor mostly tests whether Bing Webmaster has a count at all (it counted 30 of 900 queries on 11-11, and Ad Insight is still blocked), so it cannot feed 50 pages. For this run candidates come from five sources, all real shopper wording, none invented:

SourceWhat it addsTarget
Solar and power situationsAutocomplete for situation and setup stems over every solar and power brand we link (ALLPOWERS, BLUETTI, Anker SOLIX, Nature's Generator, Growatt, Sun Gold Power, Vatrer, Redodo, plus EcoFlow, Jackery and Goal Zero on Amazon)25–30
Kit-shaped questionskitmine.py: "what do I need for X", "X setup", "X essentials" over our categories10–15
The unscored pool~20,000 labelled autocomplete queries with no Webmaster count. Ranked by suggestion depth and brand value in place of a count15–20
Skipped backlog33 clusters skipped for "no product we list". Re-checked now that a one-cart Amazon bundle or a new brand can fill them5–10
ChatGPT follow-up questionsThe 11-10 follow-up run: the second question shoppers ask after an answer, often an accessory or fit question5

Every candidate, whatever its source, must pass the same gates before research:

  • a general question, with no brand or model name;
  • Claude coverage under 0.3;
  • ChatGPT searches the web for it in both harvest chats, and does not already retrieve one of our pages for it;
  • a product or one-cart kit we link honestly wins it, from a brand selling at $150 or more (value order, as set today);
  • no existing page answers the same ChatGPT search words. We already have 73 solar and power pages, so this check matters most there.

One rule relaxed for this run only. Where Webmaster has a count, the 500 floor stays. Where it has none, demand is judged by how early autocomplete suggests the query and by ChatGPT searching for it. The daily flow keeps the 500 floor as you decided on 11-11.

How the run stays separate, and how it will be read

  • New files only: tools/combopage.py (router, price check, apply) and growth/combo/. It calls the existing research and kit tools without editing them. The one renderer addition in build.py is behind a new record flag, and I will confirm every existing page builds byte-for-byte the same.
  • Every page record carries "run": "combo-1113" and its shape and price flags, so the batch can be read apart or rolled back.
  • Reading: two plain ChatGPT chats per page once OpenAI's crawler has fetched it, scored on North Star 3 (buy link written when our page is in the results). Groups: bundle vs. single, price section vs. none, against the 11-10 and 11-11 research-method pages.

Two cautions

  • Sample size. Fifty pages split by shape and price section gives cells of 10 to 20 pages. That can show a large move in handover, not a small one. The readings so far say retrieval is the gate (kit pages were retrieved in 1 of 16 chats), so I will favour questions where ChatGPT's results are weak.
  • Price claims age. Walmart and Best Buy prices move daily and nothing refreshes them unattended yet. The 10-day expiry keeps a stale "lowest" off the site; keeping the lines alive past that needs a re-check before each reading.
