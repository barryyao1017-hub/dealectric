Buy-link pilot: make our link the cheapest way to buy, and prove it on the page

View. The seven to-dos describe one mechanism. ChatGPT treats buying as a separate step: it compares sellers and picks the lowest price from one it trusts. Our link wins only when it is the cheapest way to buy that exact item and the page proves that in its first 500 characters. DLC-237 is the gate. DLC-239, 240 and 243 supply the proof, DLC-241 gets us into the buying search, DLC-242 covers bundles, and DLC-238 adds products that qualify.

Most of our coded products do not qualify. Fixing page copy can't change that. More codes and kits can (DLC-238, 242).

1. Where our link is actually cheapest

A new tool, tools/buycheck.py, reads Google Shopping in the Agent Chrome. It compares our price after the link's discount with the lowest trusted seller: the brand's store and major US retailers. Marketplaces and used listings don't count. All 105 coded products were checked on 11-11.

Amazon was dearer on every one of the first 11 where it lists the same item. We read Amazon's prices to choose products only, never to publish them. All 31 pilot products were cart-tested on the exact item on 11-11.

Two things the cart tests caught. UGREEN runs a store-wide cart offer ("Spend $79+, Save $8", "$199+, Save $20") that stacks with our code. On those 4 products the line shows what the cart charges on both sides, for example $81.99 through the link against $121.99 without it, not the $129.99 list price. Maono's store gives everyone an automatic 20% / 15% off, and our code adds nothing on top, so Maono became a no-saving control. The old Mac's daily sweep hasn't recorded a test since 11-07 (DLC-251).

4. How we'll call it

  • Where to buy: buy-link handover when our page is in the results, on the 30 treated products after each page is re-fetched, at 2 chats per product, against the pooled 9 of 55 (16%). Batch 1 alone was 3 of 20. A clear lift would be about 30% or more over 55+ retrieved chats (roughly 2 standard errors). Vatrer, a saving with no comparison line, and Maono, neither, are the side checks.
  • Plain questions (North Star 3): handover on the 2 new pilot pages and, from 11-17, the A/B pages that qualify, against matched pages whose pick has no real saving.
  • What it can't separate yet: whether the saving or the comparison line does the work. Every pilot page has both. Only the A/B pages after 11-17 have enough retrieval to split them.
