## What this says

1. **Discount questions suit Dealectric better than "best X" questions.** Assistants go looking for codes, find unverified lists on coupon sites, and say so. A page of codes we have *checked at checkout*, with the date and the products they apply to, is exactly the source they're missing. The site already promises "verified offers"; this is where that promise counts.
2. **We hold a code ChatGPT can't find.** Twice it found no Soundcore code (D01, D06) while AFSC20 sits on our product pages. Our pages aren't being found for these questions: there's no page about codes, so nothing on the site matches "Soundcore discount code".
3. **Affiliate-exclusive codes fix the leak from the first probe.** ChatGPT's shopping cards link straight to the merchant, so a click-based affiliate link earns nothing. A code tied to our publisher account earns the commission when the shopper types it at checkout, however they reached the store. D10's "AFFEV5%" suggests BLUETTI already hands out codes like this.
