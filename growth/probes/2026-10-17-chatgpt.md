## What this says

1. **Small niche sites get cited, not just big publishers.** Solar Power Auditor, ExploringOffgrid, Tested Watts, Wire & Watts, powerstations.reviews, Portable Energy Lab, GeekBitz, TechWise, Smart Gadget Kit, asumetech. A new site with sharper data can take these spots. Audio is the exception: it's RTINGS / SoundGuys / What Hi-Fi territory, and hard to win.
2. **Power stations are the best opening.** BLUETTI is already the top pick for Q18 and Q20, and every power-station question is answered from small blogs. The questions are about sizing and runtime (fridge, CPAP, UPS, solar watts), which is exactly what normalized Wh / W / recharge data can answer better than prose.
3. **Qi2 / Qi2.2 is the second opening.** Baseus and ESR already show up (Q04, Q05, Q09, Q10), the spec is new and confusing, and the cited sources are thin.
4. **ChatGPT's shopping cards link straight to the merchant** (bluettipower.com, baseus.com, soundcore.com) with no affiliate tag. Even when our brand wins the answer, we earn nothing unless the shopper clicks through from a Dealectric page that's cited. So being the *cited source* is the thing to win, not just having our brands picked.
