Reading it

  • The money is in solar. Orders run $478–$1,791 and both brands give us a cart-tested code to carry. That is where ChatGPT hands over our link and shoppers buy.
  • Everything else is reach without revenue. It's cited and visited, mostly via ChatGPT, but shoppers rarely click through and nobody has bought. My guess, not tested: at these lower prices, ChatGPT's own product cards with store links win, and many of these products carry no code.
  • Blind spot: 74 of these products link straight to Amazon, so their clicks never reach our log. Amazon reports 0 orders since the account went live Nov 3.
