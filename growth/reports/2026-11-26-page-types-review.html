Which page types ChatGPT sends shoppers to

A review of every page hypothesis since 23 Oct, the 420 comparison pages they produced, and the pipelines that build them, scored against real ChatGPT shopper visits, clicks and sales in D1.

Bottom line: page count has stopped buying traffic. Since 14 Nov ChatGPT visits have held at about 25–30 a day while the site added 130 comparison pages. One page shape works (size or budget: "best X under $N", "best N Wh kit"), and it works outside solar too. The best pages of that shape, the solar kit comparisons, lost almost all their visits on 19 Nov. Sales did not drop with them, because most sales come from ChatGPT quoting our code, not from page visits.
  1. Size/budget pages draw 6× more visits than "best X for Y" or question pages (0.58 vs 0.10 per page in the last 8 days, pages at least a week old). It holds in solar and in non-solar clusters.
  2. Five page families are near zero: openness-first gap loop, gapmine demand×coverage, combo, open-query and catalog-gap pages. 266 pages, 30 visits in the last 8 days.
  3. The three solar kit comparison pages went from 10+ visits a day to 1–3 on 19 Nov, Diagnosed 26 Nov: ChatGPT kept ranking and citing them and kept writing our buy links into answers; shoppers stopped opening the pages, most likely because Black Friday changed what they asked. Separately, gpt-6-2 (since about 23 Nov) ranks them lower.
  4. 47 of 63 sales came with no click (code typed at checkout, mostly BLUETTI DEALECTRIC). Visits are a weak proxy for money; "does the answer carry our code and price" is the better one.
  5. Building is hand-driven. Every batch since 13 Nov ran through a one-off wrapper in a Claude session. The only unattended discovery path (gapmine → gappage) is drafts-only and feeds the page shape that performs worst.

ChatGPT buy-link clicks did not fall with the visits: about 17 a day through 18 Nov and 20 a day since (ALLPOWERS and BLUETTI still 99 of 160). Sales ran 31 in 19–26 Nov. So ChatGPT kept writing our links and codes into answers while shoppers stopped opening the pages.

Diagnosis (26 Nov)

ChatGPT did not stop finding or citing these pages. Shoppers stopped opening them while still clicking the buy links in the answers. Ruled out:

  • Counting. D1 logs untagged views too. On these pages they are a steady handful of monitoring bots on rotating networks, before and after the drop. No shoppers are hiding there.
  • Crawl. OAI-SearchBot fetched each page about every 6 hours throughout, so ChatGPT's stored copy stayed current.
  • Our edits. The 2000 Wh page's opening was the same up to the drop (same pick, price $853 → $913). Its big rewrite came on 21 Nov, two days after.
  • Retrieval. Chats on 20–22 Nov (gpt-6) ranked the 2000 Wh page first and cited it in 6 of 6. Panels-included was top 3 in 6 of 6.
  • Our links in answers. Buy-link clicks written into answers held at about 12–13 a day. ChatGPT's live fetches of our /go/ links rose from 62–83 a day to 89–137 a day.

Most likely, but not provable because shopper prompts can't be seen: in Black Friday week, people asked deal questions rather than "best 2000 Wh kit".

A separate, newer change: gpt-6-2

Since about 23 Nov the ChatGPT app answers with gpt-6-2. I re-asked the 21 kit questions from 17–18 Nov (29 chats). Our pages are still retrieved (26 of 29) and cited about as often (41% vs 45%), but they rank lower: top 3 in 5 of 29 chats, against 24 of 42 before. gpt-6-2 returns about 17 results instead of 12, and puts Black Friday lists from Popular Mechanics, PopSci, Bob Vila and TechRadar above us. One of those was in the top 5 in 18 of 29 chats. Our own pages also take 2–5 slots per chat in 17 of 29, competing with each other.

Whether rank recovers once the Black Friday lists age out is DLC-362, due 3 Dec. Readings that compare against gpt-6 baselines now mix two models.

What I would do next

  • Diagnose the kit drop before building more kit pages. Run DLC-297's reading now, plus the stored-copy dates. If our edits caused it, it is the cheapest traffic to win back.
  • Build only size/budget pages, in brands with codes and sales. Stop new situation, question, open-query and combo pages. Keep the old ones live, but take them out of the hand fact-checks to cut maintenance.
  • Score pages by the answer, not the visit. Track whether where-to-buy answers carry our code, price and link per product. That is where sales come from.
  • Fold the six wrappers into one config-driven abpage run, and point the unattended gapmine path at size/budget queries for brands with sales, or turn it off. As it stands it feeds the weakest shape, which works against North Star 2.

Method and limits

  • D1 dash_hv / dash_hc / sales, read 26 Nov. A visit is one browser (user agent, country, ASN) per day. Totals: 538 ChatGPT visits, 63 sales rows since 29 Oct.
  • Counts are small. Most pages have 0–3 visits, so per-family rates move a lot on a handful of pages; the shape and vertical splits are directional.
  • Shape and vertical are inferred (slug patterns; the majority category of a page's product rows). Pages published 21 Nov or later are left out of the 8-day columns.
  • Chat findings come from earlier sessions' runs and notes (DLC-208, 250, 283, 320–322; the 14 Nov top/bottom analysis); I did not re-run them.
