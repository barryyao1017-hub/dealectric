# Outreach research brief (fast loop step 7b, tier 3)

Outreach is the Promote tier that matters most (Barry, 2026-11-01). The research subagent gets this brief plus the
page's URL, target queries, shared core and open concern. It researches and drafts only; nothing is posted.

## What to find

Places where a real person is deciding, right now, on a purchase our page helps with, and where a full answer
(with our link only where the venue allows it) settles their question.

## Selection rules, in priority order

1. **Fresh.** Search newest-first: sort subforums and Q&A by latest activity, use search engines' past-week and
   past-month filters, and add the current month and year to queries.
   - Tier A: a post in the last 14 days.
   - Tier B: last post 15–45 days ago and the question is still unresolved.
   - Older threads only if they rank on page 1 of Google or Bing for a target query (still being read). List them
     separately as "evergreen" and give the rank you saw.
   - Record for each: opened date, last-post date, reply count, views if shown.
2. **Genuine demand for products we list.** Score each candidate:
   - 3: the asker (or a recent follower in the thread) is about to buy, is undecided between products we cover or
     names our category and budget ("which 2 kWh station for my 30 amp trailer, under $1,000?").
   - 2: owns or has ordered one and has an open question our page answers, and others in the thread are shopping.
   - 1: a technical question with no purchase in view.
   - Drop 1s unless the thread is evergreen with high views. Quote the words that show the demand.
3. **Active venue.** Prefer forums, subforums and communities with current traffic on the topic (count the new
   threads on it in the last 30 days and say how you counted). A dormant forum's fresh thread ranks below an active
   forum's.
4. **Still open.** Nobody in the thread has already given the answer our page gives. Say what is missing.
5. **Link rules known.** Read the venue's rules before drafting. Where links to sites we profit from are banned
   (RV LIFE forums, most brand forums), the reply carries no link and no site mention.

Articles with a factual error (correction notes): only ones published or updated in the last 60 days, or ranking on
page 1 for a target query. Give the date and the rank.

## Where to look

Owner and brand forums, Quora, product Q&A on brand and retailer pages, blog comment sections, community Q&A
(Stack Exchange), and the venues the target queries' result sets surface. Reddit and Facebook groups are blocked for
our tools: list the subreddits or groups worth a manual check, with the search Barry should run, instead of guessing
their contents.

## Output (`growth/focus/fastloop-runs/<run>/outreach.md`)

1. A table sorted by demand, then freshness, then venue activity: URL, venue, opened and last-post dates, tier
   (A/B/evergreen), demand score with the quote, what's missing, link rules, action (reply with link and disclosure,
   reply without link, correction note, skip).
2. Drafts for the top candidates. Each reply answers the asker's actual question fully on its own, in their terms,
   with specifics from our page's research; discloses the affiliation where it links; never reads as an ad.
3. What was checked and rejected, and why (stale, no demand, already answered, rules), so the next run doesn't redo it.

## Re-runs

Run it again at each live check (step 8) while the page is being promoted: new threads appear weekly. Start from the
previous `outreach.md` "checked" list and look only for threads newer than its date.
