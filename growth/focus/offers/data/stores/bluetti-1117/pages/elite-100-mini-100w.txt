Review
Compact enough to take anywhere. Capable enough to matter.
At 23.6 lbs and the size of a small carry-on, the Elite 100 mini is a compact portable power station built to fit where larger units don't — in the back seat, beside your camping gear, or in the corner of your RV. You still get reliable LFP battery technology and a 6,000+ cycle lifespan, just in a form that's much easier to take along.
1kWh Power in a Letter-Size Footprint.
At just 9.3 × 8.5 inches, this 1kWh portable power station is small enough to slide into an RV compartment, tuck into a closet, or ride in the back seat without taking over the space. Its slim, upright profile makes it easy to move — home to car to campsite — so backup power is always where you need it, never in the way.
Right-Sized Power for What Matters Most.
Keep a portable camping fridge cold for 34+ hours. Run camp lights for up to 55 hours. Charge your phone 40+ times. Keep Wi-Fi online for nearly two days during an outage. Whether you're camping, traveling, or preparing for a short blackout, the Elite 100 mini gives small appliances and everyday essentials the power they actually need. You don't need more power. You need the right power, ready when it counts.
9 Ports. Power What You Need, All at Once.
3 AC outlets, 6 DC outputs, and dual USB-C ports (140W + 65W). Keep the portable fridge running while your phone, laptop, camera, and drone charge at the same time — no unplugging, no waiting your turn. Built for camp and everyday use, within a 700W total output.*
 *Total output not to exceed 700W. Actual performance may vary by device combination.
Less Space to Store. More Years to Use.
The UltraCell™ LFP battery inside the Elite 100 mini is built for 6,000+ charge cycles — that's up to 17 years* of reliable power. With 50 built-in safety protections and 20 rigorous validation tests, it handles daily use, weekend trips, and middle-of-the-night outages without skipping a beat. 
*Based on one full charge cycle per day. Cycle-life results are based on laboratory testing under specified conditions. Actual battery life varies by usage, temperature, charging habits, and storage conditions.
Recharge at Home, on the Road, or Off-Grid.
80% in 90 minutes at home. Solar power at camp. 12V/24V vehicle charging on the road. Or pair with BLUETTI Charger 1 for a boost while you drive. Four ways to recharge — so wherever the day takes you, you're never stuck waiting long. Charging times are based on laboratory testing under optimal conditions. Actual performance varies with input power, sunlight, temperature, battery level, and other conditions.
Power Out? You Won't Even Notice.
The built-in 10ms UPS* kicks in the moment the grid goes down — fast enough that your desktop, Wi-Fi, CPAP, or aquarium pump never misses a beat. Work through the outage. Sleep through the night. Keep the routines that matter running. 
*UPS performance may vary by device. Confirm power and UPS requirements of medical or highly sensitive equipment before use.
Smarter Backup Starts Here.
Light When You Need It Most.
Your power, always in control.
Warm light for the campsite. Cool light for the workbench. SOS mode when it really counts. The built-in LED light bar runs up to 264 hours* — so whether it's a power outage or an unexpected night out, you're never left in the dark. 
*Maximum runtime is based on laboratory testing in the specified mode. Actual runtime varies by brightness setting, battery level, temperature, and other conditions.
