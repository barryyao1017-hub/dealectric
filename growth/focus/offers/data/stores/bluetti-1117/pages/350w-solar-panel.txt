Unleash 350W of Solar Power
Charge your compatible power station faster with a powerful 350W output, helping you spend less time waiting and more time enjoying your adventures, RV trips, outdoor events, or reliable home backup.
Compatible with BLUETTI Power Stations and More
Designed with industry-standard MC4 connectors and a 33V open-circuit voltage (Voc), this solar panel is compatible with a wide range of power stations, including BLUETTI models and many third-party brands.
When paired with a BLUETTI power station, advanced Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) technology automatically maximizes solar charging efficiency, delivering faster, more stable, and more reliable charging performance.
Engineering Excellence Meets Solar Innovation
Built to perform in demanding environments, this solar panel combines durable materials, reinforced fiberglass, and a premium ETFE coating with an IP67 weather-resistant rating to withstand rain, dust, and harsh outdoor conditions. Designed for exceptional durability and long-lasting performance, it's ready to power your adventures for years to come.
Designed for Maximum Performance and Convenience
Featuring industry-standard MC4 connectors for quick setup and easy compatibility, this solar panel makes connecting to your power station effortless. The adjustable kickstand helps capture optimal sunlight throughout the day for more efficient charging, while the foldable design makes storage and transport easy—perfect for camping, RV trips, off-grid adventures, and emergency backup.
