BLUETTI RVSolar 5kW Integrated Power System
Smarter Energy for Life on the Road & Off the Grid
A Simpler Way to Build RV Power
No more guesswork, mismatched components, or wiring headaches. The RVSolar is an all-in-one solar power system built specifically for life on the road, designed to just work the moment you turn it on.
A Complete RV Power System in One Kit
Stop Building Your RV Power System Piece by Piece.
The RV5 replaces separate inverter, inverter charger, solar controller, alternator charger, DC system, and protection units with one integrated platform, reducing wiring complexity and improving system reliability.
Ready in Hours, Not Days
Start your RV journey sooner with less stress. Pre-configured components take the pain out of installation, saving you days of tedious labor so you can hit the road faster. Plus, BLUETTI's expert support is also available whenever you need help.
Stay Cool. Power Everything Else
With 5,000W continuous AC power, 10,000W surge, and 1,620W DC output, you can run your A/C, fridge, water pump, and electronics at the same time. Enjoy the comfort of full hookups even when you are miles off grid. 
Note: For 12V auto-leveling systems with startup currents over 100A, we recommend pairing with a dedicated B1210 battery or your vehicle's existing 12V lead-acid battery.
80% in 50 Minutes with Shore Power.*
Recharge your system through shore power, solar, your vehicle alternator, a generator, or an EV charger** so you can stay powered whether you are parked, driving, or boondocking.
*Charging time varies by conditions. 
**EV charging cable sold separately.
Built for Real RV Use
An IP65-rated battery and a vibration-resistant build mean it holds up fine on the road, even over rough highways and long travel days. And at under 50dB, it runs quietly in the background, so you can relax inside your RV without the constant noise of a loud fan.
Track Your Whole Setup at a Glance
Monitor battery status, power usage, charging inputs, and system health from one touchscreen or your phone. Clear visibility makes it easier to stay in control and troubleshoot faster.
Your RV, Unbound. Your Adventure, Uncompromised
Effortless Off-Grid Living With the BLUETTI RV5 Power System
