Why Choose BLUETTI Elite 100 V2?
Go Light. Power Big
At just 25 lbs and 17L, the Elite 100 V2 is ultra-compact and easy to carry with its hidden handle. Whether you're off camping, hitting the road, or prepping for outages at home, it's the power you can always count on.
Charge Your Way – Up to 80% in Just 45 Minutes via AC
On-the-Go Charging – Full in 1.8 Hours
Fast Solar Charging – 1,000W MPPT, Full in 70 Minutes
AC Turbo Charging – 45 Minutes to 80%
Dual Charging – AC + DC, 1,200W Max.
Recharge via BLUETTI Charger 1 while driving. No more battery anxiety — just constant power on the road.
Built to Last, Engineered for Safety
Powered by durable LiFePO₄ cells, the Elite 100 V2 delivers up to 4,000 charge cycles for over a decade of dependable use—even in tough outdoor conditions.
Power It All, Wherever You Are
Outdoor Ready
Home Backup

Whisper-Quiet at Just 30dB
The Elite 100 V2 operates at only 30dB under 600W—quieter than a library or a gentle breeze. No hums, no buzz—just uninterrupted power that lets you sleep soundly, focus deeply, or relax fully.
Instant Backup, Zero Disruptions
With a 10ms UPS switchover, the Elite 100 V2 keeps critical devices—like medical equipment, servers, and payment terminals—running smoothly during unexpected outages.
