Meet BLUETTI APEX 200
Apex 200 is a versatile, expandable portable power station designed for home backup, RV travel, and off-grid use. It delivers 2.56 kWh of capacity and 3,200W of continuous AC output on its own, with up to 3,600W available under supported conditions.* 
Compared with selected mainstream 3kWh portable power stations, Apex 200 is 20% lighter and 50% smaller by volume, making it easier to move between your home, RV, and campsite.* Add compatible expansion batteries when you need longer backup.
Big Enough For Home Backup And RV Power
With 2,560Wh of capacity, 10 output ports, and 3,200–3,600W of continuous AC output, Apex 200 can power multiple compatible home essentials and RV devices at once.*
Start Small. Scale Big. Power More.
Start with 2.56 kWh for home essentials like your refrigerator, lights, and Wi-Fi. If an outage lasts longer, add compatible B300K or B500K batteries for more runtime. When you need to run more household equipment at once, connect multiple Apex 200 systems using the required Hub A1 and compatible accessories—scaling up to 99.84 kWh of capacity and 6,000W of combined output.* Build on the system you already have instead of replacing it as your power needs grow.
Up To 3,000 W Dual-Input Solar Charging
Bring in more solar energy and recharge faster off grid. Apex 200 supports up to 2,000W through the high-voltage MC4 input and up to 1,000W through the low-voltage XT60 input, reaching 80% in about 50 minutes and a full charge in about 1.7 hours under specified conditions. The 60–280V high-voltage MPPT range supports compatible panels connected in series, helping simplify solar wiring.*
RV Power, Built Right In.
Connect a compatible RV through the TT-30R outlet, and power 12V gear such as a refrigerator, water pump, lights, and fans through the 12V/30A Anderson output.
Monitor, Control, And Schedule Your Power.
The BLUETTI app does more than show battery level and charging status. Control supported AC outlets individually, schedule when devices run, stop nonessential loads at a selected battery level, use TOU mode to charge during lower-rate hours, and receive severe weather alerts with the option to enable automatic charging. Compatible with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Home Assistant.
Smart AC Outlet Control 
Turn supported AC outlets on or off remotely, set schedules, or stop nonessential loads at a selected battery level. 
Charge When Electricity Costs Less 
Set TOU charging in the BLUETTI app to help reduce charging costs during lower-rate hours. 
Get Severe Weather Alerts 
Receive alerts and choose whether to enable automatic charging before a potential outage.

Product Notes 
AC Output: 
Standalone continuous output is 3,200W. Up to 3,600W continuous output requires compatible expansion batteries or supported AC/solar input conditions. 
Energy Density:
 Compared with selected 3 kWh-class power stations, 20% refers to gravimetric energy density (Wh/kg) and 50% to volumetric energy density (Wh/L), not direct weight or volume reductions. 
Solar Charging: 
Up to 3,000W combined input (2,000W high-voltage + 1,000W low-voltage) requires compatible solar configurations. Actual charging performance varies with sunlight, panel setup, and operating conditions. 
Battery Expansion:
 Each Apex 200 supports up to six compatible expansion batteries. Maximum capacity of 99.84kWh and combined output of 6,000W require three Apex 200 units, eighteen B500K batteries, Hub A1, and compatible accessories, sold separately. 
Fast Charging: 
Maximum AC charging power requires a compatible high-power AC connection and specified charging cable. Actual input depends on outlet, circuit, and configuration. Charging times are based on BLUETTI internal testing and may vary. Some charging methods require additional accessories. 
UPS Backup: 
UPS mode must be enabled in the BLUETTI app. Automatic switching applies to properly connected devices. Transfer performance varies by operating conditions and may not meet every device's requirements. 
Smart AC Control: 
Outlet control, scheduling, and battery-level automation require compatible settings in the BLUETTI app. Feature availability may vary by firmware and connectivity. 
Battery Life & Safety:
 Cycle-life figures are based on BLUETTI laboratory testing to 70% of original battery capacity. Actual results vary. UL 94 5VA applies only to specified materials or components, not the complete system.
