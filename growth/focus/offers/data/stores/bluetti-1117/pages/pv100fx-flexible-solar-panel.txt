FAQS
Q1: Is the PV100 FX solar panel waterproof?
A:
Not fully waterproof. The PV100 FX is IP67 water-resistant, meaning it can handle splashes and brief exposure to water. However, avoid leaving it in the rain or submerged for extended periods.
Q2: Do flexible solar panels require regular cleaning?
A:
Yes, regular cleaning helps maintain optimal performance. Dust or dirt can block sunlight and reduce efficiency. Simply wipe it with a soft cloth or use water spray, ensuring no scratches are left behind.
Q3: What's the difference between flexible, foldable, and glass solar panels?
A:
Flexible panels:
 Lightweight, thin, and bendable, ideal for curved surfaces like RV roofs or boat decks.
Foldable panels:
 Designed for temporary use and easy transport.
Glass panels:
 More fragile and less versatile for installation, but typically offer higher power outputs.
Flexible panels, like the PV100 FX, are easier to install and less prone to damage.
Q4: Can the PV100 FX work with other solar chargers or MPPT controllers?
A:
Yes, as long as its open-circuit voltage (Voc) and current match the input requirements of your charger or controller.
Q5: Can the PV100 FX be used with other solar panels?
A:
 It is not recommended, as this may impact the efficiency and power output of your whole solar system.
Q6: Can I connect multiple PV100 FX panels together?
A:
Yes, you can connect them in series for higher voltage or parallel for higher current. Make sure the total open-circuit voltage (Voc) and current stay within your charger's specs.
The RV Solar System supports up to 16 PV100 FX panels for a total of 1,600W solar input.
Q7: How to install the PV100 FX on an RV or boat?
A:
Use adhesive, straps, or ropes through the corner holes for a secure installation. For step-by-step instructions, watch the RVSolar Installation video on the BLUETTI Support YouTube channel.
Q8: Can the PV100 FX be used in an off-grid home setup?
A:
 Yes.
Q9: Can I step on the PV100 FX after installation?
A:
We recommend 
avoiding stepping on the panel 
as it may damage the solar cells, reduce performance, or cause permanent damage.
Q10: Can I use the PV100 FX to charge third-party power stations?
A:
Yes, as long as the third-party power station:
1. Uses MC4 connectors.
2. Has compatible voltage specifications that match the output of the PV100 FX.
