Why the solar pages win

Dealectric · 14 Nov 2026 · 1,013 saved gpt-6 chats from 7–13 Nov, plus 14 fresh chats run today with screenshots

It is not luck. When ChatGPT retrieves a solar or power-station page it cites us 75% of the time; for a NAS page, 21%. Both figures come from 151 retrieved chats. The gap is in the competition, and it has two parts.

The catalog. When OpenAI's shopping catalog has a card for the product, the card supplies the name, price and buy button, and the answer needs no web source for that row. Solar kits and bundles mostly have no card, so ChatGPT writes the row itself and cites the page it took the price from.

The web results. For NAS questions the other results are the makers and the software projects' own docs (QNAP, Synology, UGREEN, Jellyfin, Frigate), and they take the citations. For solar questions no maker answers a cross-brand question, so the other results are small comparison sites, and we are the best-sourced of them.

The four winners were cited in all 8 chats, with no buy-link handover. In three of them ChatGPT linked our page as the place to compare or buy the product that had no card.

Two of the three losers were cited today, 3 of 6 chats against 0 of 21 before. All three were rebuilt with the research method in this morning's push (12:58 UTC), and the Jellyfin answer repeats a fact that only the rebuilt page carries (the HuC firmware setting), so the new copy is probably what ChatGPT read. I could not confirm that from the crawl log, which ends on 13 Nov here. What did not change: all 22 product rows in those six chats were catalog cards. A better page can earn the source pill against cards. It did not earn the buy slot.

In one charger chat ChatGPT did mention our offer, then added: "Its pricing is affiliate-linked, so I'd compare the final checkout total against the manufacturer's direct listing." Where a card exists, our lower price is treated as a claim to check, and the card's price as the fact.

What I would change

  • Gate new pages on two readings from the harvest chats we already save: the share of product rows with a catalog card, and whether a maker or official doc sits in the results. Build where rows are mostly un-carded and no primary source answers the question.
  • Lean into what the catalog cannot card: kits and bundles, brand-store-only products, and questions about a measured property across brands. The NVMe page shows this works outside solar.
  • Stop adding NAS pages. 41 pages, retrieved in 78% of chats, cited in 21% of those, no handover. The answer is supplied by cards and makers whatever our copy says. Existing pages can stay; they cost nothing.
  • The nearest handover win is the un-carded row. In three of today's chats ChatGPT used our page as the purchase link for the product with no card. It linked the page, not the buy link. This is a hypothesis from a handful of chats, and it is where I would test next.
  • Read the B replication with this split. Today's rebuilt losers suggest the research method lifts citations even on carded questions. If reading 3 confirms it, citation rate and sales potential come apart on those pages, so score handover separately for carded and un-carded pages.

Limits: 7 to 8 saved chats per page; categories assigned by keywords in the page address; today's run is two chats per page, so it illustrates the pattern and does not measure it.
