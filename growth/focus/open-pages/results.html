Which power station can run a CPAP for the most nights on one charge?

Page
Rebuilt. /power-station-for-cpap already ranked stations by nights per charge, so its record moved from compare.py to gaps.py (URL unchanged) and was rebuilt with the research method. The week-of-camping CPAP page names one CPAP model and was left as it is.
Pick
Anker SOLIX S5000, $2,599.99 on ankersolix.com (re-read 15 Nov). No code: the pick line gives the store price only.
Lead fact
About 10.7 nights from one box at a 40 W CPAP. Arithmetic: 5,024 Wh × 0.85 ÷ ((40 W + 10 W idle) × 8 h). Idle about 10 W measured by pv magazine USA; Anker states 8.8 W. 40 W covers ResMed's measured 34 W with humidifier and heated tube (ResMed's PDF, read 15 Nov).
Also on the page
Inverter idle draw decides it: the 5,040 Wh Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus idles at 58–85 W and gets 4.3–5.5 nights. Expanded systems are shown with their battery counts (S5000 + 1 battery about 21; DELTA Pro Ultra X + 2 batteries 17.2; Apex 300 + 2 B300K 14.7), plus the DC route through ResMed's converter.
