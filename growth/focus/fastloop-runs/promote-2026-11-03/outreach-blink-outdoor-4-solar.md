## Read this first: the page's core claim is out of date

**Blink now sells an official solar accessory for the Outdoor 4: Blink Solaris.** Blink's own FAQ (support.blinkforhome.com/en_US/faq-blink-solaris/blink-solaris-faq) says "Blink Solaris is compatible with the Blink Outdoor 2K+ and Outdoor 4", combines a solar panel and a rechargeable battery pack in one unit, attaches directly to the camera (the AAs come out), is IP65-rated when attached, and "cannot be mounted separate from camera". Best Buy sells it alone at $39.99 (SKU 6674947, "Accessory for Outdoor 4 and Outdoor 2K+"); Amazon ASIN B0FKPPTHSX; a price tracker gives a release date of 2026-05-20; NZ launch coverage 2026-06-04 (NZD 69 standalone). The Blink Solar Panel Mount FAQ still says Outdoor 4 is unsupported and does not mention Solaris, which is why the page missed it.

So the page's sentences "Blink sells no such panel for the Outdoor 4" and "a third-party panel is the only way to add solar to an Outdoor 4 without buying a camera bundle" are wrong as of today, and the H2 "What Blink's own mount does" is true but incomplete. **Every draft below mentions Solaris honestly; none repeats the page's claim.** If a draft is posted while the page still says "only route", the reader clicks through to a page that contradicts the reply. Recommendation: fix the page first (add a Solaris row: official, $39.99, clicks onto the camera, no separate placement, mixed early charging reviews), then post.

Also answerable now, from Blink's FAQ (support.blinkforhome.com/f-a-q/outdoor-4-battery-extension-pack-faq): the **Battery Extension Pack is compatible with the Outdoor 4** (and 2K+), is weather-resistant, claims up to 4 years, and "You can not plug a USB power cable into the Outdoor 4 while the Battery Extension Pack is attached", so it cannot be combined with a USB-C solar panel. That closes the page's "could not verify" item.

Two smaller discrepancies for the page: the generic panel the page prices at $27–30 (EverExceed, Amazon B0CRD6L3LP) showed $19.99 single / $37.99 two-pack today with a 2000 mAh battery and 4.3 stars from 1,411 ratings; and Wasserstein's Amazon listing (B0CM91YXP4) carries a "Frequently returned item" badge (4.3 stars, 95 reviews, $39.99 there vs $36.99 on wasserstein-home.com).

### Draft A: reply to "New Solaris for Outdoor 4 / 2K Camera" (Amazon forum, row 1)

> Two things worth knowing before you give up on solar for the Outdoor 4.
>
> First, Blink's own troubleshooting for Solaris is thin: the FAQ says to put it in direct sun for 45 minutes and check that the app shows "Charging" under the accessory; if it never does, "Blink Solaris is not charging your camera." It also says in-app battery and charging status is only shown for the Outdoor 2K+, so on an Outdoor 4 you may not get a reliable readout even when it works. Solaris clicks onto the camera and cannot be mounted separately, so if the camera sits in shade the panel does too.
>
> Second, the alternative is a USB-C panel with its own battery that you mount where the sun is. Wasserstein's panel for the Outdoor 4/3/XT2/XT is $36.99 on their US store today (list $48.99; 2-pack $63.99), with an internal battery, a 13.1 ft cable, a 360° mount and the USB-C adapter the Outdoor 4 needs. Generic 4 W USB-C panels with a 2000 mAh battery run about $20–30 on Amazon. The trade-offs: you take the two lithium AAs out (they don't recharge; the camera runs off the panel's battery day and night), and the USB-C port cover comes off, so the panel's plug is what seals the port. Blink's support page says the Outdoor 4 is no longer weather-resistant with the cover off, and the seal is the panel maker's claim, not Blink's. Wasserstein gives 30 days to return and a 3-month warranty, so a shaded or stubborn camera is a cheap test.
>
