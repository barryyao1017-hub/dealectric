## A weekend, in watt-hours

| Load | Per day |
|---|---|
| 12 V compressor fridge | about 700 Wh |
| Furnace blower, cold night | 300–500 Wh |
| LED lights, phones, water pump, fan | about 200 Wh |
| Total | 1.2–1.5 kWh |

A station delivers about 85% of its rating as AC, so 2 kWh gives about 1.7 kWh: a day and a half. A microwave (1,000–1,500 W) costs about 25 Wh a minute. A rooftop AC is out: about an hour per 2 kWh, and it needs a soft starter to start at all. Keep a two-way fridge on propane; on 120 V it uses 2.4–3.6 kWh a day.

## Recharging while you camp

- **Solar:** a 400 W panel returns about 1–1.5 kWh on a good day. AC200L and Renogy take 1,200 W, Elite 200 V2 1,000 W, Anker 800 W, Jackery 400 W.
- **Driving:** a DC-DC alternator charger adds 560–800 W (BLUETTI Charger 1 $219, EcoFlow 800 W $269). The 12 V socket gives about 100 W, a full day for a full charge.
- **Campground or home outlet:** 1–1.5 hours on a 15/20 A outlet.
