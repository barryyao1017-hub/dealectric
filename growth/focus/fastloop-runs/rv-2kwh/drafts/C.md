## What to look for

- **Continuous output, not just capacity.** 2,200–2,600 W runs a fridge, microwave or coffee maker, one big load at a time.
- **LiFePO4 battery** for 3,000–6,000 cycles.
- **An RV outlet (TT-30)** so the shore cord plugs in without an adapter.
- **Recharging:** fast AC charging (about 1–1.5 hours), solar input, and car charging.
- **Weight:** 40–62 lb in this class.
- **Warranty:** 5 years is standard here.
