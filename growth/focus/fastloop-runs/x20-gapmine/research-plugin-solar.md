# Research: plug-in (balcony) solar with a battery, US, 2026-11-05 (Fable agent)

## Pain points
1. Is it legal where I live, and is the plug safe? (6 of 7 threads: backfeed, 20 A circuit, anti-islanding, insurance.)
2. Does 400–800 W pay back? (5) EnergySage: an 800 W kit makes 70–110 kWh a month, $13–20 a month at $0.18/kWh,
   $40–50 in CA/NY/HI; kit $500–1,500; payback 2–5 years.
3. Is a battery worth it? (4) Most plug-in setups have none. A battery adds about $1,000 per kWh and rarely pays back
   (Zendure's own EU math: self-consumption rises from about 50% to 80%).
4. Landlord, HOA or lease (3).
5. Railing mounting without drilling, wind load, low output from vertical panels (3).

Segments: (A) renter in a state with a plug-in law → plug-in kit; (B) renter elsewhere, or the landlord says no →
power station plus panel, no grid connection; (C) wants night-time use or backup → battery system, weak economics.

## Law and certification
- States with laws (Bright Saver tracker): UT (in force 5/2025), ME, MD, VT (7/2026), CO (8/2026, 1,920 W cap),
  CT (10/2026); CA, NH, VA (1/1/2027); NJ (3/1/2027). NY awaits signature.
- California SB 868, signed 2026-09-30, effective 2027-01-01: 1,200 W AC cap per home, UL-or-equivalent certified
  device (pv magazine).
- UL 3700 launched in January 2026. Certified so far: Hoymiles HiFlow Pro microinverter (July 2026), APsystems EZ1,
  and the Mango Power S kit (Intertek, September 2026, the first whole kit).

## Honest winners (none are partners)
- Plug-in, renter in a law state: **Bright Saver 360 W kit**, $432 member + $29/yr (180 W $299 member; $399
  non-member). Hoymiles HiFlow Pro LV, UL 3700; 11 lb glass-free panels zip-tied to a railing; 120 V plug; 12-year
  inverter warranty; ships only to law states. https://www.brightsaver.org/balcony-solar-kits
- Bigger patio: **Mango Power S 1,200 W kit**, $2,399 (search snapshot), the only whole kit with UL 3700.
- With a battery: **EcoFlow STREAM Ultra**, 1.92 kWh, 800 W (1,200 W with the microinverter), UL 1741 / IEEE 1547,
  $1,279–1,599 (snapshot). EcoFlow's page limits sale and installation to Utah (other snapshots list UT/MD/CT/CO).
- Everywhere else, or the landlord says no: **power station + panel, no grid connection**, legal in every state.
  The agent's example was the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus + 220 W panel at $919 member / $1,099. Our solar-kit records are
  the same category.
