# Research: Soundcore AeroClip 2 vs C50i, and UGREEN Nexode Pro vs Anker Prime, 2026-11-05 (Fable agents)

Reddit was blocked from both agents; the evidence comes from reviews, Slickdeals and makers' pages.

## AeroClip 2 vs C50i
Pain points: call and mic quality (6 sources, the main separator); fit while running (5); comfort over hours (5);
sound leakage (4); bass (5); glasses (thin, all positive).
- **Calls, office, commute: AeroClip 2** ($169.99, https://www.soundcore.com/products/d1105-aeroclip-2-clip-on-earbuds-with-clearest-calls).
  Guinness-certified highest speech-quality score (G-MOS) for open-ear clip-on earbuds, certified 2026-08-31; 4 MEMS
  mics + 2 bone-conduction VPUs; Expert Reviews calls it head and shoulders above rivals for calls. 8 h music /
  5 h calls, 32 h with case; IP57 (buds); BT 6.0, LDAC; 6.2 g; multipoint; wireless-charging case. A firmer clamp
  ("a little tight" past 1–2 h); one reviewer's right bud slipped in exercise.
- **Running, gym, value: C50i** (https://www.soundcore.com/products/d1101-c50i-comfortable-clip-on-earbuds). $39.98
  on the clip-on collection page (compare-at $69.99); the product page shows $69.99 with a $30 on-page coupon. 7 h /
  28 h, IP55, BT 6.0, 5.5 g, multipoint. No slipping over a 10K (Slickdeals owner), multi-hour calls without pain
  (PCWorld). Leaks sound at about 50% volume (GIGAZINE; Soundcore support calls leakage unavoidable).
- Field: AeroClip gen 1 $129.99; C40i $49.99; Shokz OpenDots One $199.95 (sold out on shokz.com); Bose Ultra Open
  $279.
- Code: AFSC20 (20% off full-price items) should apply to the full-price AeroClip 2 but is untested on that SKU;
  not valid on the on-sale C50i. Cart test failed from this laptop (us.soundcore.com 301s on plain HTTP).

## Nexode Pro vs Anker Prime
Pain points: does the laptop drop or reset when a phone is plugged in (most frequent; more reports against Anker's
line: Slickdeals, Framework forum); heat and throttling at 140 W sustained (Anker 160W → ~110 W after ~25 min,
adamtalks; UGREEN retractable 140 → ~120 W after ~25 min, Cult of Mac); coil whine (Anker 250W only); real split
(both about 100 + 60 W with two devices); size and weight.
- **Desk, MacBook + phone + more: UGREEN Nexode Pro 160W 5-Port** (SKU 65636,
  https://us.ugreen.com/products/ugreen-nexode-pro-160w-usb-c-charger): $89.99, code UL65636 printed on the page,
  **cart-tested 2026-11-05: $89.99 → $64.99** (control rejected). C1/C2 140 W PD 3.1, C3 100 W, C4 30 W, USB-A 22.5 W;
  about 360 g; display; foldable plug. Notebookcheck: laptop held 56–58 W with four other devices over a 1-hour test,
  warm not hot.
- **One-laptop travel: Anker Prime 160W 3-Port A2687** (anker.com $149.99 list / $115.99 sale; Amazon B0F9NK4M7Q):
  220 g, 65×52×35 mm, 3× USB-C each up to 140 W, no USB-A, no cable; throttles to ~110 W after ~25 min at full load.
- Other UGREEN tiers: 100W 4-port display (SKU 95405) $64.99 with UL95405; 100W 3-port (15202) $74.99 with UL15202;
  65W Ultra-Slim (15817) $55.99 with UL15817. Anker Prime 100W A2688 is sold out on anker.com; Prime 67W A2669 $49.99.
