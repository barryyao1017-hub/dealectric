## 3. Winner per segment

**Segment 1 — one-piece case with built-in card slot (no separate wallet to lose).**
Winner: **Smartish Wallet Slayer Vol. 1** — 4 cards + cash in a spring-loaded side slot, $29.99 on sale ($34.99 list,
Smartish store snapshot), lifetime warranty. Deciding fact against it: **no wireless or MagSafe charging at all**
(Smartish's own copy: "wired is your most dependable path"). If the shopper charges wirelessly, the honest pick is a
MagSafe-compatible leather 3-card case: **Mujjo Full Leather Wallet Case for iPhone 17 Pro**, $55.20 sale ($69 list,
mujjo.com snapshot), 3 card pockets, MagSafe-compatible (retailer listing; wireless charging with cards loaded
UNVERIFIED). Bellroy's 3-Card Phone Case is the premium alternative (US price UNVERIFIED; AU$119 list). ESR has no
case with a built-in slot for the iPhone 17 line that I could find (UNVERIFIED — the US store was unreachable; the EU
"iphone-case-with-wallet" collection lists magnetic wallets).

**Segment 2 — case + detachable MagSafe wallet.**
Winner: **ESR Geo Wallet Boost (HaloLock)** — 20 N52 magnets, ESR-rated 3,200 g, 5 cards (or 4 cards + 3 bills),
built-in Apple-certified Find My (real-time location, play sound, left-behind alerts; wirelessly rechargeable),
RFID-blocking, $31.99 (list $52.99) on ESR's US store. Deciding facts vs Apple FineWoven Wallet ($59 Apple list,
UNVERIFIED; CDW $63.99, Walmart $58): 5 cards vs 3, 3,200 g claimed vs an estimated ~1,000 g, and roughly half the
price. Trade-off: no stand; the Find My module needs recharging (ESR does not publish battery life in the snippets
found — UNVERIFIED). For a stand, **ESR Geo Wallet Stand (HaloLock)**: 1–3 cards, fold-out stand, Find My, $39.99
(list $49.99), magnet "3,000 g" per retailer listings (UNVERIFIED).

**Segment 3 — minimalists (1–3 cards) vs folio users (cash + ID).**
Minimalist: **ESR Geo Wallet Stand** (1–3 cards, stand, Find My, $39.99) or Apple's wallet for people who want the
Apple look and don't carry it bare in a tight pocket. Folio/cash: **Smartish Wallet Slayer Vol. 1** (4 cards + cash,
$29.99) wins on capacity per dollar; **OtterBox React Folio** (iPhone 17/17 Pro/Pro Max) is the cover-style folio but
holds only one card or a little cash (OtterBox UK copy; US price UNVERIFIED, £34.99 UK / €39.99 EU). Nobody in this
category gets cash + ID + wireless charging in one piece; that is the trade-off the page should state.

## 5. Not recommended / UNVERIFIED

- **Apple FineWoven Wallet carried bare in a tight pocket**: the falling-off theme is strongest here, it holds only 3
  cards, and it blocks wireless charging while attached — only its Apple-native Find My and look justify $59.
- **Spigen Slim Armor CS**: discontinued for iPhone 16 and later (replaced by non-wallet Slim Armor MagFit).
- **OtterBox Strada**: no iPhone 17 version; last listed for iPhone 14.
- **Peak Design Mobile Wallet on a non-Peak-Design case**: Gear Patrol rates the magnet "Medium"; MagBak's comparison
  ranks it weakest of four (competitor source); MacRumors users got a replacement with the same hold.
- **MOFT magnetic wallet**: multiple reports of sliding off, and a MacRumors user's parking passes were wiped in one.
- **Smartish Wallet Slayer for anyone who charges wirelessly or on MagSafe**: it cannot.
- **Any wallet for hotel keys / transit cards**: keep low-coercivity cards out of every magnetic wallet (Apple's
  charger warning; MacRumors reports).
