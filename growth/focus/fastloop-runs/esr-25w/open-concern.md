## 1. Top block

**Reason our pick wins (178 chars):** A Qi2.2 25W stand with its 50W adapter in the box, so an iPhone 17 gets the full
25W out of the box; Apple's own MagSafe Charger needs a separate 30W-or-higher adapter.
- ESR page: "50W adapter included", "25W Qi2.2" (esrtech.com, 2026-11-01).
- Apple: "up to 25W when paired with a 30W power adapter", adapter sold separately
  (https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MGD74LL/A/magsafe-charger-1-m); Apple 30W adapter $49
  (https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MY1W2AM/A/30w-usb-c-power-adapter).
- Macworld confirms the 50W brick, "the fastest wireless charger we've tested"
  (https://www.macworld.com/article/2927742/esr-cryoboost-3-in-1-magnetic-25w-charging-station-qi2-2-review.html).
- Not the cooling claim as lead: ESR's 6.5°F is ESR's own figure; 9to5Mac and Macworld could not check it.

**Open concern: heat and throttling at 25W (283 chars):** 25W wireless charging makes an iPhone 17 warm, and iOS slows
or holds charging above 80% when the battery gets too warm (Apple); ESR's fan cuts phone temperature by 6.5°F on average
(ESR's claim) and Macworld clocked 0–50% in just over 30 minutes, the fastest wireless charger it had tested.
- Apple: "if the battery gets too warm, software might limit charging above 80 percent" (https://support.apple.com/HT211829).
- Owner threads (5): MacRumors 2467080, 2485409, 2477370; Apple Community 256145659; iphonesoft.fr 2026-01-11 citing
  Reddit reports of 45°C after 20 minutes.

**Runner-up: an old puck never reaches 25W.** Apple's tiers (HT211829): the 2020 MagSafe Charger (A2140) is 15W; A2580,
A3250, A3502, A3503 do 25W with a 30W+ adapter (a MagSafe Charger bought since Sept 2024 already does 25W); plain Qi
7.5W. A bigger adapter cannot raise a 15W puck (https://www.anker.com/blogs/wireless-chargers/iphone-17-magsafe).

## 2. Product facts: ESR CryoBoost 3-in-1 Magnetic Charging Station (25W, Qi2.2), US plug, black

- $109.99 (regular $129.99), in stock, esrtech.com 2026-11-01; $90.20 with ECTRIC18 (cart test 2026-11-01). ESR's
  banner shows BOO15 (15% sitewide), BOO20 / BOO25 (any 2).
- 25W Qi2.2 pad; 50W USB-C adapter and cable included (ESR, Macworld, 9to5Mac). Cable length unverified.
- Cooling: fan, "cuts heat by an average of 6.5°F" (ESR); fan 25 dB with an on/off button (Macworld).
- Measured: 0–50% in just over 30 min on an iPhone 16 Pro vs ~45 min on ESR's 15W stand (Macworld); 0→~60% in ~30 min
  on iPhone 16/17 with iOS 26 (9to5Mac, https://9to5mac.com/2025/12/15/esr-delivers-near-wired-charging-speeds-with-its-new-qi-2-2-3-in-1-charging-station/).
- Watch: detachable MFW-certified fast-charge module; AirPods pad.
- Compatibility (ESR): MagSafe iPhones (12 or later), iPhone 17e/16e and Galaxy S23 or later with a magnetic case under 2 mm.
- Dimensions not on ESR's page; Macworld measured 3.4 × 3.3 × 6.6 in, 11 oz. Not tilt-adjustable.

Which iPhones get 25W (Apple HT211829, iPhone 17 and Air spec pages):
- 25W: iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, 16 Plus, 16 Pro Max, with a 30W-or-greater adapter.
- 20W: iPhone Air. 22.5W: iPhone 16 and 16 Pro (third-party Qi2.2 needs iOS 26). 15W: iPhone 17e and iPhone 15 or earlier.
- Macworld: 17e 0–100% wireless 3 h 03 min vs 2 h 06 min for the 17.
- Corrections to our record: the 15W CryoBoost stand is $59.99 (not $79.99); "about 20–22 W" for 16/16 Pro is 22.5W (Apple).

## 4. Other candidate concerns (by frequency)

1. Heat / throttling (above).
2. Old puck stuck at 15W: check the part number (A2140 = 15W).
3. Adapter wattage: 30W or more (15V/2A); the 3-in-1 stands ship with 40–50W, Apple's puck with none.
4. Which models get 25W (above).
5. Case thickness: ESR needs a magnetic case under 2 mm.
6. Stops at 80%: Charge Limit or Optimized Battery Charging (https://support.apple.com/en-us/108055), or heat.
7. Battery health at 25W: no independent evidence that 25W vs 15W changes wear; heat is the risk.
