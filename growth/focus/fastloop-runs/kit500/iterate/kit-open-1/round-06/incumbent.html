What a 288 Wh solar generator runs

A 288 Wh station charges a phone 20–25 times, runs a 15 W laptop for 15–18 hours, a 40 W fan for 6–7 hours or a CPAP without its humidifier for one night; it will not run a fridge for long, a kettle or a heater. The 600 W inverters (Elite 30 V2, RIVER 3 Plus) run a 500 W appliance; the 300 W ones do not. For a fridge or longer outages, size up to 1 kWh.

How long a 100W solar panel takes to refill it

A folding panel delivers about 70–80% of its rating in full sun (Popular Mechanics measured 50–75%), so a 100 W panel puts back about 75 W: roughly 4 hours of full sun for 288 Wh, a day or more in winter or cloud. Plan on about 340 Wh a day from 100 W and 675 Wh from 200 W with 4.5 peak-sun hours. The station's solar input caps what more panel can add: 200 W at 12–28 V on the Elite 30 V2, 650 W at 11–60 V on the P1800.
