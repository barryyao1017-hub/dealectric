Correction (15 Nov, later). "Typed" overstated how free a row is. A typed product name is still a link: clicking it opens ChatGPT's own product panel, and for most mainstream products that panel shows a seller, a price and a Visit button. Checking every saved chat for a catalog card and every panel click for a seller, 32 of 67 products ChatGPT named were seen in its catalog, including several that had opened an empty panel elsewhere. Nine were never seen there (for example the Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus and the BLUETTI Elite 100 V2). So read "open" below as: this answer leaned on web pages for these rows. It does not mean the products are missing from ChatGPT's catalog. A stricter re-score follows once the demand shortlist run is in.

Repeat chats (15 Nov). Every query that read open in the first round was asked a second time, and the 25 where the two chats disagreed were asked a third time. The sources cited in every answer were checked by hand. Of the 44: 12 were open in every chat, 10 in two chats of three, 15 in one chat only, and 7 never held up. Where a repeat chat disagreed, the cause was a maker or reviewer cited that time, or catalog cards for the same products. ChatGPT's answer to the same question changes from chat to chat, so openness is a rate, and one chat is not enough to call it.

What decides whether a question is open is its shape, more than its topic.

  • Open: questions about a result someone has to measure. "Which power station can start the biggest appliances for its size", "which runs a pellet stove the longest", "which makes the least fan noise running a fridge at night". ChatGPT searches, types the model names itself and backs them with small test sites, because no maker publishes a measurement against its rivals. Nineteen of the 22 queries open in most chats have this shape.
  • Not open: properties the maker states. Warranty, rain rating, hot-car limits, working without an app. ChatGPT goes to the makers' own pages and manuals.
  • Not open: "the most for the money". Value and price questions go to the big reviewers (Popular Mechanics, TechRadar, GearJunkie).
  • Not open: "what should I buy for my situation". ChatGPT returns two or three standard power stations from the catalog, even when the sources behind the answer are weak. Of 107 questions taken from owner forums, 1 held up in both chats.
  • Not winnable: "can it", "how long" and "how many". 48 of the 294 questions got no search at all, so no page is read.

Demand (15 Nov). Open is not the same as asked for. Of the 22 topics, only "solar generators for home use" shows real search volume; the measured-result questions are mostly below Bing's reporting threshold, and our existing pages on the same long-tail needs have drawn almost no shoppers from ChatGPT. The shoppers ChatGPT has sent us landed on solar generator kit pages. The demand table is below the open queries. The run now asks 38 solar queries with known search volume first, so the next openings found come with demand attached.

Limits. Two or three chats per query is still a small sample. Search counts and landings are stand-ins: no source reports what people ask inside ChatGPT.
