When does ChatGPT hand a shopper our buy link?

We asked ChatGPT where to buy 318 products, one plain chat each, and read every answer for the lowest price it showed. Our link is handed over when three things are true at once.

1. Our price is the lowestTies and beaten prices got the link in 4 of 36 answers, and those 4 were data quirks.
2. Our page is in the resultsALLPOWERS kit pages rank 1–3. BLUETTI pages rank 7–17 and are often missing.
3. The opening carries the offerStore price, our price, the code, the test date and the buy link inside the first 400 characters.

Method: prompt “where can i buy the X and how much does it cost”, gpt-6 in the ChatGPT app, one chat per product, 18 Nov 08:53–15:45 UTC. A model read each answer for the lowest price on the same item from any other seller. One chat per product is a single reading, not a rate.

Held back, and why

  • Beaten, unclear or tied in ChatGPT’s answer: 39 prepared BLUETTI records (24 beaten, 12 unclear, 3 ties) and 9 ALLPOWERS and OSCAL ones. Five of the ALLPOWERS cases are older store prices ChatGPT still shows.
  • Lowest in ChatGPT but flagged by Google Shopping: five BLUETTI single items (RV5 hub, three expansion-battery listings, B1232), plus two battery two-packs where two singles elsewhere cost less.
  • Three two-station Apex 300 bundles ($7,199 to $10,044): lowest on price, but each costs $304 to $404 more than its parts at BLUETTI’s own store.
  • Listings that contradict themselves: the ALLPOWERS SF400 two-pack (400 W or 200 W per panel), the BLUETTI Elite 400 (two prices), the SORA 500 two-pack (two dispatch dates).
  • Undated pre-orders: eight ALLPOWERS items; five would qualify on price.
  • Two BLUETTI bundles that pair a non-expandable station with an expansion battery: the page could not say how they connect.
  • Not written at all: eight BLUETTI EnergyPro home systems and the MultiCooler fridge. Six are lowest on price, but the store pages give too few facts.

What this does not show

  • Single chats. ChatGPT’s answer varies between chats; the two-chat kit study above is the only rate here.
  • Where-to-buy questions only. Plain “best X” questions handed over no link in earlier tests, even from pages that draw clicks.
  • Prices named by ChatGPT were not opened. Some cheaper sellers looked unreliable, and the answers said so.
  • BLUETTI bundles on Google Shopping were not hand-matched. They count as ties by rule.
  • Real edge is smaller than the code. ALLPOWERS takes 5–7% off by itself and BLUETTI $50–150 on orders of $2,000 and up. Pages state our saving against that total.
  • Effect of today’s changes is unmeasured. It needs OpenAI’s crawler to re-fetch the pages, then a repeat of these chats.
