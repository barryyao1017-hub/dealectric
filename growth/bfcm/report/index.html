How I judged it

  • Search results for these queries. "Black Friday power station deals" returns 2025 roundups, brand blogs and spam pages, so there's room for a page with 2026 cart-tested prices. "Black Friday electronics deals" returns retailer landing pages (Best Buy, Samsung), which are harder to displace.
  • One section per brand, not 55 thin pages. Each brand's Black Friday info went onto its existing codes page, which already ranks for that brand's code searches. The new pages are product-type pages, which match how shoppers search.
  • Best deals by percentage off. Baseus FC41 is 41% off with its code, and UGREEN's Thunderbolt 5 enclosure is 38% off. Many brands' own headline sales go deeper ("up to 55–80% off"), but those are on products we don't list. We never claim a sale price we haven't read.
  • Prices fixed along the way. 22 records refreshed. Blackview's two prices were in GBP; they're now USD. Three products were marked sold out.
