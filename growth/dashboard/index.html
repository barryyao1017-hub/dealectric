How these numbers are counted
  • Commission: every sale CJ, Awin and Rakuten report, as recorded by the site's edge Worker (Awin within minutes, CJ every 30 minutes). Corrections are negative and included. Networks approve sales 30 to 50 days later, so some may still be reversed.
  • Orders: sales with a positive commission.
  • Visitor: one browser on one Pacific day that opened at least one page as a person.
  • Conversion: orders divided by visitors, for the same period.
  • Store click / buy-link click: a /go/ link opened by a person on the way to the store. Amazon links go straight to Amazon and are not counted.
  • Shopper: one browser on one Pacific day that opened a page or a buy link as a person. Unlike a visitor, it includes people who clicked a buy link an assistant handed over without opening one of our pages.
  • Visit: a shopper's run of page views and buy-link clicks with no gap of 30 minutes or more.
  • Last shopper activity: the newest page view or buy-link click by a person. The site logs each one within a second; the dashboard keeps an answer for up to 5 minutes before asking again, and the refresh button in Sources asks at once.
  • Left out as bots: verified bots and AI fetchers; crawler user agents; Google scanner networks; cloud-hosting and proxy networks (AWS, Alibaba, Oxylabs and others); a page opened from a VPN network with no tag and no referrer; a store click claiming to come straight from a search results page, which search engines never link; pages opened without a browser's navigation headers; prefetches; one browser opening 8 or more pages within a minute or 5 or more offers in a day; a referrer with no path, which no browser sends (a crawler on Nov 11); a Chrome user agent without the client hints every Chrome browser sends (a page monitor on rotating networks, Nov 11); untagged clicks that came from no page a person opened; our own browsers and checks.
  • Topic and hypothesis: from the page and brand records in the site's source, refreshed every 30 minutes after a change.
