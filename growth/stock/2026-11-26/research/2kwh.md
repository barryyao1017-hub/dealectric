# 2 kWh class: alternatives (DLC-358), web reads 2026-11-26

All prices and stock below are web reads from 2026-11-26. They need a same-day re-read before publishing. Pages were read with WebFetch, partly through the r.jina.ai reader, because Amazon, Growatt and iallpowers.com refuse or rate-limit direct fetches. Amazon delivery dates seen through the reader may depend on where the reader runs, so I only call an Amazon item "ships now" when the page says In Stock or a stock count.

## Bottom line

- **The Growatt INFINITY 2000 PRO family is the default winner.** It ships now from Growatt's US warehouse and earns through Awin (`awin:growatt`). Its kits come in 1× (sold out), 2×, 3× and 4× 200 W, which covers every bare-unit and panel configuration from 400 W to 800 W in both groups.
- **BLUETTI's own items don't ship sooner anywhere.** The Elite 200 V2 ships early January at BLUETTI, and the identical Amazon listing (B0DCJV9LTB, $999) showed delivery on Jan 14. The AC200L is sold out at BLUETTI and "Currently unavailable" on Amazon (B0CLGZB3L6).
- **No R2500 V2 listing turned up on Amazon US.** The bare R2500 V2 now shows "Estimated dispatch: from Dec 4, 2026", which is inside 14 days, so it may not need a line. Every R2500 V2 solar kit shows "from Dec 11, 2026".
- **The ALLPOWERS S2000 Pro gap is not acceptable as a winner, in my judgment.** It has 1,451 Wh against 1,920 Wh (−24%) and uses NMC, not LiFePO4 (2,500+ cycles against 3,500+). It derates to 2,000 W below about 60% charge, and StorageReview's sample shut off with 22% and 56% of charge left. So the five R2500 V2 kits with 100–200 W of panels have **no qualifying winner**. Every 2 kWh LiFePO4 kit that ships now is above their price bands. The S2000 Pro kit with the same panel is the conditional pick if Barry accepts the gap (same brand, ships now, about the same price).
- **No winner for Elite 200 V2 + Charger 1 or + Charger 1 + 200 W.** Nothing in stock bundles a 2 kWh station with an alternator charger inside the band. The Charger 2 bundle maps to the EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max + 800 W Alternator Charger on Amazon.
- **Pre-orders among our records and the candidates:**
  - Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 and its kit: Anker says "orders placed on or after November 26th will ship within 6–12 weeks", so they're out.
  - Anker S2000 + PS400: the record says 4–5 weeks.
  - Dabbsson 2000L and its kits: "will begin shipping gradually from December 10".
  - FOSSiBOT F2400: FOSSiBOT's own store shows "Coming soon / Pre order". Our Amazon record B0H4W51ZMH now shows a "pink" listing at $2,999 from an HK seller, so I excluded it and the record looks stale.
  - EcoFlow DELTA 3 Max and DELTA 2 Max: sold out on EcoFlow US.
  - OUPES Mega 2: sold out.

## Reviews and owner sources

- **Growatt INFINITY 2000 PRO:** coverage is thin. The Amazon listing for the same unit ([B0DP2TZY8D](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DP2TZY8D), sold by GROWATT STORE-US, $799, "Only 10 left") has a 4.4/5 owner rating from 147 ratings. Owners praise solar and AC charging speed and fridge runtime. Critical reviews mention weight, thin instructions or support, and one solar error-code report. There is also a blog review ([batteryskills](https://batteryskills.com/growatt-infinity-2000-pro-portable-power-station-2048wh-review/)). I found no lab test, and Electrek's 2024 piece is a sponsored post about the non-PRO model. This is the winner's weak spot.
- **Jackery Explorer 2000 v2:** [OutdoorGearLab](https://www.outdoorgearlab.com/reviews/camping-and-hiking/power-station/jackery-explorer-2000-v2) (Nov 2025) measured 1,710–1,740 Wh from AC (84%).
- **EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max:** [StorageReview](https://www.storagereview.com/review/ecoflow-delta-2-max-portable-power-station-review) (about 1,800 Wh usable at 350 W, per our record). For the alternator charger, [The Solar Lab](https://www.thesolarlab.com/review/ecoflow-800w-alternator-charger-review).
- **Dabbsson 2000L:** [Fstoppers](https://fstoppers.com/lighting/dabbsson-2000l-power-station-review-pushing-down-price-and-weight-2-kwh-705158) (Jul 2025, positive; criticizes the 800 W solar cap and the app needing an account).
- **ALLPOWERS S2000 Pro:**
  - [StorageReview](https://www.storagereview.com/review/allpowers-s2000-pro-portable-power-station-review) reports charge-reading problems, with shutoffs at 22% and 56%.

## Winner vs runner-up by configuration

Price band is ±25% of the original's price.

### Bare unit

**BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 ($899, band $674–1,124).** Winner: **Growatt INFINITY 2000 PRO, $799**. Runner-up: Jackery Explorer 2000 v2, Amazon $999.

Growatt ships now, costs $100 less, and matches capacity (2,048 vs 2,073.6 Wh LiFePO4). It comes within 8% on output and takes more solar (1,200 W against the Elite's 1,000 W). It adds a TT-30 outlet and expansion, and it earns through Awin. Jackery also ships now and earns on Amazon, but it costs $200 more, gives 2,200 W, and has only 400 W of solar input.

**BLUETTI AC200L ($899, sold out).** Winner: **Growatt INFINITY 2000 PRO, $799**. Runner-up: Jackery 2000 v2.

Growatt is the near-twin: the same 2,048 Wh and 2,400 W, a TT-30 outlet, 1,200 W solar, and expansion (to 6,144 Wh against 8,192 Wh). It ships now and earns. Jackery lacks expansion and a confirmed TT-30. The DELTA 2 Max is expandable, but its stock reads were poor.

**ALLPOWERS R2500 V2 ($649, band $487–811).** It ships from Dec 4, inside 14 days, so a line may not be needed. If one is shown, the winner is **Growatt INFINITY 2000 PRO, $799 (+23%)**. The runner-up is the ALLPOWERS S2000 Pro at $664.05 (same brand, but 1,451 Wh NMC with the StorageReview issues). Dabbsson 2000L ($649) ships from Dec 10, later than this unit, so it doesn't qualify.

### Unit plus about 200 W of panels

**Elite 200 V2 + 200 W ($1,199).** Winner: **Growatt + 2×200 W, $1,199**. Runner-up: Dabbsson 2000L + 210 W, $949 (pre-order from Dec 10).

Growatt's 1×200 W kit is sold out, so the in-stock option is its 2×200 W kit at the same price, with twice the panels. Dabbsson is the closer panel match, but ships-now beats a pre-order. If the page must hold panel wattage near 200 W, there is no in-stock earning option.

**R2500 V2 + SF100 / SE100 / 2×SE100 / SE200 / SF200 ($744–848).** Winner: **none**. Conditional pick: the ALLPOWERS S2000 Pro kit with the same panel, at $748.60 (SE100), $833.15 (2×SE100) or $843.60 (SE200). For the SF panels, use the SE versions, because the S2000 Pro SF kits show panel pre-order dates.

The S2000 Pro kits are the only in-stock kits within ±25%, and their battery is 24% smaller and NMC. Every 2 kWh LiFePO4 kit that ships now starts at $1,199. Dabbsson 2000L + 210 W ($949) ships "gradually from Dec 10", which is not really sooner than Dec 11.

### Unit plus about 350–400 W of panels

**Elite 200 V2 + 350 W ($1,449)** and **+ 2×200 W ($1,499).** Winner: **Growatt + 2×200 W, $1,199** (−17% and −20%). Runner-ups:
- For 350 W: Dabbsson 2000L + 420 W at $1,149, a pre-order with less output.
- For 2×200 W: Jackery 2000 v2 + 2×200 W on Amazon at $1,649, which costs more, has less output and solar input, and whose stock I couldn't confirm.

**R2500 V2 + 2×SE200 ($1,018)** and **+ SP037 400 W ($1,038).** Winner: **Growatt + 2×200 W, $1,199** (+18% and +16%), with the same 400 W of panels. Runner-up: Dabbsson 2000L + 420 W at $1,149, which ships from Dec 10, the same timing in effect.

### Unit plus about 600 W of panels

**R2500 V2 + SP039 ($1,228).** Winner: **Growatt + 3×200 W, $1,349 (+10%)**, with the same 600 W. Runner-up: Growatt + 2×200 W at $1,199, which has only 400 W.

### Unit plus about 700–800 W of panels

**Elite 200 V2 + 2×350 W ($2,148).** Winner: **Growatt + 4×200 W, $1,699 (−21%)**, with 800 W of panels. Runner-up: Jackery 2000 v2 + 2×200 W at $1,649, with only 400 W.

**R2500 V2 + 2×SP037 ($1,417).** Winner: **Growatt + 4×200 W, $1,699 (+20%)**, with the same 800 W. Runner-up: Growatt + 3×200 W at $1,349, with 600 W.

Caveat: Growatt doesn't say how four 200 W panels wire into its 12–60 V input. It sells the kit as one bundle.

### Charger bundles

**Elite 200 V2 + Charger 2 ($1,299).** Winner: **EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max with 800 W Alternator Charger, Amazon B0D1CN8MLS, $1,249**, In Stock and sold by EcoFlow Inc. Runner-up: EcoFlow DELTA 3 Max Plus + 800 W alternator at $1,449 (EcoFlow store, no stock state shown, doesn't earn).

The EcoFlow charger is 800 W against Charger 2's 1,200 W combined alternator and solar. BLUETTI's in-stock FridgePower + Charger 2 + DC Hub ($1,699) is +31%, outside the band, and has 1,800 W output.

**Elite 200 V2 + Charger 1 ($929.99, band $697–1,162).** Winner: **none**. The nearest is the DELTA 2 Max + alternator at $1,249 (+34%). Anker C2000 Gen 2 + Alternator Charger costs $1,699 and ships in 6–12 weeks.

**Elite 200 V2 + Charger 1 + 200 W ($1,448).** Winner: **none**. No in-stock kit has both an alternator charger and panels. The DELTA 2 Max + alternator ($1,249) has no panel, and Growatt + 2×200 W has no alternator charger.

### Not an alternative case

**`bluetti-2-b500k-for-ac300-ac200l` ($3,198).** BLUETTI's variant data shows it available, with no pre-order tag, so it needs no alternative line. Its host units are sold out, so it serves existing owners only.
