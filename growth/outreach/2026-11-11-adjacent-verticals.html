How this was checked, and what is still open

  • Availability and our status come from our signed-in accounts: every brand name searched in Awin's All Programs view and in CJ's advertiser search, and matched against Rakuten's full list of 1,405 advertisers. A status here is what the directory shows, so "Not applied" means the Join button is live, not that we would be approved. Awin also shows a Join button on programs that rejected us, greyed out with a reapply date; those are marked Rejected.
  • CJ and Rakuten commission is the range of terms the network lists for that advertiser. Which term we would get is set at approval.
  • Awin commission, cookies, prices, store platform and codes come from public pages (brand affiliate pages, public Awin profiles, store pages) read today. Several Awin profiles contradict themselves on cookie length or rate; those are shown as written. "Unverified" means no source confirmed it.
  • Network stats: Awin shows earnings per click and the share of tracked sales the advertiser approves. A low approval share (Himiway, Engwe, EVDANCE) means most commissions get reversed. CJ shows three-month earnings per 100 clicks.
  • Not measured: whether ChatGPT has open demand in these categories. That needs a gap-mining pass with seeds from the new brands, which only makes sense once programs are approved.
  • Program terms were not read. Price-display bans and coupon-site bans (SainSmart has one) get checked per program before applying, as in the 11-03 sweep.
  • The 11-03 sweep deliberately skipped e-bikes and white goods, which is why most of those rows are "Not applied".
