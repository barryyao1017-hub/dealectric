Where I disagree

  • Nothing we've measured shows a fresh date gets us retrieved. What we do know is that ChatGPT answers from OpenAI's stored copy, and that copy changes only when OAI-SearchBot fetches the page again. A new date does nothing until that fetch happens, and we don't control when it does. Before building steps 3–4, I'd test the stamp on half of the question pages with the citation A/B setup from DLC-208.
  • Accuracy is the stronger reason right now. All 335 question pages are less than three weeks old, so they already look fresh. Black Friday prices will move in 10 days, and the thousands of hand-written rival prices will be wrong all week. Steps 1–2 fix our own prices in time. Steps 3–4 do not.
  • The stamp has to name what was checked. A page-wide "fact-checked today" after a price-only check claims more than we did. That is the same rule the codes copy already follows ("tested daily, last passed").

What I could not verify

  • How much prices drift: there is no saved run of the weekly price check in the repo, so I don't know how many of the 397 brand-store prices are wrong today.
  • The token cost in step 4: it is based on the size of the research passes, not on a trial run.
  • How many product records are on non-Shopify stores: I didn't count them, and they make step 2 longer.
