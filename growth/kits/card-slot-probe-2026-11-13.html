DLC-223 · card-slot hypothesis · 2026-11-13

Card slot probe: a product without a catalog card gets a written link, and it's ours when we're in the results

Supported, at the level of the individual product, not the whole answer. Every answer showed catalog cards, kits included. But a product the catalog has a card for almost never got a link the model wrote itself (1 in 106 rows). A station-plus-panel kit the catalog lacks got one in 6 of 10 rows, and 5 of those 6 links were ours.

The test

24 plain gpt-6 chats in the ChatGPT app on 11-13 using cardprobe.py, which keeps the raw answer stream: each catalog card with its title and merchant, plus every link by type. Six station-plus-panel kit questions ("best solar generator kit under 500", "best 2000wh solar generator kit with panels"…) and six single-product controls (NAS, NVMe enclosure, HDMI extender), two chats each. Each product row in an answer was classed by whether the catalog drew it as a card.

  • The cards went to competitors' kits. Jackery Solar Generator 1000 v2 and 2000 v2, Anker C1000X and C2000 with panels, UDPOWER S1200 all carry catalog cards. The rows without a card were BLUETTI and ALLPOWERS station-plus-panel combos sold mainly through the brands' own stores. Those are our picks.
  • One answer showed both kinds side by side ("best 2000wh solar generator kit with panels"). Jackery and Anker got cards; ALLPOWERS R2500 V2 + 400 W and BLUETTI Elite 200 V2 + 400 W were drawn from a text query and given our links, labelled "View discounted kit" and "View kit".
  • Our page was in the results of 8 of 12 kit chats, and the model wrote our link in 5 of them. Two were /go/ buy links; three pointed to our comparison page ("View bundle comparison and offer"). Under North Star 3's definition that is 1 of 8 retrieved chats with a buy link, against a baseline of about 1 in 215.
  • Controls retrieved our page in only 1 of 12 chats. Their uncarded rows (UGREEN NAS, Synology DS224+, OWC, AV Access) mostly got no link at all; the two that did linked Tom's Hardware and AV Access. So the controls confirm the card rule but say little about our link.

What this means for pages

  • Pick products the catalog can't card. Brand-store-only bundles and station-plus-panel combos are where the buy slot is open. That also fits where sales come from: all three ALLPOWERS sales since 11-10 came through solar-kit /go/ links. A two-chat card probe on a candidate query shows whether its pick has a card, which makes it a cheap gate before building a page.
  • Make the open slot get /go/, not our article. In 3 of 5 cases the model linked our comparison page, which costs the shopper a second click. Each kit row and pick line should carry its /go/ link next to the kit's name and price, so the link the model copies for that product is the buy link.
  • The kit pilot pages are a natural next probe. Several of their picks (UGREEN travel bundles, Redodo charger kits, ESR's 3-in-1 + car bundle) may have no card. The same probe on their 11 questions would show which ones do.

Small sample: 24 chats on one day, 10 uncarded kit rows, and row classification parsed from the answer markup, so a few rows may be misread. The direction is consistent across every chat, but the rates are rough.
