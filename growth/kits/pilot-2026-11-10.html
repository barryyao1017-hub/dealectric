Kit cart pilot: six pages live, one link fills the cart

Six "what do I need for X" pages are live, each with one buy link that opens a single store's cart with every item of a researched kit and, where a code passed, the discount already applied. The hand-over reading against 8 control pages runs once OpenAI's crawler has re-fetched the pages, expected 11-12 to 11-14.

What the link does

  • Store kits (BLUETTI): dealectric.com/go/kit-… → Awin → the store's Shopify cart permalink with every variant and DEALECTRIC. Checked end to end in the Agent Chrome: it lands on checkout with every item, the code shown and Awin's click ID kept.
  • Cross-brand kits go through Amazon only: one amazon.com/gp/aws/cart/add.html link with our tag. Amazon shows a "confirm you want to add these items" page that posts to its own /associates/addtocart. Whether Amazon credits the sale can't be confirmed without a real order.
  • A single link that opens two stores' affiliate links counts as a forced click under CJ, Awin and Rakuten rules, so no kit spans two stores.
  • The daily cart sweep now re-tests every kit, so each kit's price and its "tested <date>" stay current. If a test gets no answer, the page keeps the last passing result; if an item sells out, the link stays and the price drops off.

How the questions were found

The gapmine rules (opening = demand × uncovered share) were applied to kit-shaped autocomplete stems over our brands' categories and the situations shoppers already name, plus a knowledge-depth label (0–3) and a check that our catalog can fill 2+ items from one cart.

  • Keystroke depth under-reads long "what do I need for X" questions: the podcast cluster had the strongest autocomplete push but scored 0.045. Its coverage then came out at 0.52, and CPAP camping at 0.45, so both stayed out.
  • Research ran on Opus 5.5 at high effort with five kit angles: sizing, compatibility, traps, carts and alternatives. Each page then went through compose, a verify pass, a cart test, the build and the fastloop gate at ranks 1, 3 and 6.

The reading

  • Questions: growth/focus/kit-read.json, with 6 kit questions and 8 control questions (same-week research-method pages, each with a single product as the pick), 3 plain gpt-6 chats each.
  • Metric: buy-link hand-overs among chats that had our page in the results. Our /go/ links and our tagged Amazon links both count (growth/evals/kitread.py).
  • Decision rule: kits ahead of control by at least 10 points over two readings a week apart. The baseline is about 1 in 215, so a single reading at this size only shows a large effect.
